# Luubarra Fire Dagger

**Role:** Female Elf Defiler — servant of [[Dregoth]], corrupting the Silt Stalker tribe.

**Location:** Silt Stalker tribe

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 13-14

**Stats:** Female Elf Defiler, Chaotic Evil, L10, AC2, hp24

---

## Description

Luubarra is a powerful defiler who made a pact with [[Dregoth]]. She has been subtly corrupting the Silt Stalker tribe from within, sending raiders to Giustenal where they are killed and raised as undead servants of the undead dragon king. She founded the [[Fire Dagger Clan]], the first group sent to their doom.

## Agenda

- Serve [[Dregoth]]'s cause
- Increase her personal magical power
- Send more victims to Giustenal to swell Dregoth's undead army
- Manipulate Chief [[Eevuu Silt Stalker]]

## Relationships

- **Pact with:** [[Dregoth]]
- **Created:** [[Fire Dagger Clan]] (all now undead)
- **Deceives:** [[Eevuu Silt Stalker]]
- **Allied with:** [[Caller in Darkness]] (indirectly, both serve Dregoth's ends)
