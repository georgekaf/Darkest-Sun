# Nibenay — Sorcerer-King of Nibenay

**AKA:** The Shadow King

**Role:** Sorcerer-King of Nibenay, watcher of Giustenal's activity.

**Location:** Nibenay

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 4-6, Adventure Book

---

## Description

Nibenay is one of the seven sorcerer-kings who assassinated [[Dregoth]]. He maintains active interest in Giustenal and his templars hunt for Dregoth's amulet in the adventure. His city is the primary staging point for expeditions to Giustenal.

## Agenda

- Monitor Giustenal for any signs of Dregoth's return
- Recover Dregoth's artifacts (especially the amulet)
- Maintain power in Nibenay

## Relationships

- **Enemy of:** [[Dregoth]]
- **Fellow conspirator:** [[Abalach-Re]], [[Kalak]], [[Lalali-Puy]], [[Andropinis]], [[Tectuktitlay]], [[Hamanu]]
- **Served by:** [[Nibenese Templar]]
