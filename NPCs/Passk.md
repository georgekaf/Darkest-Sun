# Passk

**Role:** Male Dwarf Warrior — drunkard with a dwarven focus on the [[Caller in Darkness]].

**Location:** Cromlin, Passk's Shack

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 19-20, Adventure Book

**Stats:** Male Dwarf Warrior, Lawful Neutral, L8, AC6, hp78

---

## Description

Passk is a dwarf with a dwarven focus: he is sworn to kill the [[Caller in Darkness]]. He is the sole survivor of his silt pirate crew, all of whom were killed by the Caller. He now drinks heavily in Cromlin, but possesses a map of Giustenal's environs and a beached silt skimmer. He is a key plot hook character for the adventure.

## Agenda

- Kill the [[Caller in Darkness]] (dwarven focus)
- Currently drowning his sorrows in drink
- May be convinced to guide adventurers to Giustenal
