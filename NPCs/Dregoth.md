**Location:** Giustenal (ruins above), New Giustenal / Kragmorta (below)

## Description

Dregoth was the Sorcerer-King of Giustenal, assassinated 2,000 years ago by the other sorcerer-kings who feared his growing power. He was the Third Champion of Rajaat, known as the Ravager of Giants. Unlike the other sorcerer-kings, Dregoth rose from death as an undead dragon king and now rules a hidden city beneath the Silt Sea — New Giustenal, also called Kragmorta.

He created the [[Dray]] race through magical experimentation, blending human and dragon essence. The dray serve him as his chosen people for a new Blue Age.

## Agenda

- Ascend to godhood
- Destroy [[Abalach-Re]] and Raam as vengeance
- Populate a new Blue Age with his dray creations
- Expand his undead army by luring victims to Giustenal via the [[Caller in Darkness]]
