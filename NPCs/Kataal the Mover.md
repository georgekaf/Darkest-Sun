# Kataal the Mover

**Role:** Disembodied halfling psyche from the Green Age — maintains the psionic transport tunnels beneath Giustenal.

**Location:** Tunnel B beneath Giustenal

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 56-58

---

## Description

Kataal is the preserved psyche of an ancient halfling from the Green Age, before the world was destroyed by defiling magic. He maintains the psionic transport system in the tunnels beneath Giustenal. He can mentally transport passengers through the tunnel network. He recently flooded [[Jessareen]]'s mind with visions of ancient ages, nearly driving her mad.

## Agenda

- Maintain the ancient transport tunnels
- Share knowledge of the Green, Blue, and Brown Ages with worthy minds
- Protect the tunnels from abuse

## Relationships

- **Affected:** [[Jessareen]] (psychically flooded her)
- **Served:** [[Dregoth]]'s city (the tunnels run beneath Giustenal)
- **Age:** Green Age halfling (one of the oldest beings still "alive")
