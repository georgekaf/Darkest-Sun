# Tenpug — Tenpug One-Arm

**Role:** Leader of Tenpug's Band — ex-slave gladiator turned artisan.

**Location:** Ancient temple near Giustenal (occupied by his band)

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 44-45

**Stats:** Male Mul Gladiator, Lawful Neutral, L8, AC5, hp62

---

## Description

Tenpug is a one-armed mul gladiator who escaped slavery in Nibenay. He leads a band of ex-slave artisans who occupy an ancient temple near Giustenal. Despite his fearsome appearance and gladiator background, he detests combat and prefers to craft pottery and metalwork. He lost an arm in the arena before escaping.

## Agenda

- Lead his band of artisans in peaceful craft
- Avoid the dangers of Giustenal
- Protect his people from slavers
