# Eevuu Silt Stalker

**Role:** Male Elf — ruler of the Silt Stalker tribe.

**Location:** Eastern Tyr region (raiding)

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 13

---

## Description

Eevuu is the chief of the Silt Stalkers, an elf raider tribe operating in the eastern Tyr region. He is wary of the dangers of Giustenal but conflicted — he listens to the prophecies of [[Luubarra Fire Dagger]] but hesitates to commit his tribe to action around the dead city.

## Agenda

- Lead the Silt Stalker raiders
- Keep his tribe safe from Giustenal's dangers
- Balance [[Luubarra Fire Dagger]]'s counsel with caution

## Relationships

- **Ruler of:** Silt Stalker tribe
- **Counseled by:** [[Luubarra Fire Dagger]]
- **Tribesmen include:** [[Fire Dagger Clan]]
