# Durex Ghodan

**Role:** Male Dwarf — tar miner, co-owner of Ghodan's Gap.

**Location:** Tar mine 2 miles south of Cromlin

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 18

---

## Description

Durex Ghodan is a dwarf tar miner who co-operates the tar mine at Ghodan's Gap with his brother [[Haltham Ghodan]]. They are free citizens originally from Raam who fled to make a profitable living mining tar for trade.

## Agenda

- Profit from the tar mine
- Maintain independence from city-state control

## Relationships

- **Brother of:** [[Haltham Ghodan]]
- **Supplier to:** Cromlin trade (including [[Hurdll Crost]])

## Ghodan's Gap

The tar mine 2 miles south of Cromlin, named after the Ghodan brothers who discovered and operate it.
