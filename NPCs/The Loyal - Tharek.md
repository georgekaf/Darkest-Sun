# The Loyal — Tharek

**Role:** Male Elf Fighter — skirmisher of [[The Loyal]], the one who stands in front.

**Location:** Roaming the wastes within 20 miles of [[BookLocations/Giustenal]] with [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 11 (the Loyal appear there, unnamed)

**Stats:** Male Elf Fighter, Chaotic Good, L5

---

## Description

Tharek fights because someone in the band had to learn how, and it was him. He is fiercely protective of Jessix and openly contemptuous of anyone who repeats Jessareen's accusation where he can hear it. He puts himself between danger and the others by reflex, which is where most of his scars came from.

## Appearance

Broad-shouldered by elf standards but still lean and long-limbed, with a hard square face, a broken nose, closely cropped dark hair and several old scars. Heavier reinforced leather armor of layered desert-creature hide, wrapped shoulders, thick bracers, dusty boots. A heavy bone short sword and a bone shortbow. Confrontational stance.

## Why this level

The Loyal are not green recruits. They went into Giustenal with Jessix twice — the first expedition reached Dregoth's templar chambers and came back with magical loot, the second cost Slinnasia her life and these five walked out of it alive. They then chose to stay, and now work the Giustenal environs full time: a Danger 5 region haunted by the [[Caller in Darkness]], ringed by tar pits and open to the ancient under-regions. Fighter 5 — a skirmisher built by years of protecting travelers; fighting is an adaptation, not the profession the source gives the Loyal.

## Agenda

- Stand between the band and whatever comes out of the ruins
- Answer anyone who calls Jessix a murderer
- Keep travelers moving while the others fight

## Origin of the name

The Campaign Book names only Jessix and leaves all five of his followers unnamed — "five young elves called the Loyal". **Tharek is a campaign-original name**, as are the personality, appearance and level recorded here. Everything the source does state is preserved: young elf preserver/thieves, witnesses to Slinnasia's death, believers in Jessix's innocence.
