# Lalali-Puy — Sorcerer-Queen of Gulg

**AKA:** The Oba

**Role:** Sorcerer-Queen of Gulg, rained fire on Giustenal's templars during the assassination.

**Location:** Gulg

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 4

---

## Description

Lalali-Puy, called the Oba, is the sorcerer-queen of Gulg. During the assassination of [[Dregoth]], she rained fire upon Giustenal's templars, preventing them from coming to their king's aid.

## Agenda

- Maintain rule over Gulg
- Prevent any sorcerer-king from achieving ascension
