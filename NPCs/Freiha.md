# Freiha

**Role:** Female [[Dray]] — tavern owner in New Giustenal.

**Location:** New Giustenal, The Pit (Area 12)

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 84-85

---

## Description

Freiha is a female dray who co-owns The Pit, a successful tavern in New Giustenal. She serves a popular alcoholic drink called munge elixir. She runs the business with her mate [[Gatlakk]].

## Agenda

- Run a profitable tavern
- Protect the secret of her munge elixir recipe

## Relationships

- **Mate of:** [[Gatlakk]]
- **Co-owner of:** The Pit (tavern)
- **Competitor of:** [[Casskka]] (Beetle's Bite Tavern)
- **Targeted by:** [[Lodgden]] (spy hired by [[Casskka]])
- **Race:** [[Dray]]
