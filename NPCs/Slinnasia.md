# Slinnasia

**Role:** Female Elf Preserver — deceased, killed by the [[Caller in Darkness]].

**Location:** Blasted Spire, Room I (corpse)

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 11-12, 38

---

## Description

Slinnasia was an elf preserver and the sister of [[Jessareen]]. She was the best friend and love of [[Jessix the Wanderer]]. She was lured to Giustenal by the [[Caller in Darkness]] and died in the Blasted Spire. Her death drives much of the NPC drama in the campaign.

## Agenda

- *(Posthumous)* Her corpse in the Blasted Spire serves as evidence of the Caller's reach
