# Abdaleem

**Role:** Male Human Silt Priest — worshipper of the Silt Sea.

**Location:** Island north of Giustenal

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 41-42

**Stats:** Male Human Silt Priest, Chaotic Neutral

---

## Description

Abdaleem is a priest of the elemental Silt Sea, a rare and strange faith on Athas. He lives on a small island north of Giustenal. He was originally from Tyr but was persecuted by [[Kalak]]'s templars and fled. He knows about the [[Caller in Darkness]] but is immune to its influence (having no psionic abilities). He hates the krag of the Blasted Spire and challenges rain/water priests.

## Agenda

- Serve and spread worship of the Silt Sea
- Destroy the krag of the Blasted Spire
- Challenge water priests' dominance
- Has no love for the [[Caller in Darkness]] but is not its target

## Relationships

- **Enemy of:** Krag of the Blasted Spire
- **Knows of:** [[Caller in Darkness]] (immune to it)
- **Fled from:** [[Kalak]]'s templars in Tyr
- **Resident of:** Island north of Giustenal
