# Moraz the Defiler

**Role:** Male Wizard — defiler in service to [[Curlonus Skaff]].

**Location:** Pirate island near Giustenal

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 21

**Stats:** Male Wizard, Lawful Evil, W10, AC6, hp26

---

## Description

Moraz is a defiler wizard who serves [[Curlonus Skaff]] and the Dust Devils pirate tribe. He seeks to accumulate magical power and his spellbook is extensive, storing many spells.

## Agenda

- Serve [[Curlonus Skaff]]
- Accumulate magical knowledge and power

## Relationships

- **Employed by:** [[Curlonus Skaff]]
- **Member of:** Dust Devils pirate tribe
- **Indirectly serves:** [[Dregoth]]
