# Hamanu — Sorcerer-King of Urik

**AKA:** The Lion of Urik

**Role:** Sorcerer-King of Urik, struck down [[Dregoth]] with his ebony sword.

**Location:** Urik

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 4

---

## Description

Hamanu, the Lion of Urik, was newly elevated as sorcerer-king at the time of Dregoth's death. He personally struck down [[Dregoth]] with his ebony sword during the assassination. He is the most martially inclined of the sorcerer-kings.

## Agenda

- Expand Urik's power
- Maintain martial discipline
- Crush any threat of Dregoth's return
