# Haltham Ghodan

**Role:** Male Dwarf — tar miner, co-owner of Ghodan's Gap.

**Location:** Tar mine 2 miles south of Cromlin

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 18

---

## Description

Haltham Ghodan is a dwarf tar miner who co-operates the tar mine at Ghodan's Gap with his brother [[Durex Ghodan]]. Like his brother, he is a free citizen originally from Raam.

## Agenda

- Profit from the tar mine
- Maintain independence

## Relationships

- **Brother of:** [[Durex Ghodan]]
- **Supplier to:** Cromlin trade
