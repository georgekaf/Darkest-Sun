# The Loyal

**Role:** Group of young elf Preserver/Thieves who follow [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]].

**Location:** Roaming the wastes with Jessix

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 11

**Number:** 5 named members (unnamed individuals)

---

## Description

The Loyal are a small band of young elves — preservers and thieves — who believe [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]] is innocent of [[Slinnasia]]'s death. They witnessed Slinnasia's death and know Jessix tried to save her. They follow Jessix in his mission to protect travelers from the [[Caller in Darkness]].

## Agenda

- Protect travelers near Giustenal
- Support [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]
- Vindicate Jessix's name

## Relationships

- **Led by:** [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]
- **Opposed to:** [[Caller in Darkness]]
- **Witnessed:** [[Slinnasia]]'s death
