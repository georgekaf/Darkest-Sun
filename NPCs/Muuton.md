# Muuton

**Role:** Male Elf Trader/Fighter — Chief of the Sky Singers elf tribe.

**Location:** Nibenay's Hill District

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 10

---

## Description

Muuton is the chief of the Sky Singers, an elf tribe that trades in Nibenay's Hill District. He is unsuspecting of [[Guvaano Twilightcatcher]]'s ties to the Veiled Alliance.

## Agenda

- Lead the Sky Singers tribe in profitable trade
- Maintain tribe safety during travels

## Relationships

- **Chief of:** Sky Singers elf tribe
- **Tribesman:** [[Guvaano Twilightcatcher]] (unaware of his Veiled Alliance ties)
- **Associated with:** [[Jessareen]]
