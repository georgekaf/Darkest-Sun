# Mon-Adderath

**Role:** Male Human Templar — [[Dregoth]]'s High Templar in New Giustenal.

**Location:** New Giustenal (Kragmorta), Temple of the Dragon

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 59-60

**Stats:** Male Human Templar, Lawful Evil, L18, AC4, hp101

---

## Description

Mon-Adderath is Dregoth's High Templar and most trusted mortal servant. He is a childhood friend of [[Dregoth]], predating Dregoth's transformation. He handles tactical planning for the Day of Light — the plan to conquer the surface world. He is the only non-dray in New Giustenal and dislikes [[Absalom]].

## Agenda

- Execute [[Dregoth]]'s will
- Plan the Day of Light invasion
- Maintain order in New Giustenal
- Compete with [[Absalom]] for Dregoth's favor

## Relationships

- **Serves:** [[Dregoth]] (childhood friend)
- **Rival of:** [[Absalom]] (dislikes him)
- **Only human in:** New Giustenal (all other inhabitants are [[Dray]])
