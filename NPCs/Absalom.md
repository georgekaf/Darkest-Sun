# Absalom — The High Priest

**AKA:** Akrag (disguised identity)

**Role:** Unique Morg (undead) — [[Dregoth]]'s High Priest. The first [[Dray]].

**Location:** New Giustenal (as Akrag: public baths, Area 11)

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 60-61

**Stats:** Unique Morg, Lawful Evil, 17HD, hp97, AC4

---

## Description

Absalom was the first dray created by [[Dregoth]]. His transformation was imperfect, and he died but rose as a unique form of undead — a morg. He serves as Dregoth's High Priest. He maintains a disguise as "Akrag," a bath-owner in New Giustenal. He secretly aids first-generation dray who struggle under Dregoth's rule. [[Mon-Adderath]] dislikes him.

## Agenda

- Serve [[Dregoth]] as High Priest
- Secretly aid first-generation dray
- Maintain his disguise as Akrag
- Compete with [[Mon-Adderath]]
