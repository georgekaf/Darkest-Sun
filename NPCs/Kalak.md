# Kalak — Sorcerer-King of Tyr

**Role:** Sorcerer-King of Tyr, joined the conspiracy against [[Dregoth]].

**Location:** Tyr

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 4

---

## Description

Kalak was the sorcerer-king of Tyr and one of the seven who assassinated [[Dregoth]]. Note: Kalak himself was later overthrown and killed in the Tyr region (per standard Dark Sun metaplot).

## Agenda

- Maintain tyranny over Tyr
- Prevent any rival from achieving dragon transformation

- **Enemy of:** [[Dregoth]]
