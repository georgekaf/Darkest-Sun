# Tectuktitlay — Sorcerer-King of Draj

**Role:** Sorcerer-King of Draj, caused earthquakes that collapsed Giustenal during [[Dregoth]]'s assassination.

**Location:** Draj

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 4

---

## Description

Tectuktitlay is the sorcerer-king of Draj. During the assassination of [[Dregoth]], he caused massive earthquakes that collapsed much of Giustenal's structures.

## Agenda

- Rule Draj with absolute power
- Ensure no sorcerer-king rises above the others
