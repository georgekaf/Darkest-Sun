# Guvaano Twilightcatcher

**Role:** Male Elf Preserver — elder wizard of the Twilightcatcher clan, Veiled Alliance ally.

**Location:** Twilightcatcher clan (roaming)

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 10-11

**Stats:** Male Elf Preserver, Chaotic Good, L12, AC6, hp27

---

## Description

Guvaano is an elder preserver wizard of the Twilightcatcher elf clan. He is secretly allied with the Veiled Alliance. He wisely ordered his clan to avoid the ruins of Giustenal, knowing the dangers. His faction is also known to have members among the [[The Loyal|Loyal]].

## Agenda

- Protect his clan from Giustenal's dangers
- Support the Veiled Alliance secretly
- Preserve magical knowledge

## Relationships

- **Clan elder of:** Twilightcatcher clan
- **Allied with:** Veiled Alliance
- **Member of:** Sky Singers tribe (under Chief [[Muuton]])
- **Associated with:** [[Jessix the Wanderer]]
