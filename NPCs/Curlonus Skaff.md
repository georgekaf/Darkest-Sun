# Curlonus Skaff

**Role:** Female Earth Cleric — leader of the Dust Devils pirate tribe.

**Location:** Pirate island near Giustenal

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 20-21

**Stats:** Female Earth Cleric, Lawful Evil, L12, AC5, hp60

---

## Description

Curlonus Skaff is an earth cleric who leads the Dust Devils, a tribe of silt pirates operating near Giustenal. [[Dregoth]] gave her a magical amulet as a focus of power. She seeks to raise an army for Dregoth, fulfilling an earth elemental's will.

## Agenda

- Raise an army of undead and pirates for [[Dregoth]]
- Increase her own clerical power
- Fulfill the will of the earth elementals she serves

## Relationships

- **Pact with:** [[Dregoth]] (received an amulet)
- **Employer:** [[Moraz the Defiler]] (her court defiler)
- **Leader of:** Dust Devils pirate tribe
