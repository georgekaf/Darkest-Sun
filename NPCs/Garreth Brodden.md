# Garreth Brodden

**Role:** Male Human Trader — House M'ke representative in Cromlin.

**Location:** Cromlin, House M'ke Pier

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 16

**Stats:** Male Human Trader, Neutral, L5, AC8, hp21

---

## Description

Garreth Brodden is the House M'ke factor in Cromlin. He manages his house's trade interests and silt skimmer construction. He publicly respects [[Hurdll Crost]] but engages in secret business dealings that may conflict with House Shom's interests.

## Agenda

- Advance House M'ke trade in Cromlin
- Compete subtly with House Shom
- Build and maintain silt skimmers

## Relationships

- **Agent of:** House M'ke
- **Competitor of:** [[Hurdll Crost]] (House Shom)
- **Resident of:** Cromlin
