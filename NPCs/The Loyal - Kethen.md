# The Loyal — Kethen

**Role:** Male Elf Thief — quick-handed and irreverent, the way [[The Loyal]] get into places that were sealed.

**Location:** Roaming the wastes within 20 miles of [[BookLocations/Giustenal]] with [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 11 (the Loyal appear there, unnamed)

**Stats:** Male Elf Thief, Chaotic Good, L6

---

## Description

Kethen jokes through everything, including the parts that should frighten him, and he is the reason the first expedition got as far as Dregoth's templar chambers at all. He is never standing quite where anyone last saw him. The humour drops away completely on the subject of Slinnasia — he will not discuss the second expedition with outsiders.

## Appearance

Long-limbed, with a narrow mischievous face, a crooked grin, shaved sides and a loose dark topknot. Mismatched dark leather pieces, dusty scarves, wrapped legs, fingerless leather gloves and several concealed bone knives. A short bone blade reversed at his belt and a compact bone bow over one shoulder. Relaxed, agile stance.

## Why this level

The Loyal are not green recruits. They went into Giustenal with Jessix twice — the first expedition reached Dregoth's templar chambers and came back with magical loot, the second cost Slinnasia her life and these five walked out of it alive. They then chose to stay, and now work the Giustenal environs full time: a Danger 5 region haunted by the [[Caller in Darkness]], ringed by tar pits and open to the ancient under-regions. Thief 6 — a veteran ruin-delver, both expeditions including the disastrous second.

## Origin of the name

The Campaign Book names only Jessix and leaves all five of his followers unnamed — "five young elves called the Loyal". **Kethen is a campaign-original name**, as are the personality, appearance and level recorded here. Everything the source does state is preserved: young elf preserver/thieves, witnesses to Slinnasia's death, believers in Jessix's innocence.
