# Hurdll Crost

**Role:** Male Human Trader — Master Trader of House Shom in Cromlin.

**Location:** Cromlin, House Shom Lodge

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 15-16

**Stats:** Male Human Trader, Lawful Neutral, L15, AC7, hp58

---

## Description

Hurdll Crost is the Master Trader of House Shom in the silt-harbor village of Cromlin. He manages all House Shom commercial interests in the region. He maintains armed forces, keeps troubleshooters on payroll, and seeks to destroy the silt pirates that threaten his shipping.

## Agenda

- Maintain House Shom profits in the region
- Destroy or neutralize the silt pirates
- Exploit trade opportunities from Giustenal expeditions
- Hire capable troubleshooters (including possibly the PCs)
