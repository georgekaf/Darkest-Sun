# Nallan

**Role:** Crafter and explorer — member of [[Tenpug]]'s Band, now deceased.

**Location:** Temple of Taraskir, Area 3 (corpse)

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 45, 47

---

## Description

Nallan was a human artisan and member of [[Tenpug]]'s Band. He was also a friend of [[Kria]]. While exploring the ruins of Giustenal for valuables, he was contacted by the [[Caller in Darkness]] and driven to suicide. He previously found and sold Dregoth's amulet to House Shom, setting off the adventure chain.

## Agenda

- *(Posthumous)* His death creates plot hook for PCs investigating the Caller
