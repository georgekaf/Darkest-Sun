# Rajaat — The War-Bringer

**AKA:** First Sorcerer

**Role:** First Sorcerer — the original defiler who created the Champions and started the Cleansing Wars.

**Location:** Imprisoned in the Hollow (beneath the Pristine Tower)

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 4-5

---

## Description

Rajaat was the first mortal to discover and use defiling magic. He created the 13 Champions (including [[Dregoth]]) to wage the Cleansing Wars, intending to exterminate non-halfling races and restore the Blue Age with halflings as masters. The Champions eventually turned on him and imprisoned him in the Hollow beneath the Pristine Tower.

## Agenda

- Escape imprisonment
- Restore the Blue Age
- Purge all non-halfling races
