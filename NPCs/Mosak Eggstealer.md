# Mosak Eggstealer

**Role:** Male [[Dray]] — Clan Father of the first-generation dray in Kragmorta.

**Location:** Kragmorta

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 63-64

---

## Description

Mosak Eggstealer is the leader (Clan Father) of the first-generation dray living in Kragmorta. These dray were the original creations of [[Dregoth]] and have been abandoned or left behind. Mosak is wary of outsiders but desperate for help against the [[Spirit of Kragmorta]] that terrorizes his people.

## Agenda

- Protect his clan from the [[Spirit of Kragmorta]]
- Distrust but seek help from outsiders
- Survive Dregoth's schemes

## Relationships

- **Leader of:** First-generation [[Dray]] in Kragmorta
- **Enemy of:** [[Spirit of Kragmorta]] (actually [[Dregoth]]'s illusion)
- **Created by:** [[Dregoth]] (indirectly)
