# The Loyal — Saelis

**Role:** Male Elf Preserver — youngest of [[The Loyal]] and the most openly idealistic.

**Location:** Roaming the wastes within 20 miles of [[BookLocations/Giustenal]] with [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 11 (the Loyal appear there, unnamed)

**Stats:** Male Elf Preserver, Chaotic Good, L5

---

## Description

Saelis believes out loud, and without embarrassment, that what they are doing matters — that travelers saved one at a time add up, and that Jessix will be cleared. The others let him say it. He is the one who talks to strangers first, and the one Varesh watches most closely when a meeting turns.

## Appearance

The youngest-looking of the five: tall, slim, softer-featured, sun-darkened, with large alert eyes and long reddish-brown hair tied loosely behind the shoulders. Pale ochre traveling robes over simple leather protection, faded green cloth strips and small carved bone charms. A bone short sword and a worn spell pouch. Upright, open stance; determined but inexperienced expression.

## Why this level

The Loyal are not green recruits. They went into Giustenal with Jessix twice — the first expedition reached Dregoth's templar chambers and came back with magical loot, the second cost Slinnasia her life and these five walked out of it alive. They then chose to stay, and now work the Giustenal environs full time: a Danger 5 region haunted by the [[Caller in Darkness]], ringed by tar pits and open to the ancient under-regions. Preserver 5 — young, but a Giustenal survivor twice over; the level reflects what he has lived through rather than his years.

## Agenda

- Reach travelers before the ruins do, and speak to them first
- Prove Jessix innocent to anyone who will listen
- Learn enough to stop being the one who gets protected

## Origin of the name

The Campaign Book names only Jessix and leaves all five of his followers unnamed — "five young elves called the Loyal". **Saelis is a campaign-original name**, as are the personality, appearance and level recorded here. Everything the source does state is preserved: young elf preserver/thieves, witnesses to Slinnasia's death, believers in Jessix's innocence.
