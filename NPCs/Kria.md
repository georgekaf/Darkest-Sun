# Kria

**Role:** Female Half-Elf Thief — member of [[Tenpug]]'s Band.

**Location:** Tenpug's Band camp or Nibenay market

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 45, 48

**Stats:** Female Half-Elf Thief, NG, T5, AC8, hp18

---

## Description

Kria is a half-elf thief who lives with [[Tenpug]]'s Band. She helps sell their crafts in Nibenay's markets. She is desperately worried about her missing friend [[Nallan]] and is short on funds. She may seek out adventurers to help find him.

## Agenda

- Find out what happened to [[Nallan]]
- Raise funds for the band
- Warn others about Giustenal's dangers

## Relationships

- **Friend of:** [[Nallan]] (missing, deceased)
- **Member of:** [[Tenpug]]'s Band
- **Works with:** [[Tenpug]]
