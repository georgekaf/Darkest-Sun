# The Loyal — Varesh

**Role:** Male Elf Thief — scout of [[The Loyal]], Jessix's eyes on the road.

**Location:** Roaming the wastes within 20 miles of [[BookLocations/Giustenal]] with [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 11 (the Loyal appear there, unnamed)

**Stats:** Male Elf Thief, Chaotic Good, L6

---

## Description

Varesh walks ahead of the band and usually sees trouble before it sees them. Cautious to the point of seeming cold, he says little, watches everything, and has an unnerving habit of answering a question several seconds late — having first checked the horizon. Of the five he is the one who argues for leaving a fight unfought.

## Appearance

Tall and extremely lean even for an elf, with long limbs, a narrow weathered face, sharp dark eyes and black hair bound tightly behind his head. Fitted sand-brown leather armor under a ragged hooded desert cloak, wrapped forearms, soft boots, and several small bone utility knives. A bone shortbow across his back and a curved bone short sword at his hip. Reserved, watchful posture, always looking ready to move.

## Why this level

The Loyal are not green recruits. They went into Giustenal with Jessix twice — the first expedition reached Dregoth's templar chambers and came back with magical loot, the second cost Slinnasia her life and these five walked out of it alive. They then chose to stay, and now work the Giustenal environs full time: a Danger 5 region haunted by the [[Caller in Darkness]], ringed by tar pits and open to the ancient under-regions. Thief 6 — a veteran ruin-delver who survived both expeditions, including the one that went wrong.

## Agenda

- Scout the routes travelers use and reach them before the ruins do
- Keep the band out of fights it cannot win
- Support [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]

## Origin of the name

The Campaign Book names only Jessix and leaves all five of his followers unnamed — "five young elves called the Loyal". **Varesh is a campaign-original name**, as are the personality, appearance and level recorded here. Everything the source does state is preserved: young elf preserver/thieves, witnesses to Slinnasia's death, believers in Jessix's innocence.
