# Nibenese Templar

**Role:** Female Human Templar in service to [[Nibenay]].

**Location:** Nibenay → traveling

**Page References:** Adventure Book

---

## Description

An unnamed female templar of [[Nibenay]] tasked with recovering Dregoth's amulet. She travels with half-giant guards. She follows adventurers who purchase the amulet from House Shom, attempting to acquire it for her king.

## Agenda

- Recover Dregoth's amulet for [[Nibenay]]
- Monitor activity related to Giustenal

## Relationships

- **Serves:** [[Nibenay]]
- **Target:** Possessors of Dregoth's amulet
