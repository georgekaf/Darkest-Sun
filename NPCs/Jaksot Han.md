# Jaksot Han

**Role:** Former silt pirate, now tavern owner in Cromlin.

**Location:** Cromlin, Dirty Lizard tavern

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 17

---

## Description

Jaksot Han is a former silt pirate who retired after losing an eye. He now runs the Dirty Lizard tavern in Cromlin. The tavern is known for frequent brawls, and Jaksot is quick to test newcomers' mettle. Despite the rough atmosphere, he is a good source of local information.

## Agenda

- Run a profitable (if rowdy) tavern
- Stay retired from piracy
- Gather and trade information
