# The Loyal — Nyrra

**Role:** Female Elf Preserver — the quiet, severe spellcaster of [[The Loyal]].

**Location:** Roaming the wastes within 20 miles of [[BookLocations/Giustenal]] with [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 11 (the Loyal appear there, unnamed)

**Stats:** Female Elf Preserver, Chaotic Good, L5

---

## Description

Nyrra speaks rarely and wastes no words when she does. She holds the band to Jessix's rule about defiling with a strictness that borders on hostility, and walks away from arguments rather than raise her voice. Anyone who mistakes her silence for meekness learns otherwise the first time something comes up out of the tar.

## Appearance

Tall and slender, with angular features, intense amber eyes and long dark hair braided with bone beads. Layered faded-red and charcoal desert robes over light leather armor — practical, not ceremonial — with cloth wraps protecting her arms from the sun. A bone short sword and a small pouch of spell components. Controlled, severe expression, one hand often half-raised as though a spell is already half-shaped.

## Why this level

The Loyal are not green recruits. They went into Giustenal with Jessix twice — the first expedition reached Dregoth's templar chambers and came back with magical loot, the second cost Slinnasia her life and these five walked out of it alive. They then chose to stay, and now work the Giustenal environs full time: a Danger 5 region haunted by the [[Caller in Darkness]], ringed by tar pits and open to the ancient under-regions. Preserver 5 — experienced enough to survive Giustenal and operate independently in its environs, still substantially below Jessix.

## Agenda

- Protect travelers without defiling to do it
- Hold the band to Jessix's standards when Jessix is not there
- Vindicate Jessix's name

## Relationships

- **Follows:** [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]
- **Member of:** [[The Loyal]]

## Origin of the name

The Campaign Book names only Jessix and leaves all five of his followers unnamed — "five young elves called the Loyal". **Nyrra is a campaign-original name**, as are the personality, appearance and level recorded here. Everything the source does state is preserved: young elf preserver/thieves, witnesses to Slinnasia's death, believers in Jessix's innocence.
