# Abalach-Re — Sorcerer-Queen of Raam

**Role:** Sorcerer-Queen of Raam, leader of the conspiracy to assassinate [[Dregoth]].

**Location:** Raam

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 4-6, 60-62

---

## Description

Abalach-Re is the sorcerer-queen of Raam, the city-state closest to Giustenal. She led the conspiracy of seven sorcerer-kings who assassinated Dregoth 2,000 years ago. She personally sealed the Sunken City beneath Giustenal using powerful magic (creating the "box of sand"). [[Dregoth]] fears and hates her above all other sorcerer-kings.

## Agenda

- Maintain power in Raam
- Prevent Dregoth's return
- Guard the secrets of the Sunken City

## Relationships

- **Enemy of:** [[Dregoth]] (led his assassination, most feared by him)
- **Fellow conspirators:** [[Kalak]], [[Nibenay]], [[Lalali-Puy]], [[Andropinis]], [[Tectuktitlay]], [[Hamanu]]
- **Fellow Champion of:** [[Rajaat]]
