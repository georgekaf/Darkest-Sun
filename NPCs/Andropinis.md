# Andropinis — Sorcerer-King of Balic

**Role:** Sorcerer-King of Balic, unleashed lightning on Giustenal during [[Dregoth]]'s assassination.

**Location:** Balic

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 4

---

## Description

Andropinis is the sorcerer-king of Balic. During the assassination of [[Dregoth]], he unleashed devastating lightning attacks on the city.

## Agenda

- Maintain power over Balic
- Guard against Dregoth's return
