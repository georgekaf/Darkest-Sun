# Lodgden

**Role:** Female [[Dray]] Thief — spy for [[Casskka]].

**Location:** New Giustenal, The Pit (infiltrated as server)

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 62

**Stats:** Female Dray Thief, Lawful Neutral

---

## Description

Lodgden is a female dray thief who works undercover as a server at The Pit tavern. She was hired by [[Casskka]] to steal [[Freiha]]'s secret munge elixir recipe.

## Agenda

- Steal the munge elixir recipe for [[Casskka]]
- Avoid detection by [[Freiha]] and [[Gatlakk]]

## Relationships

- **Hired by:** [[Casskka]]
- **Infiltrating:** The Pit ([[Freiha]] and [[Gatlakk]]'s tavern)
- **Race:** [[Dray]]
