# Casskka

**Role:** Female [[Dray]] — owner of the Beetle's Bite tavern, rival to [[Freiha]].

**Location:** New Giustenal, Beetle's Bite Tavern (Area 14)

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 62

---

## Description

Casskka is a female dray who owns the Beetle's Bite Tavern, a competitor to The Pit owned by [[Freiha]] and [[Gatlakk]]. She has hired [[Lodgden]] to steal the secret of Freiha's munge elixir recipe. Her brother is a fisherman.

## Agenda

- Compete with [[Freiha]]'s The Pit
- Steal the munge elixir recipe
- Dominate the tavern trade in New Giustenal

## Relationships

- **Competitor of:** [[Freiha]] and [[Gatlakk]]
- **Employs:** [[Lodgden]] (to steal the munge recipe)
- **Race:** [[Dray]]
