# Borys — The Dragon of Tyr

**AKA:** The Dragon, Dwarf-Butcher

**Role:** The Dragon of Tyr — the only Champion to fully complete the dragon transformation, now guards [[Rajaat]]'s prison.

**Location:** The Hollow (guarding Rajaat's prison); roams Athas

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 5

---

## Description

Borys was the Dwarf-Butcher Champion of [[Rajaat]]. He is the only one of the original Champions to have completed the full transformation into a dragon. His mind has been destroyed by the transformation process, leaving him a mad guardian of Rajaat's prison. [[Dregoth]] sought to equal Borys's transformation.

## Agenda

- Guard Rajaat's prison
- Slay any who approach the Hollow
- (Post-transformation: mindless duty)
