# Fishing Shacks

**What:** Fish processing buildings along the [[Blackjaw River]] in [[New Giustenal]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 82-84 (Area 5)

---

Large buildings on both banks of the Blackjaw River. Here, fish are sorted, prepared, and stored. The dray fishermen are worried about a sharg that has been threatening barges. Barges (no sailing vessels) drag nets through the blackjaw.

## Inhabitants

- [[Dray]] fishermen
- Sharg (threatens from the river)
