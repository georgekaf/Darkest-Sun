# Dirty Lizard

**What:** Large tavern and inn in [[Cromlin]], center of entertainment and information.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 18-19

---

Cromlin's rowdiest establishment. Known for fermented kank-honey-mead and frequent brawls. [[Jaksot Han]] (former silt pirate, one-eyed) runs the place. Up to 40 patrons crowd in at peak hours. Strangers are tested — if they brawl well, they earn respect and information.

## Inhabitants

- [[Jaksot Han]] (owner)
- Up to 40 patrons (pirates, traders, adventurers, exiles)
