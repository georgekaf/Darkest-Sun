# Upside-Down Forest

**What:** An ancient remnant of the Blue Age in [[New Giustenal]] — trees that grow from the ceiling with roots reaching upward toward sunlight.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 84 (Area 10)

---

A forest growing from the cavern ceiling. Roots reach upward toward the distant sun. Pathways of different colored living stone wind through the trees. Sweet fruit grows here.
