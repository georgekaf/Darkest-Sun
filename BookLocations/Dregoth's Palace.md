# Dregoth's Palace

**What:** Central fortress within [[Giustenal]], on the edge of the [[Sea of Silt]]. Dregoth's former seat of power.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 49, 51-55 (Area 7)

---

Less impressive than other sorcerer-kings' palaces, Dregoth's palace was still a formidable fortress. Much of it has been looted and damaged by time and weather. The palace connects to [[Dregoth's Tower]].

## Rooms

**1. Grand Entrance** — Great doors shattered. Knee-deep dust. Beautiful mural of the city beside blue water on the east wall. Engraved mural of templars on the west wall. Crumbled throne at the north end. Secret passage to Room 3.

**2. Banquet Hall** — Northern wall cracked. Mammoth cupboard (shattered plates). [[Nallan]] retrieved pottery here. Sink worm attacks from the Silt Sea through a north wall opening.

**3. Preparation Room** — Where Dregoth met with his High Templar. Locked door (-20% pick locks). Contains a metal telescope (30 gp) and silver bowls/mirrors (15 gp).

**4. Kitchen** — Dozens of slaves once worked here. Corpses shackled under rubble. Hole in roof.

**5. Storeroom** — Food/water storage. Almost entirely looted.

**6. Ballroom** — Used for Day of Cleansing celebrations and templar parties. 40-ft long carved table (worth 24 gp if removed and restored).

**7. Templars' Apartments (low rank)** — 4 beds each. Looted. A dragon medallion (holy symbol of Dregoth) can be found.

**8. Templars' Apartments (high rank)** — 2 beds each. Scraps of clothing. A collapsed room with Type C treasure beneath rubble.

**9. Dining Hall** — Great wooden tables (15 sp each). 5%/turn chance of finding plate/cutlery worth 2d6 sp.

**10. Baths** — Large bathing chambers, now dry and silt-filled.

**11. Barracks** — Palace guard quarters. Nothing but dust and bones.

**12. Spire Ante-Chamber** — Valuable artifacts from the Cleansing Wars. Tapestry of Dregoth vs [[Taraskir the Lion]]. "The Lion-King Lives!" graffito. Stairs down to tunnels. Secret door (now easily spotted). Two pedestals: marble dragon sculpture, shattered bust of Dregoth. Staircase to [[Dregoth's Tower]].

**13. High Templar's Chamber** — [[Mon-Adderath]]'s old room. Gray marble table, turquoise-inlaid desk (turquoise worth 2 gp, desk/table 3 gp each).

**14. Guest Room** — Visiting dignitaries' quarters. Looted. Corpse with ring of protection +2.

**15. High Priest's Quarters** — Incense of meditation in a jammed desk drawer. The high priest's body is staked to [[Dregoth's Tower]].

**16. Slave Quarters** — Favored slaves and concubines. Pillows, wine pitchers, leather bonds. A few corpses.
