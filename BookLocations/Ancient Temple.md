# Ancient Temple (Tenpug's Band)

**What:** Ancient temple headquarters of [[Tenpug]]'s Band of ex-slave artisans.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 10-11

---

A temple dating back to the Time of Magic, originally dedicated to the forgotten god of [[Taraskir the Lion]]. Located west of [[Cromlin]]. Guarded by statues of giants with lion heads (Taraskir's Lion Guard). Faded frescoes show ancient [[Giustenal]] with a waterfall. Hidden chambers throughout.

## Inhabitants

- [[Tenpug]] (mul gladiator leader)
- [[Kria]] (half-elf thief)
- [[Nallan]] (crafter, deceased — killed by [[Caller in Darkness]])
- Other ex-slave artisans

## Related Locations

- [[Cromlin]] — nearest settlement, east
- [[Giustenal]] — shown in frescoes
- [[Taraskir the Lion]] — temple's original dedicatee
- [[Nibenay]] — many in the band escaped slavery here
