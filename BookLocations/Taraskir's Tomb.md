# Taraskir's Tomb

**What:** Burial place of [[Taraskir the Lion]], located in the [[Sunken City]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 66-67 (Area 6)

---

A giant lion-headed statue temple, larger than the ancient temple [[Tenpug]]'s Band occupies. The building reaches almost to the sand dome, 60 ft overhead. Great lion-headed statues once flanked the doors but have been removed. The doors are sealed with strange writing: *"Here lies Taraskir the Lion. He dared challenge Dregoth, the Ravager of Giants, and so rests here in eternal punishment."*

Inside, a huge stone coffin is chained with enchanted chains (require 50 hp of +1+ weapon damage to break). Taraskir's skeleton lies within, wearing:
- **Cursed armor** (+2 AC, but curses the wearer — gives +2 to opponent attack rolls, persists even if removed until returned to the tomb)
- **+2 dragon slayer sword** (two-handed, requires 17+ Str and 6+ ft tall to wield)

Three undead honor guards (a dwarf and two elves) protect the tomb. A secret door in the floor leads to [[The Lion's Den]].
