# Bodach (Ruins)

**What:** Ruins explored by the Twilightcatcher clan as a safer alternative to [[Giustenal]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 12

---

After [[Slinnasia]]'s death at the hands of the [[Caller in Darkness]], the Twilightcatcher clan redirected their exploration efforts here. At least the dangers in Bodach can be fought with steel.
