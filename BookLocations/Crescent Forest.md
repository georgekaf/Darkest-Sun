# Crescent Forest

**What:** Forest providing timber for silt skimmer and pier construction.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 16, 22

---

Source of the great trees used to build [[Cromlin]]'s piers and silt skimmers. Used by House Shom and House M'ke for construction at [[Pier of House M'ke]].
