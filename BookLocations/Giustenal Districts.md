# Giustenal Districts

**What:** The named districts and areas within the ruined city of [[Giustenal]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 48-51

---

The ruined city is divided into several named districts, each with its own character and hazards. All are buried under 4+ inches of silt and haunted by the [[Caller in Darkness]].

## Area 4 — Merchant District
SE corner of the city. Stone buildings, thoroughly pillaged. Type A treasure possible after sustained searching. The cellar of a large tavern in the northern part contains a zhackal lair. ^a3fd2b

## Area 5 — Grand Plaza
Huge stone basins (once held topiaries, now ash-filled). A large crack runs through the plaza toward [[Dregoth's Palace]]. Many skeletons lie where they fell. ^d4d777

## Area 6 — Arena

Dregoth's arena, built to appease the nobles. Slave pens under the southern seating contain dozens of mummified bodies. The [[Caller in Darkness]] manifests its worst illusions here. ^df44e4

## Area 8 — Warrens (Poor Quarter)
SW corner of the city. Baked clay, piled stone, and fabric buildings. Blackened by soot from the [[Tar Pits of Giustenal]]. Irony: survivors knew this was the poor quarter, so few looted it. Type M treasure after 4 hours searching.

## Area 9 — Freemen District
Free citizens' homes. More sturdy construction than the Warrens. Thoroughly looted.

## Area 10 — Gardens
Surround [[Dregoth's Palace]]. Plant life withered to ash. 6 inches of silt over 1 ft of ash. Two templar bodies buried in the smaller garden — each has a steel long sword and gold earrings.

## Area 11 — Public Cisterns
Wells on each side of the palace. Only the southern ones remain. Dried mud now. A dormant bog wader hibernates in one.

## Area 12 — Nobles' District
Richly appointed buildings near the Silt Sea. Silt is especially deep here. Silt creatures are a serious danger.

## Area 13 — Traders' District
Permanent store structures along the caravan road. Magically damaged. Some Type D treasure remains.

## Area 14 — Templar District
Buildings east of the palace. Devastated by DMG magic. Silt horrors frequent the shores. A pack of silt runners lairs in the SE corner.

## Area 15 — Slave Pen
15-ft high walled compound. Bodies of dead slaves. Four undead gladiators reside among the corpses.
