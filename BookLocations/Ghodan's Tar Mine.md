# Ghodan's Tar Mine

**What:** Working tar mine operated by the Ghodan dwarven brothers, 2 miles south of [[Cromlin]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 23

---

A profitable tar mining operation run by [[Durex Ghodan]] and [[Haltham Ghodan]], free dwarven citizens originally from [[Raam]]. They employ 20 slaves as crafters and laborers. Crude tar is extracted to weatherproof items. Craft shops on site. Twice-monthly caravans head to [[Cromlin]].

## Inhabitants

- [[Durex Ghodan]] (co-owner)
- [[Haltham Ghodan]] (co-owner)
- 20 slaves (crafters and laborers)
