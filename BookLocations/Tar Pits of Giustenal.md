# Tar Pits of Giustenal

**What:** Smoldering tar barrier before the southern gate of [[Giustenal]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 39-41

---

A deadly expanse of bubbling tar pits blocks the southern approach to Giustenal. Few safe routes exist. Navigating them requires survival checks or a guide with a map (like [[Passk]]).

## Hazards

| Mishap (d20) | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| Misstep (1-5) | Fall into tar, 3d8 + 1d8/round |
| Blinded (6-9) | Smoke blinds for 1d10 minutes |
| Heat Exhaustion (10-13) | -2 to Wis/Int proficiency rolls |
| Severe Heat Exhaustion (14-16) | As above + 1d6 hp/check |
| Pit Snatcher (17-18) | 1-3 pit snatchers attack |
| Victim (19-20) | Stumble across dying creature |

## Inhabitants

- Pit snatchers
- [[Passk]] has a map (see [[Passk's Shack]])
