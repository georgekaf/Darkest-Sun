# Blasted Spire

**What:** Weathered obsidian tower, half-mile north of [[Giustenal]]'s shore. Home to a krag.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 33-36

---

A beautiful but weathered obsidian tower reaching out of the Silt Sea north of Giustenal. Four balconies with ballistae ring its upper level. [[The Rubble Reef]] connects it to the city.

## Interior Locations

**Entry Floor** — Round chamber covered in 1 ft of silt. Large hole in the center (trap to the krag's lair below). Garrison debris scattered about.

**Top Floor** — Houses ballistae on four balconies (one intact). Weapon rack with rotted spears, ruined crossbows (metal parts worth 1 gp), 20 metal bolt heads. Hole in floor to lower level.

**Krag Lair (submerged)** — Completely submerged in silt.
- **Room E:** Landing zone from the hole above
- **Room F:** Bones of victims, 4 dormant kraglings
- **Room G:** Treasure — bracers of defense AC5, ring of free action, book of exalted deeds, 330 gp, 42 sp, 3 gems
- **Room H:** Empty
- **Room I:** Body of [[Slinnasia]] (elf preserver, killed by [[Caller in Darkness]])
- Stairs down to [[Sunken City]]

## Inhabitants

- Yusus (krag, undead elf rain cleric)
- 4 kraglings (dormant in Room F)
- [[Slinnasia]]'s corpse (Room I)
- [[Abdaleem]] wants the krag's head
