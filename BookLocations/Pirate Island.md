# Pirate Island

**What:** Hidden pirate base near [[Giustenal]], home to the Dust Devils under [[Curlonus Skaff]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 16 (mentioned, detailed in Adventure Book)

---

A large cavern west of [[Cromlin]] serving as the hidden base of the Dust Devils pirate tribe. The pirates use silt skimmers to ambush vessels on the [[Silt Shoals]]. The base houses [[Curlonus Skaff]] (earth cleric) and [[Moraz the Defiler]].

## Inhabitants

- [[Curlonus Skaff]] (earth cleric, LE, L12, leader)
- [[Moraz the Defiler]] (wizard, LE, W10)
- Various silt pirates (F3)

## Related Locations

- [[Cromlin]] — their hunting ground
- [[Giustenal]] — Skaff serves [[Dregoth]]
- [[Silt Shoals]] — where they ambush ships
