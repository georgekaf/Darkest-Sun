# The Lion's Den

**What:** Underground cavern gathering place of [[Taraskir the Lion]]'s followers. Beneath [[Taraskir's Tomb]] in the [[Sunken City]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 69

---

Accessed through a secret door in the floor of Taraskir's Tomb. Used by demihuman resistance leaders who worshipped Taraskir as a god.

## Locations

**1. Spiral Ridge** — Ridge built at a slight angle. Tunnel descends half a mile.

**2. Large Cavern** — A tree of life (magically preserved). Crystal clear stream (4 ft deep bed, 6 inches of water). Dragon beetles (afraid of larger creatures). Brood isolated for generations.

**3. Stores** — Rotted foodstuffs, evaporated water. Safe haven for rest and spell memorization.

## Inhabitants

- Dragon beetles (in the large cavern)
- [[Bracorr]] (fire giant shaman — visits from [[Kragmorta]])
