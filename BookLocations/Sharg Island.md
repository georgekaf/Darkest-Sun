# Sharg Island

**What:** A lonely abandoned estate in [[New Giustenal]]. A sharg lairs in the cave beneath it.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 82 (Area 4)

An abandoned estate on an island in [[New Giustenal]]. A sharg — a deadly aquatic monster — has made its lair in a cave beneath the island. [[Dregoth]] has been studying the creature.

## Inhabitants
