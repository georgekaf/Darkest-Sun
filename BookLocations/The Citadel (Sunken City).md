# The Citadel (Sunken City)

**What:** Dregoth's citadel within the [[Sunken City]], what [[Abalach-Re]] wanted to preserve when she created the sand box.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 65-66 (Area 5)

---

Abalach-Re preserved this building with her magic, then looted most of its treasures for [[Raam]]. Some still remain. The architecture bears traditional draconic motifs of Dregoth's reign.

## Rooms

**A. Templar Offices** — Stone desks, ruined scrolls, charred records.

**B. Crafting Room** — Wards broken. Dregoth's item-crafting area. Metal-working tools survive.

**C. Hall of Counsel** — Wooden benches face an obsidian platform where Dregoth sat in dragon form.

**D. Hall of Spells** — Empty scroll cases. Hidden space with 5 wizard spells (1st-4th level).

**E. Atrium** — Dead plants, lifeless dirt mixed with ash.

**F. Testing Chamber** — Scorch marks. Cages used for [[Dray]] transformation experiments.

**G. Secret Room** — Hidden behind a secret door. Contains 3 spellbooks, 2 wands (polymorphing 4 charges, illumination 6 charges), 6 empty glass vials. Loose page with sketches of human, humanoid reptile, and full dray — notes in an ancient language.
