# House Shom Warehouse

**What:** Locked goods storage facility in [[Cromlin]], controlled by [[Hurdll Crost]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 18

---

House Shom's secure warehouse in Cromlin. Locked at all times. Crost manipulates local prices by controlling supply. Two sentry patrols of 3 guards each. Intruders are made slaves or thrown off the pier.

## Inhabitants

- [[Hurdll Crost]] (unrestricted access)
- 21 sentries (drawn from the 30 soldiers at [[House Shom Barracks]])
