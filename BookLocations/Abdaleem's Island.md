# Abdaleem's Island

**What:** Small island north of [[Giustenal]]'s uncovered ruins, home to the silt priest [[Abdaleem]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 36-38

---

A small island reachable by following the eastern side of [[The Silt Road]] north from Giustenal. [[Abdaleem]], a para-elemental priest of silt, lives here. He creates scroll-rocks that grant free action in silt for 3 hours. He knows about draxia weed (repels silt spawn). He wants the head of the krag from the [[Blasted Spire]].

## Inhabitants

- [[Abdaleem]] (human silt priest, Chaotic Neutral, 12th level)

## Related Locations

- [[Giustenal]] — south of the island
- [[The Silt Road]] — path connecting to Giustenal
- [[Blasted Spire]] — the krag Abdaleem wants destroyed
