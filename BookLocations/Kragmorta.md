# Kragmorta

**What:** Fiery cavern city; home of the first-generation [[Dray]]. Originally a Blue Age halfling settlement.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 74-80

---

A cavern of fire and lava beneath [[The Groaning City]]. Rivers of boiling magma replaced ancient waterways. Ancient grown-stone buildings from the Blue Age remain. Rope bridges made from silk wall-walker webbing span lava flows. Constant vigil against predators [[Dregoth]] released here.

Population: ~324 first-generation dray under Clan Father [[Mosak Eggstealer]].

## Locations

**1. Entrance** — Tunnel E continues past a lava flow. A shattered stone gate. Rope bridge over magma (10 ft below). Guard posted at location 2D.

**2. The First Island** — Solid rock island cut off by lava rivers. Petrified wood docks. Grown-stone buildings.
- **2A: Fire Giant Lair** — [[Bracorr]] the fire giant shaman. 18 ft tall. Brought from another world by Dregoth. Lost contact with his god.
- **2B: Hell Hound Lair** — 2 hell hounds.
- **2C: Fire Snake Lair** — 8 fire snakes. Paralytic venom.
- **2D: Dray Guard Post** — 75% presence chance.

**3. Island of the Dark Naga** — A dark naga brought by Dregoth then released. Uses dragon beetle spit as poison weapon. Treasure includes: 232 gp, 3 gems (100 gp each), scimitar hornblade, net of snaring, hat of disguise, book of vile darkness.

**4. Island of the Fire Drake** — Fire drake nests amid boulders. 30% chance present. 25-ft long, HD 20+8, 145 hp. Otherwise hunts via a lava drainage hole to the surface.

**5. Dray Settlement** — Ancient buildings. 324 first-generation dray. Notable: 12th-level warriors, 12th-level psionicists, 7th-level thieves, 11th-level cleric.

**6. Wall-Walker Hive** — Holes in the cavern wall. 24 wall-walkers in broods of 1-6. 25% encounter chance.

**7. Fresh Water Springs** — Clear, hot water, safe to drink. 4 dray guards always on duty.

**8. [[Spirit of Kragmorta]]** — Actually [[Dregoth]]'s psionic projection. Appears as a lion-headed figure. Urges dray to attack [[New Giustenal]] — ensuring their deaths swell Dregoth's undead army.
