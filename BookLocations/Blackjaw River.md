# Blackjaw River

**What:** Steaming river in [[New Giustenal]], passing over a magma flow.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 82 (Area 3)

---

A river of hot water passing over magma deep beneath [[New Giustenal]]. A great dragon-bone bridge crosses it. Fishing barges work the river. A cave half-mile east holds [[Dregoth]]'s undead army — the [[Fire Dagger Clan]] and mul slave raiders. A sharg monster threatens barges.

## Inhabitants

- Sharg (lurks beneath, threatens fishing boats)
- Undead army (Fire Dagger Clan, mul raiders — in the eastern cave)

## Related Locations

- [[New Giustenal]] — the capital
- [[Fishing Shacks]] — along the banks
- [[Sharg Island]] — abandoned estate, sharg lair
