# House Shom Barracks

**What:** Soldier and troubleshooter housing in [[Cromlin]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 20

---

Houses 30 soldiers (21 serve as sentries at the [[House Shom Warehouse]]) and 5 high-level troubleshooters who handle emergencies. The troubleshooters are paid 3 gp/month — adventurers (including PCs) can be hired for this role.

## Inhabitants

- 30 House Shom soldiers
- 5 adventurer-troubleshooters (rotating)
- [[Hurdll Crost]] (employer)

## Related Locations

- [[Cromlin]] — the village
- [[House Shom Lodge]] — Crost's residence
- [[House Shom Warehouse]] — guarded storage
