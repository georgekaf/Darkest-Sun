# Cromlin Marketplace

**What:** Open-air merchant booths in [[Cromlin]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 19-20

---

Surrounded by a stacked stone fence. A well in the SE corner is controlled by [[Hurdll Crost]] and House Shom. Magical fruit and illegal items are available from elf vendors. Crost collects 10% from all vendors.
