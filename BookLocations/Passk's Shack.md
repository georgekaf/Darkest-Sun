# Passk's Shack

**What:** Dilapidated home of [[Passk]] the dwarf in [[Cromlin]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 20-21

---

A run-down shack where Passk drinks heavily and mourns his crew — all killed by the [[Caller in Darkness]]. Contains a valuable map of [[Giustenal]]'s walls and a beached silt skimmer that can be repaired.

## Inhabitants

- [[Passk]] (dwarf warrior, drunkard)
