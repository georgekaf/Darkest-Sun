# Dregoth's Arch

**What:** Two great black obsidian arches within [[Giustenal]], each as tall as the city walls.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 48 (Area 2)

---

Two immense arches of black obsidian stand within the ruined city. Each has a dragon head keystone. They once served as ceremonial gateways during Dregoth's reign.
