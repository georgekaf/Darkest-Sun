# Cromlin Homes

**What:** Residential buildings in [[Cromlin]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 18

---

A mix of mud structures and stone/wood constructions. Many exiled elves live here. There are no street patrols, making the residential areas dangerous at night.
