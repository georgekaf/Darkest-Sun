# The Rubble Reef

**What:** Pathway of silt-covered buildings and rubble connecting [[Giustenal]] to the [[Blasted Spire]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 34

---

A treacherous path of silt-covered structures leading from Giustenal's shore to the Blasted Spire half-mile north. Slightly more difficult to navigate than [[The Silt Road]].
