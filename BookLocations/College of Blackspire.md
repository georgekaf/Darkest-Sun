# College of Blackspire

**What:** Wizard school in [[New Giustenal]], where [[Dregoth]] trains dray mages.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 85 (Area 13)

---

An ivory sandstone building with black obsidian caps, rising in three scaled spires. [[Dregoth]] personally encourages mage development. Students are brainwashed to loyalty. Graduates may join the kalin riders.
