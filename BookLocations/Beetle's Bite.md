# Beetle's Bite

**What:** Rival tavern in [[New Giustenal]], owned by [[Casskka]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 85 (Area 14)

---

Struggling tavern competing with [[The Pit]]. Casskka sells cheap blackjaw (her brother is a fisherman) and has hired [[Lodgden]] to steal the munge elixir recipe from [[Freiha]].

## Inhabitants

- [[Casskka]] (dray, owner)
- [[Lodgden]] (dray thief, spy — works at [[The Pit]] undercover)

## Related Locations

- [[New Giustenal]] — the capital
- [[The Pit]] — competitor
- [[Blackjaw River]] — source of cheap fish
