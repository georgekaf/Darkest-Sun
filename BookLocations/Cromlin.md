# Cromlin

**What:** Trading village on the Silt Coast, established by House Shom. Den of thieves and scoundrels.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 15-20

---

Cromlin sits on the Silt Coast ~20 miles west of [[Giustenal]]. It's a rough frontier village where House Shom (under [[Hurdll Crost]]) dominates trade. Slit pirates, exiles, and desperate adventurers rub shoulders here. Locals wear scarves over their mouths as custom. All traders pay 10% to House Shom.
