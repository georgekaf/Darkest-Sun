# Island Settlements

**What:** Ancient island settlements once connected by the psionic tunnel system. Destroyed in the cataclysm.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 5-6, 60-61

---

Three named islands that existed during the Green Age off the coast of ancient Giustenal:

- **Vakura**
- **Pelcunal**
- **Liuss**

Now lost or destroyed, known only through the visions of [[Kataal the Mover]] and the tunnel network that once connected them.
