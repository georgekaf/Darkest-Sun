# Pier of House Shom

**What:** Exclusive guarded pier in [[Cromlin]] for House Shom vessels.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 16

---

The private pier of House Shom. The Firewind, the largest silt skimmer in the Tyr region, docks here under [[Captain Gaff]]'s command.
