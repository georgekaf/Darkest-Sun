> **Everything leads here. Water solved, trade open, village stable — but Kharanok cannot survive without animals.**
> Complete all three hooks to finish the arc.

---

### Hook 1: The Stores Are Nearly Empty
**Quest Giver:** [[Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Storage Pits]]

Audit reveals six weeks of food. The reserve should be two full seasons. The Vakreen household has been liquidating community goods to fund their escape from Kharanok. They are not villains — they gave up. But the goods belong to everyone.

**Goal:** Reconstruct missing inventory, recover what remains in the Vakreen private pit, decide with Yethras what happens to the family.

**Clues:**
- Vakreen matriarch, confronted privately, offers no resistance. Ashamed but not apologetic — a mother who did what she had to. She will make restitution if the family can stay without being branded
- Part of the missing goods sold to Muuton's last caravan. Muuton didn't know they were stolen — he returns them, wants a favour
- A Vakreen child found a sealed clay tablet in foreign script while moving goods. [[Dorak|Dorak]] takes it without a word and doesn't return it for two days

**Reward:** Community trust restored or fractured. Dorak's silence about the tablet becomes a thread for future arcs.

---

### Hook 2: The Animals Are Dying
**Quest Giver:** [[Sira|Sira]]
**Location:** [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Pens]]

Three goats and a mule died this week — stiff, found at morning, no visible cause. Surviving animals restless. Water tests clean. The village healer has no explanation.

**Goal:** Identify the cause. Stop it before the remaining animals are lost.

**Clues:**
- Cause: a small eyeless pale lizard-creature from the gorge entering through a drainage gap at night. Drains warmth, leaves no wound. Wall-walker juvenile driven upward from the gorge
- Sira flagged the gap for repair a month ago. Never done — the assigned stoneworker was doing Vakreen private storage work instead
- With the creature removed, animals stabilise. But only seven animals remain. Minimum for village support: twelve
- Sira: *"Seven animals can't support this village. We need a breeding group, not a memorial."*

**Reward:** Pen secured. The real problem is now clear — Kharanok needs a herd.

---

### Hook 3: Hunt for Food and Scraps
**Quest Giver:** [[Sira|Sira]] + [[Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** Black Spine Mountains passes

The village cannot wait for trade to fill the food gap. Players must lead hunting and foraging expeditions  into the Black Spine (or trade with Gith if they can) — which doubles as scouting for the final operation.

**Goal:** Run at least two successful expeditions. Raise food reserve to twelve weeks. Chart animal locations and moving herds. Find the captured herd.

**Clues:**
- Jessix knows mountain goat and kank watering-holes — guides one expedition
- Second expedition finds signs of a large gith herd-raid: a nomadic tribe's penned animals taken, not killed. Tracking leads to a canyon holding eighty captured animals — goats, mules, two kank — guarded by a skeleton gith crew
- Muuton owes a favour from Hook 1. Calling it in gives six additional riders — enough to take the canyon
- One night in camp: Dorak returns the clay tablet. He says: *"The old ones kept a second pen. Outside the village. Hidden in the rock. I know where it is."*

**Reward:** Herd location mapped. Riders available. Hidden pen discovered. The final operation is ready.

---

### Final Operation: Restock the Pens
**Quest Giver:** Sira + Yethras, together
**Location:** Canyon in the Black Spine passes

The canyon herd, the gith guards, the nomadic tribe, and Dorak's hidden pen combine into a single operation — the culmination of the entire arc.

**Goal:** Recover the captured herd. Deal with the gith. Negotiate with the nomadic tribe. Bring the animals back. Establish them in the main [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Pens]] plus Dorak's hidden second pen.

**Clues:**
- Three canyon approaches: direct (twelve gith guards), high via Jessix (two hours longer, above the guards), dry riverbed via tribe's scout (requires compensation commitment)
- The gith hold the animals for trade, not food. The buyer: the same merchant house from Act 3 Hook 3 — paying gith to destabilise the region
- Dorak's hidden pen: three hours from Kharanok, carved into a sheltered canyon fold with old water channels. Pre-dates the village by centuries. Holds forty animals
- Minimum success: 20 animals returned. Full success: entire herd of 80 plus a breeding agreement with the nomadic tribe — making the Pens self-sustaining

**Outcome:**
The Pens are restocked. Kharanok has food, water, trade, security, and a functional community. The arc is complete.

**Threads that remain** (next arc seeds):
- The jar — someone killed for it, the psionic institution still waits
- The thing behind the seal — intelligent, patient, not finished
- Dorak's clay tablet — foreign script, very old
- Jessareen's questions — tracking something in the Black Spine passes
- The second sealed chamber — what Kallia Yelka found below the Cistern
