## 4-A: The Toll System Is Broken
**Quest Giver:** [[Breck|Breck]] + [[Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Main Gate]]

**Situation:** The gate toll — Kharanok's only consistent income — is dysfunctional. Caravans bypass or bribe their way through. The village operating fund is empty. No trader will respect a settlement that can't enforce its own gate.

**Goal:** Audit the toll records, recover three months of underpaid tolls from two caravan operators, establish a fair enforceable rate, and collect the first full week of legitimate toll revenue.

**Hints:**
- The records were deliberately confused — same hand as the 3-B manifest leak.
- The two underpaying operators are known to [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]]. He'll lean on them if asked — he has route leverage over both.
- One of the two operators is the one who will eventually carry Freiha back from Nibenay. Handle the back-toll recovery carefully — heavy-handed enforcement could make him refuse to transport her.
- Breck: *"We don't need to be feared. We need to be taken seriously. There's a difference."*

**Leads to:** Kharanok has operating income again. Guards, repairs, and basic services are fundable.

## 4-B: Bring the First Merchant Back
**Quest Giver:** [[Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Traders]] → Nibenay (via [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessareen|Jessareen]])

**Situation:** Freiha ran Kharanok's only permanent stall. She left eight months ago after two robberies and zero council response. She is now in Nibenay running a smaller stable operation. She has specific conditions for returning — not vague assurances.

**Goal:** Find Freiha through [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessareen|Jessareen]] in Nibenay, meet her conditions (formal council apology; lockable stone stall, not a tent; [[Storage Pits]] access for overflow inventory), and escort her back.

**Hints:**
- [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessareen|Jessareen]] arranges the meeting — but she wants something too: a direct account of what players found in the Black Spine passes. She does not explain why. She has been tracking movement reports.
- The stone stall requires [[Dorak|Dorak]]'s help (he knows the [[Old Quarry Yard|quarry]]). The [[Storage Pits]] access requires [[Kallia Yelka|Kallia Yelka]]'s sign-off — which immediately surfaces friction with Set 5.
- Freiha mentions someone in Nibenay has been asking questions specifically about Kharanok's cistern — a psionic trader. The description matches the figure from 3-B.
- Once Freiha returns, two smaller traders follow within two weeks. The [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Traders]] district begins functioning.

**Leads to:** Cistern problems can no longer be avoided. Kallia Yelka's stonewalling on Storage Pit access triggers [[Set 5 - The Water Problem]].

## 4-C: The Rare Thing No One Else Has
**Quest Giver:** [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]] (in Nibenay — same trip as 4-B)
**Location:** [[Old Quarry Yard]] → Nibenay

**Situation:** Kharanok has black stone, rare minerals, and pre-human artefacts. Muuton's Nibenay contacts are interested but need a consistent, catalogued supply — not ad hoc deals. He needs something he can promise his buyers on a schedule.

**Goal:** Identify Kharanok's exportable speciality, establish a formal export catalogue, and make the first contracted deal with a buyer Muuton introduces.

**Hints:**
- The illegal [[Old Quarry Yard]] trade was selling something genuinely rare: a black mineral with psionic-dampening properties, unique to the deepest Black Spine cuts. The buyer was a psionic institution in Nibenay paying extremely well.
- [[Dorak|Dorak]] knows where the mineral vein is but refuses to re-enter the quarry. Players must convince him — his refusal is about the sound the stone makes when struck, and what he heard behind the wall.
- The formal export contract upgrades the gate toll: caravans now need Kharanok, not the other way around.
- The psionic institution's representative, when approached, mentions "the jar" from 3-B unprompted: *"We heard someone was bringing us something extraordinary. It never arrived."* Very calm about it.

**Leads to:** Kharanok has a trade identity and an anchor contract. Commerce is self-sustaining in principle. The growing population now strains the [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/The Cistern|Cistern]].
