## 6-A: The Stores Are Nearly Empty
**Quest Giver:** [[Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Storage Pits]]

**Situation:** With honest oversight in place, the audit reveals six weeks of food. The reserve should be two full seasons. The Vakreen household has been quietly liquidating community goods to fund their own escape from Kharanok. They are not villains — they gave up on the village and have been preparing to leave. But the goods belong to everyone.

**Goal:** Reconstruct the missing inventory, recover what remains in the Vakreen private pit, and decide with Yethras what happens to the family.

**Hints:**
- The Vakreen matriarch, confronted privately, offers no resistance. She is ashamed but not apologetic — she is a mother who did what she had to. She will make restitution if it means the family can stay without being branded.
- Part of the missing goods were sold to [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]]'s last caravan at below-market price. [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]] didn't know they were stolen — he will return them, but wants a favour the players now owe him.
- The recovered inventory plus Freiha's supply contracts brings the village to eight weeks of food. Not two seasons — 6-C closes the gap.
- A Vakreen child found something while helping secretly move goods: a sealed clay tablet in a foreign script, apparently very old. [[Dorak|Dorak]] takes it without a word and doesn't return it for two days.

**Leads to:** Community trust repaired or fractured depending on how players handle the Vakreens. Dorak's silence about the tablet becomes a thread.

## 6-B: The Animals Are Dying
**Quest Giver:** [[Sira|Sira]]
**Location:** [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Pens]]

**Situation:** Three goats and a mule have died this week — stiff, found in the morning, no visible cause. Surviving animals are restless. The water tests clean. The village healer has no explanation.

**Goal:** Identify the cause and stop it before the remaining animals are lost.

**Hints:**
- Cause: a small eyeless pale lizard-creature from the gorge has been entering the pen through a drainage gap at night. It drains warmth, leaves no wound. A wall-walker juvenile or similar, driven upward from the gorge.
- Sira flagged the drainage gap for repair a month ago. The repair was never done — the assigned stoneworker was doing the Vakreen family's private storage work instead.
- Once the creature is removed, the surviving animals stabilise. But there are now only seven animals in the [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Pens]]. The next quest requires a minimum of twelve.
- Sira: *"Seven animals can't support this village. We need a breeding group, not a memorial."*

**Leads to:** [[Final - Restock the Pens]] confirmed as the necessary endpoint.

## 6-C: Hunt for Food and Scraps
**Quest Giver:** [[Sira|Sira]] + [[Breck|Breck]] ([[Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]] reluctantly endorses)
**Location:** Black Spine Mountains passes

**Situation:** The village cannot wait for trade to fill the food gap. Players must lead or organise hunting and foraging expeditions into the Black Spine. This doubles as scouting — the routes used for hunting are the same routes needed for the Final quest.

**Goal:** Run at least two successful expeditions, raise the food reserve to twelve weeks, and chart locations of huntable animals and moving herds.

**Hints:**
- [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer|Jessix]] knows mountain goat and kank watering-holes most have forgotten — specific dry-month locations. He will guide one expedition.
- The second independent expedition finds signs of a large gith herd-raid: a nomadic tribe's penned animals were taken, not killed. Tracking the trail leads to a canyon holding eighty captured animals — goats, mules, two kank — guarded by a skeleton gith crew.
- [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]] owes the players a favour from 6-A. Calling it in gives six additional riders — enough to take the canyon if the gith are handled.
- One night in camp: [[Dorak|Dorak]] returns the clay tablet from 6-A. He says: *"The old ones kept a second pen. Outside the village. Hidden in the rock. I know where it is."* He says nothing more that night.

**Leads to:** [[Final - Restock the Pens]] — players know where the herd is, have riders, and have Dorak's hidden pen location.
