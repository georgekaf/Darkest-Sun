### Hook 1: The Toll System Is Broken
**Quest Giver:** [[Breck|Breck]] + [[Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Main Gate]]

The gate toll — Kharanok's only consistent income — is dysfunctional. Caravans bypass or bribe. The operating fund is empty. No trader respects a settlement that can't enforce its own gate.

**Goal:** Audit toll records, recover three months of underpaid tolls from two caravan operators, establish a fair rate, collect the first full week of legitimate revenue.

**Clues:**
- Records deliberately confused — same hand as the Act 3 manifest leak
- The two underpaying operators are known to Muuton — he has route leverage over both
- One operator will eventually carry Freiha back from Nibenay. Heavy-handed enforcement could make him refuse
- Breck: *"We don't need to be feared. We need to be taken seriously. There's a difference."*

**Reward:** Operating income. Guards, repairs, and basic services fundable.

### Hook 2: Bring the First Merchant Back
**Quest Giver:** [[Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Traders]] → Nibenay (via [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessareen|Jessareen]])

Freiha ran Kharanok's only permanent stall selling an elixir. She left 2 years ago after two robberies and zero council response. Now in New Giustenal. Has specific return conditions or at least specific.

**Goal:** Find Freiha via Jessareen, meet her conditions (formal council apology, lockable stone stall, [[Storage Pits]] inventory access), escort her back.

**Clues:**
- Jessareen arranges the meeting — wants something in return: a direct account of what players found in the Black Spine passes. She has been tracking movement reports
- The stone stall needs [[Dorak|Dorak]]'s help ([[Old Quarry Yard|quarry]] knowledge). Storage Pits access needs [[Yelka|Yelka]]'s sign-off — immediately surfaces friction
- Freiha mentions someone in Nibenay asking about Kharanok's cistern — a psionic trader matching the Act 3 description
- Once Freiha returns, two smaller traders follow within two weeks. The [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Traders]] district begins functioning

**Reward:** Traders start coming back. Yelka's stonewalling triggers Act 5.

### Hook 3: The Rare Thing No One Else Has
**Quest Giver:** [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]] (in Nibenay — same trip as Hook 2)
**Location:** [[Old Quarry Yard]] → Nibenay

Kharanok has black stone, rare minerals, pre-human artefacts. Muuton's Nibenay contacts need a consistent, catalogued supply — not ad hoc deals.

**Goal:** Identify Kharanok's exportable speciality, establish a formal export catalogue, make the first contracted deal.

**Clues:**
- The illegal [[Old Quarry Yard]] trade was selling something rare: a black mineral with psionic-dampening properties, unique to the deepest Black Spine cuts. Buyer: a psionic institution in Nibenay
- Dorak knows where the vein is but refuses to re-enter the quarry. His refusal is about the sound the stone makes — and what he heard behind the wall
- The formal contract upgrades the gate toll: caravans now need Kharanok
- The psionic institution's representative mentions "the jar" from Act 3 unprompted: *"We heard someone was bringing us something extraordinary. It never arrived."*

**Reward:** Trade identity and anchor contract. Commerce self-sustaining. Growing population strains the [[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/The Cistern|Cistern]] → Act 5.
