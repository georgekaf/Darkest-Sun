# Kharanok: The Altar of Dust

A remote mountain settlement built around a sacred altar. Perched on black rock ledges in the Black Spine Mountains, Kharanok survives through barter, stone-cutting, and the rituals of the Altar.

- **[[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Traders|Traders]]** — Makeshift market of tents, stalls, and barter. Information hub.
- **[[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Main Gate|Main Gate]]** — Only safe entrance, heavy fortifications, toll required.
- **[[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/The Cistern|The Cistern]]** — Lifeblood of the village. Sacred spring water, strictly rationed.
- **[[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Pens|Pens]]** — Animal enclosures. Smelly, noisy, good for covert meetings.
- **[[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Caves (Sealed)]]** — Sealed entrance beneath the village. Strange sounds, forbidden ground.
- **[[Storage Pits|Storage Pits]]** — Dug-out food and supply caches. Strictly controlled.
- **[[Caravans|Caravans]]** — Resting point near the gate. Rumours, negotiations, news.
- **[[Altar of Dust|Altar of Dust]]** — Spiritual centre. Ash, smoke, offerings, omens.
- **[[Watch Tower|Watch Tower]]** — Highest fortified point. Sentries, fire signals, political control.
- **[[Hanging Bridges|Hanging Bridges]]** — Rope bridges over gorges. Defensive chokepoints, dangerous at night.
- **[[Hidden/Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/Old Quarry Yard|Old Quarry Yard]]** — Abandoned worksite. Strange sounds, illicit deals.
- **[[Shade Houses|Shade Houses]]** — Dwellings carved into rock. Cool, dark, interconnected.
