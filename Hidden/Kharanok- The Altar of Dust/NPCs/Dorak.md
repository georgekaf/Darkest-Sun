# Dorak

**Role:** Ex-mine foreman — former Gith slave, only living guide to the tunnels beneath Kharanok
**Found at:** [[Old Quarry Yard]]
**Faction:** Kharanok Local

---

## Background

Dorak spent years as a slave of the gith who work the tunnels beneath Kharanok, forced to mine crystal dust and obsidian for masters who called him a tool long before they called him a traitor. He knows those tunnels better than anyone alive in the village — better, in places, than the gith themselves. However he won or bought his freedom, it was never forgiven: his old masters still call him "traitor" and mean it literally when they find him again. He came back to Kharanok tired, bitter, and slow to trust, but the years of captivity didn't burn away his loyalty to the village — when pushed, he guides players into the mines himself, hands over tools he'd kept hidden since his escape, and warns them honestly about what's ahead even when it costs him to say it. He is afraid of the deepest tunnels — partly the gith, but also something older and stranger he's felt down there that he's never been able to put into words.

---

## Agenda

Avoid going back into the deepest tunnels himself. Help Kharanok survive without personally confronting what's down there again. Guide players as far as he's willing to go, and be honest with them about the rest, even reluctantly.

---

## Wants & Needs

- **Wants:** To never see his old masters again
- **Needs:** To know the years he spent enslaved meant something — that helping Kharanok now settles part of that debt

---

## Goals

- Get players into the mines without going further himself than he has to
- Hand over what he's kept hidden since his escape (tools, knowledge of the layout) to people who can actually use it
- Stay out of direct confrontation with the gith who once owned him

---

## Roleplay Notes

Long silences before answers — old habit from captivity, not evasiveness for its own sake. Doesn't lie, but often says nothing instead of responding. Goes quiet and stays quiet when reminded of his years as a slave, or when something in the tunnels reacts the way his old masters used to. Not evasive about ordinary inconvenient things; only about what he's genuinely afraid of. When he finally shares something he's kept to himself, he says it quickly, flatly, and changes the subject right after.

---

## Relationships

- **Former captors:** the gith mining crew beneath Kharanok — call him "traitor," would take him back or kill him on sight
- **Knows:** the tunnel layout better than anyone in Kharanok, including routes the gith themselves use
- **Provides:** tools stashed since his escape, knowledge of the richest mineral veins, warnings about what's deeper
- **Afraid of:** something old, patient, and not-gith that he's felt further down but never explained in full
- **Test he sets:** before guiding anyone deeper, he makes them camp a night atop the quarry first — he doesn't fully understand why, only that it seems to matter. It was during one such night that the moonbeast beneath Kharanok reached out to the whole party in a shared dream
