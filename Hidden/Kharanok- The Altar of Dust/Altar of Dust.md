# Altar of Dust

The **Altar of Dust** is the spiritual and symbolic center of Kharanok. It stands on an elevated point near the cliff edges, open to the sky. Ash, incense, bones, dried herbs, and ground black stone burn on it. The smoke rises slowly and mingles with the dust of the mountains.

The residents believe the Altar appeases the spirits of the mountain, protects the Cistern's water, and keeps the sealed depths sleeping. Every family leaves small offerings: animal hair, fragments of tools, ash from their hearth, or soil from an ancestor's grave.

Ceremonies at the Altar are sparse but heavy in atmosphere. No one laughs there. Even the most cynical merchants lower their voices. If the smoke changes colour or moves against the wind, residents treat it as a warning.
