# Pens

The **Pens** occupy the lowest and dirtiest section of the village. Goats, mules, mountain pack animals, and other hardy creatures able to survive in the Black Spine Mountains are kept here. The area is full of noise, smells, straw, dung, broken troughs, and fences repaired again and again.

The animals are essential to Kharanok's survival. They provide milk, meat, hides, transport, and hauling power for stone and supplies. Herders and stable hands know the surrounding trails well and are often the first to notice strange movement in the mountains.

The Pens are also a good place for covert meetings, since most people avoid the area because of the stench. Under the noise of the animals, someone can speak without being easily overheard.
