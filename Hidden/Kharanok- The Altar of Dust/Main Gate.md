# Main Gate

The **Main Gate** is the only safe and official entrance into Kharanok. It is built across a narrow mountain pass where the road pinches between black rock faces and sheer drops. The construction is heavy — dark timber, iron, stone, and sharpened stakes — designed not only to stop raiders but to intimidate anyone who approaches.

Gate guards check caravans, strangers, smugglers, and pilgrims coming for the **Altar of Dust**. No one passes without stating their reason for visiting, paying a small toll, or surrendering something valuable as surety. At night the gate closes entirely; only those with permission from the village elders may enter.

The ground around the gate is packed with trampled dust, old ash, and animal tracks. Dried charms, bones, and broken metal plates hang from the crossbeams — relics of past attacks that were repelled.
