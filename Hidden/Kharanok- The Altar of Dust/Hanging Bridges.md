# Hanging Bridges

The **Hanging Bridges** connect Kharanok's different ledges across deep gorges and sheer drops. They are made from thick rope, blackened wooden planks, metal rings, and stakes driven deep into the rock. Every bridge groans under the weight of people and animals while the wind of the Black Spine Mountains makes them sway dangerously.

Residents use them daily but never carelessly. They know which plank is rotten, which rope has started to fray, and which bridge should be avoided when the wind is strong. For strangers, however, the crossing is terrifying — beneath their feet there is only void, dust, and black rock.

The bridges serve a defensive purpose too. In a raid they can be cut, raised, or trapped, isolating sections of the village and preventing enemies from advancing. Some have small observation posts or hidden positions for archers.

At night the bridges become even more dangerous. Oil lamps illuminate only a few steps ahead, air whistles through the ropes, and shadows seem to move beneath the planks. The elders say that sometimes, when dust rises from the gorges, the bridges do not always lead to the same place.
