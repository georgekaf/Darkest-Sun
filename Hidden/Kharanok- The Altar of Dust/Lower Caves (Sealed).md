# Lower Caves (Sealed)

The **Lower Caves** lie beneath Kharanok and their entrance has been sealed with stone, iron, and old ritual markings. Residents avoid even looking in that direction. For the young, it is forbidden ground. For the old, it is a wound that must not be reopened.

It is said that Kharanok's first miners dug too deep searching for rare ore and found caves that did not belong to the natural mountain. The walls were smooth, the dust moved without wind, and voices echoed in the wrong way. After a series of disappearances, the entrance was sealed and the matter buried.

Despite the sealing, low sounds are heard from below on certain nights. Sometimes the earth trembles faintly, sometimes the Cistern darkens, and sometimes dust-trails are found outside the sealed entrance — as if something emerged and re-entered without opening the door.
