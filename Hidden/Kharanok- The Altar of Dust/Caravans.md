# Caravans

The **Caravan** area sits near the gate and serves as the first resting point for those arriving in Kharanok. Pack animals are tied up here, wagons are unloaded, and goods are checked before moving deeper into the village. It is a noisy, dirty space full of saddles, ropes, leather sacks, wheels, barrels, and tired animals.

Caravans bring what the village cannot produce: grain, cloth, oil, tools, salt, timber, and medicines. In return they take black stone, metal, mountain dust, strange minerals, and stories that no one can verify. Caravan drivers are among the few people who know what is happening outside the mountains.

This area is ideal for rumours, negotiations, and secret meetings. One can hear news of lost passes, raids, missing travellers, or strange lights visible at night through the gorges.
