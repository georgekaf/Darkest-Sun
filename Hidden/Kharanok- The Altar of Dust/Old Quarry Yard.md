# Old Quarry Yard

The **Old Quarry Yard** is an abandoned work site at the edge of the village. The black stone of the Black Spine Mountains was once quarried here — hard material used for the houses, walls, bridges, and the Altar itself. Now the area is full of broken scaffolding, cut stone, rusted tools, and half-collapsed structures.

The old workers say the quarry was abandoned because the stone began making strange sounds when struck. Others say the workers found something behind a rock wall and the village leaders ordered extraction to stop immediately. No one agrees on the same version.

Today the site is used occasionally for repairs, stone storage, and illegal deals. At night, few approach the old yard, because the wind passes through the cracks and sounds like whispering.
