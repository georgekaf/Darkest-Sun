# Traders

The **Traders' district** is Kharanok's makeshift market. It has none of the order of a city — it is a sprawl of tents, wooden stalls, crates, rugs spread on bare earth, and small storerooms carved into the rock. Tools, food, hides, metal fittings, charms, powders, old weapons, and objects recovered from the lower caves or abandoned paths are all sold here.

Kharanok's merchants are hard traders. Water, salt, and metal carry more value than coins, and most deals are made by barter. A stranger who doesn't know the prices will be cheated; a careful traveller may find rare things unavailable anywhere else.

The market is also an information hub. Merchants hear everything: who arrived, who left, who owes debts, who is hiding something, and which groups descended toward the **Lower Caves** and did not come back the same.
