# Watch Tower

The **Watch Tower** stands at the highest fortified point in Kharanok. From it the hanging bridges, passes, gorges, and roads from which a caravan or enemy might approach are all visible. It is built from timber, stone, and metal supports, with flags and canvas that beat constantly in the wind.

Tower sentries are tasked with watching for raiders, monsters, rockslides, fires, dust storms, and lost caravans. They have bells, fire signals, and mirrors to alert the village quickly. When the tower's great fire is lit, everyone knows something is coming.

The Tower is also a political control point. Whoever controls observation controls information. A guard can conceal someone's arrival, delay an alarm, or send a covert signal to people outside the village.
