## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: the court of a Stone Giant warlock in a mountain valley — an immense throne room and feasting hall built to emphasize the warlock's size over his petitioners. Staffed by ettin guards and gnoll/flind herders managing his bison herds. A flight of gargoyles recently pledged allegiance and now serve as silent sentries; one is currently hunting "verminous dwarves" that infiltrated the northern pantry.
