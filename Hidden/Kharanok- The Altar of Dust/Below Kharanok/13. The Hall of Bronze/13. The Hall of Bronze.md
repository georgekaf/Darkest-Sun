## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: centered on "the massive bronze statues of dwarven champions that decorate the great hall," mostly former dwarven living quarters. Home to the Descent — a large circular chamber over 80ft across, spanning all three city levels, with stairs linking upper and lower. Dyson calls it the densest section he'd mapped so far, and was deliberately uncertain what currently inhabits it — "definitely not" the typical goblin hordes.
