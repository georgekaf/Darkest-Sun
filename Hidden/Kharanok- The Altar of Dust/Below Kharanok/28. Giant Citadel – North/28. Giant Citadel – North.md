## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: the northern section of a stone giant stronghold — water access, a clothes-washing room, and a guardroom overlooking the dwarven mines beyond. Home to "a large clan of stone giants and their assorted henchmen, lesser giants, and hangers-on," most in the southern portion. Two entwined passage systems: one to fungus-born nightmare stalkers and the mines outside, the other a secret route (through a large floor crack) letting dwarves and adventurers observe the giants' domain without direct confrontation.
