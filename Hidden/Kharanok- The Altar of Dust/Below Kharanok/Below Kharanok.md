# Below Kharanok — Level Index

Isometric overview, all 31 areas pinned. Interactive version (pan/zoom, click a pin to jump to its section) lives at [isometric-map/index.html](file:///D:/Darkest%20Sun/kharanok%20dungeon/isometric-map/index.html).

31 areas, numbered 01–31 in walkthrough order (user-supplied connection map — an entrance-outward traversal, not a raw visual estimate). Grouped below into 8 depth floors (also user-supplied). Source maps are Dyson Logos's "Dyson MegaDelve" pack — [master index](https://dysonlogos.blog/maps/the-dyson-megadelve/) — only level 01 has been played/keyed so far. Full dungeon spans roughly 6 miles top to bottom, hexagonal footprint, situated in the Blackspine Mountains. [[01. Obsidian Mines AKA The Cannibal Caves]] is the main entrance, opening out below Kharanok itself; the other surface exits noted throughout (per Dyson's node map) lead to other points elsewhere in the Blackspine range, except Necropolis of Bryn Mynnyd (19), whose exit specifically leads to Quor'Anok.

**Sealed history:** Floor 1 was sealed off for a matter of weeks; Floor 2 and everything below it was sealed for 150 years. The ruins and built structures on every floor can be as old as 12,000 years.
