## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: also called the East Mines, a former source of iron ore for dwarven armies during a war against elves. A natural chimney descends from the Mushroom Cavern to a sump room below. Small airways once carried fresh air to dwarf workers; now the malevolent Gimchak Spiders (from a nearby deep crevasse) use them to scout and monitor whether dwarven mining might resume and disrupt their society.
