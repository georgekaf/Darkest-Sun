## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: shows "the various paths the Lost River has taken over the centuries through the bedrock" — two old riverbeds now dry as the river flows through a tight passage before rejoining its former course. Small structures are built into the cave walls by religious outcasts from the northern caves, who subsist on mushroom cultivation, occasional raids, and sympathizers' handouts.
