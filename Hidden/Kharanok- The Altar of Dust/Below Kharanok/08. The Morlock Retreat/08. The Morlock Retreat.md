## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: north of Lost River Cave, tunnels that were once riverbeds now house a war party of ~50 morlocks, who've carved their lair from limestone attempting to recreate ancient elven architectural styles. Devolved, carnivorous, and partial to humanoid flesh, they're barred from the deeper delve by ghoul acolytes they frequently clash with, and use a concealed northern passage and ladder to reach the surface for hunting raids.
