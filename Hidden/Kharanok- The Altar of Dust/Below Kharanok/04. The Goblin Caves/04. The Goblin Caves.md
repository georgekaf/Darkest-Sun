## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: home to goblin-equivalent creatures in interconnected chambers — Dyson floated breaking the standard goblin mold entirely (earwig-like humanoids, semi-sentient rhesus monkeys). The southeastern passage houses most of the tribe, who farm the adjacent Mushroom Cavern but see themselves as an "elite force" of rangers venturing outside the warren. Includes a goblin king's audience chamber.
