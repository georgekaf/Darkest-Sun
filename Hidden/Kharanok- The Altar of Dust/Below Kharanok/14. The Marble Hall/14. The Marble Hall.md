## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: a massive underground chamber nearly 150ft tall, its dwarven-architecture facade carved with oversized marble reliefs of dwarven warriors, engineers, and priests looking down on trespassers. Unclaimed by any faction, it functions as an unintended entry point into the dwarven city — stirges nest near the chamber's top, giant spiders climb up from deeper levels, giving the hall a desolate, haunted atmosphere.
