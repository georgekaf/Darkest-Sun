## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: an underground complex beneath the temple above, a forbidden sanctuary for a religious order of ghouls and ghasts. Its standout feature is a chamber holding "the ribcage of an ancient gold dragon long ago interred here and slowly fossilized into pure necromantic gold," the dragon's skull at its center holding a massive ruby wreathed in magical flame. Separate sections (each with its own stairwell) house ghoul/ghast inhabitants keeping monk-like routines of meditation and prayer, alongside grim features — a bone-filled depression, a chamber filled with rotted blood.
