## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: buried beneath the ruins of Bryn Mynnyd lies the Church of Silence — "the Death Cult of Bryn Mynnyd" or Assassin's Cult, dedicated to murder rather than necromancy. Assassin-priests train local creatures to avoid their tomb using alchemically-treated blue torches as markers. They prefer victims genuinely dead, but can raise hundreds of skeletons and zombies from corpses stored in wall niches if seriously threatened.
