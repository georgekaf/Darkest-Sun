## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: named for "the sound of the waterfall that crashes down into the chasm these mines were built up around." At the chasm's bottom, the Earthship Docks, where dwarves once stationed their vessels during an ancient war against elves. Currently sheltering the Brotherhood — nearly mute, near-identical humans reopening the mines and processing ore at the Hall of Iron. Unlike the straight-tunneled Hematite Mines, this evokes *The Hobbit*'s vertical shaft mining, seeking gems along deep natural shafts rather than ore veins.
