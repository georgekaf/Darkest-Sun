## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: "the Iron Halls served as the forge and foundry for the dwarves here until they climbed back into their great Earthships and left our world." The forges are unlit, though "the brotherhood" — a group of distinctive hairless humans mining the depths — may soon restore them. An ancient dwarven elevator connects up to the Hall of Bronze; giant spiders wander in from the neighboring Venomous Hall. The 90ft-wide Descent chamber terminates here, leading up toward the city's collapsed gates.
