## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: an enormous cavern (mapped at 20ft/square, not the usual 10ft). Its elevated eastern section sits beneath a 100ft hole in the forest floor above, letting in sunlight, birds, and even trees — a druid lives here, having flown in through the opening. The cave's name comes from a small river that descends into the mountainside via a hazardous waterfall entry. Notable features: an elevated "Fortress" with a stone ring vantage point, and "The Spires" — massive stalagmites gating the deeper sections.
