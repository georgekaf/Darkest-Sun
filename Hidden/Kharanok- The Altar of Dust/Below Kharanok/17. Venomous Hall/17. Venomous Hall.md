## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: once a celebration hall where dwarves recorded their genealogy, now infested with intelligent, ancient, hungry monstrous spiders. "The chasm that wraps around this part of the delve is filled with a heavy fog of presumably magical origin that never seems to burn away," and the spiders crawl through it between the surrounding halls' massive windows. The genealogical records still here make it valuable to adventurers helping dwarf scions claim noble rights among their kin.
