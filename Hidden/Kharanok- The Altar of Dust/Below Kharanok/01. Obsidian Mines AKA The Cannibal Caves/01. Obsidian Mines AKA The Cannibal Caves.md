Visual guide for points of Interest
![[9b29cccb-3bca-4c51-997d-2121b5c6f789.png]]**OBSIDIAN MINES**

Officially named for the obsidian vein — Crystal Dust is mined here too, but it's illegal to mine and trade, so the site keeps the obsidian name as cover.

1. **North Entrance** — Narrow cave mouth and mine access.
2. **River Walk** — Tight passage following the underground stream.
3. **Bridge Crossing** — Wooden bridge over fast-moving water.
4. **Main Excavation** — Primary mining chamber with worked stone.
5. **West Galleries** — Older side tunnels and abandoned dig sites.
6. **Deep Lake** — Large underground reservoir of dark water. This is where Crystal Dust actually forms: water drags shards down from the mine and deposits them here, recoverable once the water dries out every noon. Crystals with lots of foreign minerals mixed in are cloudy, lower quality — the clearer, higher-quality crystals are found deeper still, past this point, where Bulette and other monsters roam and the passage narrows.
7. **Lower Pools** — Small runoff pools near the basin floor.
8. **Foreman's Office** — Supervisor's room for records and planning. Same as the "quartermaster chambers" — the door to it is hidden behind crates in the Storehouse (#10), locked from the other side. Has stairs leading down to Level 2.
9. **Barracks** — Sleeping quarters for miners and guards. - (It can also host the teleportation circles) — reached via two illusionary walls; this is a second, separate hidden route into the same one-way teleportation room reachable from the Storehouse (#10). Barracks are next to the Foreman's Office (#8). The portal requires Crystal Dust: 10 Crystal Dust to activate it, then 1 Crystal Dust per use.
10. **Storehouse** — Supplies, tools, and extracted ore storage. Two rooms, mostly unexplored as of ad3: one has the hidden crate-door to the Foreman's Office (#8); the other (the one with the elevator shaft, #12) has a hidden passage on its right side leading to the one-way teleportation room also reachable from the Barracks (#9).
11. **Workshop** — Repair area for gear, carts, and equipment.
12. **South Access Shaft** — Lower exit shaft and service route. Wooden rope elevator drops ~180m (590 ft / 0.11 mi), then a rock-carved staircase covers the remaining ~1,735m (5,693 ft / 1.08 mi) of the full 1.92km shaft depth — roughly 10,200 steps, several hours on foot with rests — continuing the descent toward [[02. The Rat-Folk Tunnels]] - The shaft elevator can be broken - It can require 50 scraps or Tar to repair after Roy the One Arm Crafter inspects it. (See [[0003 - Session Prep 16-7-2026]] — needs tar *and* someone who actually knows how to work it, not just the material.)

## Room Connections
- **1 (North Entrance)** → south along the narrow river passage → **2 (River Walk)**
- **2 (River Walk)** → continues south along the river → **3 (Bridge Crossing)**
- **3 (Bridge Crossing)** is the main fork:
  - West/south, following the water → **4 (Main Excavation)**
  - East, through a door → the built room complex, starting at **8 (Foreman's Office)**
- **4 (Main Excavation)** → west, side tunnel → **5 (West Galleries)**
- **4 (Main Excavation)** → the water continues south, narrowing → **6 (Deep Lake)**
- **6 (Deep Lake)** → south, small runoff pools at the basin's edge → **7 (Lower Pools)**
- **8 (Foreman's Office)** → south through a corridor (a stairwell down to Level 2 sits at this junction) → splits into **11 (Workshop)** and **9 (Barracks)**
- **11 (Workshop)** → south → **10 (Storehouse)**
- **10 (Storehouse)** → narrow corridor south → **12 (South Access Shaft)** → leads to [[02. The Rat-Folk Tunnels]]
- **9 (Barracks)** has a small attached side room off its south-east corner (unlabeled on the map — same hidden teleportation room described below)

**Hidden connections (unrevealed to players as of ad3):**
- **10 (Storehouse)**, room with the crates → hidden door behind the crates, locked from the other side → **8 (Foreman's Office)**, i.e. the "quartermaster chambers"
- **10 (Storehouse)**, the room with the elevator shaft (adjacent to #12) → hidden passage on the room's right side → a **one-way teleportation room**
- **9 (Barracks)** → two illusionary walls → the same one-way teleportation room reachable from #10 — a second, separate hidden route to the same destination
