Visual guide for points of Interest
![[9b29cccb-3bca-4c51-997d-2121b5c6f789.png]]**OBSIDIAN MINES**

1. **North Entrance** — Narrow cave mouth and mine access.
2. **River Walk** — Tight passage following the underground stream.
3. **Bridge Crossing** — Wooden bridge over fast-moving water.
4. **Main Excavation** — Primary mining chamber with worked stone.
5. **West Galleries** — Older side tunnels and abandoned dig sites.
6. **Deep Lake** — Large underground reservoir of dark water.
7. **Lower Pools** — Small runoff pools near the basin floor.
8. **Foreman’s Office** — Supervisor’s room for records and planning.
9. **Barracks** — Sleeping quarters for miners and guards. - (It can also host the teleportation circles)
10. **Storehouse** — Supplies, tools, and extracted ore storage.
11. **Workshop** — Repair area for gear, carts, and equipment.
12. **South Access Shaft** — Lower exit shaft and service route. The lower exit shaft leads to 02. Rat-Folk Tunnels - The shaft elevator can be broken - It can require 50 scraps or Tar to repair after Roy the One Arm Crafter inspects it.
