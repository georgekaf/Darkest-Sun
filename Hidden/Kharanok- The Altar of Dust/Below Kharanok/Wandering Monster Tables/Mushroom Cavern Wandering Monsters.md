# Wandering Monsters — The Mushroom Cavern

Backup copy of Dyson Logos's die-drop wandering monster table for [[05. The Mushroom Cavern]].

## Source
[Dyson's Dodecahedron — Drop Table 1.1: The Mushroom Cavern Wandering Monsters](https://dysonlogos.blog/2015/01/01/drop-table-1-1-the-mushroom-cavern-wandering-monsters/)

## Mechanic
Uses a d8 and d6 dropped simultaneously (a physical "die-drop" table, not a rolled lookup):
- d8 placement determines which encounter type occurs
- d8 number face indicates how many creatures appear
- d6 number face specifies encounter circumstances
- d6 placement sometimes provides additional information

## Sample encounters
- 7 Goblins equipped with Javelins & Handaxes, out on Guard Patrol
- 1 Ogre with 300gp, out Collecting Mushrooms
- 3 Goblins armed with Short Bows & Short Swords, Hiding from the Dark Green Slime
- 10 Humans (6 with pole arms) carrying 14sp, fighting 5 Fire Beetles

The visual/spatial die-placement method generates circumstance and flavor automatically, rather than relying on a text table alone.
