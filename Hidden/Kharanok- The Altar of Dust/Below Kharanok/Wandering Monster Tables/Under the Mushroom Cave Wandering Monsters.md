# Wandering Monsters — Under the Mushroom Cave

Backup copy of Dyson Logos's die-drop wandering monster table for [[30. Under the Mushroom Cave]] — described by Dyson as "that little secret area underneath the mushroom cavern," the second node of the Mega Delve.

## Source
[Dyson's Dodecahedron — Another Die-Drop Wandering Monster Table](https://dysonlogos.blog/2015/01/19/another-die-drop-wandering-monster-table/)

## Mechanic
Drop 2d6 of different colors. Both dice determine group makeup: the dark die is "primary" and picks which encounter chart applies; the light die determines the specific result within that chart.

## Encounter types
Subhumans, warlocks, thorny creatures, and subhuman priests — equipped with items like short bows and short swords, some with prepared spells (Charm Person, Cause Fear).

## Sample encounters
- A single subhuman warlock with a thorny companion (warlock has Charm Person prepared)
- 2 subhumans and 2 subhuman priests carrying a totem reliquary, on a serious perimeter patrol

## Design note
Dyson notes this area has "a lot less creatures in it (and they are all of one faction and no one else currently knows about it)," which required creative adjustments to the usual table design.
