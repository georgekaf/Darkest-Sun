## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: once the grand entrance to a vast dwarven city, with massive bronze lanterns and halls lit by sunlight. When the dwarves left in their Earthships they sealed the gates and destroyed the water systems feeding the lower levels. Now decayed, dimly lit only by scattered sunlight through debris — and feathers from the harpies now inhabiting "The Tall Watch" tower above. A collapsed stairwell (lower right) connects to that tower; the harpies occasionally descend through the cylindrical Descent chamber seeking victims.
