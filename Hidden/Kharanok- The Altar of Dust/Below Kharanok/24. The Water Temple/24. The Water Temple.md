## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: where "the Frog Priests cultivate their mutant frogs and worship foul gods of the depths." Populated by frog folk and their spawn, guarded by fish-people mercenaries, ruled by two spirit nagas acting as intermediaries to their deity. The nagas run an otyugh breeding program meant to distribute the creatures throughout the delve via their mercenary allies. South of the temple lies a 600ft underground waterfall — beyond it, unknown territory.
