## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: taken over by a tribe of ogres — "base and crude creatures, but ones clever enough to avoid the city and its hazards." They farm mushrooms in an adjacent cave and raid goblins and settlements to the north near the Lost River Cave. Bridges span a deep river-carved cavern; pipes built into pillars let out an eerie whistle when doors open, alerting the ogres to investigate.
