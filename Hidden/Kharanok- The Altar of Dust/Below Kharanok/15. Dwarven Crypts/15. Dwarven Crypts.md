## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: a former mithril mine converted into burial chambers for dwarven clans. Rather than undead, it's inhabited by giant spiders near the entrance, with rot grubs and ear-seekers deeper in — plus a long, thin black dragon that subsists on both the spiders and the buried grave-goods. Three burial types: Tombs (sarcophagi and doors), Vaults (doors only), and open-air Crypts.
