## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: features a stepped temple tended by silent, robed monks who keep an "eternal obsidian pyre that burns quietly through the sunless days down here." They offer consecrated waters as a powerful healing aid to injured sentient creatures — though drinking them outside that context is poisonous. Stone-built slopes connect the area's levels. The monks are actually ghouls, and ghasts inhabit the lower temple levels, fiercely protecting them from intruders.
