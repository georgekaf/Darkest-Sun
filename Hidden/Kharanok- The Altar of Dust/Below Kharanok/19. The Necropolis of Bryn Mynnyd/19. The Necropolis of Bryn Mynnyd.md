## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: "The ruins of Bryn Mynnyd date back untold ages, and the city has been looted repeatedly by adventurers of all stripes." Remaining inhabitants: farmers living among the ruins, and Death Cult members who pass through to reach their underground temple. The ancient city mined True Iron from deep veins, eventually abandoned in favor of crypts. A massive natural chimney and an 888-step staircase connect the ruined surface city to the old crypts and mines below.
