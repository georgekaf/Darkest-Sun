## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: "Once home to 10 mithril statues of the elders of the clans who founded this city," now barren and empty, showing damage from the destruction of the city gates and cisterns. Stone bridges span 35ft up at two points, one collapsed in scavenger conflicts. A partially destroyed cistern beyond has become overgrown with an unusual fungal forest — home to intelligent, deadly, poisonous fungal creatures, distinct from the dungeon's other mushroom caves.
