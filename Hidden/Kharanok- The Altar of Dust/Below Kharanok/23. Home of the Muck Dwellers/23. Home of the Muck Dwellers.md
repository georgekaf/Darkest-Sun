## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: "strange ovoid structures" built jointly by four amphibian groups — frogfolk (religious, xenophobic), fishfolk (warriors/hunters), mutant frogs, and jellies (sentient jellyfish sorcerers). A natural hazard where the Lost River is compressed between two stone pillars called "the teeth," where fishfolk maintain nets to trap anything swept downstream by the white-water current.
