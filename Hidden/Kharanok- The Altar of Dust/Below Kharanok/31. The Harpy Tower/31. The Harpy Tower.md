# 31. The Harpy Tower

*Below Kharanok — walkthrough position 31 of 31, Floor 5 of 8 (per Dyson source, see note below). Stub page — not yet keyed or played in this campaign.*

![[harpy-tower-production.jpg]]

## Source
[Dyson's Dodecahedron — The Harpy Tower](https://rpgcharacters.wordpress.com/2015/03/31/megadelve-the-harpy-tower/)
https://dysonlogos.blog/2015/03/31/megadelve-the-harpy-tower/

Per Dyson's original text, the tower rises **above** the dwarven city rather than descending below it: "Climbing up over 2,000 feet above the dwarven city it watches over, this tower has become home to a clamour of harpies." Its lowest level sits "120 feet above the floor of the city gates level, and well over 60 feet above the top of the ruined staircase" — a (now-destroyed) staircase originally connected it down to [[12. Gates of the City]]: "The stairs connecting this tower to the dwarven city gates below were destroyed when the dwarven city was sealed off after the great war." This places it at the Floor 5 stratum, not Floor 8 — Dyson's own [node map](https://dysonlogos.blog/maps/the-dyson-megadelve/) also draws it as its own isolated branch with a surface exit, not connected into the Necropolis/Crypts cluster.
