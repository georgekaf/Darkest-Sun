## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: a small settlement of self-exiled dwarves, deep in the mines after abandoning their city above. Under siege from "thin and loathsome humanoids with pitch black skin," invisible to darkvision and only findable by torchlight. The dwarves survive on black cap mushrooms — a magical, psychotropic fungus that "tastes like a rich and sweet chocolate steak" — which appear to have manifested the very creatures now threatening them. The stalkers communicate telepathically and hunt dwarven flesh, threatening to overrun the undermanned outpost.
