## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: the earliest dwarven mining operation in the complex, now partially submerged by an underground river's overflow — most wet areas 2-4ft deep, the northernmost room and its tunnel fully submerged. Slimes and molds inhabit the damp, but the real threat is a powerful mastermind beast holding the northern area, commanding "the brotherhood" (near-identical human-like creatures under its control) plus swarms of carnivorous offspring in the water.
