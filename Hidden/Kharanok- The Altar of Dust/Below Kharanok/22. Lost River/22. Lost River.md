## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: a dangerous refuge where the Lost River re-emerges after its underground journey — characters swept downstream may wash ashore here as "the Lost River's bed widens again and the water flow slows to manageable speeds." Home to a small otyugh colony fishing the river for debris, and a truly massive giant slug that consumes whatever they leave behind. A crossroads between the Necropolis Crypts, dwarven mines, and Muck Dwellers. Features "a twisted stairwell" of smooth, mother-of-pearl-like pink material — clearly built, but not by humans or dwarves.
