## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: carved out of old mines beneath the necropolis, with niches and alcoves dug into the walls to house the dead. Damp, bone-strewn, littered with mining debris. The tombs themselves are quiet and undisturbed, but adventurers face corpse-rot, traps, strange oozes, and "strange humanoids who seem to feed off the darkness itself." Only the northern death cultists would raise the dead here, and only in extreme circumstances.
