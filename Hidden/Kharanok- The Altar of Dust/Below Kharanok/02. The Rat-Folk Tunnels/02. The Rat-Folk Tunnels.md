## Points of Interest
*Not yet keyed for this campaign.* Per Dyson's source: inhabited by "rat folk" — corrupted halflings who worship a rodent deity and practice cannibalism, drawing most of their food from the Mushroom Cavern to the north. Features a prominent round central room suited for a complex combat encounter with gallery attacks. The rat folk stay vigilant against ogre raids from the east, though the ogres typically take wider routes to reach the main cavern.
