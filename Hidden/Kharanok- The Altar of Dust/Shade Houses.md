# Shade Houses

The **Shade Houses** are Kharanok's dwellings, built or carved into the black rock. They sit higher in the village, in spots where direct sunlight does not strike all day. The walls are thick, the windows narrow, and entrances are often covered with hides or cloth to keep out heat, dust, and wind.

Inside, coolness and darkness prevail. Families store water in small vessels, hang herbs from the ceiling, and keep valuables in hidden recesses in the rock. Many houses are connected to each other by narrow passages so residents can move without stepping into the hot air outside.

The Shade Houses are the most human side of Kharanok. Children, elders, craftspeople, exhausted miners, and people trying to live normally in an inhospitable place are found here. Even so, locked doors, whispers, and small family secrets are everywhere.
