# Storage Pits

The **Storage Pits** are dug into the earth and rock in a more protected section of the village. Dry food, grain, salt, charcoal, rope, tools, spare parts, weapons, and siege supplies are kept here. Each pit is sealed with heavy stone slabs, wooden covers, or metal locks.

The area is quieter than the market but more strictly controlled. The stores belong to families, guilds, or the community, and every loss is recorded. In a place like Kharanok, food is not mere property — it is power.

Some pits are used for darker purposes. They may conceal contraband, old weapons, forbidden finds from the quarry, or objects the residents do not want the priests of the Altar to see.
