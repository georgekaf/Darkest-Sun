## Part B: Adventure Book — Deep Ash & Dust

A multi-part adventure for mid-level parties exploring Giustenal's ruins and the underworld below.

### Part One: Enter the Ruins

PCs must cross the tar pits or find another entry through Giustenal's great wall (40 ft high). The ruined city is choked with silt — movement kicks up dust clouds (-2 attack/damage penalties). Corpses lie everywhere, some preserved, some bones. Key locations on the surface:

- **City Gates** — main entry, collapsed or guarded
- **Arch of Dregoth** — massive stone arch with draconic carvings, serves as a landmark
- **Temple** — ruined temple, contested by looters
- **Merchant District** — former commercial center
- **Grand Plaza** — site of the massacre, thousands died here, heaviest concentration of corpses
- **Arena** — gladiatorial pit, now home to monsters
- **Dregoth's Palace (Ruins)** — collapsed structure at city center, entrance to the cisterns below
- **Warrens of the Poor** — maze-like slums
- **Freeman District** — middle-class homes
- **Gardens of Gram Ash** — petrified garden
- **Public Cisterns** — access to tunnels below
- **Nobles' District** — wealthy homes, stripped by looters
- **Traders' District** — market square
- **Templar Buildings** — former administrative center
- **Slave Pens** — outer district

Encounters: silt horrors, gith raiders, elf scavengers, and the **Caller in Darkness** threat.

### Part Two: The Sunken City

A section of Giustenal that collapsed into the Silt Sea (when it was still water). Now partially submerged, flooded chambers with pockets of air. Features ancient architecture preserved underwater. Transition point to the Groaning City below.

### Part Three: The Groaning City

The cavern of hanged dwarves. The **Avenue of the Hanged** is the main passage — cursed dead dwarves swing from nooses, moaning. When someone approaches, they attack with constricting sinew attacks. The moans are so terrible they cause fear checks.

Key locations:
- **Ancient Temple** — pre-Giustenal structure, contains clues about Taraskir the Lion
- **Taraskir's Tomb** — final resting place of Taraskir, former king of Giustenal (before Dregoth). Contains the magical spear **Fang of the Lion** (key weapon against certain threats). The tomb is guarded by traps and undead.
- **The Citadel** — demihuman refuge fortification
- **The Tunnels Beneath Giustenal** — network of passages leading deeper to Kragmorta

The **Spirit of Kragmorta** — a deceptive entity posing as a helpful guide. It is actually an extension of Dregoth's will, tricking first-gen dray into walking into traps where they are killed and raised as undead for Dregoth's army. Absalom knows this secret.

### Part Four: Kragmorta

The first-gen dray settlement in a volcanic cavern. **Passk** can be found in his shack — a sage who seeks the Fang of the Lion to fight Dregoth. NPCs: Mosak (Clan Father), dray guards, hunters.

Encounters: dark naga (from another plane, has book of vile darkness), fire drake, wall-walker hunters.

### Part Five: The Journey to New Giustenal

A long tunnel network connecting Kragmorta to New Giustenal. Features:
- **Ghoda's Gap** and **Ghoda's Tar Mine** — mining outpost
- **Dregoth's Arch** — massive gateway marking the boundary of New Giustenal's territory
- Natural caverns with hazards

### Part Six: New Giustenal Infiltration (Overview)

PCs enter the dray city. Options:
- Blend in (dray PCs only, requires disguise)
- Sneak through sewer/water tunnels
- Fight through the gates (suicidal)
- Absalom (as Akrag) may help

Key locations explored in adventure: Central Gates, Temple of the Dragon, Blackjaw River, and the path to the Dread Palace.

### Part Seven A: The Dread Palace — Lower Levels

Floor-by-floor infiltration. Guards: templars, bone golems, kalin riders. Rooms include:
1. Palace Guard Room
2. Audience Chamber
3. Templar Barracks
4. Armory (kalin hide armor, bone weapons, some metal +1 weapons)
5. Templar Quarters
6. Hall of the Dragon King — Dregoth's private shrine, contains the dragon skull holy relic (obsidian eyes, crimson sun/dragon claw symbol). Destroying the relic makes Dregoth *think* his godhood quest is delayed (it doesn't actually matter — godhood is impossible on Athas).
7. Dregoth's Library — magical texts from across the planes, 1d4 random magic items
8. **Transformation Room** — equipment for turning humans into dray. Subjects must save vs spells or transform (10% first-gen dray, 25% half-elves). System shock check or die. Irreversible.
9. **Chamber of the Gate** — the planar gate itself

### Part Seven B: The Planar Gate

The gate is a large ornate mirror showing the icy realm of **Stygia** (Baator). Dregoth has aligned it with Baator — an army of **baatezu** (devils) waits on the other side for Dregoth to open the Athasian side so they can invade.

Also in the room: 7 small obsidian spheres that can hold captured psyches. Dregoth has been using them to retrieve souls from the **Caller in Darkness** (the spirit storm in the Ethereal Plane). One sphere already contains **Gartinon**, Captain of the Palace Guard. PCs can use the spheres to rescue up to 6 victims from the Caller (including Slinnasia, which could heal Jessareen's madness and reconcile the Sky Singers).

### Part Seven C: The Ethereal Plane

If the gate is realigned to the Ethereal Plane, the image shows a great storm — the true form of the Caller in Darkness. Characters must make death saves or take damage from buffeting spirits. To find a specific soul, PCs need psionic powers (spirit sense, send thoughts) or magic. Once retrieved in an orb, souls can be reunited with bodies via resurrection/wish/raise dead.

### Part Seven D: Conclusion

When PCs mess with the gate, Dregoth senses them and arrives in his true undead dragon king form. **Passk sacrifices himself** to buy time. **Absalom may intervene** briefly. PCs must finish their task (destroy gate or realign it) while Dregoth attacks with everything.

If the gate is destroyed: Dregoth is distracted trying to repair it (1d4 hours). PCs can flee to the surface. Dregoth will remember them and seek revenge later — he becomes a major player in the Tyr region.

If souls are rescued: Slinnasia can be returned, healing the Sky Singers' schism.

## Part C: Monstrous Compendium Appendix

### Absalom (Unique Morg)
High Priest of Dregoth, 17 HD undead. Killed and resurrected as a morg to stabilize his dray transformation. Wears ornate robes, looks like a mummified dray. Can only be hit by magic weapons. Uses claws (3d6) or long sword +3. Casts templar spells up to 7th level. Secretly sympathetic to first-gen dray, opposes Dregoth's plan to convert them into undead. Uses metamorphosis to pose as **Akrag** (bathhouse owner) to spy on public sentiment.

### Caller in Darkness
Unique psionic entity — the merged consciousness of thousands of Giustenal's dead, trapped in an ethereal spirit storm. 18th-level psionicist with 750 PSPs, all sciences and devotions. Cannot physically interact with Athas — communicates via psionics. Searches 5-mile radius for psionic minds. Contacted victims see visions (first pleasant to lure them, then terrifying). Victims who die inside Giustenal are absorbed into the storm. Wisdom drain every 30 minutes; at 0 Wisdom the victim commits suicide.

### Dragon Beetle
1 HD, tiny crimson beetles with 3 horns. Venom only harms dray/dragons/drakes (2d10 damage). Live in nests of 2-16. Nest leaders have 3 HD, AC 5. Dray use their chitin for armor.

### Dray (Both Generations)
Humanoids created by Dregoth. First gen: mutated, savage, +1 attack/damage with natural weapons. Second gen: perfect, refined, +1 with dray-crafted weapons. Both have immunity to fire, double cold damage. Natural AC 7. Claws 1d6/1d6, bite 1d4. Speak ancient Giustenal.

### Dregoth (Kaisharga, 29 HD)
Full stats in campaign book (repeated here for reference). AC -8, MR 40%, 173 hp, THAC0 -1. Breath weapon 20d12. Full wizard spells (1st-10th), full psionics (330 PSPs, 6 disciplines, 28 sciences, 60 devotions). Aura of fear (60 ft). Requires +2 weapons to hit. Immune to charm, sleep, cold, electricity, insanity, death. Cannot be turned.

### Dwarf, Cursed Dead (8 HD)
Undead dwarves of the Groaning City. Hang from nooses, heads on broken necks. Attack with pummeling (1d8/1d8) and explosive sinew attack (entangles, pulls victim in). Moans cause fear. +1 weapons to hit. 15% MR.

### Kalin (7 HD)
Insectoid mount — mix of spider and ant, 12 ft long. AC 5, MV 18/Cl 9. Claws 1d10/1d10, mandible bite 2d6 plus grapple (auto-hit next round). Use sticky strands to descend. Rivalry with wall-walkers. Dray use chitin for armor/weapons after death.

### Kalin Rider (5 HD templar)
Rides kalin, both attack same target. AC 3, metal long sword +1. Cleric spells (3/3/1). Each squadron has a 7th-10th level defiler with 5 magic items.

### Krag (11 HD)
Undead elemental cleric — created when a cleric dies in the element opposed to their patron. Types: magma, sun, silt, rain, earth, air, water. AC 4, 20% MR. Elemental transfusion bite (changes victim's blood into the element). Control over their element 2x/day. Can create kraglings from victims.

### Kragling (3 HD / 5 HD)
Lesser or greater — creatures killed by krag's transfusion. Lesser: AC 6, 3 HD. Greater: AC 8, 5 HD. Claw/claw/bite. Lesser elemental transfusion. Reform in 1d12 days if not turned. Loyal to krag master.

### Pit Snatcher (5 HD)
Tar pit elemental — made of smoking tar, dwells in the tar pits of Giustenal. AC 4, only harmed by magic/crushing weapons. Grapples and drags victims into tar (3d8 initial, 1d8/round). Permanent black scars on survivors.

### Sharg (11 HD)
Cross between giant shark and squid. 40 ft long, AC 4, 64 hp. 4 tentacles (1d8 each, constriction 2d8/round) + bite 5d4. Pulls itself onto land for 1d4 rounds. Brought from another plane by Dregoth. Lairs under Sharg Island in the Blackjaw River. Scared by light spells (75% flee, 25% rage).

### Silt Serpent (1 HD)
Small (2-4 ft) poisonous snake adapted to silt. AC 1 in silt, AC 5 out. Bite 1-2 + Type E poison (save or die, successful save still 20 damage). Master of ambush — senses vibration through silt. Giant version: 5 HD, AC 4 (0 in silt), bite 1d8 + poison.

### Silt Spawn (2 HD)
Young silt horrors, 4 ft long, AC 8. Bite 1d8. Work in clutches of 2-12, drag victims deeper into silt through coordinated attacks. Grow into adult silt horrors. Skin waterproof — used for sacks.

### Venger (5 HD)
Undead animated by a great wrong suffered at death. Single-minded pursuit of the one who wronged them. AC 7, +2 weapons to hit. Fists 1d10/1d10. Vengeful grasp (instant death or 1d12/round). Indestructible until the wrong is righted or the target dies. Explodes on destruction by target (5d6 blast).

### Wall-Walker (6 HD)
Insectoid subterranean hunter, 5 ft long, AC 3. 8 clawed legs, stinger tail. Claws 2d12/2d12, bite 2d8. Stinger: paralyzes for 1d6 rounds. Psionic: shadow-form (invisibility), chameleon power (blend into rock). Immune to fire. Hive dwellers, rival kalin. Scales used for armor.

## Part D: Reference Cards

### Card #1: The Planar Gate
Green Age artifact — giant mirror in mahogany frame with gold imagery of extinct races. Sentient (Int 15, communicates via psionics). Operated by psionic powers: clairvoyance to scan planes, probability travel to step through. Cost: 20 PSPs to open + 8 per additional traveler. Can be destroyed (AC 2, 100 hp, +3 saves). If destroyed, irreparable.

### Card #2A/2B: Dray Player Characters
Full racial rules for both generations:
- Ability modifiers, level limits, thieving skill adjustments
- First gen: +2 Str, +2 Con, -2 Int, -1 Wis, -3 Cha (other races)
- Second gen: +1 Str, +1 Con, +1 Int, -1 Wis, -3 Cha (other races)
- First gen mutations table (1d20 — AC 10, stat changes, no infravision, etc.)
- Age, height/weight tables
- All dray: half fire damage, double cold, infravision, less water needed

### Card #3: Dregoth's Templars
Rank structure (Scale 1-18). Patrol composition. Full stat blocks for Third, Fourth, Fifth, Eighth Scale templars and kalin riders. Defiler stat block.

### Card #4: Mon Adderath
Dregoth's High Templar. 18th-level human templar, 101 hp. Childhood friend of Dregoth — the only human in New Giustenal. Immortal (from earlier enchantment) — the dray transformation failed on him. Wields metal two-handed sword +3, rod of beguiling, ring of protection +2, dust of disappearance. Secret: he loathes first-gen dray but Dregoth ordered them kept alive. Quiet, reserved, absolutely loyal.

**Two amulet types:** old version (dragon head in crimson circle — Giustenal era) and new version (crimson sun in dragon claw — current). Mon Adderath's amulet grants dray-like immunities.

### Card #5: Giustenal Exploration
Rules for crossing tar pits (Intelligence/survival checks). Choking dust penalties (-2 attack/damage, can be reduced to -1 with moist cloth, costs ½ gallon water/day). Disaster table for tar pit travel.

## Part E: Poster Map

Double-sided, full color.

**Side 1: Ruins of Giustenal (overhead)** — 15 numbered locations, tar pits, Sea of Silt border, inset of New Giustenal location, cross-section showing surface/sunken city/underground.

**Side 2: Underground tactical maps** — The Sunken City (1 sq = 10 ft), The Groaning City (1 sq = 20 ft), Kragmorta (1 sq = 20 ft), New Giustenal (1 sq = 30 ft). Full-color watercolor style with lava, caves, buildings, river.
