# Session Prep — 25 Ιουλίου 2026 (ad4)

*Day 6, Macro 21, 24th year of 190th King's Age, 6th hour. Continuing from ad3: captive Gith held for interrogation, party still needs to recover the missing Syggra herd and the stolen Food Producing Kank.*

## Goals
- Interrogate the captive Gith taken in ad3
- Go recover the remaining Syggra (goats) and a Kank for the village

## Related Quests
- [[Act 1 - Eyes Open]]
- [[Act 2 - Clear the Mountain]]

## Done
- Zephyr, a new priest of air sent by Kalia, joined the party at the start of the session as an NPC ally
- Interrogated the captive Gith (Relo used a psionic spell to turn stone into a needle for torture; Shaka played up a "Cannibal King" reputation to intimidate him)
- Learned there are more tunnels below the mines, leading through a mushroom cavern to another exit
- Learned that the mind-entity in the tunnels blessed the Gith himself and called itself "the moon" ("φεγγάρι") when it spoke to him — no second keyword came up this session
- Gith refused to name his leader, citing an oath of silence; warned that "what's yours can become ours" and that "the times are turning" (implicit threat of Gith reprisal)
- Released the Gith after cutting off one ear (Shaka bit off and spat back a piece of it first), sent back with a warning message for his kin
- Asked Kalia for rope; she refused — she'd already given them Zephyr as help and had nothing else to offer
- Went to the mine instead and took only the spare rope from the broken elevator platform, leaving the rest intact
- Spotted several Syggra near a cave entrance that bolted deeper in, startled by the party's noise; followed them inside
- Found and recovered 3 of the 5 missing Syggra, wandered into wild kank territory and lucky to still be alive — several intoxicated on fermented Feed Globules
- Fought and killed 4 large wild kank in the first chamber, near the Syggra
- Explored deeper — Gur-da tore open a passage partly blocked by rocks, with Rhugor helping — and found the insects' nest chamber
- Survived a bat swarm ambush at the cave mouth (11+ giant bats) — Shaka killed three bats charging the goats with a single strike; Gur-da dropped to 0 HP twice, stabilized both times by Zephyr
- Escaped a second, much larger insect swarm (12+) that surged out of the nest to reclaim corpses — Parias, transformed into a ghost form ("Erebos") via a shadow spell, possessed one of the nearest creatures and made it carry the unconscious Gur-da out, saving him
- Recovered 3 Feed Globules (valuable alchemical material, ferments into "Brewing Nectar") as consolation loot

## Not done / left unfinished
- The Kank was not recovered — the original quest asked for Syggra *and* a Kank; only the Globules came back as a substitute
- 2 of the 5 missing Syggra died during the session (killed by the bats, or possibly by the party in the chaos of the fight) — not recoverable
- The entity ("the moon") only spoke to the Gith himself so far — no keyword or message was directed at the party yet
- The Gith leader's name/title remains unknown

- Party ad4 roster: Parias (Kostasgk), Gur-da (Necrobutch), Rhugor (Stam), Relo (Mpelos), Shaka (Proskopos) — Cypoul (Baboulas) and Vareth (GiaMiCool) absent this session

- Episode ends on a cliffhanger as the party reaches daylight outside the cave, exhausted but alive, with 3/5 Syggra and some Globules recovered
