# Session Prep — 31 Ιουλίου 2026 (ad6)

*Day starts at 115th day, year 24 of 190th King's Age (Merchant: K.A. 190.24 – Friend's Vengeance – Macro 25th; Draxian: al-Qor-Keth, eighth Or'Dom, Nar'Ai, Year of Friend's Vengeance, 190th Or'Zar). Days 113 and 114 were downtime (2 days between ad5 on day 112 and ad6); ad6 starts on day 115 and runs into day 116 — the heat kept the party at Zharvek all through the 116th, so they only set out in the evening and marched back overnight. Continuing from ad5: 2 kank tamed and brought back alive, Gur-da healed, only 3 pieces of Obsidian mined (not enough for serious trade), Crystal Dust pond not harvested, Gith ultimatum sent unanswered, a wounded Land Shark (Bulette) still loose underground, and two new rumor leads — old-god believers 6-12 miles southeast, and Rensort survivors northeast, at Quel'Nash.*

## Done
- Party this session: Rello (Mpelos), Shaka (Proskopos), Gur-da (Necrobutch), Cypoul (Baboulas), plus Vareth (GiaMiCool) and Rhazek (slos/Giannis) newly present — Rhugor (Stam) and Ranni (Fantasias Giorgos) absent
- Extreme heat forecast for the day; party chose to explore south toward the White/Salt Desert instead of the mountain-sanctuary or trade-post leads
- Found an oasis before the Salt Sea; spirits there demanded Rhazek (a fire-server) be surrendered as atonement for past suffering at the hands of fire priests. They offered terms — the rest of the party could rest and drink if Rhazek stayed outside all day, unshaded and without water — but the offer was really a way to get the fire priest alone and close to them, and **the party refused it. No deal was struck**; they left the oasis and moved on
- Reached the Salt Sea and discussed gathering salt to preserve their meat, but never actually harvested any (see [[Salt Gathering]]); spotted and investigated a ruined settlement to the north: Zharvek — abandoned, broken doors, no sign of life
- Rello sensed a vague presence in the ruins; party moved in quietly, searched a few houses for salvage (found little)
- Fought and killed a giant legless snake creature bigger than Gur-da — Cypoul blinded it, Rhazek called flames, Gur-da clubbed it, and Shaka landed a killing sneak-attack arrow through its mouth; butchered it for meat and hide
- Found 7 exhausted human survivors sheltering in a plaza around a dry well; convinced them (posing as emissaries of the Cannibal King) to return to Kharanok in exchange for food, water, and protection — among them Tzala, a self-taught alchemist, plus 3 recruits wanting to learn to fight and 3 laborers
- Learned from the survivors that people of **House Markon** had passed through some time earlier, and that trouble with the Gith got worse after they left — no causal link stated, just the sequence. A raid followed, killing or enslaving most of Zharvek's people
- Spent the night at Zharvek; heat then pinned the party there through all of day 116, so they searched the ruins while waiting it out and only set out in the evening, marching back across the desert overnight
- On that night march, a corrupted earth spirit erupted from the sand and nearly drowned/buried Shaka — the party freed him (Rello's telekinetic grasp, Cypoul's Cure Light Wounds, Vareth's Magic Missiles, Gur-da's and Rhazek's melee/flame) and drove the wounded spirit off
- The same spirit resurfaced moments later among the trailing survivors and dragged one of the new laborers to his death before anyone could react
- Reached Kharanok at dawn of day 117 with Tzala and the remaining 6 Zharvek survivors
