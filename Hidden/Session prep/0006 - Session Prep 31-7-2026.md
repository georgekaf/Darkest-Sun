## Done
- Party this session: Rello (Mpelos), Shaka (Proskopos), Gur-da (Necrobutch), Cypoul (Baboulas), plus Vareth (GiaMiCool) and Rhazek (slos/Giannis) newly present — Rhugor (Stam) and Ranni (Fantasias Giorgos) absent
- Extreme heat forecast for the day; party chose to explore south toward the White/Salt Desert instead of the mountain-sanctuary or trade-post leads
- Found an oasis before the Salt Sea; spirits there demanded Rhazek (a fire-server) be surrendered as atonement for past suffering at the hands of fire priests — compromise reached: the rest of the party could rest and drink, but Rhazek had to stay outside all day, unshaded and without water
- Reached the Salt Sea, gathered salt to preserve meat; spotted and investigated a ruined settlement to the north: Zharvek — abandoned, broken doors, no sign of life
- Rello sensed a vague presence in the ruins; party moved in quietly, searched a few houses for salvage (found little)
- Fought and killed a giant legless snake creature bigger than Gur-da — Cypoul blinded it, Rhazek called flames, Gur-da clubbed it, and Shaka landed a killing sneak-attack arrow through its mouth; butchered it for meat and hide
- Found 7 exhausted human survivors sheltering in a plaza around a dry well; convinced them (posing as emissaries of the Cannibal King) to return to Kharanok in exchange for food, water, and protection — among them Tzala, a self-taught alchemist, plus 3 recruits wanting to learn to fight and 3 laborers
- Learned from the survivors that a House Marku caravan passed through two weeks earlier, drew Gith attention, and triggered a raid that killed or enslaved most of Zharvek's people
- On the return march that night, a corrupted earth spirit erupted from the sand and nearly drowned/buried Shaka — the party freed him (Rello's telekinetic grasp, Cypoul's Cure Light Wounds, Vareth's Magic Missiles, Gur-da's and Rhazek's melee/flame) and drove the wounded spirit off
- The same spirit resurfaced moments later among the trailing survivors and dragged one of the new laborers to his death before anyone could react
- Reached Kharanok at dawn with Tzala and the remaining 6 Zharvek survivors
