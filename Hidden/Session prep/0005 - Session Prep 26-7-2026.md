## Done
- Rhugor (Stam) absent this session; Cypoul (Baboulas) present
- New player, Fantasias Giorgos, joined as Ranni, an Elf Priest — water-affiliated healer, vegetarian, agreed to help the party after healing Gur-da
- Gur-da stabilized and healed via long rest + spells
- An extreme heat wave and incoming sandstorm made outdoor travel lethal, so the party postponed the kank hunt and went mining Obsidian with Dorak instead
- Caught 2 Gith scouts while mining — the party gave the Gith fake, intimidating identities (Parias as "Lord Parias," Shaka as "Cannibal King") while Relo withheld his own name and told the survivor to call him "the nobody" instead; Shaka then killed one and ate its heart in front of the survivor, and the party got a rough explanation of why Gith keep coming for Crystal Dust (they need it for healing/magic)
- Released the surviving Gith naked with an ultimatum for his chief: trade for the Crystal Dust peacefully, or lose everything
- Mined 3 pieces of Obsidian — enough for minor trade, not serious commerce
- Checked the Crystal Dust evaporation pond but ran out of time to collect a meaningful amount
- Fought off a Land Shark (Bulette) (ambushed from underground) on the way out; it fled underground wounded and the party let it go
- Sheltered from the sandstorm in Kharanok's rock shelters; interviewed 4 refugees for rumors, including Aiwin (a lone Elf traveler heading to Cromlin despite pirate risk, who once had ties to a group of old-god believers living 6-12 miles southeast in the mountains, contact now lost) and Kia, sole survivor of a raided village ("Rensort," also known as Quel Nas) near the Desert/Salt Plains border — hit by Gith raiders from the mountains, survivors scattered south
- In the afternoon, once the storm passed, tracked 3 separate wild kank trails and picked one to ambush
- Relo made the ground hollow to trip the kank; Ranni blinded the largest one, letting Shaka finish it with advantage
- Instead of fighting the last 2 kank, Relo remembered a soothing sound Sira, the village's animal-herder, had taught the party and used it to calm them (DC 18 CHA), leading them back to Kharanok peacefully — wanted 1 Kank, got 2 (each carrying 4 globules), plus weeks' worth of nectar
- Relo voted session MVP
