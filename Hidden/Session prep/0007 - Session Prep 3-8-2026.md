# Session Prep — 3 Αυγούστου 2026 (ad7)

*Day starts at 117th day, year 24 of 190th King's Age (Merchant: K.A. 190.24 – Friend's Vengeance – Macro 27th; Draxian: al-Dun-Du, eighth Or'Dom, Nar'Ai, Year of Friend's Vengeance, 190th Or'Zar). Continuing from ad6: the party marched home overnight and reached Kharanok at dawn today, bringing Tzala the alchemist and 6 of the 7 Zharvek survivors — the seventh was taken by a corrupted earth spirit on the road, which is still out there, wounded and willing to pick off stragglers. Zharvek itself is confirmed sacked and abandoned. Still carried over and untouched from ad5: only 3 pieces of Obsidian mined with no buyer, the Crystal Dust pond unharvested, the Gith ultimatum unanswered, and a wounded Land Shark (Bulette) loose underground. Both ad5 rumor leads remain open — old-god believers 6-12 miles southeast, and Rensort survivors northeast at Quel'Nash.*

*Actually played on Friday, 7 August 2026.*

- Find a viable trade destination for the mined Obsidian — untouched for two sessions running

- Settle the 7 new arrivals into Kharanok: Tzala as a crafter, 3 would-be fighters, 2 laborers
- Follow up on one of the standing rumor leads (old-god believers southeast, or Rensort survivors northeast at Quel'Nash)

## Done
- Party this session: Relo (Mpelos), Shaka (Proskopos), Cypoul (Baboulas), Ranni (Fantasias Giorgos), plus **Sylvar (DarkRhapsode)**, a new character — an elderly traveller out of Nibenay with dry branches wrapped around him, speaks with nature and speaks halfling, so he is now Shaka's interpreter
- Arrivals settled: Tzala put to tending the sick and working as a crafter, the 2 workers assigned to Dorak in the mine, the 3 recruits handed to Breck for watch duty (the village had only two guards, one at the tower and one at the gate)
- Kalia sent the party to find Dorak, missing in the mines since morning; Relo tracked him with The Way (direction only)
- Behind recently moved crates they opened a half-hidden passage into chambers of carved bunk-recesses (4–6 per room, large enough for a large creature) and found Dorak muttering over **two hexagonal stone plates**, hiding a rolled leather
- The interrogation turned violent and was broken up by **Breck**, who revealed himself as an old **Veiled Alliance** member carrying a message from **Sadira: House Markon needs their support**. He handed the party the Gith leather diagram and asked that the room stay secret even from Kalia
- Found a hollow slab in the floor hiding **30–40 rolled leather scrolls** — a library. Relo fetched **Zephyr** (reads Draxian and Primordial), who read them: the plates are **gates**
- **Gate mechanics established:** 10 handfuls of Crystal Dust to activate (lights both ends), 5 per trip to keep it open; without the 5 it transports once and dies. The broken left-hand plate led deeper into the mountain and was sabotaged deliberately so it works *badly*, not so it fails
- Dorak activated the intact plate with 10 handfuls. **Destination: Giustenal** — ruined city half-sunk in mud, dragon statues, boiling tar lakes
- **Ranni stepped onto the plate out of curiosity and was transported.** She hid and waited, heard the voice of a dead childhood friend warning her not to meddle with what she doesn't know, and as the party re-lit the gate (15 more handfuls) she returned — hearing someone shout **"JESSIX, SOMEONE IS HERE!"** behind her
- Breck's briefing: raids by pirates and bandits around Nibenay are increasing, something is brewing; taking on a Sorcerer King as they are is suicide, they need an army or another Sorcerer King as ally. Legend says the Sorcerer Kings once conspired to kill one of their own, and beyond Borys who guards Rajaat others may be willing to oppose them
- **Second contact with the cave creature** (the presence from the ad2 dream), reaching Relo through The Way while he actively scanned for it. It rebuilt Kharanok from his memories — still wrong, a staircase missing — and wore **Kalia's form**. It rejected Relo's old proposal, said the one holding its key was in the mine today, and warned that what they opened let an energy through the gate: something that constantly hunts people to replace its dead and sounds like the screaming of thousands. It claims it was brought to this world to help after the Sorcerer Kings and Queens split the earth and made black liquid gush out. It pinned Relo to the wall when he tried to close his mind. Its offer: it points the road, in exchange for whatever holds it captive
- Tzala gave up the **healing potion recipe** under pressure — one sandbloom flower, water, one handful of Crystal Dust — insisting Crystal Dust is forbidden even for potions and that they never heard it from her, then mapped where sandblooms grow
- Night trip to the **scrublands between Quel Nash and the Lion Temple**: 20 sandblooms gathered, two giant scorpions killed (scraps and raw meat harvested); two bipedal watchers fled when the scorpions arrived
- Production: **32 pieces of Crystal Dust** by nightfall from Dorak and the two workers

## Not done / left unfinished
- **Obsidian:** the 3 shards are still unsold — a third session with no buyer. Dorak had the workers dig Crystal Dust exclusively, not obsidian
- **Crystal Dust pond:** never properly harvested; the quantity came from fresh digging deeper in the cave instead
- **Rumor leads:** neither was followed. The party only passed near Quel Nash for the flowers
- No potions brewed — raw materials only
- Giustenal was neither mapped nor revisited; nobody went back to see who shouted, or who witnessed Ranni vanish
- Breck was not questioned about the key, though they suspect him

## Summary
- Kalia, more agitated than usual, sends the party after Dorak; the newcomer Sylvar attaches himself to them on the way out
- The hunt through the mines uncovers the hidden gate room, a violent argument with Dorak, and Breck's true allegiance
- Dorak — bitter, drunk, and admitting he threw half the corpses in the canyon himself — can't read runes but knows the crystal, and lights the gate
- Ranni's impulsive step onto the plate turns a theory into a confirmed route to Giustenal, and brings back the first evidence that someone lives there
- The cave creature raises the stakes: something came *through* the gate, and it wants its freedom bought with whatever binds it
- The day ends with a night harvest in the scrublands, a scorpion fight, and full storage pits
