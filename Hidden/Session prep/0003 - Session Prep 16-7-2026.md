- Dorak led them into the mines, explained how Crystal Dust forms: water drags shards deeper into the cave and deposits them in a lake, recoverable when the water dries out every noon

- 2 Bulette ambushed — killed after some coordination trouble
- Found Dorak's former Gith captors (8), fought them, they tried to ambush Kharanok again though the subterannean caves — 7 killed, 1 captured; revealed Dorak was their escaped slave, called "traitor"
- Dorak led them to a broken elevator shaft; the party did not explore any sealed doors or deeper tunnel rooms
- Beheaded the dead Gith as a warning and put their heads on spikes to deter future attacks, took Dorak's hidden tools, harvested Bulette meat/scraps, recovered 10 Crystal Dust
- Delivered scraps to Roy for the gate's basic repair; Breck ordered to start work
- Roy mentioned the tar source: two brothers (one named Durex) trading Cromlin↔Nibenay near ruined Giustenal; he also gave additional information about the caves — there are other entrances deeper in the tunnel network and mushroom fields further in the caves
- Episode ends with the party resting in Kharanok, captive Gith held for interrogation next session
