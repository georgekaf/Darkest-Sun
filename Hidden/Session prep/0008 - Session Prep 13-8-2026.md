# Session Prep — 13 Αυγούστου 2026 (ad8)

*Day starts at 118th day, year 24 of 190th King's Age (Merchant: K.A. 190.24 – Friend's Vengeance – Macro 28th; Draxian: al-Dun-Traa, eighth Or'Dom, Nar'Ai, Year of Friend's Vengeance, 190th Or'Zar). Weather rolled hot, but a fresh wind drops the heat one step — only the afternoon hours need cover. Continuing from ad7: the intact plate in the hidden room under the mines is a working gate to Giustenal, its mechanics understood (10 handfuls of Crystal Dust to light it, 5 per trip to hold it open), Breck has revealed himself as Veiled Alliance and carries Sadira's request for help, and Ranni has already been to the far side once and come back. Stock on hand: 32 pieces of Crystal Dust from yesterday's production, 20 sandblooms and the potion recipe, 3 unsold Obsidian shards. Still untouched from ad5–ad7: the Crystal Dust pond, the Gith ultimatum, the wounded Bulette underground, and both rumor leads (old-god believers southeast, Rensort survivors northeast at Quel'Nash).*

## Done
- Party this session: Relo (Mpelos), Parias (KostasGK), Shaka (Proskopos), Cypoul (Baboulas), Ranni (Fantasias Giorgos), plus **Zilvar (DarkRhapsode)**, a new character — a sun-burnt warrior with spear and shield, strong enough to carry the party's load. **Sylvar was not played this session.** Zilvar was kitted with a bone shield and bone plate and given two spears
- Relo recruited **Breck** to come down and guard the gate while they were away — his argument being that nobody else in Kharanok can be trusted with it, and that Breck is the one who dragged them into a fight bigger than themselves. Breck agreed but asked twice whether they really meant to cross
- **Breck's lore on Giustenal:** every sorcerer-king has his own great city, like Tyr and Nibenay; stories say Giustenal was one such city with a very powerful king, and that both king and city were lost in a great war. When exactly is not clear. He deflected the question about the voices — old places hide such things, and experience says not to welcome them
- Load-out: 15 handfuls of Crystal Dust carried by Zilvar for the crossing, 9 more on Ranni for the return, **12 left behind for Tzala's potions**. Four Kank globules taken as rations, three torches crafted from oil, threads and scraps
- **Giant spider** in one of the five mine caves on the way down: Relo broke its morale with an image of itself upside-down under Zilvar's spear, Cypoul blinded it, Shaka's arrow took its head off. Webs and egg clutches burned, **8 scraps** harvested
- Gate lit with 15 handfuls; **Breck left holding 15 more** with orders to re-open it if it dies before they return. The whole party crossed together
- **Arrival at Giustenal.** Confirmed on the ground: the grey expanse is the **Sea of Silt** — anything that steps on it sinks; only ships and known paths cross it. The city lies 2–3 miles off, walls broken, a cracked pyramid on a hill. Between them and it, the ground is split and a black mass wells up through the openings, leaving thousands of dry paths in a labyrinth — some reach the walls, some end nowhere, and the party has no paper to map it from above
- **The ruins outside the walls took them one by one.** Ranni heard her dead childhood friend again, briefly. Parias felt something brush his mind and withdraw. Cypoul saw a shadow that turned out to be cloth. The other three did not resist:
  - **Shaka** — bastard son of a halfling king, set aside when the legitimate heir was born, exiled into the desert with his mother, who cut off her own hand to feed him and died days later. Her voice called him "my little prince" and her shape in a side room became a statue pointing at the city
  - **Zilvar** — as a youth in training for the arena he took a wooden pole to the mouth and put a spear through his best friend, the only man he has ever killed; his master sold him to the mines instead of the arena for it. He heard an arena crowd chanting his name from inside the city
  - **Relo** — a child again in a slaver's camp, bound, the whip on his back, and voices promising shelter behind the walls
- Parias broke the compulsion on **Shaka** alone, catching him mid-run with his prayer. **Relo and Zilvar separated and neither was caught** — Relo ran the wall line hunting a gap or a way up, Zilvar went straight for the Gate. Ranni blocked him and was shoved down, Shaka pulled his trousers down, Zilvar threw a spear past Ranni's face and opened the great gate between the two dragon statues himself. Ranni carried Shaka in over her shoulders; Cypoul followed
- **Inside:** a wide plaza of dragon statues and ancient ruins, a road climbing toward the hill
- **The tar answers.** Humanoid shapes rise out of the boiling tar, walk to its edge and claw at the sand without being able to leave it. The grey ground is **ash**, not sand: running kicks up a cloud that fills the lungs, takes their breath and brings hunger and thirst. It does **not** erode the mind — the voices do that, and they keep working on their own: Cypoul relived the deaths of his two brothers, Shaka heard his mother again, then cockroaches and rising blood
- **Fight at the gate:** Parias took his spectral form, **Erebus**, and found that only that reaches them — his hand passes through and drains life. Relo struck with his mind; Zilvar's spear did almost nothing. Two creatures melted, but the bubbles kept multiplying, so the party ran for the hill
- **Jessix.** Six cloaked figures came out of the buildings on the hilltop; one raised a hand in greeting and its palm was black. Relo answered mind-to-mind and got a reply in the elven tongue: **Jessix the Wanderer**, with the companions who stayed loyal to him. He had been watching and had written them off, because whoever the voices take is never seen again. He was once **Jessix the Twilight Catcher**, until he was blamed for a death and lost everything he had, his family included
- **Jessix's two offers:** if their talk of freedom and a world without slavery is deeds and not words, he will show them a road into the city — he wants his name cleared and his people vindicated. Otherwise there is **Tenpug**, a leader of former slaves further east; the land that way is broken by ancient lava and impassable on foot, so it needs **a ship across the grey sea**
- Ranni asked whether one of them is called "Jessix" — the name she heard through the portal last session. It was **his own name**
- **Shaka** offered Jessix water. In this world water is among the most valuable gifts there is
- The party returned through the gate. Breck was waiting on the other side

## Not done / left unfinished
- **Breck was never asked about the key.** Relo probed him about the voices instead, got nothing, and the Veiled Alliance's actual demands went unexamined for a second session running
- **No potions brewed.** The 12 handfuls of Crystal Dust and the 20 sandblooms were left with Tzala; the party crossed before anything was made — and then spent the session in the one place where a healing potion would have mattered
- **The broken plate** was not examined again
- **The city itself was not explored.** They reached the plaza inside the walls and were driven back out; the pyramid on the hill, the arena Zilvar heard, and everything past the gate remain unseen
- **The cave creature** was not consulted, and the standing deal — Relo promising it Breck — is still unresolved and still hidden from Breck himself
- Obsidian still unsold, Crystal Dust pond still unharvested, both rumor leads still untouched, Gith ultimatum still unanswered
- Daily Crystal Dust production for day 118 was never rolled — the GM noted it as an item for the start of next session

## Summary
- The day's plan was simple — post a guard, cross the gate, find a weapon against a Sorcerer King — and Kharanok gave them a new pair of hands in Zilvar before they went down
- A spider in the tunnels cost them nothing; the crossing cost them 15 handfuls and put them on the shore of the Sea of Silt looking at a dead city behind a labyrinth of tar
- Giustenal does not fight with monsters first. It uses the dead: every one of them was called by a voice that knew exactly what they had lost, and three of them walked
- Parias's prayers were the only thing that brought two of them back; Zilvar walked in through the front gate and the rest followed him rather than lose him
- The tar and the ash drove them out again, and the hill gave them the first living people on this side: Jessix and the Loyal, who assumed they were already gone
- They leave with two roads instead of a foothold — Jessix's price, or Tenpug across the silt — and with the knowledge that the voice Ranni heard last session was a name, not a warning

## Left to do
- **Jessix's price:** clear his name, vindicate his people. Nothing was agreed — Relo promised only that they would return. What exactly he is accused of, and who accuses him, has not been asked
- **Tenpug:** east, past ancient lava fields, reachable only by ship across the Sea of Silt. The party has no ship and no route to one; Cromlin was floated at the table as the nearer trade contact
- **The voices are the real obstacle, not the tar.** The party's own conclusion at the end: they need something that stops them hearing it — Cypoul's blinding is not enough, and potions or protections should be sought from Tzala before the next crossing. Under the GM's ruling, three mind erosions while already at zero health kills outright
- **Brew the potions** — materials and recipe are in Kharanok, the crossing is not survivable at current strength without them
- **Breck** — the key, the Veiled Alliance's demands, and the fact that Relo has already promised him to the cave creature. Three separate conversations, none of them had
- **The gate's far end is unguarded.** Nothing was left on the Giustenal side, and the Loyal now know exactly where and how the party appears
- **GM truth carried from 0007, now confirmed in play:** the thing that speaks with the voices of the dead is [[Caller in Darkness|the Caller in Darkness]], the presence the cave creature described as hunting people to replace its own dead and sounding like thousands screaming. What happened to Shaka, Zilvar and Relo this session is the same phenomenon that reached Ranni last session, applied to a full party. It knows each of their losses by name
- **GM truth, now spent:** 0007's prediction held — the Loyal were watching the spot where Ranni vanished, and the shout of "JESSIX, SOMEONE IS HERE!" is why Jessix was on that hill and expecting them. He has decided they are travelers worth protecting rather than the thing that opened a hole in his ruins, but that judgement is provisional and rests on whether they come back and do what they said
- **GM open:** what is inside the pyramid and the arena, and whether the black palms of the Loyal are the same burn Jessix carries or something the city does to everyone who stays
- Older open threads unchanged: the Gith ultimatum, the wounded Bulette below, two unguarded ways into the village from underground, Dorak's reward and his sabotaged teaching, the Obsidian with no buyer
