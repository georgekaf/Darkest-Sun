# Historical Record: Zharvek — Cross-Campaign Continuity Conflict

## Subject and Period

- Subject: Zharvek — a settlement described by two campaigns in ways that cannot both be literally true
- Exact day or approximate period: the disputed window runs from ~day 101 (ad6's sack date) to day 113 (Zharvek still standing in s3 ep.19); House Markon's own visits fall on days 109–110
- Historical status: disputed

*Raised 2026-08-03 while writing the ad6 Session Prep. Cross-flagged in the campaign locations reference, the ad6 session prep notes, and the pending worldbuilding issues list.*

## Account

Two sessions in two different campaigns, sharing one world and one timeline, describe a place called Zharvek in incompatible terms.

| | **House Markon** (s3 ep.11, 13, 19) | **Altar of Dust** (ad6) |
|---|---|---|
| State | Fortified inn-village, thriving, has a livestock market (party bought five Kang there) | Ruin — broken doors, no sign of life, a handful of starving survivors |
| Direction from Kharanok | Northeast, on the road toward Nibenay, ~9 hours' march | South, past the Salt Sea (the ruin sits north of the sea itself) |
| Ruler | Damak, a dwarf, ex-slaver exiled from Nibenay | None — whoever led it is dead or enslaved |
| Destroyed | Not destroyed; still standing as of ep.19 (day 113) | Sacked ~day 101, about two weeks before ad6 (day 115) |

The timeline is the harder half: ep.11/13 show Zharvek intact and busy around **day 109–110**, which is *eight or nine days after* ad6 says it was already a ruin.

## Affected Elements

- Location: `location-zharvek`; `location-kharanok` as the reference point both directions are measured from
- Actors: Damak (Zharvek's ruler in the House Markon account); Talek Vos, riding for Zharvek in live plot as of ep.19
- Factions: the Gith — the one element both accounts share
- Timelines: House Markon (days 109–113) and Altar of Dust (days 101–115) both touch the disputed window
- Records already carrying the flag: the campaign locations reference, the ad6 session prep notes, the pending worldbuilding issues list

## Consequences Still Present

The ad6 survivors say **people of House Markon passed through Zharvek some time earlier, and that trouble with the Gith got worse after they left.** They draw no causal link — they report the sequence.

That sequence is what House Markon's own sessions show. In ep.11/13 the party visits Zharvek and finds it uneasily hosting a Gith band waiting for "Eskar's convoy"; exposing them throws the village into uproar. In ep.19 Talek Vos rides for Zharvek specifically to investigate Gith unrest near the Black Spine Mountains that nobody can account for — unrest still unresolved from the Markon side.

So both campaigns independently describe: House Markon visits Zharvek → Gith trouble escalates after they leave. One shows the escalation as an open mystery; the other shows its endpoint, a sacked village. **The accounts fit together causally even while contradicting each other on geography and date** — which is the strongest argument that they concern one place, and the reason the coincidence reading is weak.

## Conflicting Accounts

- **House Markon (s3 ep.11, 13, 19).** Zharvek northeast of Kharanok, intact, ruled by Damak, still standing on day 113, currently a live destination for Talek Vos.
- **Altar of Dust (ad6).** Zharvek south of Kharanok past the Salt Sea, sacked ~day 101, no ruler, seven starving survivors — one of whom, Tzala, now lives in Kharanok.
- **Reliability note.** The ad6 account is **hearsay from starving refugees**, not authorial narration. They could be wrong about direction, or about who passed through, without anything in the world being wrong. The House Markon account is direct party observation across three sessions.

## Campaign Continuity Ruling

**Ruled 12 August 2026 by the campaign owner. Option 4 confirmed: one place, one record.**

- **One settlement, one `location-zharvek`.** The day-110 market and the day-115 ruin are the same place at two points of one causal chain. This is now an explicit ruling, not a silent consequence of keeping a single record.
- **Position.** Zharvek lies **west of Kharanok, northwest of Quor'Anok, south of Sul'Nok and southeast of Nibenay.** Both narrative accounts were wrong on direction: House Markon's "northeast toward Nibenay" and Altar of Dust's "south past the Salt Sea" are each mistaken. **The `mapHex` already encoded the ruling** — Zharvek at column 3, row 6 against Kharanok (6,6), Quor'Anok (5,7), Sul'Nok (3,4) and Nibenay (1,5) satisfies all four bearings, so no hex changes.
- **The sack.** The survivors' "about two weeks" referred to the **repeated raids** the settlement had been suffering, not to its destruction. Zharvek was raided again and again from roughly day 101 onward and was finally emptied in the **day 113–115 window**. Nothing is contradicted: the day 109–110 visit finds a working market already under harassment, Talek Vos rides out on day 113, and the ruin stands on day 115. The refugees were accurate, not mistaken.

**Still open, deliberately:** Damak's fate, and whether anyone comes looking for Talek Vos — see Unresolved Questions.

## Player-Safe Version

Zharvek lies west of Kharanok, out toward Nibenay and south of Sul'Nok. It was a fortified roadside village with an animal market, raided again and again over a matter of weeks, and by the middle of the month it stood empty — broken doors, no movement, its people carried off. A handful of survivors reached Kharanok; one of them, Tzala, lives there now.

## GM-Only Notes

Four options, with costs:

**1 — Two different places, same name.** Coincidence; a common Athasian place-name. Costs nothing, contradicts nothing, needs no retcon.
*Against:* now the weakest option. Defensible when the only overlap was "Gith trouble", but the survivors naming **House Markon specifically** as having passed through before the escalation is too pointed to wave off. Taking this route discards a link the fiction made on its own.

**2 — Retcon ad6's ruin.** Rename the ad6 settlement; leave House Markon's Zharvek untouched.
*Against:* ad6 is written and the name is in the players' mouths. Requires editing the ad6 summary and its session prep notes. Cleanest for canon, worst for player trust.

**3 — Retcon the Markon location.** Keep the ad6 ruin as the real Zharvek, rename the inn-village.
*Against:* far more expensive — Zharvek appears across ep.11, 13 and 19, in the locations reference, and Talek Vos is riding there in live plot. Not worth it.

**4 — Same place, and the contradiction is the mystery.** Both accounts true, discrepancy diegetic: something is wrong with Zharvek itself, not with the records. The two campaigns describe one causal chain from opposite ends. Hooks it could hang on: the ad6 party saw a place not yet sacked, or the Markon party visited one already sacked; a psionic illusion maintained by whoever wants the road to Nibenay looking safe; two settlements founded by the same exiles, one the other's namesake.
*Against:* commits to eventually paying it off. Powerful if done, a loose thread forever if not.

**Working position: Option 4-adjacent, held open.** No canon change applied, neither campaign retconned. The discrepancy stays recorded as an open hook in the locations reference and in ad6's "Left to do" so it is visible from both sides, and can be promoted to a real plot thread if the Gith storyline makes it useful.
