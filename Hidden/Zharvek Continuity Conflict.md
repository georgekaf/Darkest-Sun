# Zharvek — Cross-Campaign Continuity Conflict

**Status:** open, deliberately unresolved.
**Raised:** 2026-08-03, while writing the ad6 Session Prep.
**Cross-flagged in:** `transcripts and summaries/locations.md`, `Hidden/Session prep/0006 - Session Prep 31-7-2026.md`, `Hidden/Pending Worldbuilding Issues.md`.

## The conflict

Two sessions in two different campaigns, sharing one world, describe a place called Zharvek in ways that cannot both be literally true.

| | **House Markon** (s3 ep.11, 13, 19) | **Altar of Dust** (ad6) |
|---|---|---|
| State | Fortified inn-village, thriving, has a livestock market (party bought five Kang there) | Ruin — broken doors, no sign of life, a handful of starving survivors |
| Direction from Kharanok | Northeast, on the road toward Nibenay, ~9 hours' march | South, past the Salt Sea (the ruin sits north of the sea itself) |
| Ruler | Damak, a dwarf, ex-slaver exiled from Nibenay | None — whoever led it is dead or enslaved |
| Destroyed | Not destroyed; still standing as of ep.19 (day 113) | Sacked ~day 101, about two weeks before ad6 (day 115) |

The timeline is the harder half: ep.11/13 show Zharvek intact and busy around **day 109–110**, which is *eight or nine days after* ad6 says it was already a ruin.

## The thread that connects them

This is stronger than a shared theme, and it points hard toward "same place".

The ad6 survivors say that **people of House Markon passed through Zharvek some time earlier, and that trouble with the Gith got worse after they left.** They draw no causal link — they just report the sequence.

That sequence is exactly what House Markon's own sessions show. In ep.11/13 the party visits Zharvek and finds it uneasily hosting a Gith band waiting for "Eskar's convoy"; exposing them throws the village into uproar. Then in ep.19, Talek Vos rides for Zharvek specifically to investigate Gith unrest near the Black Spine Mountains that nobody can account for — unrest that, from the Markon side, is still unresolved.

So both campaigns independently describe: House Markon visits Zharvek → Gith trouble escalates after they leave. One campaign then shows the escalation as an open mystery; the other shows its endpoint, a sacked village. **The two accounts fit together causally even though they contradict each other on geography and date.**

This makes the coincidental-name reading (Option 1) much weaker than it first looked, and Option 4 correspondingly stronger.

## Options

### 1 — Two different places, same name
Declare it coincidence: a common Athasian place-name, like a dozen real-world Springfields. Costs nothing, contradicts nothing, needs no retcon in any existing summary.

*Against:* now the weakest option. It was defensible when the only overlap was "Gith trouble," but the survivors naming **House Markon specifically** as having passed through before the escalation is far too pointed to wave off as coincidence. Taking this route means discarding a link the fiction has already made on its own.

### 2 — Retcon ad6's ruin
Rename the ad6 settlement to something else; leave House Markon's Zharvek untouched.

*Against:* ad6 is already written and the name is in the players' mouths. Requires editing the ad6 Περίληψη and Session Prep. Cleanest for canon, worst for player trust.

### 3 — Retcon the Markon location
Keep the ad6 ruin as the real Zharvek and rename the inn-village.

*Against:* far more expensive — Zharvek appears across ep.11, 13 and 19, in `locations.md`, and Talek Vos is riding there right now in live plot. Not worth it.

### 4 — Same place, and the contradiction is the mystery
Both accounts are true and the discrepancy is diegetic — something is wrong with Zharvek itself, not with the records. The House Markon detail above does most of the work here: the two campaigns are describing one causal chain from opposite ends. Hooks this could hang on: the ad6 party saw a place that had not been sacked yet, or the Markon party visited one that already had been; a psionic illusion maintained by whoever wants the road to Nibenay looking safe; or two settlements founded by the same exiles, one of which is the other's namesake.

Worth noting: the ad6 survivors' testimony is **hearsay from starving refugees**, not authorial narration. They could be wrong about direction, or about who passed through, without anything in the world being wrong — which is a cheap escape hatch if you ever want one.

*Against:* commits you to eventually paying it off. Powerful if you do, a loose thread forever if you don't.

## Decision

**Option 4-adjacent, held open** — leave it unresolved rather than retcon either campaign. No canon change applied. The discrepancy is recorded as an open hook in `locations.md` and in ad6's "Left to do" so it stays visible from both sides, and can be promoted to a real plot thread later if the Gith storyline makes it useful.
