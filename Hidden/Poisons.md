## Poison Delivery Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Injury (Bloodstream)** | Applied to weapons, spikes, needles. Enters through wound. |
| **Ingested** | Mixed into food/drink. Slower onset. |
| **Inhaled** | Gas, powder, spores. Affects area. |
| **Touch (Contact)** | Absorbed through skin or eyes. |

## Antidotes & Cures

| Method | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Neutralize Poison | Negates all poison effects |
| Slow Poison | Limits spread/damage |
| Heal spell | Cures poison damage |
| Life sap (Holy Water variant) | Heals 1d3 HP, cures some poisons/paralysis |
| Life Tree Sap | More powerful variant (see DMG treasures) |
| Save vs Poison | Standard 2e saving throw |
