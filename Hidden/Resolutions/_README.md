# Resolutions — drafts answering the unresolved details on mk-sandbox records

One file per record. Each answers the `unresolvedDetails[]` that record carries, sourced from the
printed books where the books answer them.

**Source of the queue:** the appendix at the end of
`obsidian/Hidden/Pending Worldbuilding Issues.md` — 617 items across 175 records, harvested
13 August 2026 from `main` at `7c98d24`.

**Nothing here is filed.** These are drafts. Issues get created only after the list is compiled and
the user has been through it.

## The four verdicts

Every item gets exactly one:

| verdict | meaning | what happens to it |
|---|---|---|
| **ANSWERED** | the printed source settles it | quote the book, propose the record change |
| **PRINTED SILENCE** | the book is genuinely silent | close it under the standing principle — *silence stays silent*; the campaign invents nothing and the table decides what it believes |
| **GM DECISION** | no source can settle it; it is a ruling | goes to the user, not to research |
| **PLAY-DEPENDENT** | resolves when the party acts, not before | stays open by design; record the printed *terms* rather than the outcome |

The distinction that matters: **PRINTED SILENCE and PLAY-DEPENDENT are answers**, not failures.
An item that reads "unresolved" only because nobody has checked is a different thing from one that
is unresolved because the source declines to say.

## What a draft must carry

- The record, its id, its current revision, and the exact `unresolvedDetails[]` strings it holds.
- Per item: the verdict, the evidence, and — for ANSWERED — the **printed quote**, so the reviewer
  never has to take the finding on trust.
- The proposed change to the record, in the record's own field names.
- Any **twin record** carrying the same question in different words. `actors/dorak.json` and
  `artifacts/lion-medallion.json` both hold the medallion questions; a ruling that updates one and
  not the other leaves the sandbox contradicting itself.
