## What is printed about her, and currently under-recorded

> *"After two days of confinement, a lone guard slips quietly up to their cell and identifies herself as **Krika**. Although she has been **polymorphed to resemble a githyanki**, she is really a **githzerai spy**. Krika motions for them to be quiet and **tosses a metal key** to the nearest prisoner. She whispers for the characters to **look for her atop the stairs**, if they make it that far, and leaves."*

> *"The PCs will encounter Krika again after they have fought their way through to **area 22**… This meeting is an important one, because **it provides the link to the next part of the adventure**."*

> *"The githyanki woman is actually Krika, a polymorphed githzerai spy."*

So: **female, githzerai, a spy under polymorph, operating inside a githyanki facility**, who frees the party, arms them with a key, and is the structural hinge into the next act. That last point matters — she is not an optional NPC; the adventure routes through her.

## 1. "The githzerai currently have no dedicated sandbox faction record in this actor dossier."

**STRUCTURAL — GM DECISION, and the case for yes is strong.** The githzerai are not incidental colour in this book; they are the force that broke Yathazor:

- *"githzerai, the **long-time enemies of the githyanki**"*
- *"The githyanki gave up the city when githzerai **launched an assault on various other githyanki settlements on Athas**."*
- They used *"a dangerous, experimental **psionic devastator**… a psionic bomb which sent out tremendous psi-waves"*, and a small band *"using **stolen planar keys**"* attacked Yathazor with it.
- The aftershock is still on the map: five githyanki guards *"were manning this guardhouse when a githzerai psionic devastator was used on Yathazor, **which drove them mad**"*, and five sword spirits remain *"intent on carrying out their final purpose: **protecting Yathazor from githzerai**."*

A faction with a named enemy, a signature weapon, planar mobility, a war record that reshaped a location the campaign already holds, and a live agent in play. **Same shape as the Square of Gurdek decision — see that sheet; both are one ruling each.**

## 2. "Krika's survival and future after the destruction of Bahkus's outpost are unresolved until campaign play establishes them."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT**, correctly labelled.

## 3. "Whether Krika learns Karros's real allegiance or negotiates any outcome involving Maelactorus remains unresolved."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT**, correctly labelled — and worth keeping, because a spy under polymorph learning another agent's true allegiance is exactly the kind of thing that should happen at a table rather than in a record.

## 4. "The asteroid base is not a registered campaign location; actor-krika remains deliberately distinct from the unrelated existing actor-kirka."

**Two unrelated things in one item — split them.**

**(a) The asteroid base — STRUCTURAL, GM DECISION.** The book gives it a whole act: *"Part Three: The Githzerai Asteroid."* It is a destination the adventure travels to, not a mentioned place. If it is to be reachable in play it needs a record. **Caution:** do not conflate it with **Galnaki**, the city built *"upon the asteroid fragment"* claimed by Trinth's followers — that is a different asteroid belonging to a different faction, and the two are one careless merge away from becoming the same place.

**(b) Krika ≠ Kirka — the guard rail is correct and the collision is live.** `actor-kirka` is one of **Klarbu's surviving warriors**, named alongside Selka and Melka in the Dune Runners' campaign, and has nothing to do with githzerai or Black Spine. One vowel apart, two continents apart. **Keep this guard rail permanently**; it is the same class of error as `Rakskon`/`Raskon` and `Damak Kheth`/`Damak of Zharvek`, both of which have already caused trouble.

## 5. "The issue's proposed senior-survivor status and other campaign continuations are not confirmed canon."

**Guard rail**, correct. Move to a note rather than leaving it in the open queue.
