## 1. "Why the Gray Arch is impassable to Zuzar is unresolved."

**ANSWERED as to the mechanism — and the source then contradicts itself about her.** This is the most useful thing in the batch and it needs stating precisely.

The arch's rule is printed and unambiguous:

> *"This archway was recently carved by the githyanki. Runes in the stone create a one-way door similar to a wall of force that allows only **githyanki or gith (and those in their company)** to pass through it."*

And Zuzar's own species is equally printed:

> *"This building houses **Overlord Zuzar, the gith overseer of the mine**."*

> *"**Gith Overlord Zuzar** (1): THAC0 13; Dmg 1d6 (steel-tipped spear); AC 8; HD 7; hp 35…"*

Yet two paragraphs later:

> *"**Zuzar knows that the arch (area 26) is impassable to her**, leaving her no means of escape if the plans of her superiors fail."*

**She is gith. The arch admits gith. The book says she cannot pass.** Those three statements do not reconcile, and no passage anywhere explains the exception.

**Verdict: the mechanism is ANSWERED, her exemption is a SOURCE CONTRADICTION and therefore a GM DECISION.** It must not be resolved by inference — three readings are all defensible and they lead to different fiction:

1. **The commanders keyed it against her**, personally, as part of the death sentence — the arch is her cage, deliberately.
2. **"Impassable" is political, not magical** — passing it means desertion, and desertion means being hunted, so it is no escape at all.
3. **The rune restriction is githyanki-only in practice** and "or gith" is loose drafting on the book's part.

Reading 1 makes her a prisoner with a countdown; reading 2 makes her a coward with options; reading 3 makes the arch a species gate that quietly excludes her caste. **Ask; do not pick.**

**Proposed change:** replace the bare "unresolved" with the printed mechanism, the printed contradiction, and the three readings, so the next person to touch it starts from the actual evidence rather than re-deriving it.

## 2. "Zuzar's campaign-current location, quota clock, and survival state are unconfirmed; the Overlord's Quarters meeting is source-scenario state."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT**, correctly labelled already. What is printed and recordable is the pressure she is under, which the record can carry without asserting an outcome:

- Two visiting githyanki commanders **accused her of insubordination** for failing to maintain *"a steady supply of **iron for the construction of their Nightmare Gate**"*, and **departed through the arch swearing her life would be forfeit** if she failed.
- She **met every deadline she was given**, and *"as each one was met a more impossible schedule took its place."* She is not incompetent; she is being set up to fail.
- In the Gate's final hours she has been **commanded to work her slaves to death** to meet demand.
- She is **meeting her three top officers** to decide how the slaves can best be used **in their final hours**.
- She knows **any delay endangers the Nightmare Gate timetable**, and *"a delay that would certainly mean her death."*
- If aware of the PCs, she treats them as a threat to be **destroyed as quickly as possible**, diverting forces accordingly, and will parley **only until her troops can overrun them**.

That is a clock with a stated failure condition — worth recording as a `localClocks`-style pressure rather than left as "unconfirmed".

## 3. "Whether Raskon was one of the two inspecting githyanki commanders is unconfirmed."

**PRINTED SILENCE.** The book says *"two visiting githyanki commanders"* and never names either. Close it — the campaign may cast Raskon in the role if it wants, but the source does not.

Note the standing trap on this name: **`Rakskon` and `Raskon` are two different people** in *Black Spine* — the elf thief-carpenter of Tenpug's Band, and the githyanki general. The register already carries that as open entry 22. Whoever resolves this item must not merge them.

## 4. "Quixoth's mention of Nyzethgyr does not establish that Zuzar knows or has encountered Nyzethgyr, nor that Nyzethgyr is the authority behind her death sentence."

**ALREADY CORRECTLY ANSWERED — this is not an open question.** It is a guard rail: someone recording that the death sentence traces to Nyzethgyr would be promoting inference to canon. The printed text attributes the sentence to *"her superiors"* and to the two unnamed commanders, never to Nyzethgyr.

**Proposed change:** move it out of `unresolvedDetails` and into a note. It is a settled restriction, not a pending question, and leaving it in the queue makes the queue look longer than it is.

## 5. "Whether Zuzar would genuinely defect, bargain in good faith, survive the mine, or face a slave revolt remains player-facing and unresolved."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT** by design, and the printed material makes it a real choice rather than an open shrug: she is a competent officer under a death sentence with **no escape route**, which is exactly the condition that makes defection plausible and betrayal equally plausible. The book's own guidance — she parleys only until her troops can overrun the party — should be recorded, because it tells the GM how she bargains when she is *not* desperate.
