# Resolution draft — Captain Gaff

**Record:** `actors/captain-gaff.json` · `actor-captain-gaff` · revision 1
**Printed source:** *City by the Silt Sea* — Campaign Book pp. 15–18
**Twin records:** `location-cromlin` (the Pier of House Shom), `route-silt-shoals`

---

## 1. "Why the Sky Singers gifted Gaff the sword is unknown."

**PRINTED SILENCE.** Both books say the same single clause and nothing more:

> *"He fights with an enchanted bone long sword that was a gift from elves of the Sky Singers tribe."*

Checked in the Campaign Book and in the boxed-set text; the phrase appears once in each and no passage anywhere explains the gift. The Sky Singers are detailed as a tribe in their own right and the connection is never picked up.

**Proposed change:** close under *silence stays silent*. Reword from "is unknown" — which reads like a gap someone should fill — to a statement that **the source does not say**, which is a finished answer. A gift from an elf tribe to a House Shom captain is a hook the table can invent freely; it is not a hole in the record.

## 2. "Whether Gaff survives the pirate campaign is unresolved."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT.** He is a live NPC in an ongoing pirate war; the book gives him no scripted death. Nothing to research — this resolves when the campaign resolves it.

Worth noting what the record *can* carry meanwhile, all printed: he commands **the complete respect and loyalty of his able crew**, captains the **Firewind** — one of the largest skimmers in the Tyr region, **refitted to carry more soldiers and fitted with ballista ports** — and the ship berths at the House Shom pier **whenever it is not crossing the silt shoals**. Adventurers who can demonstrate combat skill can **sign on for five silver pieces a day**.

**Proposed change:** reclassify from "unresolved" to play-dependent; add the five-silver hire rate, which is a live hook the record does not carry.

## 3. "The Pier of House Shom and the Silt Shoals route are not separately registered; the broader Cromlin location is used."

**PARTLY OVERTAKEN BY EVENTS — check before acting.**

The Silt Shoals route **is** now registered: `route-silt-shoals` exists, with `location-cromlin` as an endpoint, status *open but hazardous*. Half this note is stale.

The **Pier of House Shom** remains a feature of `location-cromlin` rather than its own record. Revision 11 of that record carries it: *"House Shom's dedicated pier keeps at least two alert guards and berths the troop-and-ballista-refitted Firewind when it is not crossing the Silt Shoals."*

**GM DECISION** on the remaining half: the 13 August ruling says a named place with its own contents, inhabitants or hazards gets its own record — and the Dirty Lizard and Passk's Shack were split out on exactly that basis. The pier has a permanent guard detail and a named flagship berthed at it. Consistency argues for splitting it; volume argues against splitting every quay in the setting.

**Proposed change:** delete the route half of the note as done. Put the pier half to the user as a yes/no, with the precedent stated.
