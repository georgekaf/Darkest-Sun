# Resolution draft — Tormar

**Record:** `actors/tormar.json` · 5 unresolved items
**Printed source:** *DSE2 Black Spine*, Book One — Into the Mines
**Twin records:** `actor-durdon`, `actor-orruk-vesh`, `actor-keehya`, `location-nibenay-iron-mine`

## The whole printed basis for this character is one clause

Tormar appears **exactly once** in the entire book, inside Durdon's dialogue:

> *"If Durdon is saved, he volunteers to accompany the group on their mission. 'By earth and stone,' he says, 'I'll smash their heads with my bare hands if you won't lend me a weapon. **They've got my buddy, Tormar.**'"*

That is the complete printed record. Every guard rail on this actor is therefore **correct and should stand** — but one printed fact is being under-claimed, and one blocking dependency has since cleared.

## 1. "Tormar's campaign-current location, condition, and survival are unknown."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT** — with one printed fact the record should assert rather than leave implicit: **he is a captive.** *"They've got my buddy"* establishes that the gith hold him at the time Durdon is found. That is not a proposal; it is the reason the character exists. What is unknown is everything after.

## 2. "His ancestry is not printed; dwarf must not be inferred merely from association with Durdon."

**CORRECT — the guard rail stands, and the trap is real.** Durdon's own oath in the same sentence is *"By earth and stone"*, which reads dwarven, and the temptation is to let it rub off on the friend standing next to him. The book never states Tormar's ancestry. **Keep.**

## 3. "No personal stat block or personality is printed, so proposed conversions and characterization are not asserted."

**CORRECT — verified.** There is no block, no description, no line of dialogue, no age, no trade. One clause, spoken by someone else. **Keep.**

## 4. "The Durdon/Orruk Vesh identity question should be resolved separately before adding a stable actor relationship."

**THE DEPENDENCY HAS CLEARED — this item is unblocked and can close.** It was ruled on **13 August 2026** (MK-Sandbox #332, recorded in the register): **keep both.** Orruk Vesh and Durdon are two different dwarf miners — Orruk Vesh survived and is recovering at the stockade, Durdon escaped separately and deeper into the tunnels. `actor-durdon` stands as its own record.

So the relationship Tormar's record has been waiting on is now safe to add: **`actor-durdon` — the friend whose captivity is Tormar's only printed fact.** Nothing attaches to `actor-orruk-vesh`.

**Proposed change:** retire this item and add the Durdon relationship, citing the ruling.

## 5. "Any specific rescue, slave-pen placement, work-gang placement, smelter death, or other fate remains a proposal until confirmed in play."

**CORRECT — keep as a guard rail**, and it is doing real work. Tormar is exactly the kind of blank that invites a GM to place him at a convenient mine face. Note the adjacent temptation: `actor-keehya`'s record carries a proposal that **Tormar works at Keehya's face** — see `Keehya.md`, item 3, where it is likewise held as a GM decision. **Two records must not quietly agree with each other into canon.**
