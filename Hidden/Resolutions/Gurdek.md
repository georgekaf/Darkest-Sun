## 1. "Why Slate chose the name Gurdek is unrecorded."

**HALF ANSWERED, and answered explicitly.** Slate did name the group, and the book explains the *Square* half in full:

> *"Slate has kept his crew together, **naming it the Square of Gurdek**. The term **'Square' comes from their habit of sitting in a square when they gather in groups of more than four**. The most important members form the corners, with lower-status characters in between them."*

So the name is Slate's, the seating is the etymology, and **rank is encoded in the geometry** — corners for the most important, the lesser between them. That is a usable table detail: walk into a gathering and the shape of it tells you the hierarchy.

**"Gurdek" itself remains PRINTED SILENCE.** The book explains "Square" and never explains "Gurdek". That half stays open, and should.

*Correction to an earlier draft of this sheet: I first read "the Square of Gurdek" as a pre-existing place name in Yathazor that the cult adopted, and flagged it as unconfirmed. The passage above settles it the other way — the group is named first and the location takes its name from the group.*

## 2. "What Gurdek actually is remains deliberately unresolved."

**PRINTED SILENCE, and deliberate — keep it.** The book never says whether Gurdek was a person, a god, a founder, an overseer, or simply a district name. Every appearance is *"the Square of Gurdek"* and never *"Gurdek did…"*. An unexplained name in a dead city that a lunatic cult has adopted is doing exactly the work it should.

## 3. "Whether Gurdek remains in contact with Slate is unconfirmed."

**MALFORMED — it has no referent.** Item 1 settles this: Slate coined the name; there is no entity called Gurdek in the text to be in contact with anyone. The item should be **deleted**, not answered.

What *is* printed about Slate's contacts: he **helped the gith excavate a wide path to Zigath's Nest**, so his cult is materially assisting the gith war effort; and his people *"believe the gith to be descendants of 'earth spirits' and the rightful rulers of all Athas."* His alliance is with the gith, and it is theological.

## 4. "Any connection between Gurdek and the wider Black Spine planar intrusion is speculative and has not been promoted to canon."

**NOT A QUESTION — a guard rail**, and a correct one. Same category as Zuzar's Nyzethgyr item: it exists to stop someone promoting a guess. Move it out of `unresolvedDetails` into a note so it stops inflating the queue.

## 5. "Gurdek has no printed stat block and Slate's statue is not Gurdek itself."

**ANSWERED ALREADY, and correctly.** Also a guard rail rather than an open question. Retire it to a note.

## What the record should gain regardless

All printed, none of it currently on the record:

- The Square of Gurdek is **fanatic earth clerics who seem friendly but are waiting for the first opportunity to destroy the PCs** — *"Though crazy, they are not immediately hostile, and a careful group of PCs may be able to avoid a confrontation completely."*
- **Morlah**, the insane bard, lives with them.
- The square contains **a vertical tunnel breach — the route the gith travel** into Yathazor.
- The cult is **known outside Yathazor**: a captured gith *"knows about the Square of Gurdek, the group of earth clerics in Yathazor who are helping the gith"*, and another gith has *"heard about the Square of Gurdek"* without ever having been there.
- **The full membership is printed, with classes, levels and genders** — see the roster below. It resolves items on six other records.

## The Square's roster — printed in full

> *"Members of the Square are: **Slate**, a 15th-level earth cleric (human male); **Pargo**, a 7th-level earth cleric/5th-level psionicist (human male); **Mefse**, a 6th-level earth cleric/3rd-level fighter (human male); **Ari Evan**, a 3rd-level earth cleric/9th-level preserver (human female); **Ranza**, 2nd-level earth cleric/12th-level thief (human male); **Olton**, a feebleminded 8th-level gladiator (human female); and **Morlah**, who's insane, formerly a 5th-level bard (human male)."*

And the recruitment mechanism, which is uglier than "converts":

> *"…succumbed to Slate's **charm spell**, so all of these dual class recruits have Wisdom scores of 17 or 18. Slate knows he has recruited no clerics who are as wise as him (**since he, himself, is immune to charm spells**), and he is satisfied in the knowledge that he is superior to all others in the Square."*

**These are not followers. They are charm-spell captures**, selected for being wise enough to be useful and not wise enough to resist — and Slate knows it, and is pleased about it. Anyone with Wisdom above 18 was never recruitable; his own immunity is what keeps him at the top.
