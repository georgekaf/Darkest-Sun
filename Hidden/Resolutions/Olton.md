## 1. "Olton's life before the failed initiation is unrecorded."

**PRINTED SILENCE.** The book establishes the initiation practice — *"In the intervening years, Slate has invited those he has initiated into Yathazor, and they have taken up residence"* — but gives no history for Olton before it. Close under *silence stays silent*; her past is free for the table.

## 2. "What, if anything, remains accessible inside her damaged mind is unresolved."

**THE CONDITION IS NAMED — the contents stay silent.** The Square's printed roster gives her in full:

> *"**Olton**, a **feebleminded 8th-level gladiator** (human female)"*

So she is a **human female**, an **8th-level gladiator**, and **feebleminded** — a specific, named condition, not vague "damage". That matters two ways: it points at a cause (feeblemind is a spell, and her cult leader is a 15th-level cleric who recruits by charm), and it sets what "reachable" means mechanically.

**And the recruitment mechanism is printed**, which reframes her entirely:

> *"…succumbed to Slate's **charm spell**, so all of these dual class recruits have Wisdom scores of 17 or 18… (since he, himself, is immune to charm spells)"*

The Square are **charm-spell captures**, not converts. Whether Olton's feeblemindedness predates her "failed initiation" or was inflicted in it is not stated — but she is a **captive of a man who takes people by magic**, which is a different record from a follower who went mad.

What remains *accessible* inside her mind stays **PRINTED SILENCE**, and it is a hook rather than a gap.

**Proposed change:** record human female, gladiator 8, feebleminded, and the charm-recruitment context. Narrow the open question to what is reachable, and note the cause of the condition as unestablished — deliberately, since it bears on whether Slate is her rescuer or her author.

## 3. "What happens if Slate leaves through stone and Olton cannot follow is a source branch that has not been confirmed in campaign play."

**MECHANISM ANSWERED — outcome PLAY-DEPENDENT.** The record treats "leaves through stone" as an untested branch. It is not speculative; it is Slate's established, printed capability, and it is how he found the city in the first place:

> *"Over 20 years ago, the demented earth cleric Slate discovered Yathazor by using his **ignore stone** ability to walk…"*

The same ability is printed elsewhere in the adventure as a way past a barrier that stops everyone else — the Gray Arch passage offers, as one of three bypasses, *"an earth cleric's ability to ignore stone and walk through the walls around the arch."*

So the branch is real and repeatable: **Slate can walk out through rock at will, and anyone who cannot follow is simply left.** That is a printed asymmetry between him and everyone around him, including Olton, and it should be recorded as a capability of his rather than a hypothetical.

**Proposed change:** record `ignore stone` on `actor-slate` as confirmed printed ability with its two printed uses; on Olton, keep only the genuinely open half — whether she is abandoned, and what that does to her — as play-dependent.

## 4. "Slate's campaign-current status and whether Olton remains with him are unresolved."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT.** Correctly open. Recordable context, all printed: Slate leads the **Square of Gurdek**, *"a collection of earth clerics"*, *"fanatical and demented"*; they believe **the gith are descendants of "earth spirits" and the rightful rulers of all Athas**; Slate **helped the gith excavate a wide path to Zigath's Nest**; and his theory *"first a fact and then an obsession."*

That last detail matters for Olton specifically — she is attached to a man whose belief system is described by the source as an obsession that overrode his judgement, and who has a private exit from any room.
