## 1. "The source conflicts on whether Ari Evan is a 7th- or 9th-level preserver and whether her earth-cleric level is 2 or 3."

**BOTH HALVES CHECKED. One conflict is real; the other does not exist.**

**The preserver level genuinely conflicts:**

| where | reading |
|---|---|
| Square roster | *"Ari Evan, a 3rd-level earth cleric/**9th-level preserver** (human female)"* |
| her own NPC block, p. 135 | *"Ari Evan / Female Human Preserver/Earth Cleric / **7th Level**/3rd Level / Chaotic Evil"* |

**9th in the summary list, 7th in the dedicated block.** Real, printed, in the same book.

**Recommended resolution: take the dedicated block — preserver 7.** A full NPC entry with abilities, spells and personality is a considered statement; a roster line is a summary, and summaries are where transcription slips happen. This is a recommendation, not a ruling — flag it for the owner rather than writing it in silently.

**The earth-cleric level does not conflict at all.** Both printed places say **3rd**: the roster says *"a 3rd-level earth cleric"*, and her block's second number is *"/3rd Level"*. **There is no "2" anywhere in her entries.** The adjacent roster line reads *"Ranza, 2nd-level earth cleric/12th-level thief"* — so the 2 almost certainly bled across from Ranza when our record was written. **That half of the item should be deleted as a transcription error on our side, not carried as a source conflict.**

## 2. "Whether Ari Evan survives the Refuge and uses the printed retaliation remains unconfirmed."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT**, correctly labelled.

Printed context the record should carry: she is **Chaotic Evil**, *"animated, talkative, and sometimes charming"*, **in her sixties**, and *"her wrinkles and scars show that the years have taken their toll on her."* A charming, talkative old woman who is also chaotic evil and holds explosive runes is a very specific table presence, and none of it is on the record.

## 3. "Whether she ever meets an actual githyanki and how that changes her ideology is unresolved."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT** and a good hook. The Square believes the gith are *"descendants of 'earth spirits' and the rightful rulers of all Athas"* — a doctrine held by people who have, so far, met the theory rather than the thing.

## 4. "Her exact present campaign location is unconfirmed; Yathazor is a source-scenario association."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT**, correctly labelled.

## The mechanical constraint the record is missing

Printed, and it applies to her specifically:

> *"**Ari Evan and Ranza have not gained enough experience to use their former, nonclerical abilities without penalizing their own progress in their new class.**"*

So her preserver magic is **not freely available** — using it costs her advancement as a cleric. That is a live limitation on a 7th-level preserver in a party's path, and it explains why a caster of that grade is not simply throwing spells. It belongs on both her record and Ranza's.

## The recruitment context

She did not join. Like the rest of the Square she *"succumbed to Slate's **charm spell**"*, and the roster's members were selected for Wisdom 17–18 — wise enough to be useful, not wise enough to resist a man *"immune to charm spells"* himself.
