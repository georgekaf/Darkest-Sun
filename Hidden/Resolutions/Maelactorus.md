# Resolution draft — Maelactorus

**Record:** `actors/maelactorus.json` · 5 unresolved items
**Printed source:** *DSE2 Black Spine*, Book Three — City of Spires, area 9 "Mysterious Vault"
**Twin records:** `actor-krika`, `actor-alukas`, the unregistered githzerai asteroid *(see `Krika.md`)*

---

## The record is missing what he is

> *"The terrible creature within the mysterious chamber is the **mind flayer Maelactorus**. This fell creature was actually **the master of the Order of the Rising Shadow**. When the asteroid jumped into the Astral Plane and the wizards were killed, **he sealed himself into this vault as a protective measure. A mistake in the wording of the wish resulted in him being imprisoned since that time.**"*

An **illithid** who was the master of the wizard cult, who **imprisoned himself by a botched wish** and has been in that vault ever since. Printed block: *THAC0 11; Dmg 2 points (×4); AC 5; HD 8+4; hp 55; MV 12; SA/SD psionics; **MR 90%**; INT 18; AL LE; ML 15; XP 7,000. PSPs 310 — domination, mindlink, probability travel, teleport.*

## 1. "Whether the party ever opens the vault is unresolved and must remain an explicit player/GM branch."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT**, correctly labelled and correctly insisted upon. The door is *"a massive metal affair that is all but impossible to force"*, and **the githzerai have always assumed the vault held treasure** — they have never opened it. The party opening what an entire complex failed to open for centuries is exactly the branch the item protects.

## 2. "What the Order of the Rising Shadow was specifically trying to achieve when it tore the mountain into the Astral Plane is unresolved."

**THE QUESTION IS MALFORMED — they did not tear it deliberately.**

> *"Long ago this mountain was home to the Order of the Rising Shadow, a cult of mysterious wizards. **Their experiments in magic eventually opened a mystical portal to the Astral Plane and yanked the mountain through. The shock of this transition proved fatal to the magicians**, but left their catacombs intact."*

The transition was an **accident that killed them all**, not an achievement. The item's phrasing — *"when it tore the mountain"* — presupposes intent the source denies. **What their experiments were aimed at remains PRINTED SILENCE**, and that is the only part worth keeping.

**Proposed change:** reword to *"what the Order's experiments were attempting is unstated; the passage into the Astral was an accidental result that killed them"* — and note the survivor: their master, who is still in the vault.

## 3. "Whether Maelactorus personally ordered or knows the reason for Alukas's imprisonment is not explicitly established."

**NOT EXPLICIT — but the printed chronology makes it very nearly impossible**, and the record should say so rather than leave it open-ended. He *"sealed himself into this vault"* at the moment of the transition and has been *"imprisoned since that time"*. A being sealed behind a door nobody has opened for centuries is poorly placed to order a recent imprisonment.

**Verdict: keep open as stated, but record the chronological constraint.** If the campaign wants him behind Alukas's fate it needs a mechanism that survives the vault — which is a GM decision, not an inference.

## 4. "What happens between Krika and the party if Maelactorus is spared is unresolved."

**HER POSITION IS PRINTED — only the outcome is open.**

> *"He will not be in the least grateful to the PCs for freeing him, but will attempt to **dominate them as he did the wizards that were here before**. It might be possible to negotiate with Maelactorus, but the DM will want to make this a difficult prospect. This prospect is **further hampered by the fact that Krika will insist that the creature cannot be trusted and must be destroyed. Her people lived for centuries under the yoke of the mind flayers and an intense hatred of that evil race lingers in them to this day.**"*

So it is not an open question what Krika does — **she will demand his destruction, and the reason is racial history, centuries of githzerai enslavement under illithids.** Sparing him means overriding her, and the book frames that as a cost the DM should make the party feel.

**Proposed change:** record Krika's printed position and its stated cause; keep only the outcome as play-dependent. Also record that **he dominated the Order's wizards** — his relationship to his own cult was mastery by domination, which recasts the Order itself.

## 5. "The issue's proposed post-release campaign arc and interpretations of Maelactorus's motives are not confirmed canon."

**Guard rail**, correct. Move to a note.

## A cross-link that resolves something on another sheet

*"When **the asteroid** jumped into the Astral Plane…"* — the mountain **is** the asteroid. So the githzerai complex in Book Three's *"Part Three: The Githzerai Asteroid"* sits inside **the Order of the Rising Shadow's former home**, dragged bodily into the Astral by its owners' failed experiment.

That matters for the unregistered-asteroid question raised on `Krika.md`: the location is not a bare rock, it is a wizard cult's catacombs with the cult's master still sealed in a vault at the bottom. **And it remains a different asteroid from Galnaki.**
