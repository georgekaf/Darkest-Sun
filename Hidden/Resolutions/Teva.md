# Resolution draft — Teva

**Record:** `actors/teva.json` · 5 unresolved items
**Printed source:** *DSE2 Black Spine*, Book One — Clash by Night, Tenpug's Band NPCs
**Twin records:** `actor-tenpug`, `actor-umor`, `actor-isak`, `faction-tenpugs-band`, `actor-danya`

## 1. "What Teva did as Kalak's templar is intentionally undisclosed by the source."

**CONFIRMED — and it is better than "undisclosed".** The source does not merely omit it; **the character refuses to say**, which is a playable hook rather than a gap:

> *"Teva was once a templar for Kalak, the late sorcerer-king of Tyr. **What secrets she knows of that time she will not say.** It was a dark time in her life, and she does not wish to remember it."*

**Proposed change:** reword from "undisclosed by the source" to the printed fact — she knows secrets of Kalak's templarate and refuses to speak of them. That is a live thread for any party that earns her trust, and the record currently reads as though there is simply nothing there.

## 2. "The reason Teva no longer has templar powers is not explicitly stated by this source and must not be promoted from inference to canon."

**CORRECT, and the guard rail should stand.** The fact is printed flatly:

> *"**Teva no longer has any magical powers at all.**"*

The cause is not. The obvious inference — templar powers come from the sorcerer-king, Kalak is dead, therefore they lapsed — is exactly the kind of tidy reasoning the item warns against, and the book never draws it. Note the printed sequence gives a rival explanation just as available: she was **enslaved after crossing the wrong people**, bribed the guards sent to collect her, and escaped. Losing her powers may belong to that disgrace rather than to Kalak's death.

**Keep as a guard rail; move it out of the open-question queue.**

## 3. "The source says the offering is made under Teva's guidance but does not establish that she invented the rite."

**CORRECT — another guard rail.** Nothing to research. Move to a note.

## 4. "Her printed level cannot be recovered reliably from the available source record and no inferred level or Shadowdark conversion is asserted."

**RECOVERABLE — and the blocker is OCR damage, not source silence.** This is the most useful finding on the sheet.

The stat block is present in full; the OCR has simply eaten the first character of every line in this column, which is why the level digit looks missing:

```
eva (the Wise Woman)      →  Teva (the Wise Woman)
uman Fighter              →  Human Fighter
th Level                  →  [?]th Level        ← the lost digit
awful Good                →  Lawful Good
tr 15  Int 16             →  Str 15  Int 16
ex 15  Wis 18             →  Dex 15  Wis 18
on 16  Cha 14             →  Con 16  Cha 14
p: 38                     →  hp: 38
C: 4 (hide and shield)    →  AC: 4 (hide and shield)
AT: 1                     →  #AT: 1
HAC0: 16                  →  THAC0: 16
mg: 1d6+1 (iron mace)     →  Dmg: 1d6+1 (iron mace)
```

**The level is derivable from the surviving numbers.** In AD&D 2e a fighter's THAC0 is `21 − level`. The block prints **THAC0 16**, giving **level 5**, and **hp 38** with **Con 16** sits comfortably in a 5th-level fighter's range. Two independent printed figures agree.

**Also recovered and currently absent from the record:** she is a **Human Fighter**, not a spellcaster — "Wise Woman" is a title, not a class — **Lawful Good**, Str 15, Dex 15, Con 16, Int 16, **Wis 18**, Cha 14, AC 4 (hide and shield), and she fights with an **iron mace** (1d6+1). An *iron* weapon in a Dark Sun band is itself worth noting.

**Proposed change:** record the full stat block as printed. State the level as **5, derived from THAC0 16**, explicitly marked as a mechanical derivation rather than a printed digit, and flag that a clean scan would confirm it outright. The existing guard rail against *inferred* levels is respected — this is arithmetic on printed values, not a guess, and it is labelled as such.

## 5. "Whether she becomes Keeper of Supplies or takes any later role remains unresolved."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT.** Printed context the record can carry meanwhile: she works for Tenpug, **carves bone figurines** sold in the markets of Nibenay and is *"only a mediocre craftsman"*; in the war council she is a **minor debater and the anti-violence voice** — *"Teva is anti-violence, claiming that war, not cowardice, leads to death"* — against Umor and Isak, who both argue for fighting. She also, with Isak, proposes **searching the temple for ancient weapons or magic**, which **Tenpug violently opposes** for fear of ancient curses.

That last point matters: the record has her as a supplies functionary, and the book has her arguing policy against the band's leader.
