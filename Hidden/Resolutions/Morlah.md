# Resolution draft — Morlah

**Record:** `actors/morlah.json` · 4 unresolved items
**Printed source:** *DSE2 Black Spine*, Book Two — Yathazor, the Square of Gurdek; encounter tables and wandering-NPC entries
**Twin records:** `actor-slate`, `actor-olton`, `actor-gurdek`, `location-yathazor`

---

## 1. "Who Morlah was before Slate found him is unrecorded."

**HALF ANSWERED — his class and level are printed.** The Square's roster gives him:

> *"and **Morlah**, who's insane, **formerly a 5th-level bard** (human male)"*

So: **human male, formerly a 5th-level bard**, and the insanity is stated as his present condition against that former profession. The record treats "who he was" as blank; it is not — he was a trained bard of real competence, and the book says *formerly*, which frames the insanity as something that ended a career.

**His life before that** — where he trained, who he travelled with, how Slate found him — is **PRINTED SILENCE**.

**And the recruitment mechanism is printed**, which bears directly on "before Slate found him":

> *"…succumbed to Slate's **charm spell**, so all of these dual class recruits have Wisdom scores of 17 or 18… (since he, himself, is immune to charm spells)"*

The Square are **charm-spell captures**. Whether Morlah's insanity preceded Slate or was made by him is not stated.

**Proposed change:** record human male, former bard 5, and the charm-recruitment context. Narrow the open item to the life before the charm.

## 2. "Whether Morlah's apparently meaningful song is genuine prophecy, coincidence, or simply broken speech should remain unresolved."

**KEEP UNRESOLVED — and the record is right to say so.** This is the correct handling and needs no research. It is also load-bearing: a former **bard** whose music now reads as possibly-prophetic is a much stronger hook than a random madman, and the "formerly 5th-level bard" line is what gives the ambiguity its teeth. He had the training; the question is whether anything is still using it.

## 3. "Whether a bard or another character can meaningfully reach Morlah is not established by the source."

**PARTLY ANSWERED — the source establishes he is hard to reach, physically.** He is written as elusive by default:

> *"**Morlah**: This is the insane bard from the Square of Gurdek. If it is too early for the PCs to meet him, **he may flee at the first sight of them**. In fact, they may simply **hear his drumming but fail to find him** when they search for its source."*

He also appears on the **wandering-encounter table** for Yathazor alongside Slate, so he is a roaming presence rather than a fixed NPC in a room.

So: **he drums, he hides, and he runs.** Whether he can be *reached* in the deeper sense — communicated with, restored — is genuinely **PRINTED SILENCE**, and the item should keep that half while dropping the implication that nothing at all is established.

**Proposed change:** record the drumming, the flight reaction and the wandering placement; keep only "can he be meaningfully communicated with" open.

## 4. "His campaign-current location and survival state are unconfirmed."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT**, correctly labelled. He lives with the Square in Yathazor; beyond that the campaign has not placed him.
