## 1. "Whose skull is on Keehya's staff is unresolved."

**HALF ANSWERED — and the answered half is the half that matters.** The record does not say what kind of skull it is. The book does:

> *"Fifteen gith guards surround a strange-looking gith wearing gaudy, ornate clothing. He rolls a long staff back and forth between his hands. **The staff is adorned with a human skull!**"*

**It is a human skull**, and in a mine worked by **thirty human slaves** under a gith overseer, that is not decoration — it is a statement made to the people holding the picks. The exclamation mark is the book's, in boxed read-aloud text.

**Whose** skull remains **PRINTED SILENCE**, and it should stay that way; an unnamed human skull carried by a slave overseer is more useful unclaimed.

**Proposed change:** record the skull as human and as an instrument of terror over the human slaves; narrow the open question to identity alone.

**Also recoverable from the same passage** — the record should carry these rather than leave them implicit: Keehya is **male** (*"He rolls a long staff"*), **"strange-looking"** even to the book's eye, wears **gaudy, ornate clothing**, and is escorted by **fifteen gith guards**. His printed block: *Gith Overseer Keehya (1): THAC0 15; Dmg 1d6 (steel-tipped spears); AC 8; HD 6; hp 30; SA spring 20 feet (+2 on attacks), psionics; SD psionics; MV 9.*

## 2. "Whether Keehya knows what the iron is ultimately for is unresolved."

**THE IRON'S PURPOSE IS ANSWERED. Keehya's awareness is PRINTED SILENCE.** These are two different questions and the record runs them together.

The purpose is stated outright in Zuzar's entry two areas away:

> *"They have accused her of insubordination for failing to maintain a steady supply of **iron for the construction of their Nightmare Gate**."*

> *"Now in the final hours of the Nightmare Gate's preparation, she has been **commanded to work her slaves to death** in order to meet demand."*

So the mine exists, in gith hands, to feed the Nightmare Gate, and the schedule has reached the point where the labour force is expendable. **Keehya runs the face where that happens.**

Whether he personally knows the destination of the ore is never stated. But the record can now say what he is *doing* even if not what he *knows* — and note the pressure runs through him: Zuzar's deadline failure is his output shortfall.

**Proposed change:** add the Nightmare Gate as the confirmed purpose of the iron and cross-link `location-nightmare-gate`; keep only Keehya's personal awareness open.

## 3. "Whether Tormar works at Keehya's face was proposed in the issue but is not confirmed canon."

Note this is entangled with a live register question: **the Durdon / Orruk Vesh identity issue** (ruled 13 August: keep both as separate dwarves). Tormar's own record flags the same dependency. Resolve Tormar's placement after that, not before.

**PLAY-DEPENDENT**, correctly labelled. B4 is where the adventure puts him, not where the campaign has established him. No research will change this.
