## 1. "Passk's exact role in the Adventure Book should be checked before play."

**ANSWERED.** It has now been checked.

The Campaign Book flags it — *"He plays an important role in the action outlined in the Adventure Book, and his habits and personality are detailed there… be sure to refer to that book before your PCs meet the dwarf"* — and the Adventure Book delivers it:

- **Guvaano**, the Sky Singers' elder wizard, sends the preservers **Jellog** and **Betrik** toward Cromlin to check rumours about the ruins of Giustenal.
- The **Veiled Alliance has heard that a dwarf named Passk knows a way into the vast city buried beneath the Silt Sea.**
- Jellog gives the PCs the task outright: *"Find this dwarf named Passk and convince him to help us. I'll send another member of the Alliance to meet you in Cromlin as soon as I get back to Nibenay."*
- In Cromlin the locals think he is dead — *"Passk? The dwarf pirate. Yeah, I heard of him. I heard he died."* — and point instead to *"a drunk dwarf [who] lives in a shack down by the sea… a member of Passk's crew."* That drunk **is** Passk: *"Oh, he's not dead. Not yet, anyway. I'm Passk, pirate captain without a crew."*

**Proposed change:** replace the unresolved line with a `knowledge` entry — *Passk knows a route into the buried city and is the Veiled Alliance's named objective in Cromlin* — and record the Alliance link on the record. The item is retired, not reworded.

## 2. "Whether his first mate's psyche remains inside the Caller is unknown."

**ANSWERED IN PART, then PRINTED SILENCE.** The record calls her only "his first mate". **She has a name: Logi**, and she is female. A second crewman is named **Kas**.

> *"We were traveling the shoals off the coast of the ruined city, me and my crew. One night, the Caller sang its cursed song. I woke up to find my first mate, Logi, slicing the throat of one of my men. I looked around, and she had killed every one of my pirates… Logi shouted at me in words I didn't understand, then she charged… she screamed again and turned to look at the nearby ruins… She looked at Giustenal with such longing, then dove over the side. It was too dark to see, but I'm sure the silt swallowed her."*

So her **fate is printed** — killed under the Caller's compulsion, drawn to Giustenal, into the silt. Whether her psyche was **absorbed into the Caller** is nowhere stated, and the book does not connect her to its composite. **That half stays silent** and the table decides.

**Proposed change:** add `Logi` as the first mate by name (and `Kas` as a named crewman) with her printed fate; keep the absorption question as the only open part, reworded so it no longer implies her name and fate are unknown.

## 3. "Whether Passk survives Giustenal or becomes a banshee if his focus fails."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT** — but the printed terms are recordable and currently absent.

> *"Do you know what a focus is? Well, mine's a mekillot's load and a half… That night I vowed to return some day to destroy the Caller, whatever it is… and I drink to keep my focus from driving me to suicide. 'Cause that's what going to Giustenal is — suicide."*

His focus is **destroying the Caller in Darkness**, and **the drinking is what holds it off**. That is a live mechanism, not colour: sober him up or take him toward Giustenal and the focus drives him.

**Proposed change:** record the focus and the drink-as-suppressant as confirmed; keep the outcome open as play-dependent rather than "unresolved".

## 4. "Whether Passk's damaged Silt Slicer is repaired or used remains unresolved."

**PLAY-DEPENDENT, with printed terms the record does not carry.**

> *"Passk hints that he has a map of the walls of Giustenal and a beached silt skimmer that can be repaired. He won't sell the map or the skimmer. He'll only give them to the group in exchange for taking him on the voyage."*

And the map's content, from Cromlin rumour: *"There was a dwarf pirate named Passk who claimed to have a map of Giustenal's walls. Said he used to walk into the ruins from the north to avoid the tar pits."*

Three printed facts the record lacks: the skimmer **is repairable**, **neither map nor skimmer is for sale at any price**, and the map's value is a **northern approach that avoids the tar pits**.

**Proposed change:** record the terms and the northern approach; the outcome stays play-dependent. The northern approach also belongs on `location-giustenal-ruins`, which already carries a tar-pit approach.
