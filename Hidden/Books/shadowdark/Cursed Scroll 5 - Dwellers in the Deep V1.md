Overview
ROOM KEY
The keyworded descriptions at the start of each location are safe to
share with the players. The bulleted details beneath are for the GM.
BACKGROUND
Nobody knows where the grinning Librarians of Leng came from.
These creatures might be demons, or they might even be living
manifestations of the Shadowdark's madness. They have dwelt in
the Library since time long forgotten, deep in the darkness, never to
be seen by mankind. There, they catalogue the nightmares, terrors,
and fears that flow through this dimension. The Librarians of Leng
know a crucial fact about every being who has walked this world:
what they fear most. To some, there is no greater treasure.
FACTIONS
Librarians of Leng. Masters of the Library. Keepers of the deep lore.
These blindfolded beings stalk in silk robes through ancient halls.
Captive deep ones, trolls, and gargoyles serve them. Horrid grins
twist their faces; they can make no other expression than painful joy.
Prisoners. Trespassers, deep delvers, and wizards who teleported to
the wrong place. The Librarians lock up any intruders so their fears
can be provoked and studied. Prisoners who still cling to sanity are
united in their desire to escape, setting all else aside.
Books. Some sentient books do not want to be housed in the
Library of Leng, so they seek a way out. Many are self-serving and
will only keep alliances for as long as they are convenient. Others are
noble and wish their knowledge to be returned to the world.

THE LIBRARY OF LENG
The Library of Leng is a castle of green-black rock that stretches
into windowless, pointed spires as if shaped from clay. High-relief
carvings of colossal figures spring out of its walls, each as tall as
the castle itself. They depict tentacled and winged horrors locked
in mortal combat with thrashing dragons. The entire structure sits
heavily on a pillar of stone surrounded by a 200'-deep ravine. A thin,
arching bridge dripping with strands of fungi crosses this expanse;
it deposits visitors at a tunnel mouth leading into the castle's base.
ORDER OF BATTLE
Intelligent creatures (excluding sentient books) in adjacent rooms
investigate unusual sounds, arriving in 1d4 rounds. A Librarian of
Leng whose morale breaks retreats toward Area 52 to release the
bezelak. If it succeeds, all random encounters have a 3:6 chance of
being with the bezelak for the next day. During this time, checks
to sneak and hide are DC 18 and encounters with the Librarians of
Leng or their servants are always hostile.
ENTRANCES AND EXITS
Area 1. A bridge crosses over the ravine and into a tunnel mouth at
the base of the Library. This tunnel leads to Area 1.
Area 12. An overflow grate in the cistern (Area 12) covers a chute
that exits the pillar of rock 60 feet below the Library. Crusted lichen
and white, chalky minerals surround the mouth of it. PCs can climb
between it and the bridge to Area 1 with two DC 15 STR/DEX checks.
Area 15. A wagon-sized carving of a beast with 10 snaking eyestalks
and a crescent moon maw (The Ten-Eyed Oracle) lurks at the rear
side of the Library above a narrow precipice of rock. It seems out of
proportion to the rest of the gigantic figures surrounding it. A secret
door built into this beast's scarred central eye opens to Area 15.
