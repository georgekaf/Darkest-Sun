BACKGROUND
The mightiest king of the sea wolves, Skorgald, lies entombed in the
sea caves, carried to Valhalla with the hoard he won in life. It's said
his rotting head rests on piles of gold, and his hands still clutch the
bejeweled crosses and crowns he reaped from those he conquered.
King Skorgald will live on in legend amongst your people. You
won't... yet! Raiding is the right of the strong, and so you go to the
sea caves to seize your fortune and make your name. If old king
Skorgald is still worthy of his hoard, then let him rise up to defend it!
GAUNTLETS
Gauntlets are deadly affairs where life is fragile and glory is hard-
won. Players begin with four 0-level characters each, playing one at
a time and replacing from their pool if their current character dies.
Remember the following:
• 0-level characters can wield all gear. Beginner's luck!
• 0-level characters die when they reach 0 HP.
• Characters can (and should) loot their fallen companions' bodies.
• Don't worry about tracking XP. 0-level characters who survive the
sea caves with respectable treasure in tow graduate to 1st level.
See Shadowdark RPG pg. 116 for further Gauntlet details.

Cracked bones and crab shells litter the sparkling shore of this cave.

and flapping echoes from the north (stingbats in Area 5).

A wash of colorful mushrooms creates a bumpy carpet over the

▶ 2. Morgamotta. Flat and purple top with a thin sheen. Edible.

everything to scratch feverishly for 1d4 rounds.
▶ 4. Ungolar. Rich black with dusty pollen that leaves inky stains.

Woven nests of dry, black seaweed line the walls. Puddles of water

A broad cave slopes gently down toward a sloshing, midnight-blue

Pitted, wooden tables stretch the length of this spacious hall. A
bedraggled man sits alone at a seat, eating merrily from one of the

24. SEA WOLF TOMB
A stone coffin lies in each alcove. The lids are carved with life-sized
warriors in helms and chainmail, arms crossed over their chests.

Four carved pillars stand in a broad hall. The distant slosh of water

scratched out and the word "betrayers" gouged above it.

A stone coffin rests in each alcove. A likeness of a man is carved into
