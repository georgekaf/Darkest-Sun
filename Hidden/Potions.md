# Potions, Fruits & Oils of Athas

Compiled from all book sources. On Athas, potions are drawn from magic fruits (OSE GMG p.68). Oils are topical preparations.

---

## Magic Fruits (OSE GMG p.69)

| Fruit | Effect | Duration | Notes |
|-------|--------|----------|-------|
| **Fruit of Mind Bar** | Grants psionic Mind Bar power — no psionics needed | (standard) | Blocks mental attack |
| **Fruit of Psionic Restoration** | Restores +1d6 psionic power slots | 12 hrs | Eating more within 12 hrs: -1d4 slots instead |
| **Fruit of Psionic Boost** | Next 1d4 powers cast 1 rank higher | 12 hrs | Multiple no extra effect |
| **Fruit of Silt Breathing** | Breathe freely in silt | 2d6+2 turns | |

## Oils (OSE GMG p.69)

| Oil | Effect | Duration |
|-----|--------|----------|
| **Oil of Armor** | +3 AC, +2d4 HP | 24 hrs |
| **Oil of Masking** | Hidden from smell, hearing, infravision, psionic detection. Visible only to normal sight | 24 hrs |

## Crafting

| Profession | Capability |
|------------|-----------|
| Alchemist | Recreate potions at double normal time, half cost |
| Bard (Poison Master) | Create potent poisons from plant extracts and creature venom |
| Defiler/Preserver | Create magic items using magic fruit juices as base |
