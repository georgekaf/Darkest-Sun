# Temple of the Dragon

**What:** The religious heart of [[New Giustenal]]. Three magnificent structures where [[Mon-Adderath]] and [[Absalom]] deliver sermons to [[Dregoth]]'s dray subjects.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 82

---

Three structures of gray stone with crimson scales. Dragon's maw doorways with toothlike portcullises. The largest temple, near the [[Dread Palace]], is for wealthy dray. The complex includes training halls, dining rooms, sanctuaries, and barracks for templars.

## Inhabitants

- [[Mon-Adderath]] (High Templar, delivers sermons)
- [[Absalom]] (High Priest, delivers sermons)
- 338 templars
- Wealthy second-generation [[Dray]]
