# Break Shore

**What:** Destination on the far side of the [[Silt Shoals]] from [[Cromlin]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 15

---

The far end of the Silt Shoals route. Mentioned as a trade destination. The route to [[House M'ke Village]] branches off from here, known only to a few navigators.
