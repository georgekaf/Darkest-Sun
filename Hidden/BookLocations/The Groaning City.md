# The Groaning City

**What:** Subterranean city beneath [[Giustenal]]. First built by Blue Age halflings, used by Green Age psionicists, then demihuman refugees. [[Dregoth]]'s army slaughtered the inhabitants.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 70-73

---

A large natural cavern just beneath Giustenal. Buildings of grown stone (Blue Age) mixed with traditional construction (Green Age). Lion and tree motifs added by demihuman followers of [[Taraskir the Lion]]. The haunting voices of the dead still echo through the empty streets — hence the name.

## Locations

**1. Shattered Gates** — Two massive stone gates at the northern tunnel entrance. Date from Dregoth's time.

**2. Avenue of the Hanged** — Titanic trees line the central street (now blackened stalks). 50+ near-mummified dwarf corpses in yellow robes with lion emblems — these are "cursed dead." They moan and attack living beings within 50 ft. Save vs death magic or suffer -2 penalties to all rolls.

**3. Ruined Pool** — Stone water basin. Dry silt now.

**4. Waterfall (Lion's Head)** — Lion head built into the eastern wall over a natural waterfall. Water once flowed from the mouth through an aqueduct. Now dry.

**5. Fields** — Fungi and subterranean plants grown since the Blue Age.

**6. Destroyed Buildings** — Mix of grown stone and traditional. Heavily fire-damaged by Dregoth's soldiers.

**7. Order of the Claw Compound** — Iron fence with twisted iron. Plaque: "Order of the Claw." Warriors dedicated to Taraskir the Lion God. All died defending the refuge.

**8. Protected Building** — A protector wight (unique) guards the last treasures of the Order: Taraskir's golden claw gauntlet (100 gp, too large for any but a giant's hand) and the holy book of Taraskir the Lion God.

**9. Merchant District** — Ruined storefronts. Evidence of recent pillaging (from [[Kragmorta]] raiders).

**10. Overlook Estates** — Choice homes on a hill built into the eastern wall. Ceramic ducts for water. Type R treasure (3 hrs searching). Some homes inhabited by cursed dead (50% chance).

**11. Hall of the Lion** — Assembly/meeting hall. Stout stonework badly damaged. Defenders killed or hanged. A planar gate was once kept here — [[Dregoth]] later moved it to [[New Giustenal]].

**12. Walled Compound** — Last refuge, designed for defense. Unused (the attack came too fast). Looted by Kragmorta explorers.

**13. Closed Gate** — Leads to Tunnel D. The path the demihumans used to enter. Dregoth came through the [[Blasted Spire]] side. The gate is still intact.

## Inhabitants

- Cursed dead dwarves (Avenue of the Hanged)
- Protector wight (Area 8)
