# Giustenal — The Ruined City by the Silt Sea

**What:** Ruined capital of [[Dregoth]], dead sorcerer-king. Haunted city on the edge of the Silt Sea.

**Page References:** Campaign Book Chapter 4 (pp. 39-57), also pp. 5-9, 28-33

---

Once the gleaming capital of Dregoth's domain, Giustenal fell 2,000 years ago when seven sorcerer-kings assassinated Dregoth and rained destruction upon the city. Now it is a corpse-city — walls cracked, streets buried under silt, haunted by the [[Caller in Darkness]].

The city sits on the shore of the [[Sea of Silt]]. Its walls are 40–50 ft high, patrolled only by ghosts and silt serpents. The [[Tar Pits of Giustenal]] smolder before the southern gate. The [[Silt Road]] snakes in from the north over buried rooftops.
