# Sky Singers' Market

**What:** Semi-permanent elf market in [[Hidden/BookLocations/Nibenay]]'s Hill District, run by Chief [[NPCs/Muuton]]'s tribe.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 5, 10-11

---

The Sky Singers are an elf tribe that maintains a semi-permanent market in Nibenay's Hill District. They trade exotic Athasian goods, information, and sometimes serve as guides to [[Giustenal]].

## Inhabitants

- [[NPCs/Muuton]] (chief, unaware of [[Guvaano Twilightcatcher]]'s Veiled Alliance ties)
- [[Guvaano Twilightcatcher]] (elder preserver)
- Sky Singers tribe members
