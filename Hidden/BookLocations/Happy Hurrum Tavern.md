# Happy Hurrum Tavern

**What:** Tavern in [[Hidden/BookLocations/Nibenay]]'s Hill District where [[NPCs/Jessareen]] stays.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 5

---

A modest tavern in Nibenay's Hill District. Jessareen the elf bard is a regular here, singing strange, haunting songs about [[Slinnasia]] and the visions she received from [[Kataal the Mover]]. Good source of local rumors about [[Giustenal]].

## Inhabitants

- [[NPCs/Jessareen]] (bard, regular patron)
- Various Nibenay locals and travelers
