# Nibenay — The Shadow City

**What:** City-state ruled by [[Hidden/BookLocations/Nibenay]], the Shadow King. Primary staging point for expeditions to Giustenal.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 5, 9-12

---

Nibenay is the city-state closest to Giustenal and the last major settlement before the wastes. Its Hill District hosts the semi-permanent markets of the [[Sky Singers]] elf tribe. [[NPCs/Jessareen]] can be found at the [[Happy Hurrum Tavern]], singing strange songs and spreading news.

## Sub-Locations

**Hill District** — Where the Sky Singers' Market operates. Tents and temporary structures. Adventurers outfit here before heading to [[Cromlin]] or [[Giustenal]].

**Sky Singers' Market** — Semi-permanent elf market run by Chief [[NPCs/Muuton]]'s tribe. Exotic Athasian goods.

**Happy Hurrum Tavern** — [[NPCs/Jessareen]]'s usual haunt. A bard known for her melancholy songs about [[Slinnasia]].
