# Giustenal — Maps

Every map on hand for Giustenal, matched to the location page it serves. Sources are
fan cartography from athas.org, **not** the printed *City by the Silt Sea* poster maps —
where they disagree with the book, the book wins.

Image files live in `Giustenal Maps/` beside this page. Originals stay in
`athas.org/`, each with its own `.md` holding a full description and an image-generation
prompt for reproducing or extending it.

## The Giustenal City — surface overview

**Location:** [[Giustenal]] · [[Giustenal Districts]]
**GM reference** for the surface ruins.

![[giustenal-maps-v0-sejb3dxfy85c1.webp]]

Organic sprawl rather than a planned grid: dense orange building clusters separated by
dark gaps of rubble and open ground. A regular field of small black dots covers a large
central area — read as ruined columns or a buried structure. Blank parchment is silt.

## New Giustenal — the living city below

**Location:** [[New Giustenal]] · [[Dread Palace]] · [[College of Blackspire]] · [[Common Section]] · [[Temple of the Dragon]]

![[giustenal-maps-v0-g0vibexfy85c1.webp]]

City-scale map of Dregoth's hidden city. A channel runs north–south through the centre;
districts sit either side, with plazas, a circular central structure and outlying
clusters. Red dots mark key sites.

**Cross-check before use:** the campaign book places the **Dread Palace in the northern
section**, walls of blachest obsidian faced with wyrm bone, and describes the **great
central pillar** running floor to ceiling with common housing built around it. Confirm
those two features on the map before handing it to anyone.

## Kragmorta — the fiery city of the first generation dray

**Location:** [[Kragmorta]]
*(Filed on athas.org under the name "Keramitra"; identified as Kragmorta by the GM.)*

![[giustenal-maps-v0-vfy9kdxfy85c1.webp]]

A flowing mass of deep crimson dominates the centre — the rivers of magma that replaced
the cavern's cold, clear water when the upheavals of the Cleansing Wars set the world's
heart loose. Ochre ruins cling to the margins, matching the book's buildings *"grown from
the very stone of the cavern floor"*. One small blue-green pool sits in a corner.

**Cross-check when running it:** the campaign book puts **rope bridges of wall-walker
silk at four crossings** over the lava — cut by the dray if the city is threatened — plus
the shattered stone gate at the Tunnel E entrance, the dray settlement, the wall-walker
hive, the fire-snake nest, the hell hound lair, the building taken over by Bracorr the
fire giant shaman, and the dark naga's island. Check which of those the map actually shows before using it as a tactical aid.
