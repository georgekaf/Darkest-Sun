# The Silt Road

**What:** Path over the buried northern walls and rooftops of [[Giustenal]], extending into the [[Sea of Silt]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 29-30

---

The northern approach to Giustenal. Walls and rooftops extend nearly a mile into the Silt Sea, covered in 1–5 ft of silt. Full of holes and broken patches — treacherous footing. The eastern wall route leads to [[Abdaleem's Island]]. [[Passk]] has a map of this area.

## Inhabitants

- Silt spawn
- Silt horrors
- Razorwings
