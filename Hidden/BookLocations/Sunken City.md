# Sunken City

**What:** A portion of [[Giustenal]] that sank during the cataclysm, sealed by [[Abalach-Re]] in a magical box of hardened sand.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 62-67

---

A cube of magically-hardened sand resting at a 30-degree angle on the Silt Sea floor. Accessed from stairs in the [[Blasted Spire]] krag lair. Buildings are cut in half by the box's edge. No fresh air (drowning rules apply). Bodies are eerily well-preserved.

The constant tilt means Dexterity checks are required for balance. Failure = slide 10 ft toward the lower (north) end.

## Locations

**1. Grand Meeting Hall (House Zobi Hall)** — Meeting hall of House Zobi (largest merchant dynasty of Giustenal, now extinct). Cut in half by the sand spell. Secret tunnel leads to old Giustenal buried in the Silt Sea.

**2. Grand Apartment** — Once occupied by a noble [[Dregoth]] favored. Under 10 ft of stagnant water. An undead noble (life-absorbing spirit) waits here for Dregoth's return. Secret passage to the [[Blasted Spire]].

**3. Stagnant Pool** — 10 ft deep black water. Helm of free action on a corpse 20 ft from shore.

**4. Bone Piles** — Trash from [[Abalach-Re]]'s pillaging. Dozens of corpses. An undead stalker/assassin animates from the northern pile. Type I treasure after 2 hours searching.

**5. [[The Citadel (Sunken City)|Citadel]]** — What Abalach-Re wanted to preserve. She looted most but left some. Draconic motifs throughout.

**6. [[Taraskir's Tomb]]** — Giant lion-headed statue temple. Sealed. "Here lies Taraskir the Lion." Enchanted chains, cursed armor, +2 dragon slayer sword.

**7. Store Fronts** — Map shop. Shells, dragon-stamped coins, obsidian spear, silverware. Engraved wall map of the region.

**8. Homes** — Poor shape. Type I treasure (magic items -10%).

**9. Courtyard** — Destroyed agafari-like giant trees.
