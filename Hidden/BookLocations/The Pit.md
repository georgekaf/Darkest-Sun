# The Pit

**What:** Popular tavern in [[New Giustenal]], known for its munge elixir.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 84-85 (Area 12)

---

Run by [[Freiha]] and her mate [[Gatlakk]]. Famous for munge elixir — a potent alcoholic drink made from fermented mushrooms. Highly profitable. Rival [[Casskka]] (owner of [[Beetle's Bite]] Tavern) has hired [[Lodgden]] to work undercover here and steal the recipe.

## Inhabitants

- [[Freiha]] (dray, co-owner)
- [[Gatlakk]] (dray, co-owner)
- [[Lodgden]] (dray thief, undercover spy)
