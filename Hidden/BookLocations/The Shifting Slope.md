# The Shifting Slope

**What:** Huge flake drift — over 1 mile long and 15+ ft high, located 5 miles SW of [[Giustenal]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 26

---

A massive natural formation of volcanic rock flakes. Impossible to climb without magical aid. [[Ghodan's Gap]] — a wagon-width passage — runs through it, used regularly by the Ghodan dwarven brothers.
