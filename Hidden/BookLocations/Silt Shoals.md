# Silt Shoals

**What:** Navigable route through the [[Sea of Silt]] between [[Cromlin]] and [[Break Shore]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 15

---

A winding path through the Sea of Silt. Navigators keep routes secret to prevent piracy. The route to the [[House M'ke Village]] is known only to a brave few. Silt pirates under [[Curlonus Skaff]] ambush vessels here.
