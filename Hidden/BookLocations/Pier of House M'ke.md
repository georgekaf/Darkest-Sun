# Pier of House M'ke

**What:** House M'ke pier and silt skimmer construction yard in [[Cromlin]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 18

---

House M'ke operates a pier and skimmer construction facility in Cromlin, managed by [[Garreth Brodden]]. They use slave-crafters to build and maintain silt skimmers from [[Crescent Forest]] wood or giant beetle shells.
