# The Tunnels Beneath Giustenal

**What:** Ancient psionic highway network built by Blue Age halflings, later modified by Green Age psionicists and [[Dregoth]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 58-62

---

40-ft wide tubes of solid, smooth rock. Spherical indentations every 500 ft once held obsidian orbs containing psionic psyches. Some orbs still function — providing light, fresh air, levitation, and teleportation. The tunnels connect upper [[Giustenal]] to the [[Sunken City]], [[The Groaning City]], [[Kragmorta]], and [[New Giustenal]].

## Tunnels

**Tunnel A** — Quarter-mile long, collapsed at the end (the city sank). One intact psionic orb — levitates travelers, then drops them after a few minutes.

**Tunnel B** — Guarded by [[Kataal the Mover]] (halfling psyche in a psionic item). Once led to island settlements (Vakura, Pelcunal, Liuss). Kataal offers transport. If refused, floods visitors with visions of Athas's history (as he did to [[NPCs/Jessareen]]). Leads to collapsed section.

**Tunnel C** — Once connected the [[Groaning City]] to other subterranean cities. Vertical tunnels go up to the surface and down to Kragmorta/New Giustenal. All orb receptacles are empty ([[Dregoth]] moved them).

**Tunnel D** — Vertical drop from a cave connected to [[Dregoth's Tower]]. Descends ~1 mile before leveling off into the Groaning City cavern. Orbs smashed by demihumans. Spikes pounded into the stone for climbing.

**Tunnel E** — Leads to [[Kragmorta]] cavern. No spherical receptacles. A lava river (25 ft wide, 6 ft deep) crosses the tunnel ~0.25 mile before the cavern. Beyond is Kragmorta.

**Tunnel F** — Descends to the lowest level ([[New Giustenal]]). Cruder receptacles with orbs stolen from Tunnel C. [[Primik the Mover]] guards this tunnel — transports only those with Dregoth's holy symbol. Alerts guards if unwelcome visitors enter.

**Tunnel G** — Leads to [[New Giustenal]]. Illuminated by continual light stones. Four 4th-level dray templar guards stationed here (duty is lax).

## Inhabitants

- [[Kataal the Mover]] (Tunnel B)
- Primik the Mover (Tunnel F)
- Dray templar guards (Tunnel G)
