# Dregoth's Tower

**AKA:** The Black Tower

**What:** Obsidian tower rising from [[Dregoth's Palace]], hanging over the [[Sea of Silt]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 55-57

---

Made of the blackest obsidian, pitted and rough. A bleached skeleton — Dregoth's High Priest — is staked to the tower's exterior by iron spikes, a dragon medallion around its neck.

## Floors

**3rd Floor — Dregoth's Chamber** — Small glass windows shattered. Furniture collapsed. Books rotted. Balcony provides an excellent view of the Silt Sea.

**2nd Floor** — Almost barren. Large windows. Turquoise dragon inlay in the floor (10 gp loose, 40 gp if reconstructed). Staircase is damaged (25% chance of falling).

**1st Floor — Laboratory** — Looted and burned. Glass and metal machines, broken vials, ash. Three stone-barred holding cells. A venger occupies one cell — a failed transformation experiment.

## Inhabitants

- Venger (in the laboratory cell)
- High Priest's skeleton (exterior, staked)
