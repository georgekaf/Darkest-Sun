# Fanner Fields

**What:** Scrubby agricultural fields in [[New Giustenal]] for raising fanners.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 84 (Area 6)

---

Scrubby fields where fanners are raised. Fanners are fat, single-horned, collared beasts brought from another plane by [[Dregoth]]. They serve as a meat source for the [[Dray]] population.
