# Ghodan's Gap

**What:** A wagon-width passage through [[The Shifting Slope]] flake drift.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 26

---

An opening just wide enough for a wagon to pass through the Shifting Slope. Named after the Ghodan dwarven brothers ([[Durex Ghodan]] and [[Haltham Ghodan]]) who use it regularly to reach their tar mine and [[Cromlin]].
