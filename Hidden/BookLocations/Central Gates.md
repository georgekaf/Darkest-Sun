# Central Gates

**What:** The fortified entrance to [[New Giustenal]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 81-82 (Area 1)

---

Two towers staggered behind two massive gates of stone and dragon bones. Each tower houses 4 4th-level templars. Five bone golems stand guard at the inner gates.

## Inhabitants

- 8 dray templars (4th level)
- 5 bone golems
