# Giustenal Environs

**What:** The wasteland region surrounding the ruined city of [[Giustenal]].

**Page References:** Campaign Book Chapter 2 (pp. 10-27), Terrain Tables pp. 24-27

---

Stony barrens, sandy wastes, and volcanic terrain surround Giustenal. Littered with deadly natural hazards and monster lairs. The area is patrolled by the [[Sky Singers]], the [[Silt Stalkers]], [[Tenpug]]'s Band, and [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]].

## Terrain Features (Random Terrain Table, pp. 24-27)

| Feature | Description | Hazard |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Blackwash | Tar pit covered by silt dust crust | Collapse, drowning in tar |
| Grotto | Deep/shallow crevices, miles long | Creature lairs (30% occupied) |
| Jagged Plains | Sharp mica/quartz particles, dark red | 1d4 damage/turn |
| Magma Forest | Columns of smooth rock (up to 15 ft) | Climbing hazards |
| Flake Drift | Piles of volcanic rock flakes | Impossible to climb without magic |
| Tar Pit | Bubbling molten tar pits | 3d8 damage/round, pit snatchers |
| Wind Path | Cleared path from constant wind | None (safe passage) |

## Named Features

- **[[The Shifting Slope]]** — Huge flake drift, 1+ mile long, 5 mi SW of Giustenal
- **[[Ghodan's Gap]]** — Wagon-width passage through the Shifting Slope
- **[[Ghodan's Tar Mine]]** — Dwarf-run tar mine, 2 mi south of [[Cromlin]]
