# New Giustenal

**AKA:** Kragmorta (Dragon Bone City)

**What:** [[Dregoth]]'s underground capital, the lowest level beneath [[Giustenal]]. A city built from dragon bones brought from other planes.

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 81-86

---

Built from dragon bones in a cavern ~2 miles below the surface. Lighted by continual light spells on stalactites. Two rivers flow through: the Blackjaw and Little Blackjaw. Population: ~3,000 second-generation [[Dray]]. No slaves exist here — the dray consider themselves a master race.
