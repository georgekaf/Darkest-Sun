# Silt Piers

**What:** Docking structures in [[Cromlin]] for silt skimmers.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 16

---

A series of 20-ft tall piers constructed from [[Crescent Forest]] timbers. Private owners charge 2 sp/day for docking. 1–6 silt skimmers are typically docked here at any time.
