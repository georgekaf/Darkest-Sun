# Akrag's Pools

**AKA:** Public Baths

**What:** Public baths in [[New Giustenal]], owned by "Akrag" — secretly [[Absalom]] in disguise.

**Page References:** Campaign Book p. 84 (Area 11)

---

Dark and seedy public baths. A known location for assassinations — murdered templar bodies are pushed through the drains. The owner, Akrag, is actually [[Absalom]] (Dregoth's High Priest, first [[Dray]]) in disguise.

## Inhabitants

- [[Absalom]] (as "Akrag," owner)
