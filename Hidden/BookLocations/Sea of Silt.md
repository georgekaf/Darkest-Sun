# Sea of Silt

**What:** Vast inland sea of powdery gray silt. Once a true sea of water, now a choking dust-choked wasteland.

**Page References:** Throughout the campaign book

---

[[Giustenal]] sits on the edge of the Sea of Silt. The silt is powdery and gray, impossible to swim in. The [[Silt Shoals]] are the only navigable routes. The [[Silt Road]] crosses a northern arm. Silt spawn, silt horrors, razorwings, and silt serpents haunt its shores.
