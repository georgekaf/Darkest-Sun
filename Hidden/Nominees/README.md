### Open decisions blocking Black Spine canon

These recur across multiple files and should be ruled on before submission:

1. **Danya.** Day 104 correction moved her to Kharanok as an Order psionicist. This vacates the band's Keeper of Supplies post and orphans Arcus's rescue. See [[Danya]], [[Alshal]], [[Arcus]].
2. **Durdon vs `actor-orruk-vesh`.** Same narrative slot — wounded dwarf mine survivor with tunnel knowledge. Merge, or keep both. See [[Durdon]].
3. **Zigath's appearance.** Printed "pudgy, gaudy" vs sandbox "tall, scarred elder." Sandbox `sourceScope` says appearance is project-authored, so it wins — recorded, not silently applied. See [[Zigath]].
4. **Tent-8 debate.** Danya's relocation leaves Jolon arguing against an empty chair. [[Isak]] is the proposed promotion.
5. **Naming rulings** for the three gith chieftains and the Hejkin preserver — all unnamed in the source. Ids are role-based so renaming is cheap.
6. **Missing faction records:** the Order (Danya), the Square of Gurdek (seven members, doctrine, base), the Hejkin clan.
7. **Tenpug's temple geography.** `location-tenpugs-temple` and `location-lion-temple` both exist; sandbox cites both City by the Silt Sea (Giustenal) and Black Spine (~50 miles from Nibenay).
8. **`actor-krika` vs existing `actor-kirka`.** The githzerai spy and the missing Klarbu villager are one letter apart, both female, both underground. Rename, or accept. See [[Krika]].
9. **Zigath's chain of command.** Three senior gith psionicists — the [[Chieftain of the Rune]], [[Askai]], [[Zigath]] — in one mountain, with no stated hierarchy anywhere in the source.
10. **[[Nyzethgyr]].** Named once, in one line of overheard dialogue, never explained. Five candidate identities laid out; **pick one before running Adventure Six.**
11. **"The Blind Dark."** Two printed references in Yathazor, one explicit refusal to explain. See [[Nlakni]] and [[The Lich-Queen]].
12. **Is Gith the lich-queen?** Never stated either way. See [[Gith]].
13. **Defiling with no vegetation.** [[Gremlish]] is a defiler in a steel Astral prison. Needs a ruling before he is run.
14. **Yrinolir** — sentient scepter with an Ego and arguably authorship of the whole plot. Probably wants an `artifact-` record rather than living inside [[Queen Trinth]]'s file.

### Open decisions blocking City by the Silt Sea canon

1. **Does [[Absalom]] know the [[The Spirit of Kragmorta|Spirit of Kragmorta]] is [[Dregoth]]?** Two completely different characters follow. **Decide before Chapter Six.**
2. **Do Dregoth and [[The Caller in Darkness]] know about each other?** His murder created it; it lives directly above his city and eats psionicists. The set never says.
3. **Is the lion-headed god of the Groaning City [[Taraskir the Lion]]?** Strongly implied, never stated — and it makes the Spirit's disguise a deliberate insult.
4. **Is [[Curlonus Skaff]] redeemable?** The module assumes a fight. She is a deceived cleric with an amulet that proves it.
5. **Is [[Lodgden]] stalling on purpose?** A level 8 rogue, embedded for months, with *"little success."*
6. **Does [[Gatlakk]] know the munge recipe?** Decides whether he is a target or a hostage.
7. **Who is the disguised Nibenay templar** who tried to buy Dregoth's holy symbol from [[Kria]]? Raised once and dropped.
8. **Adopt the partial-transfer house rule for [[Kataal the Mover]]?** The printed version costs a PC outright.
9. **Is the "flood the basin" option live?** [[Abdaleem]] knows a water cleric could destroy this stretch of the Silt Sea. It is a bloodless route to ending Dregoth.
10. **Adopt the "undeceive it" resolution for the Caller?** It kills because it mistakes release for death; no printed way to end it exists.
11. **Location merge:** `location-lion-temple` and `location-tenpugs-temple` appear to be the same site near Giustenal. Resolved in [[Taraskir the Lion]]; needs sign-off. *(Same as Black Spine open decision 7.)*
12. **Missing boxed-set components.** The MONSTROUS COMPENDIUM appendix and cardstock cards (#1 planar gate, #4 Mon-Adderath, Absalom, the Caller) are **not in the vault** — several stat blocks are constructed and should be replaced if found.
13. **Printed inconsistencies to rule on:** munge is *"a strong elixir"* and *"a sweet tasting drink"*; the pirate defiler is both **Moran** and **Moraz**; **Akrag** is at location 11 but cited as location 14; the High Templar is spelled **Mon-Adderath / Mon Adderath / Man Adderath**.

## Workflow rules for actor files

- Always use the canonical actor Markdown template (frontmatter + full section list) — never improvise structure. Check an existing nominee file for the current shape if unsure.
- Appearance prompts (Banana Pro / image-gen): rewrite as natural prose under `### Appearance`; keep the raw prompt verbatim under its own `## Token art prompt (Banana Pro / image-gen reference):` heading, as a blockquote, placed after `## Stat Block or Rules Notes`.
- GM-written intro/sample-dialogue scenes: keep verbatim as a blockquote under `### Manner and Voice`, labeled "Sample dialogue / introduction scene (as written by the GM):".
- Only one `## Stat Block or Rules Notes` header per file — check before inserting, don't create a duplicate.
- After adding either block, re-check the file still matches the canonical template section order.
