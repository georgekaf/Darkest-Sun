# Artifact: Yrinolir

*(New artifact. Proposed id `artifact-yrinolir`. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 13 August 2026 against `artifacts/` on the default branch. Filed as an **artifact, not an actor**, on the campaign owner's ruling of 13 August 2026: the scepter is an object that is owned, and everything that would have been actor fields — its Ego, its agenda, its influence over its wielder — is carried in the notes below. See issue #354, where Yrinolir is one of the unadopted candidate identities for Nyzethgyr.)*

## Summary
Queen Trinth's scepter: a rune-covered platinum shaft topped with the crystallized, still-beating heart of a pit fiend. It is the source of her power, the reason Galnaki is sealed inside a sphere of force, and — the module hedges — possibly the author of her entire plan to conquer Athas.

## Classification
- Artifact type: scepter / relic of the rifts
- Origin: **forged in the rifts between dimensions**
- Current holder: Queen Trinth — `actor-queen-trinth`, existing sandbox record
- Current location: Galnaki, the githyanki fortress-city on the Astral Plane
- Status: intact and in use

## Physical Description
A thick platinum shaft covered in runes, with a great ruby set atop it. The ruby is **the crystallized heart of the pit fiend Nalcariz**, and it still beats with a dusky light.

## Powers and Effects
- **Sealed off a region of the Astral Plane.** With it, Trinth enclosed Galnaki and the space around it in a bubble of force, cutting the city off from the rest of the plane. **If the scepter is lost, that barrier falls.**
- **Holds the vortex to Athas open.** Killing Trinth is explicitly not enough — while the artifact exists, the gateway to Athas remains.
- **Charm on touch.** Trinth touches kneeling supplicants with it "to receive her blessing"; a failed save means the victim throws down his weapon and pledges to serve her for life.
- **Bound to its holder.** *No one can command the scepter while the queen lives* — she is linked too closely to it. Anyone merely holding it can still be charmed by her.
- **Punishes the good.** Anyone grasping the scepter takes **1d4 damage per round unless of evil alignment**.
- It can be pulled from Trinth's hands by any character scoring a hit against AC 4.

## Destruction
- **Only unmade where it was made** — in the rifts between dimensions. The portal linking Galnaki to Athas, or the one linking the githzerai base to Limbo, both qualify.
- The method: **a great magical attack directed at the scepter as it passes through such a gate.**
- The githzerai discovered this only days before the party arrives; Krika grasps its importance immediately, because losing the scepter drops Trinth's barrier and lets a githzerai army plane shift across and attack.

## The actor-side material *(recorded here rather than as a second record)*
Filed on the owner's ruling: Yrinolir behaves like a participant, and that is documented on the object.

- **It has an agenda, or appears to.** The module's own hedge on Trinth's plan to conquer Athas: *"Whether this was her doing, the item's doing, or a combination of the two is impossible to say."*
- **It corrupts its wielder.** Trinth's intention to eventually overthrow the githyanki lich-queen is obscene to almost all of her followers — *"It is only the corrupting influence of Yrinolir that enabled Trinth to think of such a thing."*
- **It rewards study.** Trinth did not know its full power when she found it; months of careful research revealed what she needed, and everything she has built since followed from that understanding.
- **It selects for evil.** The damage clause means a good-aligned holder is being actively rejected while an evil one is not.
- **Ego, in Shadowdark terms:** treat the scepter as having its own will where it matters — whose hand it tolerates, and whether Trinth's ambitions are hers. Mechanics deliberately left unfixed pending play.

## Relationships

- `actor-nyzethgyr` — **unadopted possibility.** Issue #354 lists Yrinolir as candidate 5 for the "someone or something" displeased with the mine's progress. **Ruled 13 August 2026 to stay ambiguous**; recorded here as a link, not an identification.
- `actor-raskon` — the general building the Nightmare Gate that the scepter keeps open.
- Nalcariz, the pit fiend whose crystallized heart is the ruby. No record; not proposed as one.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The artifact is the campaign's real objective.** The climax is not slaying Trinth; it is getting the scepter into a dimensional rift and destroying it there, which simultaneously drops the barrier, closes the gateway to Athas and opens Galnaki to a githzerai assault.
- **The grab is available earlier than players expect.** During the "blessing" scene, the scepter can be taken off Trinth on a hit against AC 4 — and the module notes the party will never get a better opportunity. Anyone who takes it cannot use it while she lives, and can be charmed while holding it.
- If Yrinolir is ever adopted as Nyzethgyr, the scepter becomes the mover behind the whole Black Spine operation rather than Trinth's tool. That remains open.
