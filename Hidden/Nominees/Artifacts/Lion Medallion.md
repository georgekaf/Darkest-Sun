## Summary
A lion's-head medallion of almost fire-red gemstone. It throws fireball, which is the least important thing about it: it is the object that keeps the moonbeast bound beneath Kharanok, and the price that entity has named for showing the party its road.

## Status and Custody
- Status: **carried, undeclared** — in the possession of its holder and never spoken of
- Current location: Kharanok (`location-kharanok`), moving daily between the quarry and the mines
- Owner or bearer: **[[Dorak]]** — proposed `actor-dorak`
- Keyed to: **[[The Moonbeast]]** — proposed `actor-moonbeast`. The bond runs both ways: the object holds the entity, and the entity can feel the object's holder nearby.
- Last confirmed day: **117** — the moonbeast reports feeling the holder among the people in the mine that day. That is the only confirmation on record, and it is second-hand.

## Player-Safe Description
None. **No player character has seen it, and no NPC has described it.** The only thing the party witnessed was Dorak pocketing *something* the moment they walked in on him in the gate chamber on day 117 — they never asked what.

## Materials and Construction
- A **lion's head**, carved or cut in relief.
- Cut from a gemstone of an **almost fire-red** colour.
- Worn or carried against the person; small enough to palm into a pocket in one movement.

## Known Powers
- **Fireball.** In the hands of an illiterate old miner in a village whose defences are two guards and a broken gate, this alone makes him the most dangerous armed presence in Kharanok after Breck.
- **Binding.** It holds the moonbeast inside the caves beneath the village. Whether that is a property of the medallion itself or of an older working the medallion anchors is unestablished.

## Activation
Unknown. Nothing on record describes how the fireball is invoked, whether it is limited by uses or by day, or whether the binding requires the holder to do anything at all — his simply having it may be the whole mechanism.

## Costs, Risks, and Limitations
- **The binding is the risk.** Anything that separates medallion from holder — theft, death, a trade, a fireball thrown in anger and lost — puts the release condition in play. Nobody involved knows this except the entity.
- **It draws the moonbeast.** The creature is searching for the holder specifically. Dorak walks into those tunnels almost daily.
- **Old magic, not the medallion alone, keeps the entity in the caves.** How the two relate — whether the medallion *is* the old magic or merely its key — is undecided and matters enormously.
- **[[Breck]]'s Mind Seal does not protect Dorak.** The one man in Kharanok the moonbeast cannot perceive is the one who does not have it.

## Interested Actors and Factions
- **[[The Moonbeast]]** — wants it brought to it. It has already offered [[Relo]] a road in exchange.
- **[[Dorak]]** — holds it, and has never mentioned it to anyone.
- **[[Relo]]** — has been offered the trade and does not know what the object is or who has it. He is watching the wrong man.
- **[[Breck]]** — suspected by the party of holding it. Innocent. **Whether he knows what Dorak carries is unestablished**, and he has been shadowing Dorak for days.
- `faction-veiled-alliance-nibenay` — no knowledge on record, but an unregistered fireball-throwing relic in a village Breck wardens is squarely their business.

## Knowledge Partitions
- **Dorak:** knows he has it. Whether he knows what it binds is **an open GM question** — he has worked those tunnels for years while the entity has been reaching into minds down there since at least ad4.
- **The moonbeast:** knows exactly what it is and that its holder was in the mine on day 117. Does not have a name.
- **The party:** knows only that the creature wants "what binds it" brought to it, and believes the holder is Breck.
- **Breck:** unestablished.
- **Kharanok at large:** nothing.

## GM-Only Truth
- This medallion is the campaign's release switch. Everything the party is currently planning — the return trip to Giustenal, the bargain with the creature, the surveillance of Breck — runs past it without touching it.
- The party has already threatened its holder twice, failed to pay him the share they promised him, and left him alone in charge of their only gate. He has told them he wants to leave Kharanok.
- **Lion iconography recurs in this region** — the half-buried [[Temple of the Lion]] (`location-lion-temple`) near Kharanok is dedicated to a forgotten lion-creature; `artifact-taraskirs-lion-armor` exists; Tenpug's Temple is lined with lion-headed guard statues of Taraskir's Lion Guard. **Whether this medallion belongs to that tradition is undecided and is not asserted here.** It is the obvious first thread to pull if the object ever needs a history.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
- The object cannot be filed in the sandbox until `actor-dorak` exists — see **Dependencies**.
- If the party ever learns who holds it, the scene is not a fight. It is a negotiation with a frightened man who has already said what he wants: to get out, and to be paid.
- Fireball is a loud secret. The first time Dorak is genuinely cornered, keeping it hidden and staying alive become the same problem for him, and he will have to choose.

## Unresolved Questions
- **How did Dorak come by it?** A fireball relic in the hands of a man who cannot read is not explained by anything on record.
- **Does he know what it binds?**
- **Is the medallion the old magic, or merely its key?** The answer decides whether handing it over frees the moonbeast or merely moves the lock.
- Does it have limited uses, a command word, a cost?
- Does it belong to the Temple of the Lion tradition, or is the lion coincidence?
- What the object should be called in canon — "the Lion Medallion" is descriptive, not a name anyone in the world has used.
