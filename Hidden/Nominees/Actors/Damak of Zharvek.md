# Actor: Damak of Zharvek

*(**New actor.** No record exists. He has lived on `location-zharvek` as a feature string since revision 1 and now has a fate that needs somewhere to sit.)*

## The name, first, because it collides

**`actor-damak-kheth` is a different person.** That record is **Damak Kheth**, a human elder slave-overseer at Kheth's Field near Nibenay, tied to `plot-kheths-field-early-revolt`. This actor is a **dwarf** who oversaw **Zharvek**, on the Black Spine caravan road. The two share a first name and nothing else.

**Filed as `Damak of Zharvek`** by the campaign owner's ruling of 13 August 2026, so the slug does not collide and the bare name never has to be disambiguated in play. Proposed id: `actor-damak-of-zharvek`.

## One-Sentence Summary
The dwarf who ran Zharvek's roadside market, exiled from Nibenay for trying to free a slave, and taken alive when raiders emptied his village — now a prisoner at Cromlin, waiting to be handed to Dregoth.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A dwarf, exiled and weathered, who ran a small market town on a hard road. No further description was given in play.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)* The manner of a man who kept an unprotected village functioning on a raiding road by being useful to everyone who passed through it — and who was exiled from a city-state for the one time he chose a person over the arrangement.

### Public Reputation
In Nibenay, an exile who tried to free a slave. On the Black Spine road, the man you dealt with at Zharvek.

## Confirmed Facts
- **A dwarf exiled from Nibenay after trying to free a slave.** *(ep.11, in-world days 109–110.)*
- **He oversaw Zharvek** through days 109–110, when House Markon stayed there and bought five trained kanks at its animal market.
- **He is not among the seven survivors** who left Zharvek with the Altar of Dust party on day 116.
- **Taken alive on the night of day 114**, when **Zarron's pirates — the Silt Stalkers under Luubarra Fire Dagger** — emptied the settlement under cover of the gith raiding it was already enduring.
- **Imprisoned at Cromlin**, held for delivery to **Dregoth**.
- **The rest of Zharvek's people have already been delivered.** He and **Talek Vos** are the two still held.
