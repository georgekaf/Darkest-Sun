# Actor: Durex Ghodan

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-durex-ghodan`. **Paired with [[Haltham Ghodan]] — the source never distinguishes them. Shared material lives here; Haltham's file cross-references it.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
One of two dwarf brothers from Raam who saw a tar pit on a trading trip, recognised what it was worth, and built the only weatherproofing industry on the Silt Coast out of twenty slaves and a hole in the ground.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Proposed — the source gives none.)* A dwarf running an industrial operation in an open tar pit. Everything about him and everything he owns is tar-stained.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)* Enterprising, and the module says so twice — **"two enterprising dwarves"** who **quickly realised the potential** and moved on it inside a single trip. Neither brother is described further.

### Public Reputation
Half of the Ghodan brothers. Their gap through the Shifting Slope is named for them, which is the closest thing to fame on the Silt Coast.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Durex and Haltham Ghodan, brothers and free citizens from Raam.**
- **They first encountered a tar pit on a trip to the House Shom trading village of Cromlin.**
- **They quickly realised the potential of the tar business, and took building materials and 20 slaves — both crafters and labourers — to a large pit about two miles due south of Cromlin.**
- **Now they have a thriving enterprise.** **They use the tar to weatherproof a variety of useful and necessary items that they produce in the craft shops they set up next to the tar pit.**
- **The Sky Singers and other local traders provide bladders or leather** which the brothers' crafters fashion into **wineskins, crew-size water containers, sand-proofed armour, sails for silt skimmers, and anything else that needs to be sealed against the elements.**
- **Twice a month, a Ghodan caravan travels to Cromlin to sell their weatherproof wares to House Shom.**
- **These caravans are usually worth 11–20 (1d10+10) gold**, and **have become frequent targets of bandits, raiders, and even gith** — who **value the goods for their intrinsic value more than for what they could fetch at market.**
- **The caravan leaders try to vary their exact routes from time to time to throw off the raiders, but the trip is a short one and there is little room for deviation.**
- **For this reason the route is considered extremely dangerous, and guards will be well paid for escorting the trains through safely. Currently this pay is 5 sp per guard, and no more than six guards are ever hired for a single trip.**
- **Ghodan's Gap** is an opening in the **Shifting Slope**, a flake drift over a mile long and more than 15 feet high about five miles southwest of Giustenal. **The gap is named for the dwarf brothers who often use it**, and **is just wide enough to let a wagon pass.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The caravan escort is the cleanest low-level hook in Chapter Two.** Two miles, twice a month, **extremely dangerous**, 5 sp per guard, **no more than six guards.** It is a printed job with a printed rate and printed enemies — bandits, raiders, **and gith.**
- **The gith detail is the interesting one.** *Even gith* raid these caravans, and they **value the goods for their intrinsic value rather than market price** — meaning gith are taking waterskins and sealed armour because they *need* them. **In a campaign that also runs Black Spine, gith raiding for survival gear two miles from Cromlin is not background colour.**
- **Ghodan's Gap is a named route toward Giustenal** and the brothers use it regularly. **Anyone who wants to approach the ruins from the southwest needs that gap**, and the dwarves who use it most are for hire, talkative, and commercially motivated.
- **They make silt skimmer sails.** [[Passk]]'s beached skimmer needs repairing; [[Garreth Brodden]] runs the yard; **the Ghodans make the canvas.** Three separate NPCs are required to get one ship moving, which is a good small quest.
- *(Proposed.)* Twenty slaves, an isolated site, no walls, and a valuable product. **The works are one bad night from being taken by whoever wants them.**

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** run the caravan escort as the party's first paid job in the region. It is cheap, printed, and puts them on the dangerous road with a reason.
- Differentiate the brothers at the table — the source does not, and one line each fixes it permanently. See [[Haltham Ghodan]].
- The gith raiding for survival goods is a strong, quiet piece of foreshadowing if the campaign touches the Black Spine gith at all.
- The slaves are an unremarked moral fact in a module that otherwise notices such things. A party may notice.
