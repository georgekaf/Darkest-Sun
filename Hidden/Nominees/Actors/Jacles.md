The young templar-wife who ran the iron mine before it fell — a woman whose entire remaining presence in the world is a wrecked office, a torn-apart house, and a ledger of how many slaves died per measure of ore.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Proposed — the source gives none.)* Known only through her rooms. Her home was once well appointed: fine furniture, stylish rugs, fine curtains. Her office had polished, gleaming wood walls and well-built furniture.

All of it is now ruined. The furniture is wrecked, the rugs soiled, the curtains hanging in shreds. Interior walls have been punched through and torn apart by gith searching for hidden treasure. The office is defaced with crude graffiti and splashes of dried blood.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)* Unrecorded. What survives of her is administrative: she kept precise accounts of ore extracted and of slave mortality, in the same ledger, in the same units.

### Public Reputation
Within Nibenay, before the fall: the officer responsible for a strategically vital iron mine. Afterwards: presumably a name on a list of losses that [[Vakskra]] is working hard to keep off the record.

## Confirmed Facts
- **The last templar to hold the office at the Nibenay iron mine was a young woman named Jacles.**
- In that office she recorded the amount of ore taken from the mine, **the number of slaves that died in the process**, met official visitors, and otherwise oversaw operations.
- Her home at the stockade (area 7) was once well appointed, filled with fine furniture, stylish rugs and fine curtains, and housed her most respected visitors.
- After the mine was taken, her office was used by [[Askai]] and then by [[Drak]]. Her house is now used as a headquarters by the largest and most powerful gith present.
- Most items of value have already been squabbled over and taken by the gith. The interior walls have been torn down in their search for hidden treasure.
- The mine fell when miners dug too deep and struck a gith nest; the gith poured out in huge numbers, armed with metal weapons and using clever tactics, and quickly overwhelmed the Nibenese troops.
- **The module never states what happened to Jacles.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Her fate is a blank the campaign can fill for free.** Four options, in rough order of usefulness:
  1. **In the slave pens below.** The gith took many miners into the mountain to be slaves "or worse." A templar-wife among them is an excellent find in Adventure Three or Five, and an appalling political problem for the party.
  2. **In the cold smelter.** Adventure Five's black flame consumes life-force and leaves no remains. If the party wants to know what happened to Jacles, the honest answer might be *nothing is left to find*.
  3. **Dead at the fall**, and the blood in her office is hers.
  4. **Escaped**, and back in Nibenay, which puts her directly into conflict with Vakskra's news-control goal.
- **She is the paper trail for the central question.** *Why were the miners digging that deep?* The campaign's entire premise starts with a mine breaking into a gith nest. Somebody ordered that depth, and her records are where that order would be written down.
- **Option 1 collides productively with Vakskra.** A living Jacles is the one witness who can contradict the official account, and Vakskra has an explicit secret goal of preventing exactly that. If the party rescues her, they have acquired a political weapon and a liability in the same person.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: option 1.** Put her in the slave pens at the gith mining facility in Adventure Five (area 19 holds 38 slaves). It costs nothing, it pays off a name dropped three adventures earlier, and it hands the party a genuine dilemma with a templar-wife they have already allied with.
- Her ledgers should physically exist in the operations building and should be findable. A party that reads them learns the mortality rate, the extraction quota, and the depth order.
- If she is dead, leave her house exactly as printed and let the ruin do the work.
