# Actor: Breck

## One-Sentence Summary
Kharanok's last remaining gate guard — bitter, loyal, holding the gate mostly alone; and, secretly, the Veiled Alliance's ranking warden on the eastern chain, which is exactly why he never leaves that post.

- Alliance role: Warden of the Kharanok chain — the ranking Alliance figure east of Nibenay, answerable only to the handler tier ([[Melketh the Blind Seer]])
- Veil-name: "Breck of the Open Gate"
- Current location: Kharanok (Main Gate)

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A middle-aged, hardened village gate guard. Sunburned, tan skin with a dust-caked face, cracked lips, and rough stubble. Tired, suspicious eyes, a square jaw, short practical dark hair, and a blunt, severe expression. Wears worn, patched leather armor over simple desert clothes, with a faded sand-colored guard cloak, wrapped forearms, dusty boots, a battered belt, and a small toll pouch. Carries a bone or obsidian utility knife and an old spear or polearm, usually held at rest. No shining metal or ornate decoration — nothing of the heroic knight about him.

He reads as exhausted but disciplined — loyal out of habit rather than idealism, bitter from being the last guard still holding his post.

*(Player-safe only. Nothing in his bearing advertises what he can do. A character who watches him closely might notice he never stands where two people can reach him at once, and that the "old spear" is kept in better condition than anything else he owns.)*

### Manner and Voice
Bitter, loyal. Speaks in short refusals. Complains about the watch, the scraps, the council — never about the traffic through his gate, which he tracks in complete detail and mentions to no one.

### Public Reputation
Known as the one who still shows up to guard the gate even though the watch has fallen apart. Pitied slightly. Underestimated entirely.

## Confirmed Facts
- Works the gate essentially alone (ad1); despite an injured arm, he refuses to let Relo pass until he gets the OK from Elder Yethras — while the other guard, Oren, struggles to stay awake. Breck is the more alert, disciplined one of the two, holding the line even while hurt.
- ad3: the party delivered 50 scraps for basic gate repair; Breck was given the order to start the work. Full fortification still needs 200 more scraps ([[0003 - Session Prep 16-7-2026]]).
- Design doc quest-giver credit: Act 1, Hook 1 (with Yethras) and Hook 2 ("The Guards Have Deserted").

*(Alliance rank and level are a GM revision, not actual play — see Sources. Nothing above is contradicted by it: a warden posted to a gate behaves exactly like a stubborn guard who will not leave his post.)*

## Traits and Pressures
- Ambition: Low — genuinely. He turned down the handler tier; the gate matters more than the rank.
- Caution: High (5) — he is alert on watch because the watch is the work, not because he is dutiful.
- Secrecy: 5 — fifteen years under cover without a single exposure.
- Loyalty: High, to Kharanok *and* to the Alliance. He has never yet had to choose, and dreads the day.
- Cruelty: None observed.
- Risk tolerance: Low for himself, near-zero for the chain.
- Safety: Physically exposed (gate stands open unguarded much of the time) — and he keeps it that way, because a gate nobody guards properly is a gate nobody watches.
- Wealth: None beyond his post. Alliance funds pass through him; none stick.
- Status: Respected but overworked in Kharanok. Senior in the Alliance, known by veil-name to perhaps five people.

## Resources and Capabilities
- Whatever scraps/materials the party or village supplies for gate repair.
- Complete informal record of who has entered and left Kharanok, held entirely in memory.
- A compartmented contact chain running west toward Nibenay; he knows his links and no more, by doctrine.
- One cached kit — a real weapon, travel water, and forged transit tokens — buried outside the walls, never brought inside.
- Authority to grant or refuse safe passage through Kharanok on the Alliance's behalf.

## Current Activity
Guarding the Main Gate, overseeing repair work as scraps arrive — and using the repair as cover to make the gate genuinely defensible for the first time in years.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Breck is a level 10 fighter.** He is comfortably the most dangerous person in Kharanok, and possibly the most dangerous the party has met without knowing it. The exhaustion is real; the incompetence is theatre.
- He is the **ranking Veiled Alliance member east of Nibenay** — the Warden of the Kharanok chain. He was offered the handler tier and refused it, because the gate is the asset.
- The injured arm in ad1 was real, and he held the post anyway rather than let an unvetted traveller (Relo) through unchecked. That was not stubbornness. That was screening.
- **Exposure cost:** if Breck is burned, the entire eastern route between Nibenay and the Altar of Dust collapses, and every preserver who has passed through Kharanok in fifteen years becomes traceable. He will kill to prevent this, and it is the one thing that would put him against the party.
- The unused "corrupt captain" hook is now load-bearing: the captain was not merely greedy, he was selling Alliance transits. Breck wants him found. Breck cannot be seen looking.
- He genuinely likes Kharanok. If the village and the chain ever demand opposite things, the GM should treat that as his breaking point, not a formality.

## Proposed Developments
- The "corrupt former captain" thread is now the natural way to reveal him — the party investigating it will eventually find someone else already investigating it.
- A reveal-under-pressure scene: the party sees him fight properly, once, and has to reconcile it with the man who complains about scraps.
- **Continuity consequence, not yet applied:** `faction-veiled-alliance-nibenay` lists `presence` as Nibenay, Kheth's Field, Black-Thread Hollow and Thornmouth — **Kharanok is not among them**. Promoting Breck extends Alliance reach to Kharanok and to the Altar of Dust route. That record lives in `mk-repos/mk-sandbox` (read-only) and has **not** been edited; it needs a GM event/ruling to add `location-kharanok` to the faction's presence, plus an `actor-breck` record, which does not currently exist in `actors/`.
- If the Alliance ever needs Kharanok's gate closed against Nibenay, Breck is the one who can do it — a very large lever the campaign has not yet pulled.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Fighter
- Level: 10
- Armor Class: 15 (patched leather over chitin plates he does not advertise; +1 with spear set to receive)
- Hit Points: 62
- Movement: near
- Strength +4, Dexterity +2, Constitution +3, Intelligence +1, Wisdom +3, Charisma +2
- Alignment: Lawful — oath-bound twice over, to the village and to the Alliance
- Morale: 10. He does not rout; he is standing where he intends to stand.
- Attacks: 2 attacks per round.
  - *Obsidian-tipped spear* +7, 1d8+4 (his real weapon, kept better than it looks; reach)
  - *Bone utility knife* +7, 1d4+4
- **Weapon Mastery (spear):** +1 to attack and damage, included above.
- **Grit (Strength):** advantage on Strength checks to hold ground, force or bar a gate, or grapple.
- **Hold the Line:** while Breck holds a gateway, doorway or breach, enemies attempting to pass him must succeed on a DC 15 Strength check or be stopped for the round.
- Not a spellcaster. No preserver training, no defiling — the Alliance values him precisely because a man with no arcane signature can stand in the open for fifteen years and never register as anything.
- **Running him at the table:** never let the numbers leak before the reveal. He resolves problems by refusing, delaying, and sending for Yethras. The stat block exists for the one scene where refusing stops working.
