## GM-Only Secrets
- **Tormar is a slot, not a character, and that is his value.** He is a name the players will remember because it was said with feeling, attached to a person they have never met. The campaign gets to decide what they find, and every option is already supported by printed material:
  1. **Alive in the slave pens** — Adventure Five, area 19, holds 38 slaves in two pits. Rescuable.
  2. **Alive in a work gang** — Adventure Five's quarry and mine sub-encounters are full of named-less human slaves. He can be one of them.
  3. **In the cold smelter** — Adventure Five, area 18: slaves enter for a work shift and never return; the black flame consumes life-force at a hit point per round and leaves *no remains*. This is the cruellest option and the best supported.
  4. **Already dead** in the mine tunnels, and found on the way in.
- **Option 3 is recommended if the campaign wants Adventure Five's horror to land personally.** The cold smelter is described but not felt. A named person the party has been carrying since Adventure Three makes it felt.
- If Durdon was sent back to the camp as the module advises, he is not present to see whatever the party finds — which means somebody has to go and tell him.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** decide Tormar's fate before Adventure Five and plant it, so the discovery is a payoff rather than an improvisation.
- If rescued, he is a free extra survivor with mine knowledge and a debt.
- If lost, the scene is Durdon being told, and that scene is worth keeping.
