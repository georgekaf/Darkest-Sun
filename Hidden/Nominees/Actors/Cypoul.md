An escaped slave turned water priest who hears what the spirits say and answers them — including the fifteen at the oasis who demanded a companion be handed over, and were refused to the party's own cost in water.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Chainmail — the heaviest armour in a party that mostly wears leather — with a bone shield and a crude club.

Argues on principle. When the oasis spirits demanded Rhazek, his answer was that this particular fire priest had done them no harm.

## Confirmed Facts
- **ad5, day 112:** with the party for the kank hunt.
- **ad6, day 115:** at the oasis, **he is the one who hears the spirits** warning the party off — the water is theirs, sacred, and none may drink while they keep "the one who serves fire" with them. They demand Rhazek as atonement for what fire priests did to them.
- **ad6:** **refuses**, arguing Rhazek has done them no harm. When they counter-offer — everyone drinks, Rhazek stays outside all day with no shade or water — the party refuses that too. No deal. Looking out over the water he counts **about fifteen spirits** hovering above it.
- **ad6:** blinds the giant snake in the Zharvek ruins, setting up Shaka's killing shot.
- **ad7, day 117:** gives Relo a blessing for luck before the descent; **heals Relo** after the scorpion's claw; fails with Ranni to blind the second scorpion and **loses his blessing twice** before resting.
