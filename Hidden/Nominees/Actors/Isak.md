# Actor: Isak

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-isak`. **Thin source — see Sources.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The craftsman who will not give up his home to a band of stinking gith, said exactly that in open council, and found that it moved the room further than any of the reasoned arguments did.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Proposed.)* A working artisan, thickset through the forearms from whichever trade he keeps — the source does not say which. Dust-coloured work clothes, calloused hands, and the settled physical confidence of a man who has been in one place long enough to think of it as his.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed, extrapolated from his printed argument.)* Blunt and unembarrassed. Isak does not make structured arguments; he makes statements about what he is and is not prepared to accept, and lets other people work out the reasoning. He swears in council. He is not trying to persuade anyone, which is exactly why he does.

### Public Reputation
One of the band's ordinary craftsmen, with an unusual amount of weight in a debate — because when he says *this is my home* he is voicing the thing two hundred people are feeling and not saying.

## Confirmed Facts
- A member of Tenpug's Band and one of the minor debaters in the first meeting tent.
- Argues **in favour of fighting**, on the grounds that he does not like the idea of giving up his home to a band of "stinking gith."
- **His argument gains emotional support** — the printed source flags this specifically, distinguishing it from the analytical arguments around it.
- Is one of the four advisers Tenpug summons into the command tent alongside the PCs, together with Jolon, Danya and Sala — a deliberately balanced council, half pro-war and half pro-flight.

## Current Activity
Arguing in tent 8 for staying and fighting, and being seated at Tenpug's council to do it again in a smaller room.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Isak is the promotion candidate for tent 8's vacant principal-debater slot.** The printed source pairs Jolon against Danya as the two principals; the campaign has relocated Danya entirely (see [[Danya]]), leaving Jolon arguing against nobody. Isak is already in that tent, already pro-war, already noted as carrying emotional weight, and already at Tenpug's council. Promoting him is a one-line fix that costs the module nothing.
- *(Proposed.)* He is not brave. He is territorial, which produces identical behaviour right up until the moment the temple is actually breached, at which point the two diverge sharply.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended and near-costless:** make Isak the principal pro-war debater in tent 8. This repairs the hole Danya's relocation leaves in the module's central set-piece.
- Give him a trade. The source never says what he makes, and the band has potters, tanners, carvers, weavers and weaponsmiths to choose from. Whichever is chosen gives him a tent, a workbench, and a reason to be somewhere during the siege.
- He is a good candidate to survive Adventure One and become the band's voice in later adventures, when Tenpug is increasingly absent leading armies.
