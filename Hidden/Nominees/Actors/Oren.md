## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A lean, underfed man with sun-darkened skin, a dusty face, and anxious eyes behind a forced, over-friendly smile. He stands slightly hunched, hands too ready to gesture or placate — over-helpful, over-agreeable, like someone trying too hard to look harmless. Fear shows through a thin layer of false confidence. His sentry gear is practical but neglected: worn desert clothing, patched light leather armor, a faded village-guard sash or marker, wrapped forearms, dusty boots, a simple belt, a small coin pouch kept close to the body, and a signal mirror or polished shard tucked at his side. He carries a cheap knife and a short spear or light crossbow, held awkwardly rather than confidently — nothing of the elite soldier about him. His body language is that of a mediocre liar under pressure: shoulders tight, smile too quick, eyes slightly averted, one hand half-raised as if about to explain himself. He isn't cruel or monstrous — he's trapped, frightened, guilty, and useful if protected.

### Manner and Voice
Sleep-deprived, nervous, evasive when pressed about the deserters/his own activities — reads as an over-agreeable, mediocre liar under pressure.

### Public Reputation
Known as the exhausted last sentry holding the watchtower alone.

## Confirmed Facts
- ad1: the more exhausted of the two gate/watch guards on duty; struggles to keep watch, while Breck — despite an injured arm — holds the gate firm and refuses to let Relo pass until Elder Yethras gives the OK.
- ad2: interviewed at the watchtower by Parias and Sepsis. Initially refuses to talk, but eventually, frightened, describes nighttime sightings — villagers near the quarry suddenly screaming and jumping into the canyon, or running out of the mine to smash their heads against the quarry rocks, without having touched alcohol first.
- Design doc: tied to Act 1, Hook 1 as the remaining sentry after two others deserted. Design doc clue: a partial bootprint at the Old Quarry Yard, among stranger tracks, matches Oren's boot size.
