# Actor: Alukas

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-alukas`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A beholder imprisoned under an asteroid for centuries, released by a githyanki spy's misfiring teleport, who wiped out an entire githzerai outpost and then sat down in their library to read their records and work out what was going on — and succeeded.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc — **beholder**
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: none. Beholders are solitary and xenophobic by nature.
- Current location: the githzerai asteroid base, area 13 — **the library**
- Current route, when traveling: levitation; the Limbo gate in area 14 if the party bargains
- Role: The thing that destroyed the githzerai outpost

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
> *"Floating in the center of the room is a great spherical thing some five feet in diameter. A wide gash of a mouth filled with jagged teeth cuts across what must be the creature's face. A single great eye rests above the mouth and a cluster of eyestalk waves back and forth atop the spherical body."*

The room around him is **a great library** — walls carved with shelves full of books and scrolls, benches and tables covered with the broken remnants of magical and scientific equipment, and **a pile of broken bodies along one wall, most of them half eaten.**

### Manner and Voice
Intelligent, patient, and entirely transactional. **He will not wait around to talk with the PCs — he attacks at once** — but **Alukas is no fool. He will do all he can to defeat them, but will not fight to the death. If it becomes clear that he is about to be killed, he will sue for peace.**

Once bargaining, **he drives a hard bargain and points out repeatedly that his knowledge is the only thing standing between Trinth and her success on Athas.**

### Public Reputation
None. Nobody living knew he was here — **including the githzerai who built an outpost on top of him.**

## Confirmed Facts
- **A beholder**, imprisoned in the asteroid **centuries ago by the Order of the Rising Shadow**.
- The githzerai took over the abandoned catacombs afterwards **without knowing he was there.**
- **[[Karros]]**, a githyanki spy inside the outpost, was about to teleport to his masters in Galnaki to report the githzerai presence. He slipped into a dead-end tunnel and activated a magical icon — **but the proximity of the magical fields holding the eye tyrant prevented it from working. Instead of transporting him off the asteroid, the spell collapsed the wards containing the beholder.**
- **The first thing the beholder did upon being released was to destroy Karros.**
- **He then wiped out every githzerai stationed there.**
- **He specifically destroyed their silver swords with his disintegration ray**, recognising the threat — which is why every body is found with an empty scabbard and only daggers to hand.
- **After the massacre, Alukas settled into the library to examine the githzerai records and figure out what was going on.**
- **He recently discovered that Trinth had come into possession of a magical artifact of great power, and that with it she plans to attack a realm in the Prime Material plane.**
- **In exchange for his release — passage out via the gate in area 14 — he will share what he has learned.**
- What he can tell them: **Trinth has Yrinolir**, a magical scepter of incredible power — **a thick, rune-covered platinum shaft with a great ruby set atop it. The ruby is the crystallised heart of the pit fiend Nalcariz, and still beats with a dusky light.**
- **He can also tell the party that the master of the Order of the Rising Shadow was an illithid** — information **that will horrify [[Krika]]**.
- Statistics: THAC0 3; Dmg 2d4; AC 0 (body) / 2 (eyestalk) / 7 (central eye); hp 50; MV Fl 3 (B); INT 15; AL LE; ML 18; XP 14,000.
- Eye stalk powers: **charm person, charm monster, sleep, telekinesis, flesh to stone, disintegrate, fear, slow, cause serious wounds, death ray.** The **central eye projects an anti-magic ray that affects even the creature's own lesser eyes.**

## Resources and Capabilities
- **Ten eyestalk powers plus an anti-magic central eye.** Against a party without a plan, this is one of the hardest fights in the campaign.
- **Everything in the githzerai records** — including the Yrinolir research, which is the key to the entire finale.
- **Centuries of whatever the Order of the Rising Shadow left behind**, in a library nobody else has read.
- Knowledge that **the Order's master was an illithid** — and, by implication, awareness of [[Maelactorus]]'s vault.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the campaign's best-earned exposition and the party has to beat him for it.** Alukas holds the Yrinolir research — what the scepter is, what it is made of, and (via Krika's parallel account) **how to destroy it.** Without that information the finale does not work. **He will trade it for his freedom, and he will not fight to the death.** That is a negotiation the players get to have with a beholder, which is not a sentence most campaigns produce.
- **Killing him is allowed and costs nothing mechanically** — Krika can supply the same intelligence from the library after a few hours' reading. **But letting him go releases a beholder into the multiverse with a grudge and everything he has just read.** The module offers no consequence for this. A campaign should.
- **He is also the only creature who can tell them what is in the vault.** Alukas was imprisoned by the Order; he knows their master was an illithid; **[[Maelactorus]] is still sealed in area 9.** If the party asks the right question they can be warned. If they open the vault first, they find out the other way.
- **The empty scabbards are a clue and they are deliberate.** Every githzerai body has a scabbard and no sword, and only daggers scattered on the floor. **Krika will be genuinely astonished.** The answer — a creature with a disintegration ray that recognised silver swords as the threat and removed them first — points straight at what they are dealing with before they meet it.
- **He fought smart, and should fight smart again.** Anti-magic cone forward, eyestalks spread across the arc, and he takes the party's casters and their magic weapons out of the equation first. **He has already done this once, to trained soldiers, and won without a scratch.**
- *(Proposed.)* He has been alone with a library for weeks after centuries in a box. Whatever he actually wants now is not what a beholder wanted before it was imprisoned.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** run the empty-scabbard clue properly. Let the party notice it in area 1, be told by Krika that it makes no sense, and only later understand it.
- Make the peace-suing real. When he drops low, he **stops** and talks. Players expect a fight to the death from a beholder; giving them a negotiation instead is the memorable version.
- If released, bring him back. He owes them nothing, knows what they are doing, and can go anywhere.
- Let him warn them about the vault, for a price. It reframes Maelactorus from a surprise into a decision.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: — (beholder)
- Level: — (treat as a 10th-tier solo threat)
- Armor Class: **20 (body) / 18 (eyestalks) / 13 (central eye)**
- Hit Points: 50
- Movement: **fly near (slow, hovering)**
- Strength —, Dexterity +0, Constitution +3, Intelligence **+3**, Wisdom +2, Charisma +1
- Alignment: Lawful (evil)
- Morale: 18 — **but he sues for peace rather than dying; see below**
- Attacks: 1 attack per round.
  - *Bite* +9, 2d4
- **Eyestalks (10):** charm person, charm monster, sleep, telekinesis, **flesh to stone**, **disintegrate**, fear, slow, cause serious wounds, **death ray**. He can use **1d4** eyes against attackers within a 90-degree frontal arc, 1d6 within 180 degrees, 1d8 within 270 degrees, and **all ten if attacked from all sides.**
- **Central Eye — Anti-Magic Ray:** a 90-degree cone to the front in which **no magic functions at all, including his own lesser eyes.** Spells cast in or passing through it cease to function.
- **Called Shots:** eyestalks have 5–12 hit points each and can be destroyed individually without harming the body; destroyed stalks regenerate at one per week. Destroying the central eye eliminates the anti-magic ray.
- **Levitation:** he floats. He does not walk and cannot be tripped or grappled to the ground.
- **Sues for Peace:** **when it becomes clear he is about to be killed, he stops fighting and negotiates.** This is printed behaviour and should not be overridden.
- **Running him at the table:** open with an unannounced attack, fight with real tactical intelligence — anti-magic on the casters, disintegrate on anything enchanted — and then stop cold and start talking the moment the fight turns. The tonal whiplash is the encounter.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Alukas (Beholder) (1): THAC0 3, Dmg 2d4, AC 0/2/7, HD Special, hp 50, MV Fl 3 (B), INT 15, AL LE, ML 18, XP 14,000. Armour classes converted; hit points retained. The eye-count-by-arc rule is from the *Monstrous Compendium* beholder entry reprinted in Book Three.
