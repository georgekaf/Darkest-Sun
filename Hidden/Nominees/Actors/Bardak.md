# Actor: Bardak

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-bardak`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A worn and beaten dwarf priest of earth who gave up on ever seeing Athas again and now wants only to kill as many githyanki as he can before one of them finishes him — and who will not leave a single cell locked on the way.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
> *"This cell contains a worn and beaten dwarf. Despite his brutal treatment, his eyes burn with hatred that defy his captivity."*

He wears **the same sort of silken tunic the characters do**, has been stripped of his weapons and equipment, and is **fitted with a collar that smothers his psionic abilities.**

### Manner and Voice
*(Manner proposed; the behaviour is printed.)* Short, hard, and entirely past negotiation. Bardak is not despairing — the module is specific that his eyes *burn with hatred that defy his captivity.* He has simply narrowed his objectives to one.

**If released and used as an NPC, he insists on releasing all of the other prisoners.** He does not treat this as debatable.

### Public Reputation
None. A dwarf the queen's people took and have not yet used up.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the counterweight to Ela, and the pair are a designed choice.** Ela offers the patrol schedule — how to move quietly. Bardak offers the storerooms and insists on opening every cell — how to move loudly. **A party can have both pieces of information and cannot have both plans.**
- **His insistence on freeing everyone is a trap with a good heart.** The block holds two defiant githyanki who will start shouting for help the moment they see an escape, four well-treated political prisoners who will expect to take command and will sacrifice the PCs freely, twelve renegade halflings who will turn on the party once clear of the dungeon, a released chimera lair, and **[[Gremlish]]**. **Bardak wants all of it open.**
- **He is being informed on and it is the module's own setup.** Gremlish is planted in the cell across the hall specifically to ferret out secrets, and has won Bardak's confidence. A party that works this out has a way to feed the githyanki bad information; a party that does not has already lost the element of surprise.
- **He is not suicidal, he is finished.** *He long ago gave up any hope of getting back to Athas.* If the party can credibly show him a route home — and they can, because the portal at the heart of Galnaki is exactly that — **his entire character changes.** That reversal is available, free, and completely unwritten.
- *(Proposed.)* His dwarven focus is unfulfilled and he knows it. See [[Roi]] and [[Pimns]]: a dwarf who dies in a foreign plane with nothing finished has a specific fate in this setting, and Bardak is walking into it deliberately.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** let him name the storerooms early, and let his all-the-cells insistence be a real argument the party has to win or lose. It is the best decision point in the prison sequence.
- Expose Gremlish through Bardak rather than directly — the dwarf defending the man who has been informing on him is a better scene than a detect-evil check.
- If the party tells him they know the way back to Athas, play the change. He has been holding himself together with one idea and they have just taken it away and replaced it with something worse: hope.
- The focus/undeath thread ties him to Roi and to the chasm banshees. If the campaign has been running that, this is its third appearance.
