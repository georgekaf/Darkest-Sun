# Actor: The Loyal — Kethen

## One-Sentence Summary
The Loyal's thief and lockbreaker — irreverent, quick-handed, the reason the first expedition reached Dregoth's templar chambers, and completely silent on the subject of the second.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: [[The Loyal]] (`faction-the-loyal`), follower
- Current location: the wastes within 20 miles of the Ruins of Giustenal (`location-giustenal-ruins`)
- Current route, when traveling: with the band
- Role: Thief; entry, locks, and everything the band is not supposed to be able to reach

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Long-limbed, narrow mischievous face, crooked grin, shaved sides and a loose dark topknot. Mismatched dark leather pieces, dusty scarves, wrapped legs, fingerless leather gloves, several concealed bone knives. A short bone blade reversed at his belt, a compact bone bow over one shoulder. Relaxed, agile stance.

### Manner and Voice
Fast, funny, disrespectful to everyone including Jessix. Talks through danger. Goes flat and quiet at the mention of Slinnasia.

### Public Reputation
The member travelers actually remember, usually fondly and usually with something missing from a pocket.

## Confirmed Facts
- Source states only that Jessix roams with five young elves called the Loyal, preservers and thieves, who witnessed Slinnasia's death and believe him innocent (Campaign Book p. 11-12).
- Both Giustenal expeditions are established in the source's account of Jessix. These five survived both.

- Concealed blades, and the habit of never being where he was last seen.
- First-hand memory of the layout the first expedition took into the templar chambers.

## GM-Only Secrets
- He blames himself for the second expedition, not Jessix. The jokes are load-bearing.

## Proposed Developments
