# Actor: Sylvar

## One-Sentence Summary
An elderly human traveller out of Nibenay with dry branches wrapped around him, handed to the party by Kalia as one more mouth she did not want to feed, who turned out to speak with nature and to speak halfling — and so became the voice through which Shaka finally talks to everyone else.

## Classification
- Subtype: player-character
- Control: player-character — played by **DarkRhapsode**, campaign *Altar of Dust*
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: none; travels with the Altar of Dust party
- Current location: Kharanok (`location-kharanok`)
- Current route, when traveling: none standing
- Role: Druid — nature magic, and the party's halfling interpreter

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Old, ragged, with dry branches wound around his body. Leather armour, a staff, a bone shield.

### Manner and Voice
Says he likes wandering the desert and that his age does not take manual labour — then does more useful work in one afternoon than the labourers do. Translates other people's words *improved*: Shaka's blunt orders reach the party as courteous requests to "the worthy water clerics."

### Public Reputation
New. Kalia wrote his name down and docked him half a water ration for five days for talking back to her.

## Confirmed Facts
- **ad7, day 117:** first appearance. Kalia offloads him onto Relo in the square. He introduces himself as Sylvar, says he comes from Nibenay, likes to roam the desert, cannot do heavy labour at his age — but can speak with nature.
- **ad7:** speaks halfling, and becomes **Shaka's interpreter** from this session on, including for Shaka's pronouncements.
- **ad7:** helps Relo lift the hollow-sounding flagstone with The Way, uncovering the library of thirty to forty rolled hides under the gate room.
- **ad7:** sent to fetch Tzala the alchemist and relay Shaka's request for healing potions; gets the recipe out of her — sandbloom, water, a handful of Crystal Dust — under her condition that nobody learns it from her.
- **ad7:** stands up to Kalia when she berates Dorak, asking her to calm down and let people work for the sake of those who freed her. She asks his name, writes it down: **half a water ration for five days**.
- **ad7, night:** finds a cluster of nine sandblooms in the scrublands, then three more.
- **ad7, night:** in the scorpion fight, calls glowing insects onto one scorpion so it lights up and is easily struck, and wraps thorns and vines around Shaka's arrows to make them magical.
- **Name:** heard in the raw transcript as «Σίλβαρ»/«Σίλβαν»/«Σίλβαρντ»; normalised to **Sylvar** (`fixes/sylvar.json`).
