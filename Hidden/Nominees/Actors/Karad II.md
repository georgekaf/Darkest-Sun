## The name

**He is `Karad II`. The city is `Karand-Vath`. They are not the same name and the two have been conflated.**

At the table in episode 21 a player recapped the tomb as belonging to *«ο Καραντ Βαθ ο δεύτερος, μαζί με τη γυναίκα του»*, and the GM corrected it immediately: *«Καραρτ, σκέτου, όχι Βαρθ»* — **Karad alone, not Vath**. The correction is the ruling. *Vath* belongs to the city, whose canonical sandbox spelling is **`Karand-Vath`** with the *n*.

**Romanisation ruled by the campaign owner, 13 August 2026: `Karad II`.** The Greek at the table is *«ο Καραντ ο δεύτερος»*; the filed form is the Roman numeral, not `Karad B'`. Proposed id: `actor-karad-ii`.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(GM's own description, episode 21.)* A **golden crown** on his head. **White eyes.** A **white beard soaked in blood** — blood running literally from his mouth. **Golden metal plate armour**, the body **wrapped in gauze**. At his side, **a rod of wood or bone — the party could not tell which**.

### Manner and Voice
Not established. He has not spoken. His first act on rising was to **kick the sarcophagus aside**.

## Confirmed Facts

**Who he was, and what was done to him** *(episode 18, in-world day 105 — Gmohh's vision, granted through the guardian's hammer)*:

- **He was the last king of Karand-Vath**, and was **once good and merciful, like his father before him**.
- **Aged and without a future, he accepted a philtre of immortality from Nibenay himself** — one for himself and one for **his queen**.
- **The philtre turned him into a bloodthirsty monster. He slaughtered his own children first.**
- **His own subjects imprisoned him alive** inside a sarcophagus. **Cracks have opened in it over the years, from the inside.**
- **The sarcophagus was hung deliberately over a shaft-trap**, so that if he ever broke out he would **fall deep and never climb back**. The shaft is not a hole in the roof. It is the containment.
- **His corpse carries powerful magic of Nibenay inside it.** The Spider Witch wanted it for a revenge plan of her own against Nibenay, and traded hard for it.
- **The previous guardian never broke his oath**, across the whole span of that imprisonment.

**The rising** *(episode 21, in-world day 105)*:

- **He was buried in Karand-Vath together with his wife.** *(Episode 21 recap of the tomb.)*
- **His sarcophagus hung from chains, strapped**, above a small stone bridge, with a lever mechanism one floor above to lower it.
- **The sarcophagus was already broken from above** — someone had tried to force it open before the party arrived.
- **Blood was dripping from the sarcophagus** before it was opened.
- **Day 105** — the party lowered it to cut the straps, intending to drop his corpse down the shaft onto the red crystal. **The lid was thrown into the air and he rose.**
- **The GM stated he has enormous strength.**
- **The episode ends on initiative.** Nothing about the fight is on record.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The shaft is the trap his subjects built, and the party's plan is to use it exactly as designed** — drop him down it so he never comes back. They arrived at that plan for an entirely different reason, to feed the red crystal with his blood. Nobody at the table has connected the two.
- **He is a Nibenay artefact as much as a corpse.** The magic in him is the Sorcerer-King's, which is why the Spider Witch bargained for the body rather than simply taking the tomb.
- **Gmohh's oath has been broken by his own party's plan.** The terms are recorded plainly in `actors/gmohh.json`: protect the king's coffin from being opened. The party opened it deliberately, to use the corpse. Whatever follows, that is on the record already and needs no invention.
- **The prior break-in is unexplained.** The lid was damaged from above before the party ever reached it, and blood was already coming out of it.
- **His wife has not moved.** Yet.
- **The rod at his side has not been identified.**
