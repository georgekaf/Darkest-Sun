# Actor: Teva

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-teva`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The band's wise woman — a former templar of Kalak who lost her powers when her sorcerer-king died, will not say a word about what she did in his service, and is now the moral authority of a community that has no idea what it is deferring to.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A human woman past middle age, still solidly built, with the upright carriage of someone trained to be looked at. She keeps hide armour and a shield close to hand — not worn, but never far — and an iron mace that is far too good a weapon for a bone carver to own and that nobody has ever asked her about.

Her hands are always powdered with bone dust. The figurines she carves are competent and unremarkable, and she knows it.

### Manner and Voice
Warm, articulate, and precise in a way that stands out badly among ex-slaves. Teva speaks in complete sentences, uses words the rest of the camp does not have, and catches herself doing it. Her door is open to anyone with a problem and she is genuinely good at the work — she listens, reframes, and sends people away steadier than they arrived.

She becomes flatly unresponsive on exactly one subject. Ask about Tyr, or Kalak, or the templarate, and she will change the subject once, politely. Ask again and she will simply stop talking.

### Public Reputation
The wisest person in the camp. It is under her guidance that the offering bowl is kept, and the band's whole relationship with the temple's dead builders is essentially her invention. Nobody thinks of it that way.

## Confirmed Facts
- Human. Was once a templar of Kalak, the late sorcerer-king of Tyr.
- Will not discuss that period. The printed text is explicit: *what secrets she knows of that time she will not say.*
- Was enslaved after crossing the wrong people, bribed the guards sent to collect her, and escaped.
- **Has no magical powers at all any more.**
- Now works for Tenpug carving bone figurines that sell for small sums in Nibenay's markets. She is a mediocre craftsman and this is stated outright.
- Has an excellent education and knows a great deal about philosophy. People come to her for advice.
- The offering made to the ancient giants of the temple is made under her guidance.
- Opposes the war — she saw enough fighting as a templar and does not want any more. Flight appeals to her, but she thinks it unlikely the gith would accept a bribe.
- Minor debater in the first meeting tent, arguing that *war, not cowardice, leads to death.*

## Resources and Capabilities
- Genuine education: philosophy, rhetoric, letters, and the internal workings of a city-state's templarate.
- Counsel — she is the person Tenpug goes to before he goes to the council.
- Ritual authority over the offering bowl and, by extension, the band's entire relationship with the temple.
- Real combat training, unadvertised: hide, shield, and an iron mace she plainly knows how to use.
- Literacy. In a camp where Tenpug cannot read his own scrolls, this is a genuine and underused asset.

## Current Activity
Carving bone in the great hall, receiving a steady traffic of people with problems, and quietly losing the war debate to a camp that wants to fight.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Her powers did not fade. Kalak died.** Templar magic is granted by a sorcerer-king; when Kalak was killed at Tyr, every templar of his lost everything at once. Teva has never explained this to the band because explaining it means saying what she was. The band assumes she is a woman who gave up magic. She is a woman whose god was murdered and who felt it happen.
- **She invented the offering rite.** The band's superstition about the temple's spirits — the bowl, the untouched rooms, the fear of curses — traces to her guidance. She did it to give two hundred traumatised ex-slaves something to hold that was not vengeance. It worked far better than she intended, and it is now the single obstacle preventing Tenpug from searching the chambers that contain the band's only chance of survival.
- **She therefore has the authority to undo it**, in one sentence, in front of the whole camp. She has not, because doing so exposes that she made it up. This is the actual dramatic hinge of Adventure One and it sits inside a minor NPC.
- **What she did for Kalak is deliberately blank.** The printed source refuses to fill it in. Recommended that the campaign also refuse, at least until a player pushes hard enough to earn it.
- She is a trained fighter with an iron mace in a camp of craftsmen. If the temple is breached, she will be found where the fighting is worst, and nobody will have expected it.

## Proposed Developments
- **Primary hook:** the party works out that the curse-fear is hers, and has to decide whether to expose her to get Tenpug to open the sealed rooms. That is a genuine moral choice with the siege as its clock.
- If the campaign ever wants a line into Tyr, its templarate, or the aftermath of Kalak's death, Teva is a fully-formed source sitting unused in a slave camp.
- Her combat capability is a good second-battle reveal, paired with [[Arcus]]: the camp discovers it has been sitting on two real fighters the entire time.
- She is the natural person to be given the Keeper of Supplies post if the GM does not want to leave it vacant after Danya's relocation — she is literate, numerate, and already trusted. See [[Danya]].

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Fighter (former templar — **no spellcasting**)
- Level: 5
- Armor Class: 15 (hide and shield)
- Hit Points: 30
- Movement: near
- Strength +1, Dexterity +1, Constitution +2, Intelligence +3, Wisdom **+4**, Charisma +2
- Alignment: Lawful
- Morale: 11
- Attacks: 1 attack per round.
  - *Iron mace* +5, 1d6+2 (metal, and therefore worth more than everything else she owns combined)
- **No Powers:** Teva has **zero** spellcasting or granted abilities. This is mechanically load-bearing, not flavour — a templar without a sorcerer-king is exactly a fighter with an unusually good education.
- **Counsel:** once per rest, on a short conversation, she can grant an ally advantage on their next Wisdom or Charisma check. This represents the actual thing she does all day.
- **Educated:** advantage on checks involving philosophy, history, letters, city-state politics, or the reading of documents. She is the only reliably literate person in the band.
- **Trained Under Arms:** proficient with shield and mace to a standard nobody in the camp can account for.
- **Running her at the table:** she should read as the wise elder and nothing else, until a player pulls a thread. Every deflection about her past should be polite, complete, and slightly too smooth. The moment to break her is the siege, not before.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Human Fighter, level illegible in the source OCR (column interleave, p.12–13), Wis 18, hp 38, AC 4, THAC0 16, iron mace 1d6+1. Level set to 5 by inference from THAC0 16 and hp 38. Printed name appears as both "Teva" and "Eva" across the column break — **"Teva" adopted**, matching the section heading.
