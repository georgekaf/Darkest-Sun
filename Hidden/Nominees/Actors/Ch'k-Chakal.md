# Actor: Ch'k-Chakal

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-chk-chakal`. **Thin source — see Sources.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The band's thri-kreen engineer — currently stranded outside the gith patrol screen with the carpenters, and, because thri-kreen do not sleep, the single most important person in the campaign two adventures before anyone realises it.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: **absent** — away in the Crescent Forest on a materials trek when the PCs arrive
- Faction or allegiance: `faction-tenpugs-band`
- Current location: the Crescent Forest, outside the gith screen; home station `location-tenpugs-temple`
- Current route, when traveling: timber and materials treks, temple ↔ Crescent Forest
- Role: Engineer and builder

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Proposed.)* A mantis-like thri-kreen standing well over six feet, sand-coloured chitin scored and patched from years of construction work rather than combat. Four arms, two of which are permanently occupied with tools. Fitted harness rather than armour, hung with measuring cord, bone chisels, and wedges.

The chitin around the upper joints is polished smooth by the rope harness he uses for hauling timber. It is the only part of him that looks cared for.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)* Clipped, literal, and constructive. Ch'k-Chakal treats every problem as a materials-and-load question and is visibly relieved when one turns out to be exactly that. He clicks while thinking, which the band has long since stopped noticing.

He does not participate in the meeting tents. He was not present for the debates and would not have attended if he had been.

### Public Reputation
Valued and slightly apart. The band is entirely comfortable with him and does not quite think of him as one of them — which is roughly how he thinks of the band, so the arrangement holds.

## Confirmed Facts
- Thri-kreen **engineer** of Tenpug's Band.
- Works out of the empty tent (area 5) alongside the carpenters, pounding, carving, and making what he can out of the little wood available.
- The carpenters and Ch'k-Chakal go on supply treks into the Crescent Forest for materials and for whatever else the band needs.
- **He is away on exactly such a trip when the adventurers arrive.**
- The carpenters and engineers return in three days *provided they can find a way through the gith patrols surrounding the temple area*. If the PCs have made no effort to clear those patrols, the DM is instructed to assume they do not get through — and the gith may throw the bodies of the dead down into the camp to taunt the band.
- About 30 people return to the band if an opening is made. With them, the band fields 18 BATTLESYSTEM figures instead of 15.
- Thri-kreen do not sleep.

## Resources and Capabilities
- **Engineering.** He is the reason the band's fortification plans are viable at all. The module is explicit that building defences is hard *because the carpenters and engineers are away*.
- Timber knowledge — what the nine great logs in the wood pile can and cannot be made into, and whether the old watch tower can be raised again.
- Thri-kreen physiology: no sleep requirement, four limbs, natural weapons, and the ability to keep watch indefinitely.
- Standing route knowledge between the temple and the Crescent Forest.

## Current Activity
Somewhere in or beyond the Crescent Forest with roughly thirty of the band's carpenters and engineers, unable to get home.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the counter to the gith lord's dream assault, two adventures early.** Adventure Two's psionic attack works only on sleepers. The printed text states plainly that thri-kreen do not sleep and so are not affected — and that a thri-kreen still conscious can spot the imp [[Kalisore]] flying in, find the black stone it plants, and break the spell by picking it up. If Ch'k-Chakal marches with Tenpug's army, that role is already cast. If the PCs never rescued him, it is not.
- **This makes the carpenter extraction retroactively enormous.** A party that ignores the patrol-screen problem in Adventure One loses thirty fighters, all of the band's construction expertise, *and* the one creature in the army immune to the attack that defines Adventure Two. None of this is signposted in the module. It should be.
- *(Proposed.)* He has been eating. Thri-kreen are obligate carnivores and there are gith patrols between him and home. The band does not need to be told about this and he does not intend to volunteer it.

## Proposed Developments
- **Strongly recommended:** foreground the extraction. Between [[Fala]]'s forest knowledge and [[Ara]]'s patrol timings, the party has everything needed, and the payoff lands two adventures later.
- If the campaign wants the loss to bite instead, the gith throwing bodies into the camp is printed and is available.
- If he survives to Adventure Two, give him the imp scene and let it be his. It is the only moment in the campaign where being a thri-kreen is the entire answer.
- Longer term: an engineer is exactly what an army besieging a stockaded iron mine needs. He is useful in Adventures Two and Three in ways the module never uses him for.
