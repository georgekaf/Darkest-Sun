# Actor: Sira

## One-Sentence Summary
Kharanok's animal-herder — blunt, mountain-wise, and the one who taught the party how to calm wild kank instead of fighting them.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: Kharanok village
- Current location: Kharanok (Pens)
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Animal-herder

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A lean, weathered desert woman with sun-darkened skin, sharp observant eyes, and a practical, unsentimental expression — not aggressive, but clearly tired of being ignored. Dusty hair braided or tied back, wind-burned face, cracked lips, wiry build from years working the pens and mountain trails. Wears practical herder's clothing built for desert and rocky terrain: a patched hide vest or light leather armor, wrapped arms, rough cloth layers, a sand-colored scarf, a worn utility belt, animal-handling rope, a small bone knife, trail markers, and a battered leather folio strapped across her chest or held at her side. Dusty boots, knee wraps. No shiny metal, no ornate decoration, nothing noble or magical about her. She reads as someone who tracks signs others miss — claw marks, dust trails, animal patterns, hidden mountain paths.

### Manner and Voice
Blunt, direct. Knows the mountain trails.

### Public Reputation
The person to go to for anything involving Kharanok's animals.

## Confirmed Facts
- Tends an injured kank early on (ad1); assigns the party the Syggra-recovery problem — 10 stolen by Gith, only adults taken, young left to starve.
- ad2: gives the party torches from the village's limited stock, after they promise (again) to recover the rest of the stolen Syggra.
- ad4/ad5: taught the party a soothing sound used to calm kank instead of fighting them — a domesticated-stock handling technique. Used directly in ad5 to tame two wild kank and lead them home peacefully. See [[Taming Wild Kank]].
- Referred to consistently by ad5 as "the village's animal-herder" (explicitly not "kank-keeper").
- Design doc quest-giver credit: Act 1, Hook 3 ("The Herder Knows Something") — tracks strange signs for weeks (dust trails, claw marks, dragging sounds), dismissed by Yethras, keeps secret records, shares only if taken seriously.
