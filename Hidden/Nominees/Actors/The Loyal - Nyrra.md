# Actor: The Loyal — Nyrra

## One-Sentence Summary
The Loyal's severe young preserver, who enforces Jessix's rule against defiling more rigidly than Jessix does, and who says almost nothing until something comes out of the tar.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: [[The Loyal]] (`faction-the-loyal`), follower
- Current location: the wastes within 20 miles of the Ruins of Giustenal (`location-giustenal-ruins`)
- Current route, when traveling: with the band
- Role: Preserver, the band's arcane weight

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Tall and slender, angular features, intense amber eyes, long dark hair braided with bone beads. Layered faded-red and charcoal desert robes over light leather armor, practical rather than ceremonial, with cloth wraps protecting her arms from the sun. A bone short sword and a small pouch of spell components. One hand often half-raised, as though a spell is already half-shaped.

### Manner and Voice
Clipped, quiet, severe. Does not repeat herself. Refuses arguments rather than winning them.

### Public Reputation
Barely known. Travelers remember the band, not her — which suits her.

## Confirmed Facts
- Source states only that Jessix roams with five young elves called the Loyal, preservers and thieves, who witnessed Slinnasia's death and believe him innocent (Campaign Book p. 11-12).
- Both Giustenal expeditions are established in the source's account of Jessix. These five survived both.

## Traits and Pressures
- Caution: 4
- Secrecy: 4 — not conspiratorial, simply private.
- Loyalty: Absolute to Jessix's principles; her loyalty to Jessix the person runs through them.
- Cruelty: None, but no mercy at all toward defilers.
- Risk tolerance: Low, except where preserving costs more than defiling would — she pays that price on purpose.

## Resources and Capabilities
- Preserver magic, cast under strict self-imposed limits.
- Component pouch maintained out of desert materials; she can resupply where a city wizard could not.
- The band's judgement on what magic they encounter, including anything they pull out of the ruins.
