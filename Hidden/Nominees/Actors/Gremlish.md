## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the best-constructed trap in Book Three and it is entirely fair.** Every element is visible in the text: he is unchained, he is scarred, he claims to be out of spells, he volunteers a treasure lead, and he is friends with the one prisoner who insists on opening every cell. **A careful party has four independent tells before he ever moves.**
- **The chimera lair is a genuine kill box.** Area 17 holds two chimerae that have already eaten three githyanki examiners, in a room dented and blackened from their attempts to escape. **They attack anyone who enters.** Gremlish walks the party in and turns invisible.
- **Psychic surgery makes him unturnable.** He cannot be reasoned with, bribed, or reminded of Athas. Trinth did not recruit him — **she rewrote him.** This is also the party's clearest look at what the queen actually does to people, three adventures before they meet her, and it is sitting in a cell being friendly.
- **He is a coward with a lethal spell list, which is a specific tactical problem.** Invisible, out of range, and holding *cloudkill* and *wall of force*. He will not fight the party fairly and does not need to. **A party that lets him escape the prison complex has an invisible 10th-level defiler behind them for the rest of the adventure.**
- **The side-switching clause is printed and worth honouring literally:** *in any battle that looks as if it will go against the characters, he will switch sides and attack the party.* He is not waiting for the chimerae. He is waiting for them to start losing anything.
- *(Proposed.)* The burn and cut scars on his face are surgical. They are where Trinth's people worked.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** plant him early and let him be genuinely useful once — a real piece of information, freely given — before the treasure lead. The betrayal is worth more if he earned something first.
- Route the exposure through [[Bardak]]. The dwarf defending him is a better scene than a detection spell.
- If the party leaves him locked up, he should still get out, and should turn up later at the worst possible moment. He has *knock*.
- His scars and his manufactured loyalty are the campaign's cheapest preview of Trinth. Let a PC with psionics notice something wrong in his head and be unable to say what.
