# Actor: Ari Evan

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-ari-evan`. **Level conflict in source — see Sources.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A charming, energetic woman in her sixties who has concluded that the gith are a superior race, that their dominion over Athas is inevitable, and that her best move is to position herself early as a high-ranking servant of it.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc

- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: the Square of Gurdek — senior member
- Current location: `location-yathazor` — the Refuge, areas 6–7
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Preserver; the Square's ideologue and its only outright evil member

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A human woman in her sixties — **wrinkles and scars showing that the years have taken their toll**, and moving with the energy of someone forty years younger. Bracers of defence instead of armour. A quarterstaff, a steel dagger +1, and six steel darts.

Her parlour is the only tidy room in the Refuge: a stone table covered with paper and scrolls, all neatly organised.

### Manner and Voice
**Animated, talkative, and sometimes genuinely charming.** Her wit and insight have grown rather than faded, and she is the easiest member of the Square to talk to — which is exactly why she is the one who talks to guests.

Her doctrine, delivered readily and at length:

> *"The only way to survive is to adapt. We see in this city a beautiful example of adaptation. Here one could live without fear of the burning sun or the biting wind . . . or fear of the sorcerer-kings. Slate is a bold pioneer who sees that the ways of the earth spirits can lead us to a better life. And the gith—well, they've been living here, underground, for centuries. Maybe it's time we learned how they do it rather than fighting them. I've lived a long life and I've seen plenty of surprising developments. Our coming to recognize the gith as brothers and sisters instead of enemies wouldn't be much of a surprise as I reckon it."*

### Public Reputation
Within the Square: the articulate one, the one who writes things down. To visitors: the friendly face of a group of lunatics.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Human female preserver / earth cleric**, and **chaotic evil** — the only member of the Square with an evil alignment. Intelligence 17, Wisdom 17, Charisma 15.
- In her sixties, scarred and wrinkled, with a young woman's energy.
- **She strongly believes the gith are superior beings in ways humans simply do not understand, and hopes to qualify as a high-ranking servant in the gith-dominated world that will "inevitably" develop.**
- Like Ranza, **she has not gained enough experience to use her former, non-clerical abilities without penalising her progress in her new class** — so she uses her preserver magic **only when it is obvious the Square is in danger.**
- **Her *teleport other* wild talent was what made the move to Yathazor possible**, letting her send in [[Olton]] and [[Morlah]], who cannot *ignore earth*.
- If the Square doubts the PCs' motives, **Ari Evan uses ESP to scan their minds** — leaving first "to get more food," then casting from hiding.
- **If Slate and Ari Evan have time to prepare before combat, Slate casts *bramblestaff* on her staff.**
- **If the Square is beaten and scatters, she uses her scroll to call an invisible stalker and summon monsters to send against the party.**
- Her scrolls in area 6 hold the supposed history of Yathazor as explained by Slate, cosmological musings, half-baked theories about the relation between wizard and clerical magic, and **horrible poetry praising the "noble" Slate**. The PCs must read for **at least an hour** before it becomes obvious there is nothing useful in them.
- Her bedroom chest is **fire-trapped (1d4+3)**. Inside are her spell "books": **seven ceramic tablets**, each protected by **explosive runes — 6d4+6 to the reader with no saving throw**, and the same or half to anyone within 10 feet. Each tablet must roll a save vs magical fire of 3 or better to survive the blast.
- Also in the chest: personal effects, a piece of amber (15 sp), and a very fine amethyst (21 sp).
- Equipment: bracers of defence AC 3, steel dagger +1, quarterstaff, six steel darts, *fruit of persuasiveness*, and a **scroll of preserver spells** — death spell, disintegrate, geas, globe of invulnerability, invisible stalker, monster summoning IV, reincarnation — **itself guarded by explosive runes.**
- Wild talents: teleport, teleport other. 80 PSPs.
- Recruited by charm, initiated by Gurdek, insane but with identity and memory intact, resistant to mind reading.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the Square's actual danger, not Slate.** Slate is higher level and leaves at half hit points. Ari Evan has *fireball*, *chaos*, *stoneskin*, and a scroll with *death spell* and *disintegrate* on it — and the printed instruction that **after the Square is beaten she uses that scroll to call an invisible stalker and summon monsters against the party**. The Refuge fight is not the end of her.
- **The ESP scan is the hinge of the whole encounter.** If the party keeps its purpose hidden they can stay unmolested; if not, the Square attacks before they get a chance to rest and recover. Ari Evan leaving the room "to get more food" is the tell, and it is easy to miss and fair to catch.
- **Her ideology is not Slate's.** Slate believes the gith are heirs of the earth spirits. Ari Evan believes the gith are *going to win*, and has picked a side accordingly. She is a collaborator, not a convert, and she is the only member who would survive Slate's death intact.
- **The ceramic tablets are a trap for greedy players.** Seven tablets, explosive runes, 6d4+6 with **no saving throw** to the reader plus splash. A party that loots her bedroom without care will take real damage, and will destroy the spells they were trying to steal.
- *(Proposed.)* She has already worked out that the gith answer to something else. She has not mentioned it to Slate, because Slate's theory is what keeps the Square together and her position in it.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** make her the negotiator. She should be the one who greets the party, feeds them, charms them, and quietly decides their fate. The reveal that the pleasant old woman is the chaotic evil one is worth more than any combat beat in the Refuge.
- If she survives and scatters, run the printed retaliation — invisible stalker and summoned monsters, hitting the party when they think the adventure has moved on.
- Her scroll is a major magic item and a legitimate reward for beating her properly.
- If the campaign wants a human who ends up serving the githyanki in Adventures Six or Seven, she has already applied for the job.
