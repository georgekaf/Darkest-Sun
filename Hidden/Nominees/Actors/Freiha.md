# Actor: Freiha

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-freiha`. **One of only three parties the module names as potential friends inside New Giustenal — and there is a contract killer working her floor. The shared record for the Pit, munge and the [[Casskka]] plot lives here; [[Gatlakk]], [[Casskka]] and [[Lodgden]] cross-reference it.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A female dray who built the best tavern in New Giustenal on a fermented mushroom liquor nobody else can reproduce, and who has no idea that her competitor has hired the new server to steal the recipe or kill her.

## Confirmed Facts
- **A female dray named Freiha runs this successful tavern with her mate, [[Gatlakk]].**
- **The Pit is known for a strong elixir called munge that is made from fermented mushrooms.**
- **None of the other establishments in New Giustenal have managed to duplicate the process, so Freiha and Gatlakk have turned quite a profit in the last few years.**
- **So much, in fact, that [[Casskka]] of the Beetle's Bite has hired a rogue named [[Lodgden]] to either steal the couple's secret, or send them to the Gray.**
- **So far, Lodgden has met with little success — though she has managed to secure a job as a server in the Pit.**
- **The impatient Casskka is urging her to finish her business quickly.**
- **Freiha the tavern owner (location 12) is named as one of the people who could become the PCs' friends**, alongside some of the fishermen (location 5) and **Akrag** (see [[Absalom]]) — *"depending on the actions of the group."*
- The Pit sits in a city of **3,000 dray with no slaves**, whose diet is supplemented by **cultivated fungi fields (area 7)** and **sweet fruit from the Upside-Down Forest (area 10)**.
- The rival house, the **Beetle's Bite (area 14)**, **hasn't done very well since the competition developed munge**, and **its sole redeeming feature is cheap blackjaw** — Casskka's brother is a fisherman and gives her a discount on the fish.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the party's best route into New Giustenal society and she is on a clock.** *"The impatient Casskka is urging her to finish her business quickly."* **Whether the party arrives before or after Lodgden acts is the GM's call, and it determines whether Freiha is an ally, a corpse, or a grieving widow.**
- **The whole plot fits in one paragraph and plays like a full adventure.** A rival, a hired rogue already embedded as staff, a secret worth killing for, an impatient client, and a printed statement that Freiha *could become the PCs' friend*. **Nothing else in New Giustenal gives the party a reason to care about an individual dray.**
- **Saving her is the cheapest possible way to earn a safe house in the enemy capital.** The module's own advice is *"the PCs should strive to keep themselves from being noticed by Dregoth. Finding friends in the city aids in this endeavour."* **A tavern owner who owes them her life is exactly that.**
- **[[Lodgden]] is Lawful Neutral, level 8, and has a wild talent for appraisal.** She is a professional, not a thug, and she has been failing for a while. **She may be stalling because she does not want to kill anyone.** See [[Lodgden]].
- **The munge monopoly is a motive with more than one interested party.** In a city with no slaves, cultivated fungi fields and a state church, **the one man who could simply take the recipe by decree has not.** Why the templarate has never involved itself is unexplained and useful.
- *(Proposed.)* She is the only person in New Giustenal likely to serve a stranger without reporting them. **That is worth more to the party than any weapon in the palace.**

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: put the party in the Pit early**, and let them notice the server watching the back rooms before anything happens.
- **Let the party choose the outcome.** Warn Freiha, turn Lodgden, expose Casskka, or arrive too late. All four are good and all four change what New Giustenal is for the rest of the campaign.
- If she lives and knows why, the Pit becomes the party's base of operations in a city that wants them dead.
- Decide what munge actually does mechanically. *"A strong elixir"* in a city of dray is worth defining.
- Give her and Gatlakk one distinguishing feature each — the module gives them a shared paragraph and nothing else. See [[Gatlakk]].
