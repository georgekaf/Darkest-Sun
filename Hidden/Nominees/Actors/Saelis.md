# Actor: Saelis

## One-Sentence Summary
The youngest of the Loyal and the only one who still says out loud that saving travelers one at a time will add up — a preserver whose idealism the others protect as carefully as they protect him.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: [[The Loyal]] (`faction-the-loyal`), follower
- Current location: the wastes within 20 miles of the Ruins of Giustenal (`location-giustenal-ruins`)
- Current route, when traveling: with the band
- Role: Preserver; the band's face when they approach strangers

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
The youngest-looking of the five: tall, slim, softer-featured, sun-darkened, with large alert eyes and long reddish-brown hair tied loosely behind the shoulders. Pale ochre traveling robes over simple leather protection, faded green cloth strips, small carved bone charms. A bone short sword and a worn spell pouch. Upright, open stance; determined but inexperienced expression.

### Manner and Voice
Earnest, direct, unguarded. Explains more than he should to people he has just met.

### Public Reputation
The one who speaks first, and the reason travelers trust the band at all instead of running from five armed elves.

## Confirmed Facts
- Source states only that Jessix roams with five young elves called the Loyal, preservers and thieves, who witnessed Slinnasia's death and believe him innocent (Campaign Book p. 11-12).
- Both Giustenal expeditions are established in the source's account of Jessix. These five survived both.

- Caution: 2 — the band's main liability.

- Risk tolerance: High, from inexperience rather than courage.
- Status: Protected. He resents it.

## GM-Only Secrets
- He is the one the others would rescue first, and he knows it, and it is slowly turning into something harder — the safest route to a defiler's bargain in this band runs through him, not through Nyrra.

## Proposed Developments
- Easiest hook for a party: he approaches them openly and tells them far too much.
- If the campaign ever wants a Loyal casualty, his death is the one that breaks the band.
