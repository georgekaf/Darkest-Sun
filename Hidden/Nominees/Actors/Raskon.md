# Actor: Raskon

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-raskon`. **Distinct from [[Raxxon]]**, the elf of Tenpug's Band — the printed module's near-identical "Rakskon" was superseded by the canonical-names ruling, which incidentally cleared this collision.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The githyanki general who built the Nightmare Gate, watched it break in front of him, blamed a subordinate, and now has one chance to recover his standing by personally supervising the enslavement of the people who ruined him.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active — **or dead, with a brother waiting; see [[Raskine]]**
- Faction or allegiance: `faction-trinth-githyanki` — general
- Current location: `location-nightmare-gate`, then the Galnaki prison complex
- Current route, when traveling: through the Gate; and by plane shift, as all githyanki
- Role: General; constructor of the Nightmare Gate; overseer of the PCs as slaves

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
He steps out of the Gate itself. **Brilliant green light pours out and a powerful figure steps into sight** — the hooded drummers around the arch having just risen and removed their masks to reveal that they are githyanki.

Chain mail +3 and a long sword +1. Genius intelligence.

### Manner and Voice
**He gloats.** In the prison he is not conducting an interrogation; he is enjoying himself:

> *"I hope you are all comfortable. You shall spend the rest of your lives here as slaves of the queen. The queen has big plans for you and I'm pleased to say that I'll be overseeing your progress myself. I trust that you'll all recognize that this fate is as inescapable as the prison that surrounds you."*

**He answers questions with a sneer.** He does not really want to give the characters any helpful information — **but he does not expect them ever to have a chance to use it**, so if it can be passed off as part of an insult, he will usually answer.

He punctuates the conversation with **threats, verbal abuse, and the occasional volley of blows.**

### Public Reputation
A general of Trinth's, and one whose stock has fallen. **The characters were responsible for his failure on Athas and his position in the city is not all that secure.**

## Confirmed Facts
- **Githyanki general, charged by Trinth with the construction of the Nightmare Gate.** He named it.
- Trinth called him — *one of her most trusted officers* — specifically because the region around Yathazor resists plane shifting and she needed a fixed portal to bring an army through.
- **He commands the Gate's activation ritual.** At the climax of Adventure Six the drummers are githyanki in hoods, and Raskon steps out of the opened Gate.
- **In the battle, Raskon orders the four gish to attack while staying out of harm's way himself if at all possible.**
- Statistics: THAC0 17, AC 2 (chain mail +3), HD 7, hp 45, **INT Genius**, ML 14, XP 5,000, PSPs 180, all attack and defence modes, Score 16. Spells: charm person, colour spray, magic missile ×2, invisibility, stinking cloud, lightning bolt.
- **He blamed the captain [[Trasrani]] for the damage to the Nightmare Gate on Athas**, and had him imprisoned. Trasrani knows the imprisonment was unfair.
- In Adventure Seven **he visits the PCs in their cells to gloat**, accompanied by **five githyanki guards**, one of whom carries a sword that appears to be made of solid silver.
- **The characters were responsible for his failure on Athas and his position in the city is not all that secure. He hopes that by using the characters to further the queen's ends he can recover some of the status he lost.**
- Under questioning he gives, in the form of insults: that they are in **Galnaki, the city of the queen**; that **Trinth is a great and glorious queen who someday will rule all of Athas**; that **the PCs are to be "the fuel that ignites our complete rule of Athas"**; and, of the metal, that **it is "merely one of the many tools at our disposal."**
- **If the characters attack him, the five guards respond quickly and brutally.**
- **If the PCs killed him in Adventure Six, he is replaced in Adventure Seven by his brother [[Raskine]]**, who is understandably vengeful.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **His gloating is the module's exposition delivery and it is deliberately leaky.** He does not want to help them, but he cannot conceive of them escaping, so he answers. **Do not let this run too long** — the printed instruction is that if the questioning gets persistent he remembers himself and leaves. Give the players three or four good answers and then take him away.
- **He is the reason [[Trasrani]] will betray the party.** Raskon blamed him publicly for the Gate. Trasrani wants back in. **If the party releases Trasrani, the githyanki has an obvious route to rehabilitation: hand Raskon the people who ruined him.** The two characters are a single mechanism and should be run as one.
- **He does not fight.** In Adventure Six he orders the gish forward and stays clear. In Adventure Seven he brings five guards. A party that finally corners him should find a general who has never personally had to.
- **He is probably not in on it.** Trinth's actual objective — breeding psionic githyanki to overthrow their own lich-queen — is hidden from her followers because they would find it obscene. **Raskon is a loyal officer serving a queen who is planning treason against his race.** If the party learns this before he does, they have a lever on the campaign's second-most powerful githyanki.
- **The brother is a fail-safe, not a character.** [[Raskine]] exists so the gloating scene happens regardless of Adventure Six's outcome. Whether he is more than that is a campaign decision.
- *(Proposed.)* The silver sword carried by one of his escort is a githyanki **silver sword** — the weapon that cuts silver cords on the Astral Plane, and which githyanki will never willingly let fall into non-githyanki hands. Raskon walking one into a cell block is either a status display or a threat.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** play the prison visit as a performance for an audience of one — himself. He is a man rebuilding his reputation in front of prisoners because there is nobody else left who has to listen.
- Connect him to [[Zuzar]] explicitly. If he was one of the two commanders who threatened her life over the iron, the party has met his handiwork three adventures running without seeing his face.
- If he survives to the finale, he should be the one covering Trinth's retreat, and he should do it badly.
- The silver sword in his escort is a lootable object of enormous significance to both gith races — see the note above.
