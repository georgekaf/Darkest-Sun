# Actor: Rhazek

## One-Sentence Summary
A slave-born mul priest of fire whose faith is the one thing about the party that other powers refuse to tolerate — the water spirits of the Bloody Red Oasis demanded he be handed over before they would let anyone drink, and he answered by threatening to boil their lake dry.

## Classification
- Subtype: player-character
- Control: player-character — played by **slos (Giannis)**, campaign *Altar of Dust*
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: none; travels with the Altar of Dust party
- Current location: Kharanok (`location-kharanok`)
- Current route, when traveling: none standing
- Role: Priest of fire — front-line caster and the party's cook

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Mul — the sterile human-dwarf cross, bred for labour. Leather armour and shield, a mace, a holy symbol he does not hide.

### Manner and Voice
Short-tempered where his faith is concerned. Threatened an oasis full of spirits rather than accept being singled out. Otherwise practical: he is the one who butchers and cooks when there is meat and hungry people.

### Public Reputation
Anywhere elemental water is venerated, a fire priest is a grievance walking. The spirits at the oasis knew what he was before anyone spoke.

## Confirmed Facts
- Confirmed present from **ad6** ("The Sand Also Rises") onward. Previously misspelled *Razek* in the ad6 file; corrected.
- **ad6, day 115:** at the oasis south of the mountains, the spirits refuse the party water while they keep *"the one who serves fire"* with them, and demand he be handed over as atonement for what fire priests did to them in the past. **Cypoul refuses.** Rhazek, angry, threatens to burn the lake until it evaporates entirely.
- **ad6:** the spirits counter-offer — everyone else may drink and rest, provided Rhazek stays outside all day, no shade, no water. The party **refuses the terms and does not surrender him.** No deal is struck. Cypoul counts roughly fifteen spirits hovering over the water.
- **ad6:** calls flames onto the giant snake in the Zharvek ruins during the fight Shaka ends with a shot through its open mouth.
- **ad6:** cooks the snake meat on the spot for the seven starving survivors at Zharvek — the meal that opens the negotiation which brings Tzala and six others back to Kharanok.
- **ad7:** not present.
