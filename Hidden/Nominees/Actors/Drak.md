## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Proposed — the source gives none.)* A gith of unusual size and unusual filth, holding a room built for a templar. The operations building was once a fine place, with polished gleaming wood walls and well-made furniture; it is now defaced with crude graffiti and splashes of dried blood, and Drak has done nothing to change that because he cannot see anything wrong with it.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed, extrapolated from the single printed adjective.)* Aggressive on contact and aggressive on reflection. The module calls him *offensive*, which is doing a lot of work — read as personally repellent as well as combatively inclined. He is loud in a small room and enjoys being looked at.

### Public Reputation
Among the gith at the mine: Askai's man, and therefore obeyed. After Askai falls: obeyed less.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Drak only exists in one branch.** The module places him in the operations building specifically *after* Askai has been mentally defeated. If the party killed Askai outright instead of driving him insane, the printed text says a replacement assumed command and the fort is well defended — and Drak is the obvious candidate for that replacement, running a much tighter defence. If they never beat Askai at all, Askai is still in that room and Drak is nobody. **Three states, one clause of source text.**
- **He is the best-placed interrogation target for what lies below.** The party is about to go into the mine tunnels toward Zigath's Nest with almost no information. Askai's own lieutenant, taken alive at the stockade, is the natural bridge — and the module never suggests it because it never develops him at all.
- *(Proposed.)* He does not know what happened to Askai. He knows his master went quiet and the room became available. A lieutenant who thinks his commander has abandoned him behaves very differently from one who knows his commander was destroyed.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** give him the fort's defence in the killed-Askai branch, and give him a name the players hear before they meet him. He is the only individual antagonist at the stockade and currently has no lines.
- Use him as the bridge into Adventure Three. Anything he says about the tunnels, the nest, or Zigath is information the module otherwise withholds entirely.
- If the campaign wants the stockade assault to have a face rather than a headcount, this is the face available.
