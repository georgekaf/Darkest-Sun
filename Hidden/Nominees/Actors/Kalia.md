# Actor: Kalia (née Yelka) (update to existing actor-kalia, revision 3)

## One-Sentence Summary
Pragmatic rations and cistern steward who quietly organized Kharanok's council after her husband's death — and put Elder Yethras forward as its face.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: Kharanok village council (organizer)
- Current location: Kharanok
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Rations/resource and cistern steward; de facto organizer of the village council

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A middle-aged desert woman with a controlled, rigid posture, a tired face, sharp professional eyes, dry lips, and tightly bound hair — a severe expression held together by discipline and, underneath it, fear. She reads as efficient, clipped, and precise, never openly villainous. Dressed in practical administrative desert clothing: clean but worn linen layers, a sand-colored robe or long vest, a narrow belt hung with keys, wax tablets or a ledger case, a bone stylus, small water-measure tokens, and a sealed ration pouch, with simple sandals or wrapped boots — neater than most villagers, but not wealthy or ornate. No jewelry except perhaps one small official token or cistern key. Her body language suggests rigid control under pressure: shoulders tight, hands close to her ledger or key ring, stance slightly guarded, as if always keeping people away from something behind her — exhausted and defensive beneath a layer of bureaucratic authority. What reads from the outside as "too efficient, something is off" is, underneath, a grieving widow holding a village together by sheer will and refusing to let it show.

### Manner and Voice
Pragmatic, efficient, clipped.

**Sample dialogue / introduction scene (as written by the GM):**
> The Cistern is colder than the rest of Kharanok.
>
> The air here is damp stone, old prayers, and measured fear. Water trickles through carved channels into dark basins marked with age-worn cuts and tally lines. Every vessel is counted. Every hand that reaches for water is watched. Every voice lowers without being told.
>
> Kallia Yelka stands at the north side of the chamber, beside a stone table covered in wax tablets, ration tokens, clay seals, and a ledger bound in cracked leather.
>
> She does not look up immediately.
>
> "Names," she says.
>
> Her voice is flat, precise, and already impatient.
>
> A young assistant beside her glances at the newcomers, then down at the ledger. Kallia lifts one hand without looking. The assistant stops moving.
>
> "Not yours. Theirs."
>
> Only then does she raise her eyes.
>
> Kallia is a middle-aged human woman with a tired, severe face and the posture of someone who survives by never wasting motion. Her hair is bound tight. Her clothing is practical and clean, but worn at the cuffs from years of work around wet stone and mineral grit. A ring of cistern keys hangs at her belt. None of them make a sound when she moves.
>
> "You are the new denizens Yethras mentioned."
>
> She says it without welcome.
>
> "This is not a market. Not a shrine. Not a place for curiosity. This is the reason Kharanok is still standing."
>
> She closes the ledger with one hand.
>
> "If you need water, you will be recorded. If you need access, you will be denied unless the request comes with council authority and a practical reason. If you are here to inspect the channels, the answer is no."
>
> A slow drip echoes from the far end of the chamber.
>
> Kallia's eyes flick south.
>
> Only for a moment.
>
> Then she looks back at the newcomers, composed again.
>
> "The Cistern is functioning. The rations are controlled. The spring is stable enough for current demand. Anything else you have heard is village talk from people who mistake fear for expertise."
>
> She picks up a bone stylus and taps it once against the closed ledger.
>
> "So. State your business clearly. Then decide whether you are here to help this village survive, or merely to make its problems louder."

### Public Reputation
Known publicly as the cistern overseer and a resource distributor — "too efficient, something is off." Her deeper role organizing the council behind Yethras is not publicly known.

## Confirmed Facts
- Born **Kallia Yelka** — "Yelka" is her maiden name, from before her marriage.
- Husband died during the Gith invasion.
- By Day 109, had assumed a stronger role distributing Kharanok's scarce resources, rewarding useful survivors with additional water (existing actor-kalia entry).
- Took on this leading role specifically *after* her husband's death — direct consequence of that loss, not a separate development.
- Gathered a handful of people and formed a small council; deliberately put Elder Yethras forward as its spokesperson/public face.
- Design doc credits this role to "Kallia Yelka, Cistern overseer" — now confirmed to be the same person as Kalia, under her maiden name, rather than a separate NPC.
