# Actor: Passk

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-passk`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A dwarf silt pirate whose first mate was made to murder the crew in their sleep, who woke up with a focus he cannot possibly complete — kill the Caller in Darkness — and who has been drinking in a shack in Cromlin waiting to become a banshee.

- Faction or allegiance: none; former silt pirate
- Current location: `location-passks-shack`, Cromlin (area 10)
- Current route, when traveling: none — his skimmer is beached and damaged
- Role: Former silt pirate captain; obsessive

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A dwarf in **studded leather**, in a dilapidated shack. **78 hit points at 8th level** — physically he is one of the toughest individuals in the region.

**He currently has no weapons, as he sold them to pay for drinks.**

### Manner and Voice
Sullen, drunk, and hopeless — **until the moment someone credible offers him a way to Giustenal**, at which point **the dwarf's sullen mood will change almost instantly.**

**Passk is obsessed, but he is not insane.** That distinction is printed and matters.

### Public Reputation
A washed-up pirate drinking himself to death in a shack at the edge of Cromlin.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the third instance of the dwarven-focus thread and the only one who states it outright.** Per the vault's `Rules/Ancestries.md`, dwarves pursue a focus they must fulfil before death **for fear of undeath otherwise.** [[Roi]] refuses to hold one. [[Pimns]] and [[Solaq]] died with theirs unfulfilled and became banshees at the chasm. **Passk knows exactly what is coming for him and says so.** If the campaign has been running that thread, this is where it becomes explicit.
- **His focus is functionally impossible and that is the horror.** *Kill the Caller in Darkness* — an entity that is an aggregate of thousands of absorbed psyches, that killed a whole crew through a proxy, and that no one in the region can even locate reliably. **He has bound his afterlife to a task he cannot do alone.** The PCs arriving is not a coincidence to him; it is the only chance he will ever get.
- **He is the module's designated entry vehicle and he is cheap.** He has the wall map *and* a repairable skimmer, and **his price is passage.** A party that treats the drunk in the shack as scenery walks to Giustenal without either.
- **The distinction "obsessed, but not insane" is load-bearing.** He is a competent 8th-level warrior with a psionic combat talent who has correctly assessed his situation. **He should never be played as comic relief.**
- **He plays an important role in the Adventure Book** — the module says so explicitly and tells the DM to read it before the PCs meet him. **Check that before running him.**
- *(Proposed.)* His first mate may still be in Giustenal. The man went overboard *trying to reach it*, and the Caller absorbs the psyches of those who die there. **Passk has never considered that the person he most wants to kill and the person he most wants to find might be the same conversation.**

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** run him as a genuine recruitment. He should be assessing the party as hard as they are assessing him, and he should decline a party that does not convince him.
- If the campaign is running the focus thread, put a dwarf PC or NPC in the room. Passk saying *I'm going to be a banshee* to another dwarf is a very different scene.
- The first mate in Giustenal is a free, well-supported payoff.
- Buy him a weapon. It is the cheapest possible way for a party to change an NPC's entire disposition.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Fighter
- Level: 7 *(Shadowdark scaling of 2e 8)*
- Armor Class: 14 (studded leather)
- Hit Points: **78** — retained as printed; he is extraordinarily durable
- Movement: near (slow — dwarf)
- Strength +3, Dexterity +2, Constitution +3, Intelligence +1, Wisdom +1, Charisma +1
- Alignment: Lawful (neutral)
- Morale: **14 in Giustenal; 6 anywhere else.** He does not care about other fights.
- Attacks: 2 attacks per round.
  - *By weapon, +1 damage from Strength* — **he currently owns none.** Arm him and he becomes one of the party's better fighters.
- **Wild talent — Combat Mind:** PS 8, cost 5 + 4/round, from 105 PSPs. Initiative and tactical advantage while maintained.
- **Focus (the Caller in Darkness):** Passk has a declared dwarven focus. **Advantage on all rolls made in direct pursuit of killing the Caller.** If he dies with it unfulfilled, **the GM should treat undeath as a live outcome, not flavour** — see [[Pimns]] and [[Solaq]] for the precedent this campaign has already set.
- **Drunk:** until recruited, disadvantage on all checks and attacks. **This lifts the moment a credible party offers him passage** — the module says his mood changes *almost instantly.*
- **Running him at the table:** sullen and finished, then transformed, and never funny. He is a dying man with a deadline he did not choose.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Male Dwarf Warrior 8, LN, AC 6 (studded leather), MV 9, hp 78, THAC0 13, #AT 3/2, damage by weapon +1 Str, PSPs 105, wild talent Combat Mind (PS 8, cost 5+4/round). Scaled to Shadowdark level 7 with hit points retained.
