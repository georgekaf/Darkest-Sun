## One-Sentence Summary
An elf blamed by an entire tribe for a death he tried to prevent, who left rather than argue, and who now roams the wastes around Giustenal saving travellers from the thing that actually killed her.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
An elf in leather, moving fast. **Dark splotches stain his hands** — the thing everyone points at, and the thing everyone is wrong about.

He carries a **bone long sword +2**.

### Manner and Voice
**Should anyone ask Jessix if the tales are true, he will prove less than talkative. Most don't believe him any more and he has grown tired of trying to convince them otherwise.**

**His friends will tell sincere listeners the truth, however.**

### Public Reputation
**Something of a tragic legend in the Giustenal environs** — and, thanks to [[Jessareen]]'s songs, widely believed to be guilty. **Her recounting of the tale paints Jessix in a poor light, and most who hear it are forever convinced of his guilt.**

## Confirmed Facts
- **Male elf rogue 11 / preserver 9**, chaotic good. AC 5, hp 35, Charisma 17. **104 PSPs; wild talent *catfall*.**
- Formerly **Jessix Twilightcatcher** of the Sky Singers.
- **He and a few of his best companions twice entered the ruins of Giustenal, and now refuse to go within an arrow's shot of the accursed site.**
- **On the first trip, Jessix and his friends looted chambers belonging to Dregoth's templars**, unearthing several minor magical items that **earned tremendous profits at Nibenay.**
- **Encouraged by his success, Jessix insisted the elves return.** The second expedition met with disaster.
- **His best friend, a female elf named [[Slinnasia]], fell prey to the [[Caller in Darkness]].** She was **a young and beautiful preserver destined to become a powerful tribal leader.**
- **When she died, Jessix was held responsible. The dark splotches that stain his hands are held as evidence of his guilt.**
- **The truth:** he **tried his best to save Slinnasia, but the Caller in Darkness dominated her mind and urged her to take her own life inside the ruins.** **The black stains on his hands are the result of an encounter with a pit snatcher — an encounter which delayed him from reaching Slinnasia in time to save her.**
- **He was never truly exiled**, but realised the tribe was turning against him and left. **Five of the young elves who had accompanied him to Giustenal followed him.** These five became **[[The Loyal]]**.
- **Now Jessix and his companions roam the Giustenal environs and do their best to protect travellers from the terrible dangers of the region.** **Should a party need assistance within a 20-mile radius of Giustenal, there is a 5% chance Jessix and the Loyal will be near enough to help.**
- **Jessix knows a route through the tar pits of Giustenal.** He is **reluctant to share it, because he thinks a venture into Giustenal is an invitation to disaster** — but **if someone seems especially sympathetic to his past, he could be talked into drawing a map.** **Such a map eliminates the modifier for tar pit encounter checks.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the party's best possible entry point into the whole campaign and he does not want to be.** He has the tar-pit route, he has looted the templars' chambers, and he will not help — **unless the players engage with his past sincerely.** The module makes sympathy the key, not a Charisma check. **That is the correct way to run him.**
- **The 5% rescue chance is a gift the GM should spend deliberately.** Somewhere in the first act, when the party is genuinely in trouble in the environs, Jessix and five elves should arrive, solve it, and leave without explaining who they are.
- **[[Jessareen]] is the obstacle and she is also broken now.** She blamed him for her sister; **since her own encounter with [[Kataal the Mover]] beneath Giustenal her songs are strange and confused.** The two of them are on converging arcs and the module never brings them together. **A scene where Jessareen recants — or cannot — is available and unwritten.**
- **The stains are a pit snatcher's, and a party that fights one will recognise the marks.** That is a physical, verifiable exoneration sitting in the *Monstrous Compendium* appendix, and any party that encounters a pit snatcher can clear him.
- *(Proposed.)* He does not want to be exonerated. Being guilty is the only thing keeping him out of the ruins, and the ruins are where Slinnasia is.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** run the rescue first, the reputation second, and the truth third — from the Loyal, not from him.
- The pit-snatcher-stain exoneration is a clean, earnable payoff. Let a player work it out.
- If the campaign wants Jessix to enter Giustenal again, the only lever is someone else in danger inside it.
- A Jessareen/Jessix confrontation is the strongest untapped scene in the environs.
