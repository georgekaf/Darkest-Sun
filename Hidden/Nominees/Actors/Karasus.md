# Actor: Karasus

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-karasus`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A wild mul gladiator the queen has already finished with — mind wiped completely clean, standing in a cell staring at nothing, recoverable only by a properly worded *wish*.

- Faction or allegiance: none; a captured Athasian
- Current location: the Galnaki prison complex, area 12
- Current route, when traveling: —

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the campaign's answer to "what does Trinth actually do."** Everything else about her intentions arrives as rumour, boast or inference — the queen wants Athasian psionics, the psionicists don't come back, she can *operate on your mind.* Karasus is the demonstration, sitting in a cell, requiring no explanation.
- **Read him against [[Gremlish]] deliberately.** Trinth's psychic surgery produced two results in the same block: a blank, and a convert. **The convert is friendly, unchained, fully spelled, and lying to them right now.** A party that works out the connection has understood the queen before they ever meet her.
- **The *wish* requirement is a real hook and the module drops it.** *Only a properly worded wish can bring back the mind of the mul.* That is a campaign-length quest attached to an NPC most parties will walk past. If the players take him with them, they have adopted a problem that cannot be solved with anything they own.
- **He is portable and helpless.** A mul body with no mind, in a prison break, in a city of githyanki. Whether the party carries him is a genuine cost/character decision and the module never flags it as one.
- **"Wild mul" is a specific creature entry.** The ranger/druid identification points at the *Monstrous Compendium* Dark Sun appendix — meaning Karasus is not merely a mul but a feral one, which raises the question of whether the blankness is entirely the queen's doing or whether something was already different about him. The module does not pursue this.
- *(Proposed.)* He is the reason the queen's experiments have stalled. She keeps destroying what she is trying to study.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** put him in the party's path *before* Trinth is ever described, and let them draw the conclusion themselves.
- Make carrying him possible. If they do, he should be a burden with no payoff for the entire adventure — and then the campaign should give them a *wish* at some point, and mean it.
- If the party abandons him, that should be allowed to be the correct decision and still feel bad.
- Pair him explicitly with Gremlish in the same session. The two outcomes side by side are worth more than either alone.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Gladiator *(former)* — **wild mul**
- Level: —
- Armor Class: 10
- Hit Points: treat as a mul gladiator's frame; **he will not defend himself**
- Movement: near — **only if led**
- Strength +4, Dexterity +1, Constitution +4, Intelligence **—**, Wisdom **—**, Charisma **—**
- Alignment: none
- Morale: **—.** He does not fight, flee, or react.
- Attacks: **none.** He does not attack, even in self-defence.
- **Mind Wiped:** completely blank. Immune to persuasion, intimidation, telepathy, interrogation and psionic contact — **there is nothing to contact.** Cannot follow instructions, hold objects reliably, or feed himself.
- **Restoration:** **only a properly worded *wish*.** Nothing lesser works — not restoration, not heal, not psychic surgery in reverse.
- **Identification:** a ranger or druid recognises him as a **wild mul** on sight.
- **Running him at the table:** he is scenery that used to be a person. Do not give him a moment of lucidity. Do not let a player reach him with a good roll. The horror is that there is no check to make.
- **Conversion note:** the module gives Karasus **no stat block at all** — only a description, the wipe, and the *wish* requirement. Nothing above is a conversion; the ability scores are omitted deliberately because the source removed them.
