# Actor: Slate

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-slate`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A 15th-level earth cleric with a face of grey stone who was driven insane by the thing that initiated him, decided the gith are the rightful heirs of a githyanki city, and has spent twenty years assembling a congregation of people he charmed and broke to believe it with him.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: the Square of Gurdek — leader and founder; no faction record yet
- Current location: `location-yathazor` — the Refuge, area 4 on the poster map
- Current route, when traveling: a daily circuit to the eastern cavern mouth (3b) to re-lay its glyph
- Role: Earth cleric; founder and head of the Square of Gurdek

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A stately, confident man who gives an impression of power and wisdom. **His face is dark grey and almost as hard as rock — where another man would have wrinkles, he has cracks.** White hair cut very short.

Chain mail +1, a shield +4, and a steel long sword +3. He also carries a plain **wooden spear**, which he uses for one purpose only: it is the material component for *heartseeker*.

### Manner and Voice
Calm, articulate and entirely at peace. **Slate is blessed by a single-mindedness that does not allow him to question his own actions**, and it reads exactly as serenity. He is welcoming, hospitable, generous with food, and completely mad, and the two are not in tension.

His only moments of discomfort come in dreams, when he sometimes relives the terrifying touch of Gurdek that he experienced as a young man.

### Public Reputation
To his own people: a visionary. Ranza's assessment — *"The man's a visionary, I tell you. Not everyone could bear the weight of wisdom like he does"* — is representative and was volunteered without prompting.

To anyone else: unknown. He and his congregation live in a city no one on Athas knows exists.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Human male earth cleric, 15th level**, chaotic neutral. Wisdom 17, Charisma 3.
- Born **Harno**. As an eager young initiate he was buried in the earth for the standard ritual — but the elemental entity that came to him **was not a standard creature of the Elemental Plane of Earth.** It was some unknown and insane thing.
- **It slew his mentor and ravaged his mind, but it did grant him the powers of an earth cleric.** When he clawed his way back to the surface he no longer remembered his name, his master, or his past.
- Stumbling into the nearest village, he found people reacting to him with fear. In a mirror he saw why: **his face was grey and stony.** He took the name Slate.
- **He named the entity Gurdek, for reasons only he knows.**
- After decades of effort he learned to initiate others, and put a plan into action: he would talk with selected humans at length to gauge their insight and wisdom, then **charm and initiate the ones who seemed especially wise.**
- Each recruit was visited by Gurdek. **Some were rejected and driven mad; most made the transition.** The touch of Gurdek has bent all their psyches — they can maintain a facade of normality but are quite insane. Unlike Slate, they retained their identities and memories.
- In game terms he was creating **dual-class earth clerics**. Recruits with Wisdom below 17 could not make the transition and were rejected by Gurdek. **No one with Wisdom 19+ succumbed to his charm spell**, so every recruit has Wisdom 17 or 18 — and Slate, immune to charm himself, is satisfied that he is wiser than all of them.
- **More than 20 years ago he found Yathazor**, by using *ignore stone* to walk through the rock surrounding it.
- For years he theorised about the builders. He ruled out the hejkin because they do not work stone; ruled out the kenku because the buildings were designed for beings incapable of flight; ruled out the gith at first because they lack the sophistication — **then developed the theory that the gith are descendants of the builders and the rightful heirs of the city.** What began as a theory became, in his mind, first a fact and then an obsession.
- The group is called the **Square of Gurdek**, from their habit of sitting in a square when gathering in groups of more than four, with the most important members at the corners.
- About 50 days ago gith from the main nest found Yathazor — and Slate. **He welcomed them, told their leaders his theories, and helped them find a way through Yathazor and dig a connecting tunnel to Zigath's isolated nest.**
- **Every day he goes to the eastern cavern mouth and casts a glyph of warding on it**, set to inflict 15d4 on any creature weighing more than 300 pounds, to keep large monsters like behir out of Yathazor.
- Before going, **he always has the psionicist [[Pargo]] use *precognition* to be sure he will be safe.** If the PCs are likely to attack him, Pargo warns him not to go and he does not appear.
- If his life is in jeopardy — a 50% loss of hit points, for example — **he uses *ignore stone* and slips away into the earth.**
- He is immune to charm.

## Traits and Pressures
- Ambition: High, and entirely theological.
- Caution: Very high (5) — precognition before a routine errand, and an escape route through solid rock.
- Secrecy: 2. He is not hiding anything. He is delighted to explain.
- Loyalty: To Gurdek, and to the theory.
- Cruelty: Considerable and unrecognised. He has charmed, initiated and broken people, including two who are still following him around as wreckage.
- Risk tolerance: Low for himself, total for his recruits.
- Safety: Excellent — high level, heavily equipped, precognitive, and able to leave through a wall.
- Wealth: The Square's treasury (126 gp) and personal gems, hidden in a hollow boulder he shaped himself.
- Status: Absolute within the Square. Nonexistent outside it.

## Current Activity
Sitting on his patio above the Refuge, watching the city and dreaming of its past and future, while sculpting a twelve-foot statue of an entity he has never seen.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The Square is not hostile on sight, and that is the trap.** They welcome the PCs, share food, and don their armour while doing it — *being friendly doesn't mean being stupid*. Meanwhile [[Ranza]] stays out of sight for three turns making sure nobody is sneaking up. If the PCs' purpose stays hidden they can stay unmolested; more likely **the Square attacks before the party gets a chance to rest**, having retreated to "meditate" and planned it in private.
- **He is right about everything except the conclusion.** The builders were real, the city is real, the gith are genuinely descended from its builders. His single error — that this makes them heirs rather than casualties — is the entire difference between a scholar and the man who dug the tunnel that lets a gith army through.
- **His theology has a hole he cannot see.** He ruled out the hejkin because *they do not work stone*. The hejkin live in the same mountain. Nobody has ever put the two parties in a room.
- **He will not die in the Refuge.** At 50% hit points he leaves through the floor. If beaten, the survivors of the Square scatter into Yathazor and **sneak back to attack the party by surprise**, and Ari Evan uses her scroll to call an invisible stalker and summon monsters. The Refuge is not a boss fight; it is the start of a running problem.
- **The party cannot avoid him for long.** The tunnel onward is in his basement.
- *(Proposed.)* Gurdek is still in contact. The nightly dreams are not memory.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** play the hospitality completely straight for at least one full scene. The Square are charming, generous and interested, and the horror is retrospective.
- Ranza is the designed lever — see [[Ranza]]. A party that identifies him as the least loyal member can get hints, favours, or outright betrayal.
- The Slate/hejkin non-meeting is a free scene with two mad neighbours who have opinions about each other.
- If Slate escapes, he is the best recurring antagonist available for Adventures Five and Six — he can walk through the mountain, he knows the whole route, and he thinks the party are mountain-killers.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Priest (earth)
- Level: 10 *(Shadowdark cap; represents 2e level 15)*
- Armor Class: 18 (chain mail +1, shield +4)
- Hit Points: 68
- Movement: near
- Strength +1, Dexterity +0, Constitution +2, Intelligence +2, Wisdom **+4**, Charisma **−3**
- Alignment: Chaotic (neutral)
- Morale: 12 — **but he withdraws at half hit points regardless**
- Attacks: 1 attack per round.
  - *Steel long sword +3* +9, 1d8+4
  - *Wooden spear* +3, 1d6−1 — carried **solely** as the heartseeker component
- **Ignore Earth:** can pass through stone freely. **This is his escape and he will use it at 50% hit points.**
- **Animate Rock:** brings a statue to life — 1d3 hp per cubic foot. Yard statues: 18 cubic feet, ~33 hp, AC 15, +6 for 3d6+12. The Gurdek statue: 30 cubic feet, ~55 hp, same attack, Huge.
- **Heartseeker:** cast on the wooden spear, or on [[Ranza]]'s wooden daggers for a backstab.
- **Ironskin:** absorbs 1d6+7 attacks. He casts this before any fight he sees coming — which, with Pargo's precognition, is all of them.
- **Stone of Sharpening:** +3 damage on first strike, +2 on the second, +1 on the third.
- **Resistant to mind reading:** any attempt to read his thoughts requires an Intelligence check; a natural 20 dazes the reader for 1d6 rounds on contact with his incomprehensible thoughts.
- **Immune to charm.**
- **Running him at the table:** serene, hospitable, and utterly certain. Give the players his speech about the earth spirits in full — it is printed and it is good. He should be likeable for one scene and then never again.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Male Human Earth Cleric 15, Wis 17, Cha 3, hp 73, AC −1, THAC0 12, steel sword +3, chain mail +1, shield +4, PSPs 66. Compressed to Shadowdark level 10 — the cap — which is correct: he should read as the most powerful individual the party has met.
