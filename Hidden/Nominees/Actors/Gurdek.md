# Actor: Gurdek

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-gurdek`. **Entity rather than person — no stats, no scene, no confirmed form. Filed as an actor because it has agency, a name, and a body count.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
An unknown and insane thing from the Elemental Plane of Earth that answered an initiation ritual it was not invited to, killed the priest conducting it, ruined the initiate's mind, granted him full clerical power anyway, and has been visiting everyone he recruits ever since.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc — **entity / patron**
- Control: **not controllable** — GM force, not an NPC to be played
- Status: active, or at least still arriving when called
- Faction or allegiance: none. The Square of Gurdek is named for it; there is no evidence it is aware of them collectively.
- Current location: the Elemental Plane of Earth, presumably
- Current route, when traveling: arrives during earth-cleric initiation rituals
- Role: Initiating entity; the thing that made Slate

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
**Unknown — and Slate has never seen it either.**

The only image of Gurdek anywhere is the statue Slate is sculpting on his patio, **based entirely on a twisted imagination**: a crude, horrible figure over twelve feet tall, in dark stone, representing a male humanoid creature with **an almost formless face**, whose **arms end in lumps of short, broad spikes**, with **a raised polished square in the centre of its chest**.

The module is explicit that this is not a likeness. *Gurdek might not even have a physical form.*

### Manner and Voice
No dialogue. No negotiation. It arrives, it touches, and something is different afterwards.

What it did to Slate: **it slew his mentor, ravaged his mind, and granted him the powers of an earth cleric.** What it does to recruits: **some it rejects and drives mad; most it lets through, bending their psyches so that they can maintain a facade of normality while being quite insane.**

### Public Reputation
The name is Slate's invention — **"Slate called the entity Gurdek, for reasons only he knows."** Outside the Square, nothing on Athas knows this thing exists or has a name.

## Confirmed Facts
- When the eager young **Harno** was buried in the earth for the standard earth-cleric initiation ritual, **the elemental entity that came to initiate him was not a standard creature of the Elemental Plane of Earth.**
- **It was some unknown and insane creature from that plane.**
- **It slew Harno's mentor.**
- **It ravaged Harno's mind** — he emerged remembering neither his name, his master, nor his past — **but it did grant him the powers of an earth cleric.**
- It also changed him physically: his face came out **grey and stony**, and villagers reacted to him with fear.
- He took the name Slate, and **named the entity Gurdek, for reasons only he knows.**
- **Every recruit Slate later initiated was visited by Gurdek.**
- **Some were rejected and driven mad** — [[Olton]] and [[Morlah]] are the two survivors of that outcome. **Most made the transition to earth cleric.**
- **The touch of Gurdek has bent all their psyches.** They can maintain a facade of normality and are quite insane. Unlike Slate, they retained their identities and memories.
- In game terms, recruits with Wisdom below 17 **could not make the transition and were rejected by Gurdek.**
- Everyone Gurdek has touched is **resistant to mind reading** — any attempt requires an Intelligence check, and a natural 20 **dazes the reader for 1d6 rounds by contact with their incomprehensible thoughts.**
- **Slate's only moments of mental discomfort are in dreams, when he sometimes relives the terrifying touch of Gurdek that he experienced as a young man.**

## Goals
Unknown. The module attributes no motive to it whatsoever, and the honest position is that it may not have one in any sense a person would recognise.

*(Proposed, if the GM wants one: it is answering calls it was not meant to receive, and it grants real power every time. Whether that is predation, error, or something being cultivated is a decision the campaign has not made.)*

## Traits and Pressures
Not applicable. It is not modelled as an actor with pressures.

The one thing the source does assert is **insanity** — not malice, not hunger, not ambition. It is described as an unknown and insane creature, and everything it touches comes back mad.

## Resources and Capabilities
- **It grants genuine, full earth-cleric power.** Slate reached 15th level on it. This is the uncomfortable fact at the centre of the Square: the thing works.
- It can kill an experienced priest during a ritual.
- It can destroy or permanently alter a mind, and does so on every contact.
- It leaves a signature: **resistance to mind reading**, shared by every one of its initiates. This is the only detectable trace it leaves in the world.
- It has physically altered at least one body.

## Current Activity
Unknown. Present in Slate's dreams; presumably still answering when called.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Gurdek is the campaign's only completely unexplained element, and it should probably stay that way.** Black Spine resolves everything else — the gith are psi-blasted githyanki, the steel comes from the githyanki, the Reclamation is a lie, the queen is Trinth. Gurdek is never mentioned again after Adventure Four. It is a genuine loose thread in an otherwise closed campaign.
- **The Square is a working demonstration that the thing grants real power.** Six people have taken Gurdek's bargain. Four function. Slate hit 15th level. If a PC earth cleric ever hears the whole story, the interesting question is not *what is it* but *would you*.
- **Possible readings, none supported and none contradicted:**
  1. **An insane earth elemental**, exactly as printed, with no deeper significance. The cleanest option.
  2. **Something using the initiation ritual as a channel** — a predator that has learned where earth clerics are made.
  3. **A Black Spine connection.** The campaign's entire premise is a planar intrusion under a mountain. An unknown entity from another plane that has been quietly harvesting Athasian minds is either an enormous coincidence or a second front. **The module does not link them. The campaign could.**
  4. **Not from the Plane of Earth at all**, and Slate — who lost his memory of the event — is the only witness.
- **The statue is the tell that nobody knows anything.** A twelve-foot idol of an entity its own high priest has never seen, sculpted from imagination, with a polished square where a heart would be. If Slate ever casts *animate rock* on it, the party is fighting a statue of a god made up by a madman.
- *(Proposed.)* The dreams are not memory. It is still in contact with Slate, and Slate has never considered that possibility.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: leave it unexplained.** Its value is that it is the one thing in Black Spine with no answer behind it.
- If the campaign does want to develop it, option 3 above is the highest-yield: an entity that has been quietly making mad clerics on Athas for decades, in the same mountain the githyanki chose, is a second intrusion the party can discover long after they think the campaign is over.
- The animated Gurdek statue (30 cubic feet, ~55 hp) is the boss of the Refuge in all but name and should be staged as such.
- If a PC earth cleric is present, Slate offering to initiate them is the sharpest scene available in Adventure Four.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
**No stat block. Gurdek does not appear.**

What *can* appear is the statue:

- **Animated Gurdek Statue** *(if Slate casts animate rock on it)*
  - Armor Class: 15
  - Hit Points: 55 (30 cubic feet, 1d3 hp per cubic foot)
  - Movement: near (slow)
  - Attacks: 1 per round — *spiked stone limbs* +6, 3d6+12
  - Size: Huge. Intelligence: none. Morale: 20 — it does not stop.
  - **Running it:** it is not Gurdek. It is a madman's guess at Gurdek, animated by that same madman, and it should be described as crude and wrong rather than divine.
- **Signature of the touched:** any creature initiated by Gurdek is resistant to mind reading. Intelligence check to read them at all; a natural 20 dazes the reader for 1d6 rounds. This is the only mechanic Gurdek has left in the world.
- **Conversion note:** the entity has no printed statistics of any kind. The statue's figures are converted from the printed *animate rock* application (1d3 hp per cubic foot, 30 cubic feet, THAC0 11, 3d6+12, AC 3, ML 20, XP 3,000).
