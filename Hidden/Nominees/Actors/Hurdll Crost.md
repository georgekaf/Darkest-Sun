House Shom's Master Trader in Cromlin — a 15th-level trader who knowingly lets every merchant in the village under-report their earnings because it brings business, and who turns anyone caught trespassing into a slave or throws them off the pier.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A human trader in **studded leather**, with a **metal long sword +3** — an openly worn metal weapon, in a village where that alone announces who is in charge. **58 hit points, Charisma 17, Wisdom 16.**

He lives in a **lavishly decorated** room in the Shom lodge, **far larger than necessary**, as is typical with House Shom.

### Manner and Voice
*(Manner proposed; behaviour printed.)* Genial in the way of a man who does not need to be anything else. **He spends at least half his time indoors, attempting to escape the choking dust of the nearby sea**, watching the marketplace from a walkway above it.

### Public Reputation
The authority in Cromlin. **As one of House Shom's last profitable outposts, the authorities here tend to turn a blind eye to the shady practices of anyone who remains to do business with them.**

## Confirmed Facts
- **Male human trader 15**, lawful neutral. AC 7 (studded leather), hp 58, Cha 17. **187 PSPs; wild talent Double Pain** (PS 10, cost 7).
- Weapon: **metal long sword +3**, damage 1d8+3.
- **He is House Shom's Master Trader in Cromlin.**
- **A tenth of all earnings in the village must be given to him.** **Prices are extremely inflated** as a result — expect **a 10% markup** over standard.
- **Reported earnings are considerably less than actual earnings. Crost realises and ignores this discrepancy, as it helps lure business opportunities to the village.**
- **If a trader's reported earnings are too low, or if a merchant has gotten on Crost's bad side, Crost's overseers will audit the offender's transactions for several weeks to collect Shom's share.**
- **He keeps a detailed inventory of the House Shom warehouse and only he has unrestricted access.** **Many believe Crost uses the information as part of an elaborate money-making scheme:** when an item is in high demand he **sells about half at the normally inflated rate, then slowly leaks a few more onto the market at 30–50% above usual**, each one always *"the last one in stock."*
- **Anyone caught on the warehouse grounds without legitimate business is taken to Crost for punishment. Crost turns these criminals into slaves, or tosses them off the Shom pier if he deems them too dangerous to keep in captivity.**
- **He only enforces the sorcerer-king prohibitions against magic when he has other reasons for doing so.**
- **The silt pirates are the one scourge Hurdll has committed himself to destroying**, as they are a serious drain on the village's economy. **The Shom flagship *Firewind* has been modified to carry more soldiers and ballista ports.**
- **Adventurers can sign on for five silver pieces a day if they demonstrate combat skills.**
- **He keeps 30 soldiers on the payroll at all times**, plus **11 overseers and officers** living in the lodge.
- **He has adopted the unusual habit of keeping a group of high-level help on hand as well** — drawn **from among adventurers, travellers, and even escaped slaves.** **Crost cares little for their past, so long as they are resourceful and relatively competent.** They are **called out in emergencies or when the soldiers face a challenge they can't match.** **The job pays three gold pieces per month, and the PCs could be hired into the position.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the party's employer, and the module says so outright.** *The job pays three gold pieces per month, and the PCs could be hired into the position.* **Crost keeps a rotating band of adventurers for problems his soldiers cannot handle — that is the party.** This is the cleanest, most mercenary hook in Chapter Two and it comes with a village, a paymaster and a standing anti-pirate war.
- **The under-reporting tolerance is a weapon, not laziness.** He knows what everyone is hiding and audits selectively. **Every merchant in Cromlin is one bad week from being ruined by paperwork, and they all know it.** That is how a trader rules a den of thieves without street patrols.
- **He enforces the magic prohibitions "only when he has other reasons for doing so."** Elf vendors in the marketplace stock spell components and magical fruit. **Any spellcasting PC operating in Cromlin is operating on Crost's sufferance**, and he will remember it.
- **The warehouse is robbable** — 2d6 × 10 gp of jewellery, metal fragments and weapon parts — and **being caught means slavery or the pier.** Two sentry patrols of three guards each. **The module invites the heist and prices it.**
- **He is 15th level with 187 PSPs.** In a village of 3rd-level pirates and 4th-level sentries, **Crost is not a quest-giver who can be strong-armed.** Double Pain on a party that tries is a very short conversation.
- *(Proposed.)* House Shom is failing across Athas and this is one of its last profitable outposts. **Crost's real fear is not pirates; it is being recalled.**

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** hire the party. It solves Chapter Two's structure problem instantly and gives them a reason to be at every location in Cromlin.
- The anti-pirate campaign leads directly to [[Curlonus Skaff]] and the Dust Devils, which is the module's own Part Two.
- The warehouse heist and the audit-as-threat are both usable against the party if they cross him.
- If the campaign wants Cromlin destabilised, removing Crost does it — nobody else holds the village together.
