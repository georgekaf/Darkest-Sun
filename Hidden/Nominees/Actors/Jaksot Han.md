A silt pirate who lost an eye in a raid, decided to go into a safer line of work, and opened the roughest tavern in Cromlin — which the module notes was not a good choice.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Proposed except the eye.)* A former pirate **missing his right eye**, lost in a raid. He keeps a large tavern that is Cromlin's centre of entertainment.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)* A man running a business that has, twice a day, become a brawl for years. He has stopped intervening in most of them.

### Public Reputation
The owner of **the Dirty Lizard** — Cromlin's centre of entertainment, and a place where **fights and drunken arguments break out constantly.**

## Confirmed Facts
- **The silt pirate Jaksot Han decided to go into a safer line of work after losing his right eye in a raid.**
- **Opening the Dirty Lizard wasn't a good choice.** **Fights and drunken arguments break out constantly, and the skimmers who frequent the place are quick to test the abilities of newcomers.**
- **The Dirty Lizard is a large tavern and Cromlin's centre of entertainment.**
- **From midday on, there are always as many as 40 skimmers, merchants, thieves, con artists, or free citizens on hand**, drinking **the thick, fermented kank-honey-mead the Dirty Lizard is known for.**
- **When adventurers enter the bar, they are gawked at, loudly insulted, and even pushed around.** **If a fight breaks out, 1d6 of the instigator's companions join in.** **There is a 75% chance that the rest of the crowd will break out into a free-for-all** — *chairs fly through the air, ceramic bottles break over unsuspecting heads, and sometimes a bone knife slides quickly and quietly between some poor sod's ribs.*
- **Should the PCs come out on top of a Dirty Lizard brawl without killing or seriously injuring any of the patrons, they will have proven themselves to the crowd.** **After that, they will almost certainly be asked to relate news of their travels and be drawn into conversations of local events.**
- **If the PCs enter and avoid engaging in violence, the crowd ignores them.** They may get **some meaningless chatter**, but **the good stuff is saved for those who make a good showing of themselves while entertaining the crowd.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The Dirty Lizard is a printed social mechanic and Jaksot Han is its landlord.** Win a brawl **without killing or seriously injuring anyone** and the room opens up — news, local events, and the real information. **Avoid the fight entirely and get nothing but meaningless chatter.** That is an unusual and excellent piece of design: **the module rewards controlled violence specifically.**
- **He is the information broker the party will not realise they have.** Every rumour in the Giustenal environs passes through his room. **[[Jessareen]] carries news between settlements; Jaksot Han is where it pools.**
- **He and [[Passk]] are the same story with different endings.** Both silt pirates, both broken by one bad night, both still in Cromlin. **One opened a business; one is waiting to become a banshee.** Putting them in a scene together costs nothing and makes both of them real.
- **His past is a liability nobody has collected on yet.** Crost is running an anti-pirate war from a lodge three buildings away.
- *(Proposed.)* He knows where the pirate base is — *hidden in a large cavern to the west of the village* — and has never said, because saying it would raise the question of how he knows.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** run the brawl exactly as printed, including the 75% free-for-all and the reward for restraint. It is the best-designed social encounter in Chapter Two.
- Use him as the party's rumour source once they have earned the room.
- The Passk connection is a free scene with real emotional weight.
- If the campaign wants the pirate base found without a sea battle, Jaksot Han is the shortcut — and getting it out of him should cost him something.
