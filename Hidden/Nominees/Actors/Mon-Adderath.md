Dregoth's High Templar, ageless since his master was still a Champion of Rajaat, who packed up his belongings and walked into the dark beneath a dying city two thousand years ago and has run its administration ever since.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Mon-Adderath was (and is) Dregoth's High Templar.**
- **He was already imbued with agelessness and had served Dregoth since the Dread King had become a champion of Rajaat.**
- **After returning as the undead dragon king, Dregoth bestowed immortality on his two trusted templars so that they could assist him in the centuries to come** — Mon-Adderath and **Absalom**.
- **His chamber in the surface palace (area 13, High Templar's Chamber) is hardly touched by damage and time**, though the west wall was cracked from the ante-chamber side. **Only a grey marble table and a desk inlaid with turquoise remain** — *"Mon-Adderath packed up his personal belongings when he and Dregoth retreated beneath the city."* **The turquoise is worth 2 gp if pried loose; the desk and table would fetch 3 gp each at most markets if transported whole.**
- **Every templar of 3rd level or better is considered a full templar, and is designated as such by the High Templar himself.**
- **Whenever a templar increases a level after the second, he is blessed by a thick, yellowish scale that rises from his spine.** **Only Mon-Adderath and Absalom have the necessary power to make this mark, using a spell granted to them by Dregoth.**
- **Mon-Adderath is currently a Templar of the Highest Scale (18)** — the highest rank in the church. **Absalom is of the Fifteenth Scale.**
- **Beneath the steeple in each temple is a huge room where Mon-Adderath or Absalom often deliver sermons on Dregoth.**
- **His statistics are on Card #4** of the boxed set.
- He commands, in effect, a force of **338 full-time templars** in a population of **3,000 dray**, plus a **total militia** in which every dray of fighting age marches on Dregoth's word.

## Current Activity
Running a church, an army and a bureaucracy beneath a dead city, and waiting for the Day of Light.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The empty chamber is the best characterisation the module gives anyone.** Giustenal is levelled, thousands are dead, his king has been murdered and has risen as something else — **and Mon-Adderath went back to his rooms and packed.** Everything else in the palace was left where it fell. **Play him as that man.**
- **He is a single point of failure and nobody has noticed.** **Only he and Absalom can promote a templar.** Remove both and Dregoth's hierarchy freezes permanently at its current ranks. **Remove only Mon-Adderath and the entire promotion apparatus falls to the one man who is secretly working against Dregoth's plan.**
- **He is the loyalty benchmark.** In a cast where Skaff is deceived, Absalom is subverting, Mosak is being farmed and the Spirit is a lie, **Mon-Adderath is exactly what he appears to be.** That is genuinely unnerving and should be played straight.
- **He is a walking archive.** Two thousand years of unbroken memory, awake, sane, and conversational. **He knows what Giustenal looked like, what Taraskir's cult actually was, what the sorcerer-kings did in the palace that day, and what Dregoth was like before he died.** Whether any of that can be got out of him is a matter of leverage, not access.
- *(Proposed.)* He does not know about Absalom. If he found out, the response would be institutional and immediate — **and Absalom's protection of Kragmorta ends.**
- *(Proposed.)* He has never once questioned the Coruscation. Two millennia of certainty is its own kind of vulnerability.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: use him as the face of the regime.** Dregoth should be felt, not met; Mon-Adderath is who the party actually negotiates with, defies, or is sentenced by.
- **Do not give him a secret.** The cast is already full of double agents. His value is that he is real.
- Make the promotion monopoly matter. A party that grasps it has a bloodless way to cripple the templarate.
- Mine him for history. He is the only living witness who can be *talked to*.
- Decide what happens if he learns about Absalom, and hold it in reserve.
