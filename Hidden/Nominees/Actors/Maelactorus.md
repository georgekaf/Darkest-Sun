# Actor: Maelactorus

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-maelactorus`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The mind flayer who secretly mastered the Order of the Rising Shadow, sealed himself into a vault with a *wish* when the asteroid was torn into the Astral Plane — and got the wording wrong, and has been in there ever since.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc — **illithid**
- Control: autonomous
- Status: **sealed** — released only if the party deliberately dispels the vault
- Faction or allegiance: none surviving; formerly master of the Order of the Rising Shadow
- Current location: the githzerai asteroid base, area 9 — **the Mysterious Vault**
- Current route, when traveling: teleport, probability travel, astral projection, dimension walk
- Role: Ancient prisoner; the asteroid's real secret

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
> *"An almost palpable blackness fills the chamber beyond the massive door. It seems to seep across the floor like an expanding pool of thick oil. Within the rippling void a pair of crimson eyes flash open and a hiss of absolute evil fills the air. Slowly, a dark and terrible form moves forward."*

An illithid: roughly six feet, mauve skin glistening with slime, an octopus-like head with white pupil-less eyes and **four tentacles around a round, many-toothed lamprey mouth**, three reddish fingers and a thumb on each hand.

### Manner and Voice
No spoken language — **illithids communicate with any creature by innate telepathy**, often accompanying their thoughts with hissing and the eager lashing of their tentacles.

**He will not be in the least grateful to the PCs for freeing him.** He will **attempt to dominate them as he did the wizards who were here before.**

**It might be possible to negotiate with Maelactorus, but the DM will want to make this a difficult prospect.**

### Public Reputation
None. **The githzerai have always assumed the vault held some great cache of treasure.** They never opened it and never knew.

## Confirmed Facts
- **A mind flayer.** 55 hit points, AC 5, **magic resistance 90%**, Intelligence 18, 310 PSPs.
- **He was actually the master of the Order of the Rising Shadow** — the cult of mysterious wizards who once inhabited this asteroid, and whose experiments in magic opened a mystical portal to the Astral Plane **and yanked the mountain through.**
- **The shock of that transition proved fatal to the magicians**, but left their catacombs intact.
- **When the asteroid jumped into the Astral Plane and the wizards were killed, Maelactorus sealed himself into this vault as a protective measure. A mistake in the wording of the wish resulted in him being imprisoned since that time.**
- The vault door is **a massive metal affair that is all but impossible to force**, warded by — **and now containing** — the master of the Order, **with a powerful wish spell.**
- **The githzerai have never been able to open it** and assumed it held treasure.
- **The only means by which the chamber can be entered is the successful casting of a *dispel magic* on the mystical fields surrounding it.** Those enchantments are **assumed to have been cast by a wizard of 20th level.**
- **He will attempt to dominate the party** as he dominated the wizards before them.
- **[[Krika]] will insist that the creature cannot be trusted and must be destroyed.** Her people **lived for centuries under the yoke of the mind flayers**, and an intense hatred of that race lingers to this day.
- Psionics: **body equilibrium, domination, mindlink, probability travel, teleport**; animate shadow, astral projection, attraction, awe, contact, control body, dimension walk, ESP, ego whip, id insinuation, inflict pain, levitation, phobia amplification, post-hypnotic suggestion, time shift.
- **[[Alukas]] knows the Order's master was an illithid**, and can tell the party so as part of his bargain.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is entirely optional and the party has to work to release him.** The vault cannot be forced. It takes a deliberate *dispel magic* against 20th-level wards. **Nobody has to open this door.** The githzerai never did. **Everything that follows is the party's own doing**, and the module is quietly unambiguous about that.
- **The reward for opening it is nothing.** The githzerai assumed treasure. There is no treasure — there is a mind flayer. **The vault is a trap baited entirely with player curiosity.**
- **His imprisonment is self-inflicted and darkly funny.** He sealed himself in with a *wish* to survive the transition and **got the wording wrong.** The most intelligent creature on the asteroid outsmarted himself and has had centuries to think about it.
- **The Krika problem is the real encounter.** If the party opens the vault and tries to negotiate — which the module says is *possible* but should be *difficult* — **Krika will demand his destruction, and Krika is the only route back to Athas.** Choosing the illithid over her strands them in the Astral Plane. **The module sets this up and never resolves it.**
- **He explains Alukas.** The Order imprisoned the beholder; Maelactorus was the Order's master. If the party has already dealt with Alukas, **the two prisoners are connected**, and Maelactorus is the one who knows why.
- **310 PSPs and 90% magic resistance in a party's face is a genuine TPK risk.** Mind blast stuns for 3d4 rounds. Four tentacles and one round is a dead PC. **This is a harder fight than Trinth's arena and it is entirely avoidable.**
- *(Proposed.)* He does not know how long it has been. His first act after domination would be to ask.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** let the vault be genuinely tempting and give no hint of what is inside. Krika's belief that it holds treasure is the bait and she believes it sincerely.
- If the party opens it, run the domination attempt immediately and let the negotiation be nearly impossible, as instructed.
- Use him to explain the asteroid. A party that survives him — or binds him — can learn what the Order was, what tore the mountain into the Astral Plane, and why a beholder was in the basement.
- If he escapes, he is a loose illithid with astral travel and a centuries-old grudge. That is a campaign, not a loose end.
