# Actor: Raskine

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-raskine`. **Conditional actor — exists only if the PCs killed [[Raskon]] in Adventure Six.** Minimal source: one sentence.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
Raskon's brother, who steps into the dead man's job and the dead man's gloating scene, and who — unlike his brother — has an actual reason to want these particular prisoners to suffer.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: **conditional** — active only in the branch where Raskon died at the Nightmare Gate
- Faction or allegiance: `faction-trinth-githyanki`
- Current location: the Galnaki prison complex
- Current route, when traveling: as githyanki — plane shift
- Role: Raskon's replacement; overseer of the PCs

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Proposed.)* Recognisably his brother's kin — the party may well notice the resemblance before they are told, which is the best way to run the reveal. Githyanki, armoured, escorted.

### Manner and Voice
He inherits Raskon's scene and Raskon's speech, and **he is understandably vengeful.** That single printed word is the whole difference between the two characters.

*(Proposed.)* Where Raskon gloated because he was enjoying himself, Raskine gloats because he is furious and has been given the people responsible. The same words, delivered by someone who means them.

### Public Reputation
The brother of a general the PCs killed.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He exists so the module's best exposition scene survives the players killing the wrong man.** Raskon's prison visit delivers Galnaki, the queen, the PCs' intended fate, and the metal — and the module refuses to lose it. **Raskine is a continuity patch with one adjective attached.**
- **That adjective is worth using.** *Vengeful* changes the scene from a gloat to a grievance. Raskon could not imagine the PCs escaping. **Raskine has watched them kill a githyanki general.** He should be more careful with them and less careful with himself, which is a genuinely different and more dangerous character.
- **He is the better final antagonist of the two.** If the campaign wants someone at Trinth's side in the finale who has a personal reason to be there, Raskine has one and Raskon does not.
- *(Proposed.)* He may not believe his brother was to blame for the Gate — in which case [[Trasrani]] is in a cell on a false accusation Raskine has no investment in defending, and could be released by the githyanki themselves. That removes the traitor-in-the-party subplot and replaces it with something stranger.
- **Do not run both brothers.** They occupy one slot. If Raskon lived, Raskine does not appear at all and should not be name-dropped.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** if the branch triggers, let the party notice the resemblance before anyone says the name. *"He looks like the one you killed"* is a better opening than an introduction.
- Give him one line Raskon would never say — something that acknowledges what they did — and otherwise run the printed scene unchanged.
- If the campaign wants to develop him past his one function, the Trasrani question is the cheapest available hook.
