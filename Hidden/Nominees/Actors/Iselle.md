## One-Sentence Summary
An 11th-level priestess of air lying invisible in a rock niche with two hit points, losing one every turn, whose survival is the price of a preserver's cooperation and whose recovery hands the party the healer they have not had all campaign.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
**Invisible**, lying in a narrow niche set about four feet up in the rock. A human woman of five feet seven, in nothing but rags — **she has no possessions save the rags she wears.**

Even found, she is barely there: in shock, unresponsive, and dying at a fixed rate.

### Manner and Voice
Nothing at first. **She is in shock and has lost all of her memorised spells.**

Once healed or comforted she is coherent, and once completely healed **she begins to pray for spells** — which is the first thing she does, before anything else, and says a good deal about her.

*(The module gives her no dialogue at all. Anything she says is the GM's.)*

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is a hostage the module does not call a hostage.** Quixoth's entire bargain is built on her, and the timer is real: 2 hit points, minus one per turn. A party that debates the terms for six turns has killed her, and with her the preserver's cooperation, the three spells, and any goodwill.
- **She is the campaign's answer to attrition.** By Adventure Five the party has fought through a siege, a desert march, a dream assault, a stockade, a mine, a gith nest and a dead city. An 11th-level air priest offered free, on one condition, is the module quietly refilling the tank. Treat her recovery as a real turning point.
- **Air, specifically, is the right domain and probably not an accident.** Everything below Yathazor is enclosed: gas-filled var caverns, a flammable dung pit that will engulf a room in a single round, a cold smelter, smoke used as a weapon. An air priest is the counter to most of it.
- **She is the second designated PC replacement in one encounter.** The module offers both Quixoth and Iselle for that purpose within three pages. If the party lost people in Zigath's Nest, Adventure Five is where the module hands them back.
- *(Proposed.)* She has been conscious for some of the captivity and has seen the cold smelter from the inside of the slave rotation. Quixoth's account of it is secondhand; hers would not be. This is the cleanest way to give her something to say.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** run the timer honestly and out loud. *"She has two hit points and she is losing one a turn"* is a scene, not a stat line.
- Give her the cold smelter testimony. Quixoth reports that slaves go in and do not come out; Iselle can be the one who knows what it sounds like. That makes the rescue pay off narratively as well as mechanically.
- If the campaign wants a good-aligned NPC to survive the underground and matter later, she is the only candidate on the board.
- If a PC has died, offer her before offering Quixoth — a healer is what the party is missing.
