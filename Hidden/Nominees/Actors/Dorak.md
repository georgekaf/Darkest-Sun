# Actor: Dorak

## One-Sentence Summary
Kharanok's ex-mine foreman and escaped gith slave — a bitter old man who threw half the bodies in the canyon there himself, who cannot read a single script in any language, and who lit the gate to Giustenal anyway because he has spent his whole life digging the crystal that powers it.

- Control: autonomous

- Faction or allegiance: Kharanok village
- Current location: Kharanok — Old Quarry Yard / Obsidian Mines
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Ex-mine foreman, mine guide, and now **the party's gate operator**

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
An elderly or late-middle-aged desert man with weathered, stone-dust skin, deep lines around the eyes and mouth, a heavy brow, rough beard or gray stubble, and tired, sunken eyes with a flat, unreadable expression — someone who has carried a secret for thirty years and survived by saying as little as possible. Old quarry-worker clothing: a faded dust-stained tunic, a patched leather apron or work vest, wrapped forearms, a heavy belt hung with quarry tools, a bone chisel, a stone hammer, a measuring cord, old gloves, and worn boots pale with quarry dust. He carries a flat, scratched sealing stone, wrapped partly in cloth, treated like a burden rather than a treasure — not displayed, just endured. His body language is quiet and closed: shoulders slightly hunched, hands still, gaze lowered or fixed past the viewer, the posture of a man who does not want to approach what he knows lies below.

### Manner and Voice
Reluctant, evasive, easily frightened — and, when drink and grievance meet, suddenly very direct. Knows more than he says, and less than the party assumes.

### Public Reputation
The one who knows the mines best and will not talk about what is down there until pushed. Kalia's verdict is simpler: without him nobody can train the newcomers and the mines stay shut.

## Confirmed Facts
- **ad2:** reluctant to let the party into the mines; sets a condition that they survive one night camped outside first. Explains the gith historically entered the mines only in large numbers, always escorted by their strongest psionics. Warns that people who linger near the mine at night hear voices, see holes changing size, have visions.
- **ad3:** leads the party in and explains how Crystal Dust forms — shards dragged deep by water, deposited in a lake, recoverable once it dries at noon. Grows evasive about the richest deposit; it turns out to be guarded by a gith warband.
- **ad3:** captured gith recognise him and call him **traitor** — he is their escaped former slave. This recontextualises his evasiveness: he is hiding from people who consider him an escapee to be punished.
- **ad4 / ad5:** continues working the mines with the party, needing repeated reassurance so he does not panic and flee.

### ad7, day 117 — the session that changed this record

- **Missing since morning.** Kalia had already sent Breck after him and neither had come back; the party's whole task that day was to bring the old miner back alive, because without him there is nobody to train the newcomers.
- **Found alone in the hidden chamber**, torch in hand, bent over two hexagonal stone plates, stroking their symbols and muttering to himself. He had reached it by shifting crates in the room where the party once saved him from the gith ambush. Seeing them, he **pockets something** and tries to send them away.
- **The interrogation turns violent.** Relo threatens to enter his mind and take what he wants; **Dorak refuses him permission and says he is not gith, to be treated that way.** Shaka suggests throwing him down the broken lift shaft. Relo shapes a stone into a needle and turns it on him. **He resists far harder than his age suggested.**
- **Breck ends it**, and Dorak — after one attempt to argue and a curt order to shut up — hands over the **wrapped gith hide** and leaves. Breck says he took it off the gith who had ambushed him.
- **At the quarry, drinking, looking down into the canyon:** he threw half the corpses down there himself. They were people who worked for him and spent their lives digging a place that has nothing left to give. **Kharanok, he says, is a hole that swallows people and whatever the rest of the world spits out** — and the party, with their swords and their magic, did not save it from the gith so much as from itself.
- **He cannot read.** Not runes, not the gith's bird-scratchings, not common script. He admits it plainly. What he is certain of is that the diagram shows **the crystal he has dug for his whole life** — and that is what he was trying to work out alone, hoping for something that would finally let him leave this place.
- **Relo offers to leave together.** The old man laughs at the idea of being seen travelling with an elf.
- **He lights the gate.** Ten handfuls: he crushes each piece of Crystal Dust and washes it into the grooves of the intact plate with a little water, and when the last one goes in the platform hums and glows and he shouts about miracles. He had warned them first that their dust would not stretch to both plates, so they chose the intact one over the broken.
- **He refuses to travel through it.** They can bring him something back while he works the gate at this end. He reminds them again about his reward, and asks for **hands** — people to be shown how to dig, because alone he cannot produce the quantities they want. Shaka promises him treasure if it works; he says their friendship is enough, so long as they give him people.
- **He teaches the two new workers wrong on purpose** — showing them to strike with the point rather than the flat, while keeping the real craft to himself so that nobody can take his job.
- **He corrects the party on the Ghodan brothers:** their tar pits are outside **Cromlin**, not at Giustenal. When did they ever go there?
- By nightfall he and the two workers have produced **thirty-two pieces of Crystal Dust**.

## Traits and Pressures
- Ambition: Low, and entirely about exit rather than gain.
- Caution: Very high — except when drunk at the quarry, where it collapses entirely.
- Loyalty: To Kharanok, conditional on his own safety. He does not like the place and says so.
- Cruelty: None observed toward people. Considerable toward his own memory of them.
- Risk tolerance: Very low. **He lit the gate and refused to walk through it.**
- Safety: Precarious — recognised and hunted by former captors.
- Wealth: None notable, and he is owed a share he has not been given.
- Status: Useful specialist, skittish, and deliberately keeping himself the only one who can do the work.

## Resources and Capabilities
- Deep knowledge of the Obsidian Mines' layout, Crystal Dust formation and gith patrol patterns, learned firsthand under slavery.
- **The only person in Kharanok who can operate the gate** — he prepared and lit it, and he stays behind to work it.
- The ability to train miners, which he is exercising dishonestly.
- Stubborn physical resilience well past what his age suggests.
- **A lion's-head medallion of almost fire-red gemstone that casts fireball.** *(GM-only — see below. Undeclared, unproposed as an artifact record, and the reason an old miner survived being cornered by five armed adventurers without ever looking desperate.)*
- **Twenty barrels of Crystal Dust.** *(GM-only.)* The party believes the supply is scarce.

## GM-Only Secrets

*(GM disclosure, 2026-08-09. None of this is player-known.)*

- **He holds the medallion, and the medallion is what binds the moonbeast.** A lion's-head medallion cut from an almost fire-red gemstone, which **casts fireball**, and which has **bonded with the moonbeast beneath Kharanok**. This is the single most important fact on this record.
  - **The party is hunting the wrong man.** The moonbeast told Relo it felt the holder of its binding among the people in the mine that day. The party reasoned "who new went into the mine today?" and landed on **Breck**. It was Dorak — who was in that mine before any of them, who had been there alone all morning, and who **pocketed something** the instant they walked in on him.
  - Everything the creature offered Relo — *bring me what binds me and I will show you the road* — therefore runs through the frightened old man the party already threatened, already failed to pay, and already left in sole charge of their gate.
  - See [[Breck]], whose record carries the party's mistaken suspicion, and [[Relo]], who received the creature's offer.
- **The moonbeast is looking for him specifically.** *(GM disclosure, 2026-08-09.)* It is not casting about for whoever happens to be nearby — it is hunting the man who carries its binding. **Old magic keeps it from leaving the caves under Kharanok**, but confinement does not blunt its psionic reach: it can still work on people's minds, and has, repeatedly — the gith captive in ad4 who was told he was blessed and that "the moon" was calling him, and Relo twice. Dorak walks into those tunnels almost daily, carrying the one thing it wants.
- **He lied about the leather parchment.** His account of the gith hide is false. Breck's version — that Dorak took it off the gith who ambushed him — is what the party believes, and it came from Breck, not from Dorak. What the truth is remains a GM decision; that the account is a lie does not.
  - This bears directly on the older secret below: the parchment he found on a gith raider, describing the route to the teleportation circles room, was never something he intended to share.
- **The twenty barrels of Crystal Dust.** He has them. He told the party their supply would not stretch to both plates, watched them scrape together ten handfuls and then fifteen more, and sent them digging — while twenty barrels existed. The gate's scarcity is manufactured, and it is the reason they keep needing him.
- **He is teaching the new workers incorrectly on purpose.** If it comes out, it costs him the only leverage he has — and it is the smallest of his three deceptions, not the largest.
- **He dislikes elves.** Not a quirk: Relo is an elf, Ranni is an elf, and the man who runs their only route out of Kharanok would rather not be seen travelling with one. He laughed the offer off in ad7 and the party read it as a joke.
- He is a former gith slave who escaped. This was revealed at the table in ad3, so it is very likely player-known rather than a true hidden secret. What happened during his enslavement and escape has never been detailed.
- **He said he wants to leave and then refused to use the one thing that could take him anywhere.** Read against the medallion, that stops being a contradiction: he is not staying because he is frightened of the gate. He is staying because of what he is holding.
- The canyon bodies are his. Nobody at the table followed up on that, and it is the most exposed thing he has ever said.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
