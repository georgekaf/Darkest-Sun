# Actor: Kalisore

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-kalisore`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
Askai's imp — a two-foot devil who flies invisibly into a sleeping army, sets down a black stone in the middle of it, and then crouches beside it in the dark waiting to see whether anything in this world is still awake.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: servant of [[Askai]]; no faction record
- Current location: Tenpug's army encampment, in the sandy wastes short of the gith base camp
- Current route, when traveling: flies, invisibly, between Askai and wherever the stone needs to go
- Role: Servant, courier, saboteur

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Two feet of dark red devil — leathery bat wings, a barbed tail, sharp twisted horns, and gleaming white teeth. Almost never seen in that form.

What a watcher actually catches, if anything, is a shape crossing the stars, or a large spider or a giant rat moving with too much purpose through a camp where everyone is asleep.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed — the module gives it no dialogue.)* Contemptuous and businesslike. Kalisore does not regard the errand as beneath it so much as it regards everything as beneath it, and it has learned that saying so out loud costs time.

It will speak if cornered, mostly to buy seconds.

### Public Reputation
None. The whole point of Kalisore is that nobody is supposed to know it was ever there.

## Confirmed Facts
- An **imp**, and the servant of the gith lord Askai.
- Sent to **place a special magical item in the middle of Tenpug's army**, so that Askai can properly complete his spell.
- Once the stone is placed, the psionic assault reaches even thri-kreen — each must save or fall unconscious and join the dreaming.
- After placing the stone, **it sulks around it** rather than leaving.
- Any thri-kreen still awake may make an Intelligence check to spot the imp flying into the camp. Everyone else is already asleep.
- If thri-kreen are hunting or patrolling the camp, there is a 1-in-6 chance per round they come upon the imp hiding with the stone among the sleepers.
- **Kalisore will desperately try to defend the stone if it is found.**
- **If the stone is picked up by anybody, the spell is ruined** — all sleepers stop dreaming and can be woken normally.
- If a thri-kreen spots the imp flying in, or gets the stone before combat begins, the mind contacts are over and Askai's nightmare attacks are at an end.
- The stone is left behind whether or not the imp was discovered and defeated.
- Imp capabilities: invisible at will; polymorph into a large spider or a giant rat; poison sting; regenerates 1 hit point per round; **can be hit only by silver or magical weapons**; immune to fire, cold and electricity; saves as a 7 HD creature.

## Current Activity

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Kalisore is the off-switch and it is guarding itself.** The entire second night of Adventure Two hinges on a single object that any conscious character can simply pick up. The imp exists to make "simply pick it up" into an encounter. Run it as a stealth problem, not a fight — the fight is what happens when the stealth fails.
- **Silver or magic, or nothing.** In a party of Athasian ex-slaves, this is a genuine and nasty constraint. It is worth checking, before the session, whether anyone at the table can actually hurt it. If nobody can, the encounter becomes about moving the stone rather than killing the imp, which is better anyway.
- **It is the campaign's first outer-planar creature**, and it arrives two adventures before the githyanki. An imp on Athas is an anomaly — the world is famously cut off. Given that Black Spine's whole spine is planar intrusion, a devil in a gith lord's service is either a very early tell or a loose end. Recommended: treat it as a tell, and let a knowledgeable PC be quietly appalled by it.
- **Captured, it is the best information source available.** It knows Askai's name, location and intent, none of which the party otherwise learns until Part Four. An imp is lawful and will bargain. Nothing in the module anticipates the players taking it alive; everything about the creature invites it.
- *(Proposed.)* It does not want to be here. It is a courier being used as a spike, and if offered a way out that does not involve failing its master visibly, it will take it.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** let the imp be catchable and let it talk. It converts an unwinnable horror sequence into an intelligence coup, and rewards the party for having a thri-kreen — which rewards them, in turn, for having rescued [[Ch'k-Chakal]] two adventures earlier.
- If it escapes, it is a recurring nuisance with no loyalty worth speaking of and every reason to reappear.
- Its planar origin is a free, early hook toward the Astral thread. One PC recognising what it actually is would be a genuinely chilling beat.

- **Running it at the table:** the imp's job is to make a two-second action cost the players a scene. It fights only to protect the stone, and it fights dirty, invisible, and from above. If the players find a way to grab the stone and run rather than engage, that is a win and should be allowed to be one.
