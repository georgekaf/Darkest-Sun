# Actor: The Loyal — Varesh

## One-Sentence Summary
The scout of the Loyal — a lean, watchful elf thief who has walked the Giustenal environs so long that he reads the ruins the way other people read weather, and who argues for withdrawal more often than any of the others.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: [[The Loyal]] (`faction-the-loyal`), follower
- Current location: the wastes within 20 miles of the Ruins of Giustenal (`location-giustenal-ruins`)
- Current route, when traveling: whichever traveler road the band is shadowing that week
- Role: Scout and forward eyes for [[NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer]]

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Tall and extremely lean even for an elf, with long limbs, a narrow weathered face, sharp dark eyes and black hair bound tightly behind his head. Fitted sand-brown leather armor under a ragged hooded desert cloak, wrapped forearms, soft boots, several small bone utility knives. A bone shortbow across his back and a curved bone short sword at his hip. Reserved, watchful posture, always looking ready to move.

### Manner and Voice
Says little. Answers questions a few seconds late, having checked the horizon first. Flat delivery; no warmth, no hostility either.

### Public Reputation
Travelers who have been escorted out of the environs remember a thin elf who appeared on a ridge, said almost nothing, and was right about where the danger was.

## Confirmed Facts
- Source states only that Jessix roams with five young elves called the Loyal, preservers and thieves, who witnessed Slinnasia's death and believe him innocent (Campaign Book p. 11-12).
- Both Giustenal expeditions are established in the source's account of Jessix: the first reached Dregoth's templar chambers and returned with magical loot; the second ended in Slinnasia's death. These five survived both.

## Traits and Pressures
- Caution: 5 — the defining trait.
- Secrecy: 3 — no agenda hidden, just habitually uncommunicative.
- Loyalty: High, to Jessix and to the band, in that order.
- Risk tolerance: Low for the band, moderate for himself when scouting alone.
- Wealth: None. Everything the band takes goes into supplies and water.

## Resources and Capabilities
- Detailed working knowledge of the routes, sightlines and tar-pit margins around the ruins.
- Ability to move unseen in open ground, which in the environs is the difference between escorting and burying.
- The band's only reliable early warning of the [[Caller in Darkness]]'s reach.

## Current Activity
Shadowing traveler routes in the environs, usually a half mile ahead of the rest of the band.

## GM-Only Secrets
- He no longer fully believes travelers can be saved in numbers that matter; he keeps doing it because Jessix does. If Jessix ever falls, Varesh is the one most likely to walk away.

## Proposed Developments
- The natural first contact for any party crossing the environs — seen before he is met.
- If the party arrives via the Kharanok gate (ad7), Varesh is the plausible reason they are found before something else finds them.
