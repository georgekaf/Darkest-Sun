# Actor: Solaq

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-solaq`. Paired with [[Pimns]] — the two should be adopted or dropped together. **Their shared background, the intelligence they carry, and the encounter's design notes are documented in full on [[Pimns]]; this file covers what is specific to Solaq.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The other dwarven banshee at the chasm — freed in the Nibenay arena, re-enslaved, dead in a failed rebellion beside the woman he was going to have a life with, and notably the one the module leaves *neutral* rather than evil.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc — **undead (dwarven banshee)**
- Control: autonomous
- Status: **dead, and present**
- Faction or allegiance: none
- Current location: the Cave of the Winds, encounter 4 — the promenade above the great chasm
- Current route, when traveling: **cannot leave**
- Role: Dwarven banshee; obstacle; witness

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
One of two dwarven warriors among some twenty skeletal remains lying in crumpled heaps near the centre of the cavern floor, flesh stripped clean by giant ants, in a room where the wind through the chasm makes a low continuous moaning.

### Manner and Voice
Alternating maledictions with Pimns, in a fixed two-round sequence. He opens with a death field rather than a scream — **he is the one who acts before he cries out**, which is a small distinction the printed combat order preserves and which is worth playing.

### Public Reputation
None.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Solaq, 5th-level dwarven male gladiator.** Strength 17, Constitution 18, **Intelligence 13, Wisdom 12, Charisma 14** — noticeably more capable across the mental scores than Pimns.
- **Alignment neutral** — *not* the neutral evil the module assigns Pimns. Both are banshees; only one is written as evil.
- He and [[Pimns]] **won their freedom in the Nibenay arena**, vowed death over captivity, were rebellious and often beaten, and died together in the failed rebellion at the chasm's edge.
- **"Their focus, their dreams, and their chance for a life together all died at the chasm's edge. Here they remain."**
- Printed combat sequence: **first round — Pimns screams a malediction while Solaq creates a 20-point death field. Second round — Solaq screams a malediction while Pimns psionically attempts to double pain.**
- He carries the same speak-with-dead intelligence as Pimns: the gith captors and their magical weapons; **the gith entering both great archways briefly and returning angry**; [[Gort]] starting the rebellion and dying with two guards and many slaves; and that **neither of them knows how the survivors crossed the chasm, because they were dead at the time.**

## Knowledge
As [[Pimns]] — the captors, the archways, Gort, and the gap in their own account. **Given his Intelligence 13 against Pimns's 9, Solaq is the better of the two to interrogate**, and a GM running the speak-with-dead scene should route the detail through him.

## Current Activity
Standing where he died, beside her.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Solaq is neutral and Pimns is neutral evil, and the module never explains it.** Two dwarves, the same arena, the same rebellion, the same death, the same haunting — and only one of them turned. That is either an oversight in a 1994 stat block or the single most interesting detail in the encounter. **Recommended: treat it as real.** He is the one still holding on to something.
- **He is therefore the negotiable one.** If the campaign wants the chasm resolved without a fight, Solaq is where to apply pressure — a neutral undead with Intelligence 13 and Charisma 14 who died wanting a life rather than wanting revenge.
- **He is also the better witness.** Same knowledge, far higher mental scores. Speak with dead should get more out of him than out of her.
- **His death field opens the fight before either of them has screamed.** Play the order exactly: the room goes cold and lethal, and *then* she screams. It is a better sequence than the reverse.
- See [[Pimns]] for the [[Roi]] connection, the archway intelligence, and the laying-to-rest question — all of which apply to both.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** make the alignment split load-bearing. If the party can reach Solaq, Pimns will not follow him, and a resolution that frees one and not the other is a genuinely bleak and appropriate outcome for this campaign.
- Route the speak-with-dead intelligence through him for the mental-score reason.
- If the campaign adopts a laying-to-rest method, he goes first and she does not go at all without him.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Gladiator *(in life)* — **undead: dwarven banshee**
- Level: 4 *(Shadowdark scaling of 2e 5)*
- Armor Class: 10
- Hit Points: 40
- Movement: near
- Strength +3, Dexterity +1, Constitution +3, Intelligence +1, Wisdom +0, Charisma +2
- Alignment: **Neutral** — printed, and deliberately not evil
- Morale: 12 — bound to the site
- Attacks: 2 attacks per round.
  - *Fists* +4, 1d2+3 — negligible, and not the threat
- **Death Field:** 20 points of damage in an area, deployed on the **first** round. This is his opening move and it precedes any scream.
- **Malediction (scream):** deployed on the **second** round. Save or suffer severe damage.
- **Undead:** immune to sleep, charm, hold, fear and cold.
- **Bound to the Site:** cannot leave the promenade.
- **Speak With Dead:** he will answer, and answers better than Pimns does.
- **Running him at the table:** the quieter of the two, and the one a clever party can reach. Give him the pauses.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Solaq, 5th-level Dwarven Male Gladiator, **AL N**, AC 10, MV 12, HD 5, hp 40, THAC0 16, #AT 2, Dmg 1d2 (fist), ML Steady (12), Str 17, Dex 13, Con 18, Int 13, Wis 12, Cha 14. As with Pimns, the source supplies banshee abilities through the combat sequence rather than a banshee stat line.
