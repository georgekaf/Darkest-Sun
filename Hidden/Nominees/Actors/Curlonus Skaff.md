# Actor: Curlonus Skaff

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-curlonus-skaff`. **The party will meet her as a pirate chief. She is actually [[Dregoth]]'s recruiting sergeant, and she does not know it.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
An earth cleric who was searching the ruins of Giustenal, met something she believed was a servant of elemental earth, took over a collapsing raider tribe on its instructions, and is now building a hundred-strong pirate army that will be turned into dray or undead the moment it is large enough.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous — **and manipulated**
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: **the Dust Devils** (her own tribe); **elemental earth**, as she understands it; **[[Dregoth]]**, without knowing it
- Current location: `location-dust-devil-island` — a low, flat island of jutting rock in the Silt Sea, tents around an ancient ruin
- Current route, when traveling: raiding the **shoals between Cromlin and Break Shore** aboard two captured silt skimmers
- Role: Leader of the Dust Devils

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Not printed.)* An earth cleric in **carru leather**, carrying an **obsidian club**, wearing **an amulet bearing a crimson sun trapped in a dragon's claw.**

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed except the recorded method.)* A woman who solved a leadership problem by **challenging the previous leader and winning**, then immediately laid out a business plan: *"First,"* she explained, *"we need to secure a couple of silt skimmers . . ."*

Direct, organised, and **devout** — she believes she is doing elemental work.

### Public Reputation
The reason the shoals between Cromlin and Break Shore are dangerous. **She took a tribe in disarray and on the verge of collapse and turned it into prosperous pirates in only a few short months.**

## Confirmed Facts
- **Curlonus Skaff, an earth cleric, took control of the raiders and turned them into prosperous pirates in only a few short months.**
- **She was searching the ruins of Giustenal when she encountered Dregoth.** **The undead dragon king convinced Skaff that he served the powers of elemental earth.**
- His instruction is quoted: ***"Raise an army from among the raiding tribes, and we can fulfil the will of the elemental powers."***
- **He gave her an amulet bearing Dregoth's newest symbol — the crimson sun trapped in a dragon's claw — explaining that it was a conduit to the earth powers.** **In fact he has sent her a number of visions to support this claim, using the amulet as a focus for his own psionic abilities.**
- **She searched along the coast of the Silt Sea until she found a small raiding tribe she could take control of. The Dust Devils were the perfect choice, as the tribe was in disarray and on the verge of collapse.** **Skaff challenged the leader, won, and presented a new method of operation.**
- **The tribe has already grown larger and more powerful thanks to a number of successful raids.**
- **When the tribe's members number 100 strong, Dregoth will summon them to Giustenal. Then, depending on his mood, he will transform them into dray or turn them into undead.**
- **Her chamber is Room C of the ancient ruin** on the island — the pirate leader's personal quarters.
- **Among her personal possessions is a locked wooden box (she has the only key)** filled with gems and jewellery worth **15 gold pieces, 22 silver pieces, and 1d20 × 10 ceramic pieces.**
- **She keeps *candles of invocation* burning in her chamber at all times**, giving her **access to higher-level spells as long as the candles continue to burn.** **If the island is invaded, her first action will be to summon an earth elemental to help defend her camp.**
- **She secured a ring bearing the royal signet of Raam — Abalach-Re's own — and it took many bribes and threats to do it.** **Dregoth wants a personal item of the sorcerer-queen to use as a focus for spells he is developing, as part of his plans to gain revenge.**
- **She left the ring for Dregoth in the palace at Giustenal, and [[Nallan]] found it before Dregoth could retrieve it.** The ring is worth **400 gold pieces**, *"if the bearer can locate a buyer willing to risk Raam's wrath."*
- Her band numbers **28 pirates in the village**, plus two skimmer crews of **eight pirates and a pirate captain each**.

## Traits and Pressures
- Ambition: **High.** She walked into a failing tribe and took it by combat.
- Caution: Moderate. She keeps *candles of invocation* lit permanently — a standing precaution, and an expensive one.
- Secrecy: 3 — the tribe knows she raids; it does not know what for.
- Loyalty: **To her patron, absolutely.** That is the tragedy.
- Cruelty: She raids skimmers and kills crews to plunder at leisure. **Her tribe's tactics are to incapacitate, board, and kill.**
- Risk tolerance: High — she extorted a sorcerer-queen's signet with bribes and threats.
- Wealth: Modest personally. **The locked box is worth less than a good sword.**
- Status: Absolute within the Dust Devils.
- **Faith:** the load-bearing trait. Everything she does follows from believing an undead dragon king is an elemental power.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is a victim wearing a villain's costume, and the party will probably kill her without ever finding out.** That is the interesting problem. **Everything needed to save her is on her body** — the amulet, the visions, the borrowed symbol.
- **The amulet is the campaign's best delivery mechanism and the module weaponises it:** *"Dregoth is psionically linked to the amulet Skaff wears. If one of the PCs decides to carry the amulet, he or she will receive a vision later in the adventure."* The vision **should occur before the PCs actually reach the ruined city**, at night as the PC drifts off, and it opens with the line she has heard a hundred times: **"The powers of earth command you, Curlonus Skaff—"** — the PC hears Dregoth address *her name*, from inside her amulet. **That is how the party learns what she was.**
- **The hundred-head clock is a live campaign timer.** Every successful raid is progress toward her tribe being summoned to Giustenal and converted. **If the party never deals with the Dust Devils, the Dust Devils become dray or undead — Dregoth's army grows either way.**
- **Killing her does not stop it.** The scheme is Dregoth's, the method is repeatable, and he is running the identical play on the **Silt Stalkers** ([[Eevuu Silt Stalker]]) and on the first generation dray ([[The Spirit of Kragmorta]]). **Three tribes, one con.** A party that spots the pattern has understood the antagonist.
- **The Raam ring is a whole second plot.** Dregoth wants a personal focus item from **Abalach-Re** for spells he is developing **as part of his plans to gain revenge**. Skaff got it. Nallan stole it by accident and died holding it. **Whoever picks it up off Nallan's corpse is now carrying the thing an undead dragon king extorted a sorcerer-queen to obtain.**
- *(Proposed.)* She can be turned. She is a cleric of a genuine element being impersonated by an undead thing that hates the living — **and she is the one person Dregoth has personally lied to who is still alive to be told.**
- *(Proposed.)* The candles are finite. A siege that outlasts them takes her whole upper spell range with it.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: use the amulet vision exactly as printed**, and let a PC wear it long enough to hear Dregoth speak to her. It converts a loot drop into a revelation.
- **Offer her the truth before the fight, not after.** A party that shows her the dragon's claw on her own amulet and names the thing under Giustenal has a shot at an ally with two skimmers and a hundred raiders.
- Run the hundred-member count as a visible clock. Let the party hear the number from a captured pirate.
- Let Gaff push for the pursuit and **[[Passk]]** push against it — the module gives both NPCs their printed positions and they conflict. Good scene.
- Decide what happens to the Raam ring. It is a 400 gp object and a diplomatic incident.
