# Actor: Quixoth

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-quixoth`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A 10th-level preserver who has been following the party invisibly for three encounters, burning his last spells to stay alive, and who will trade everything he knows about the gith mine for help with one badly injured priestess.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active — **at large, invisible, out of spells**
- Faction or allegiance: none; captured Nibenese mine personnel
- Current location: the guard station overlooking the gith mining facility, deep below the Black Spine
- Current route, when traveling: shadowing the PCs, invisibly, since the chasm crossing
- Role: Preserver; escaped captive; the party's guide to the gith mine

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Not seen until he chooses. A human man of about six feet, thin from captivity, wearing **bracers of defence AC 6** — the only equipment he managed to keep.

He is the presence a rogue with a hear-noise rating of at least 25% gets **one chance per encounter** to "accidentally" notice, and that a thri-kreen may simply feel as the sense of being watched.

### Manner and Voice
Careful, formal, and deliberately unthreatening. He announces himself from off to one side, in a quiet soothing tone, **specifically so as not to startle armed strangers in a tunnel**:

> *"Greetings, rescuers. Please do not be startled. My name is Quixoth. A compatriot and I were accidently captured along with the slaves when the gith attacked the mines."*

**He then remains silent and lets the players ask questions, answering as truthfully as possible.** He does not oversell, does not exaggerate, and marks the limits of his own knowledge unprompted.

### Public Reputation
Presumed dead or enslaved. He was taken in a surprise raid alongside the mine's labour.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Human male preserver, 10th level**, neutral. Intelligence 17, Constitution 16, Dexterity 16. 41 hit points.
- **He and a compatriot were accidentally captured along with the slaves when the gith attacked the mines.**
- He has been **invisible and shadowing the PCs since the far side of the chasm**, studying them before making contact. **The DM is instructed to safeguard him** — his influence should be very subtle until he reveals himself.
- **A rogue with hear noise 25%+ has one chance per encounter to "accidentally" hear him.** A thri-kreen may feel the party is being watched.
- **He kept his bracers AC 6.** He has **read magic, strength and haste** left; **all his other spells were used during his escape.**
- He followed the gith dung crew out of the mine **but could not return, for fear of being detected by the uoo-taas** — the blind, psionic, salt-dependent creatures the gith keep as guard animals.
- **He offers to bargain: information for aid and rescue.**
- His compatriot is **[[Iselle]] of the Wing, a priestess of air**, badly injured and hidden in a niche. **He does not know how long she will survive without healing.**
- **He demands the PCs go to Iselle first (area 15) and try to help her**, and **will only cast his remaining spells for the party if they do.**
- **He refuses to accompany the PCs into the fray — for now — preferring to stay and guard Iselle.**
- He may be used as a helpful NPC **or as a replacement player character** if someone dies.

### What he knows, verbatim
- *"I do know the general workings of the mine, but I do not know the exact compliment of gith or the number of captured slaves that remain alive."*
- *"We were captured in a surprise raid by the gith. After a long time underground we were blindfolded. I heard horrible sounds. Part of me was curious, but I feared for my life. At one point, cloth was wrapped around my feet as we walked. I do not know why. It was removed later."*
- *"During my incarceration I used a spell to comprehend the gith language. I overheard the gith commander talking to one of the guards, regarding the preparation for someone he called 'queen or nest-mother.' I remember the commander said that all was going well, but someone or something named '[[Nyzethgyr]]' was still displeased with the progress of the mine."*
- *"Something sinister is occurring at the smelter. Not the main one in the center of the compound, but the smaller one in the back. Slaves enter for a work shift but never return."*
- *"Slaves are kept in guarded pits in the back of the operation. They are rotated every ten hours as or so. A half-dozen gith guard the pens."*
- *"There is something terrible beyond the great arch. Several slaves have attempted to escape via the arch. There is a flash of pinkish light when they run in, then a scream, and that's the last they are heard from."*
- *"The gith guards and workers rotate through the great archway. Where they come from or go to is beyond me. **They are due to rotate again in 3 hours!**"*

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The foot-cloth line is the best-placed clue in the campaign and it is trivially missable.** Quixoth reports being blindfolded, hearing horrible sounds, and having cloth wrapped around his feet for part of the march. The party — if they came through Yathazor — has seen the pile of ten pairs of leather slippers near Zigath's table and the gith taboo about the hallowed ground. **He walked through the sacred city and does not know it.** A party that connects those two facts learns the geography of the entire underground.
- **He is a hard bargainer disguised as a grateful victim.** He will not spend his three remaining spells, and will not move, until Iselle is helped. This is not stated as a threat and it is entirely non-negotiable. Play it warmly and hold the line.
- **The three-hour rotation is a live timer.** *"They are due to rotate again in 3 hours!"* The party has a window on the great archway, and it is the only route onward.
- **He is the campaign's designated PC replacement.** A 10th-level preserver, freely offered by the module. If anyone died in Zigath's Nest, this is the answer.
- *(Proposed.)* He is not being modest about his spell reserves. He is genuinely down to three, and if the fight comes to him before Iselle is up, he has nothing.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** run the reveal exactly as printed — off to one side, quiet voice, apology first. It is a well-written entrance and it establishes immediately that this NPC is not a threat, which is rare enough in this campaign to be worth preserving.
- Give the rogue the hear-noise chance in each of the three encounters he shadows them through. A party that catches him early gets a very different, more suspicious scene, and it should be allowed.
- If the party refuses to help Iselle, honour it: he keeps the spells, keeps the intelligence beyond what he has already given, and stays with her. He does not retaliate.
- Pair his testimony with [[Durdon]]'s from Adventure Three. Between them the party has *the Reclamation*, *the new queen*, *the super-lord*, and *Nyzethgyr* — four pieces of the same picture from two prisoners.
