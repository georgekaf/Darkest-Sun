## One-Sentence Summary
A blue-haired elf water priestess who walked into Kharanok among the refugees, healed a dying half-giant on her first day, and — on her second outing with the party — stepped onto an unknown teleport plate out of pure curiosity and became the first of them to stand in the ruins of Giustenal.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Elf. Blue hair and strange tattoos, striking enough that the village noticed her the day she arrived. Hooded desert wear; she covers up well when she has reason to.

### Manner and Voice
Curious past the point of caution — the defining trait so far at the table. Refuses to eat meat, and does not make a speech about it. Young enough in the work that the rest of the party treats her as the junior member.

### Public Reputation
In Kharanok: one of the refugees, and the one who can heal. Outside it: nothing yet.

## Confirmed Facts
- **ad5** (day 111–112 window): appears in Kharanok among recently arrived refugees while the party is carrying the badly wounded Gur-da back. Elf, blue hair, tattoos, a priestess connected to the element of water, refuses meat. She heals Gur-da and agrees to join the party's kank expedition.
- **ad5:** blinds the largest wild kank with a spell, letting Shaka kill it outright with advantage.
- **ad5:** attended. **ad6:** did not attend. **ad7:** attended.
- **ad7, day 117:** Relo shows her the pit where the party's Crystal Dust is stored and asks her to carry what she can, since he cannot bear much weight.
- **ad7, day 117:** fascinated by the lit gate plate, she climbs onto it and sits down in the middle to feel what the magic does. Nobody reaches her in time. She is transported alone to a structure above the **Giustenal** ruins — dozens of miles away — and sees the half-sunk city, the dragon statues and the boiling tar lakes from a rise.
- **ad7:** she hides, hooded, and waits for the light to return, unable to judge how much time passes or even to memorise the place. She hears the voice of **a childhood friend she lost to the sandstorms**, telling her not to meddle with what she does not know and asking why she left her. The voice stops as she is about to answer.
- **ad7:** the party relights the gate with fifteen more handfuls; she returns at the moment a voice behind her shouts **"JESSIX, SOMEONE IS HERE!"** She was gone only a few minutes.
- **ad7:** she reports the wasteland, the abandoned city, the black liquid unlike the tar they know, the dragon statues, and the voice — noting she could not tell whether it spoke Common or her own language, which the party reads as proof that something living is out there.
- **ad7:** fails, with Cypoul, to blind the second giant scorpion during the night harvest in the scrublands.
- **She is a wild talent.** Her power is **Body Equilibrium**: for 10 rounds she adjusts her body weight to match the surface she stands on, letting her walk on water, quicksand, silt or even a spider's web without sinking or breaking through. *(GM confirmation, 9 August 2026.)*

## GM-Only Secrets
- She is a marked mind. The Caller does not forget one that answered, and she came back through the gate without dying inside the walls — so nothing was taken, and nothing was finished either.
- **Her wild talent is why the Caller found her.** By its printed rules it probes only full psionicists and wild talents, of the ancestries that lived in Giustenal when Dregoth died — elves among them. Ranni is both. Standing on that rise she was not a bystander who happened to be in range; she was exactly what the five-mile net is searching for, and it answered within minutes.
- The player does not know this connection. Body Equilibrium reads as a harmless utility talent — walking on silt, crossing tar. Its real cost so far is that it made her visible.
