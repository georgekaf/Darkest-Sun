## One-Sentence Summary
A nine-foot githyanki who found an artifact, sealed off a piece of the Astral Plane, rebuilt the inside of it to feel like Athas, and intends to breed a generation of githyanki psionicists on stolen Athasian minds before turning on her own lich-queen.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
> *"The queen is an impressive figure, standing a full 9 feet tall. She is dressed in clothing and armor that could purchase a city on your world. In her hand she holds a scepter. The ruby at the tip pulses with an angry red light."*

The scepter is **Yrinolir**: a thick, rune-covered platinum shaft topped with a great ruby — **the crystallised heart of the pit fiend Nalcariz**, which still beats with a dusky light.

### Manner and Voice
Imperious, impatient, and entirely uninterested in being lied to. She opens with:

> *"Spies in MY palace! If you wish a clean death in combat, you will tell me who sent you and what you have learned. If not, I can always operate on your mind. Well, what say you?"*

**She is using her *truthear* devotion and expects them to lie.** When their story is finished she demands to know **why Athas has so many people with strong powers of the mind** — and whatever they say, **she does not believe them.**

After the arena, if they survive:

> *"Congratulations, you have survived the trials of Trinth. You have earned your freedom. Exit the arena through the doorway before you."*
>
> *"I have decided to allow you a rare honor. Kneel and receive my blessing."*

### Public Reputation
In Galnaki: absolute. In the wider Astral Plane: an ambitious exile whose self-imposed isolation the rest of her race made no attempt to end.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Githyanki, self-proclaimed queen of Galnaki.** Nine feet tall. AC −5, hp 102, 200 PSPs, magic resistance 25%.
- **When Trinth came into possession of the artifact Yrinolir, she began to conceive a great plan. Whether this was her doing, the item's doing, or a combination of the two is impossible to say.**
- She assembled an army of faithful followers, claimed an asteroid fragment, and built **Galnaki** on it.
- She researched **an ancient story handed down in her family for generations** — of a strange, almost uninhabitable planet on the Prime Material Plane whose harsh conditions had twisted its inhabitants, **making them far more powerful as they fought to survive.**
- She dreamed of finding that world, claiming it, and **using the powers she would find there to seize control of the entire Prime Material Plane** — and then to **overthrow the githyanki lich-queen.**
- Her agents found **Athas**. She then found **Yathazor**, an ancient githyanki city buried beneath a chain of mountains, and chose it as her base.
- **Then, with horror, she found the gith.** She recognised them as a devolved offshoot of her own race, **saw the hand of the githzerai in it, and vowed revenge.** She decided the gith were an inferior race, to be permitted to live only while useful — **in the end she intends to have them all killed, so the githyanki need never look upon this horrible reflection of themselves again.**
- **The area around Yathazor is difficult for her people to plane shift into and out of.** To bring an army through she needed a fixed portal, so she called **Raskon**, one of her most trusted officers, and commanded him to build one. **He dubbed it the Nightmare Gate.**
- **With Yrinolir, she enclosed Galnaki and a great region of space around it in an impenetrable globe of force.** Within that barrier she **recreated the conditions her army would encounter on Athas — the atmosphere, the gravity, and even the temperature — so that when the time came, her army would already be acclimated to a hostile planet.**
- **Her plan to overthrow the lich-queen is not known to her followers.** To almost all of them, **the idea of any githyanki attacking another is obscene.** The module states plainly: **only the corrupting influence of Yrinolir enabled Trinth to think of such a thing.**
- Her private chambers are **seven rooms sealed in solid steel and laced with lead to block unwanted scrying.** In them are **detailed accounts of her experiments on the psionic creatures of Athas.**
- Her actual objective: **learn the secret of Athasian psionic power, then alter the githyanki children born in Galnaki to produce a whole generation of tremendously powerful githyanki psionicists.** With that army she would return to the Astral Plane, drive out the lich-queen, and claim the entire realm.
- **She demands that captured spies be taken before her**, and deals with them by **dropping them into the arena beneath her throne and watching them die in combat.** Those who suffer this fate **are promised their freedom. Although this is a lie**, it does give the characters a chance to reach the throne room.
- Her jade throne has **three hidden buttons**: one drops the silver force-field floor into the arena; one can make it transparent for spectating; one **magically transports anyone standing on it to the chamber housing the Galnaki end of the Nightmare Gate**; and a third opens a secret door to her private quarters.
- After the arena she offers the survivors a "blessing." **If any of them kneel, she touches them with Yrinolir, attempting to charm them.** She was impressed with them, but **assumes they are too badly beaten up to think of attacking her. The characters will never get a better opportunity.**
- **The scepter can be pulled from her hands by any character with a hit on AC 4.** Anyone who grasps it **takes 1d4 points of damage per round unless they are of evil alignment.** **No one can command it while the queen lives** — she is linked too closely with it — and **anyone holding it can be charmed by her.**
- **If the party actually threatens her life she flees** for a secret door beside the stairs. Githyanki knights cover her retreat. **It takes her one round to reach the door; on the second round she races down the stairs, headed for the room with the Nightmare Gate in it.**
- Equipment: a **vorpal sword +5**, and Yrinolir, which can act as **a +5 rod of smiting**.
- She has **+1 on saves versus illusions and enchantment/charm spells; opponents suffer −1 on their saves against such spells when she casts them.**
- **She cannot dimension door away from danger inside her own sphere.** She yells for more guards; attacking the githyanki on the stairs prevents reinforcement from that direction.
- **When she is killed, the whole place begins to break up** — cracks lace the walls, the asteroid shakes, supports pull apart. **Yrinolir then speaks in the characters' heads:** *"I alone can get you out of here now. Without the queen to control it, the Nightmare Gate will explode. If you want to live, you must act quickly."*

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She may not be the author of any of this.** The module hedges twice, deliberately: the plan may be *"her doing, the item's doing, or a combination of the two,"* and **only the corrupting influence of Yrinolir enabled her to conceive of attacking her own race.** Played straight, Trinth is the campaign's final villain. Played on the hedge, **she is the artifact's second-to-last victim**, and the actual antagonist is the beating heart of a pit fiend on a stick. See Nyzethgyr for a related reading.
- **The answer to her central question is in Adventure Four and she will never get it.** She wants to know why Athasians are so psionically powerful. Part One of *The City of Calamity* raises exactly that: the githzerai psionic devastators may have jarred non-psionic creatures into releasing buried talents, or wiped out the psionic parasites that plagued sensitives. **Her own enemies may have made Athas what it is, while making the gith she despises.** A party that learned this in Yathazor is holding the thing she has been torturing prisoners for.
- **Killing her is not enough and the module says so.** *"Slaying the queen is not enough to protect Athas from the githyanki, as the artifact Trinth is using still leaves the vortex open."* The adventure requires the scepter to be destroyed in a dimensional rift — and the only rift available is the collapsing Gate the party needs to escape through.
- **The kneeling scene is the ambush the players get to spring.** She is offering a blessing with a charm attached, she has decided they are too beaten to try anything, and the module states outright: **the characters will never get a better opportunity.** Do not rush past it.
- **She flees rather than dies.** One round to the secret door, one round down the stairs, heading for the Gate. The final fight is a chase.
- **Her chambers are the best unclaimed loot and lore in the campaign** — seven lead-lined rooms of experimental records on Athasian psionics, plus a horde the module tells the GM to hand-pick rather than roll.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** foreground the Yrinolir hedge. Let a player wonder aloud whether the queen is driving this, and never confirm it. The scepter speaking in their heads the moment she dies is the module's own answer and it is chilling.
- Have her ask the psionics question early — in the prison, through Raskon — so that when she asks it in person the players already know they might have the answer.
- The lich-queen thread is a campaign's worth of material sitting behind the finale. Trinth's followers do not know; the political prisoners in her own dungeon **are the lich-queen's agents**, held so word of the Athasian conquest does not spread.
- If the campaign continues past Black Spine, whoever holds Galnaki after Trinth inherits a functioning Gate to Athas and an army bred to survive it.
