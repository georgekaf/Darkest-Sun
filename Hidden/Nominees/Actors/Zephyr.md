## One-Sentence Summary
A shaven-headed priest of air, played as a player character but fielded as an NPC ally, who reads Draxian and Primordial — and who is therefore the only reason anyone in Kharanok understands that the stone plates under the mines are gates.

## Confirmed Facts
- **ad4:** introduced in Kharanok as a **new priest of air sent by Kalia** to reinforce the party.
- **ad4:** when the party asks Kalia for more rope, she refuses — she has already given them Zephyr and has nothing else to offer.
- **ad4:** joins the search for the scattered syggra and a kank. In the cave fight, poisoned and briefly paralysed by a wild kank's bite, alongside Rhugor and Gur-da.
- **ad4:** **stabilises Gur-da** when he goes down under repeated bat attacks.
- **ad7, day 117:** brought down into the hidden gate room by Relo to read the library of thirty to forty rolled hides. She **reads Draxian and Primordial**.
- **ad7:** establishes the gate mechanics from the scrolls — the plates are gates leading almost anywhere; the broken left-hand plate led deeper into the mountain; the intact one leads far away and works in both directions, lighting the plate at the far end simultaneously. **Ten handfuls of Crystal Dust to wake it, five per trip to hold it open; without the five it transports once and dies.**
- **ad7:** tells Relo that activation from the far side should work in theory, given the same raw material.
- **ad7:** warns that whoever broke the second plate did it so that it would work **badly**, not so that it would fail.
- **ad7:** in this session she is **an NPC ally, not played as a PC.**
