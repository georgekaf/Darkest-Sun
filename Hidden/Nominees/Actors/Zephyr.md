A weathered human priest of air, played as a player character but fielded as an NPC ally, who reads Draxian and Primordial — and who is therefore the only reason anyone in Kharanok understands that the stone plates under the mines are gates.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A lean, weathered human man with short-cropped white hair and a stern, disciplined expression. Deeply lined face, sun-darkened skin — years under a harsh sun. Layered, dust-stained robes in muted tan, beige and faded rust-orange, wrapped tight at torso and waist; cloth bandages on forearms and lower legs; simple open sandals. Practical and worn, not decorative.

Almost all his equipment is bone, hide, cord and cloth. A large rectangular frame of lashed bones rides at his side like a portable rack, stretcher or scavenger's frame; he carries a long heavy bone club decorated with feathers and leather bindings, with smaller bones, cords and primitive tools strapped across his chest. He reads as an Athasian desert scavenger, hunter and bone-worker — someone used to travelling with no access to metal and making his gear from whatever can be recovered.

### Manner and Voice
Stern and disciplined. Eyes widening at every scroll he opens. Reads first, explains after.

### Public Reputation
Kalia's contribution to the party's strength, and she has said plainly that Zephyr was the help she had to give — there is nothing else coming.

## Confirmed Facts
- **ad4:** introduced in Kharanok as a **new priest of air sent by Kalia** to reinforce the party.

- **ad7, day 117:** brought down into the hidden gate room by Relo to read the library of thirty to forty rolled hides. He **reads Draxian and Primordial**.
- **ad7:** establishes the gate mechanics from the scrolls — the plates are gates leading almost anywhere; the broken left-hand plate led deeper into the mountain; the intact one leads far away and works in both directions, lighting the plate at the far end simultaneously. **Ten handfuls of Crystal Dust to wake it, five per trip to hold it open; without the five it transports once and dies.**
- **ad7:** tells Relo that activation from the far side should work in theory, given the same raw material.
- **ad7:** warns that whoever broke the second plate did it so that it would work **badly**, not so that it would fail.
- **ad7:** in this session he is **an NPC ally, not played as a PC.**
