# Location: The Citadel (Sunken City)

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-sunken-citadel`. Parent `location-sunken-city` resolves and already records Abalach-Re's sealing and her templars' search.)*

## Summary
Dregoth's citadel inside the Sunken City — the building Abalach-Re thought worth preserving when she sealed the district in solid sand, and then stripped of most of what made it worth preserving.

## Classification
- Location subtype: ruined citadel, seven mapped rooms
- Region: The Sunken City, area 5
- Terrain: sealed beneath the sand dome
- Parent location: `location-sunken-city`
- Status: preserved but looted
- Controller: nobody
- Contested by: whatever the sealing trapped in with it

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
The draconic motifs of Dregoth's reign, intact in a way nothing on the surface is — because the sand that buried this district was made solid before the weather could get to it.

## Physical Features — the seven rooms
- **A. Templar Offices** — stone desks, ruined scrolls, charred records.
- **B. Crafting Room** — Dregoth's item-crafting area; the wards are broken. **Metal-working tools survive**, and some were made to work metal.
- **C. Hall of Counsel** — wooden benches facing an obsidian platform where Dregoth sat in dragon form.
- **D. Hall of Spells** — empty scroll cases, and a hidden space holding **five wizard spells, 1st–4th level**.
- **E. Atrium** — dead plants, lifeless dirt mixed with ash.
- **F. Testing Chamber** — scorch marks, and **cages used for the dray transformation experiments**.
- **G. Secret Room** — behind a secret door: **three spellbooks, a wand of polymorphing (4 charges), a wand of illumination (6 charges), six empty glass vials**, and a loose page sketching a human, a humanoid reptile and a full dray, annotated in an ancient language.
