# Location: The Rubble Reef

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-rubble-reef`. Both endpoints resolve: `location-giustenal-ruins` and `location-blasted-spire`.)*

## Summary
A path of silt-covered buildings and rubble reaching from Giustenal's shore out to the Blasted Spire half a mile north — walkable, in the sense that the tops of drowned buildings are walkable.

## Classification
- Location subtype: crossing / route feature
- Region: Sea of Silt, off the Giustenal shore
- Terrain: submerged ruins under silt
- Parent location: — (spans between two existing locations)
- Status: passable, treacherous
- Controller: nobody
- Contested by: whatever lives in the silt

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
Not open silt but not solid ground either — the buried roofline of a city, half a mile of it, with the silt lying over everything and hiding which step holds.

## Physical Features
- Silt-covered structures and rubble forming a continuous path from the Giustenal shore to the Blasted Spire.
- **Half a mile** in length.
- **Slightly more difficult to navigate than the Silt Road** — the book's own comparison, and the Silt Road already has a record.

## Population and Occupants
- None.

## Resources
- None. It is a way across, not a place.

## Dangers
- Silt-covered ruins are where silt serpents nest, and silt horrors work these shores.
- Every hazard is under the surface and none of it is visible.

## Opportunities
