# Location: The Tar Mine (Ghodan Brothers' Pit)

*(New location. **No `actor`-side gap and no location record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-09 against `mk-repos/mk-sandbox/locations/` (56 records). Proposed id `location-tar-mine`. Both owners already exist as vault nominees: [[Durex Ghodan]], [[Haltham Ghodan]].)*

## Summary
A working tar pit two miles due south of Cromlin, turned into a profitable weatherproofing industry by two free dwarven brothers out of Raam, worked by twenty slaves, and supplying the Sky Singers and House Shom with everything that has to be sealed against silt.

## Classification
- Location subtype: industrial site / craft settlement
- Region: Giustenal Environs, Silt Coast
- Terrain: tar pit amid stony barrens
- Parent location: — (independent; nearest settlement `location-cromlin`)
- Coordinates or map reference: **two miles due south of Cromlin**, which itself sits about 20 miles west of Giustenal
- Status: active and thriving
- Controller: Durex and Haltham Ghodan, brothers, free citizens of Raam
- Contested by: bandits, raiders and gith — on the caravan route rather than at the pit itself

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
The smell arrives first. A large pit of boiling tar, and beside it a row of craft shops thrown up from imported building materials — the only structures for miles that were built rather than left behind. Slaves work the pit and the shops both. Everything here is black to the elbow.

## Physical Features
- A large natural tar pit, one of many in the barrens south of Giustenal.
- Craft shops built next to the pit from materials hauled in for the purpose.
- Slave quarters for twenty — crafters and labourers both.

## Population and Occupants
- **Two owners:** Durex and Haltham Ghodan, dwarves, brothers, free citizens from Raam.
- **Twenty slaves**, brought in at the founding: both crafters and labourers.
- No other population recorded.

## Resources
Track only resources that create decisions.
- Water: not recorded — nothing here suggests a source. **Anyone stopping here has to bring it.**
- Food: not recorded.
- Trade: **major, and specialised.** Wineskins, crew-size water containers, sand-proofed armour, silt-skimmer sails, and anything else that must be sealed against the elements.
- Verdance: none.
- Guards: hired per caravan, **never more than six**, at **5 sp per guard**.
- Other: raw tar, in quantity, next to a region where tar is otherwise a hazard rather than a commodity.

## Important Areas
- The pit itself.
- The craft shops — where the actual value is made.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-durex-ghodan` and `actor-haltham-ghodan` — proposed ids; both have vault nominee sheets.
- `faction-house-shom` — the buyer. Caravans go to Cromlin twice a month.
- `faction-sky-singers` and other local traders — the **suppliers**, providing the bladders and leather the brothers' crafters work.

## Dangers
- **The caravan route, not the site.** Twice-monthly runs to Cromlin worth 11–20 gp are frequent targets of bandits, raiders and gith. The gith want the goods for their own use rather than for resale.
- The route is short, so there is little room to vary it — the leaders try, and the run is still rated **extremely dangerous**.
- Standing region hazard: **blackwashes** — silt-covered tar pits that look like solid ground, betrayed only by small vents like ant mounds with hazy air above them.

## Opportunities
- **Guard work**, openly advertised, at a known rate — the single easiest legitimate hook into the Giustenal environs for any party.
- A supply relationship worth having if the party ever needs anything sealed, floated or sand-proofed.
- Twenty slaves in one place, two owners, and no garrison worth the name.

## Connections
- Destination: **Cromlin**
  - Existing location ID: `location-cromlin`
  - Existing route ID: **none exists** — propose one; the caravan runs it twice a month.
  - Travel notes: two miles due south of Cromlin. Short, unavoidable, and the reason guards are paid at all.

## Local Clocks and Pressures
None established. A raid clock on the caravan run would be the natural first one.

## GM-Only Secrets
- The pit is the reason the brothers are wealthy and the reason they are a target. Nothing at the table has tested it yet.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
