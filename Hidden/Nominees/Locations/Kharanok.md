## Summary
A mining settlement in the Black Spine foothills, liberated from gith occupation on day 103, rationing water and running on too few hands — and now, unknown to almost everyone in it, sitting on top of a bound ancient entity and a working gate to the Ruins of Giustenal.

## Classification
- Location subtype: settlement (mining)
- Region: Black Spine foothills
- Terrain: mountain settlement above worked mines
- Status: active, rebuilding, under-garrisoned
- Controller: the settlement council — in practice **[[Kalia]]**, who took over resource distribution after her husband died in the gith invasion
- Contested by: gith, still active in the deeper mine reaches

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
Dust glittering in the rising sun, and a queue at the cistern for the day's water ration before anything else happens.

## Physical Features
*Existing features retained.* Proposed additions from days 113–117:

- **The gate chamber beneath the mines is open and working** — see [[Obsidian Mines]]. Two hexagonal plates; the intact one runs both ways to Giustenal for ten handfuls of Crystal Dust plus five per trip.
- **A hide library** of thirty to forty rolled scrolls beneath a flagstone in that chamber, barely read.
- **The passage was re-hidden** at Breck's request, and Kalia has deliberately not been told.

## Population and Occupants
**Proposed additions — the population changed materially on day 117:**

- **Seven new residents**, brought back from the ruins of Zharvek on day 117: **[[Tzala]]**, a self-taught alchemist now tending the sick and working as a crafter; **three recruits**, handed to [[Breck]] for watch duty; **two workers**, assigned to [[Dorak]] in the mines; and one more.
- **[[Sylvar]]**, an elderly traveller out of Nibenay who speaks with nature and speaks halfling — now the party's interpreter. Kalia docked him half a water ration for five days for talking back to her.
- **[[Ranni]]** arrived earlier with the refugees on day 112 and stayed.
- **The watch was two men** — one at the tower, one at the gate — until the three recruits arrived. It is now five.

## Resources
- Water: rationed daily at the cistern; the binding constraint on everything.
- Food: unchanged.
- Trade: **Crystal Dust production restarted** — thirty-two pieces on day 117 alone, with two trainees now working under Dorak.
- Guards: **five**, up from two.
- Other: **healing potions are now possible** — Tzala's recipe is sandbloom, water and a handful of Crystal Dust. Twenty sandblooms were harvested in the scrublands on the night of day 117. Brewing had not started.

## Important Areas
- The cistern and the square.
- The shade houses, where the sick are tended.
- The old quarry above the canyon — where Dorak drinks, and where villagers have killed themselves against the stones.
- The mine entrance, gith heads still on spears outside.
- **The hidden gate chamber**, below.

## Important Actors and Factions
Retained, plus:
- **[[Breck]]** — not merely the alert guard the record describes. **Veiled Alliance**, and he has told the party so. Now trains the recruits.
- **[[Dorak]]** — the record already names him as the ex-foreman and former gith slave. He is now **the gate's operator**, and *(GM-only)* the holder of the medallion that binds the thing beneath the village.
- **[[Tzala]]** — new. Alchemist, potion-maker, on Kalia's orders tending the sick.
- **[[The Moonbeast]]** — *(GM-only)* bound beneath the settlement, and the cause of two things already on this record.
- `faction-veiled-alliance-nibenay` — **its `presence` list does not include Kharanok**, and Breck's posting means it should. Flagged previously on [[Breck]]; still unactioned.

## Dangers
- Gith in the deeper mine reaches, unchanged.
- **The madness attributed to the mine.** Oren's report is already a feature on this record: villagers emerging from the mine screaming, running for the ravine, or killing themselves against the quarry stones. **It has a cause** — see GM-Only Secrets.
- **The gate cuts both ways.** Whatever the party opened, the moonbeast reports that an energy came *through* it into these caves on day 117.

## Opportunities
- A one-step route to the Giustenal region for anyone who can pay in Crystal Dust.
- Potion production, once Tzala starts brewing.
- Nine new pairs of hands where there were none.

## Connections
- Destination: **Ruins of Giustenal** — `location-giustenal-ruins`. **Propose adding.** Two-way teleport plate in the mines, established day 117. Not a road; there is no overland route.
- Destination: Temple of the Lion — `location-lion-temple`. Retained.
- Destination: Quel Nash — `location-quel-nash`. Retained. **The sandbloom scrublands lie between Quel Nash and the Lion Temple**, at the foot of the mountains — that is where the night harvest happened.
- Destination: Quor'Anok — `location-quor-anok`. Retained. *(Local oral tradition names Kharanok the Ninth Refuge and Quor'Anok the Eighth, with rumours of underground waters connecting them — already on the record, and worth re-reading now that the tunnels beneath Kharanok are known to run a long way.)*

## Local Clocks and Pressures
- The broken lift at the South Access Shaft, unchanged.
- **Proposed:** Kalia not knowing about the gate. Three people are actively keeping it from her, in a village of a few hundred, and one of them is teaching her miners badly.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **[[The Moonbeast]] is bound in the caves beneath this settlement.** *(GM disclosure, 2026-08-09.)* Old magic holds it there; its psionic reach is not affected.
- **It explains two features already on this record**: the day-110 shared dream with "an unknown presence claiming to have been imprisoned nearby for thousands of years", and Oren's villagers running for the ravine and dying against the quarry stones. Those were never connected at the table.
- **[[Dorak]] carries its binding** — [[Lion Medallion|the Lion Medallion]] — and the creature is hunting him without knowing his name.
- **[[Breck]]'s [[Mind Seal]] makes him undetectable to it.** The party suspects Breck of holding the binding; the entity that could settle it is the one being blind to him.
- **Sadira's request for Alliance support sits unanswered in this village**, carried by Breck — a direct line from Kharanok into the House Markon campaign.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
- The village is now the junction of three separate threads — the bound entity below, the gate to Giustenal, and the Alliance's Nibenay problem — and its ruler knows about none of them.
