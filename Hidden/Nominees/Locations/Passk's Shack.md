# Location: Passk's Shack

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox.** Filed as its own record on the campaign owner's ruling of 12 August 2026: sub-places of Cromlin with named actors living in them get their own records. Proposed id `location-passks-shack`. Its inhabitant, Passk, **resolves to an existing sandbox record** — `actor-passk`, revision 1, filed as #393 and landed.)*

## Summary
A dilapidated shack at the edge of Cromlin where a ruined silt pirate drinks — and the only place in the Giustenal Environs holding both a map of Giustenal's walls and a skimmer that could carry a party there.

## Classification
- Location subtype: private dwelling
- Region: Giustenal Environs — Cromlin village, area 10
- Terrain: village building
- Parent location: `location-cromlin` — **resolves to an existing sandbox record** (revision 9)
- Status: standing, badly maintained, occupied
- Controller: Passk, its only resident
- Contested by: nobody

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
A shack that has not been repaired in a long time, at the edge of the village. Somebody is inside, and has been drinking for a while.

## Physical Features
- A run-down single dwelling.
- **A beached silt skimmer**, damaged but repairable, associated with the shack rather than the piers.

## Population and Occupants
- Passk, dwarf, alone. Formerly captain of the skimmer **Silt Sheer**; his crew is dead.

## Resources
- **A map of the walls of Giustenal.** Passk will not sell it.
- **The beached skimmer**, repairable. Also not for sale.
- Both are available on one condition only: that the party takes him with them.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-passk` — **existing sandbox record** (revision 1). Dwarf warrior, level 8, currently unarmed because **he sold his weapons to pay for drink**.
- Caller in Darkness — the thing that destroyed his crew, and the focus of his life. **Resolves to an existing sandbox record.**
- `actor-jaksot-han` and the Dirty Lizard — where he drinks. Both existing records.

## Dangers
- Passk is obsessed but not insane. He is also a level 8 warrior with no weapons and nothing left to lose.
- Approaching him as a curiosity rather than as a crew wastes the encounter.

## Opportunities
- **Convince him that the party is strong enough to survive the rigors of Giustenal and his mood turns instantly.** Only then does he hint at the map and the skimmer.
- Neither is purchasable at any price. The price is passage.

## Connections
- Destination: **Giustenal** — the destination on his map, and the reason he is still alive. `location-giustenal-ruins` exists on the record.
- Destination: **Dirty Lizard** — area 6, where he can usually be found. Proposed id `location-dirty-lizard`.
- Destination: **the silt shoals** — where the *Silt Sheer* worked before the crew died.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **What happened aboard the *Silt Sheer*:** the Caller in Darkness urged Passk's first mate to kill the rest of the crew in their sleep. Passk woke in time to save himself; the first mate went overboard trying to reach Giustenal.
- His focus is to kill the Caller. His skimmer was damaged escaping and no crew will sail with him.
- **He believes that failing his focus dooms him to become a banshee**, and he is drinking to keep from thinking about it.
- The Adventure Book details his habits and personality and gives him a role in its scenario; the campaign book directs the DM there before the party ever meets him.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
- The cheapest way into Giustenal for any party that lacks a route is a drunk dwarf who wants passage.
- If the party takes him and then abandons the focus, they have made an enemy who has already outlived one crew.
