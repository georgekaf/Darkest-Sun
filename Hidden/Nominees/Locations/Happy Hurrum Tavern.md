# Location: The Happy Hurrum Tavern

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-happy-hurrum`. Parent `location-nibenay` resolves.)*

## Summary
A tavern in Nibenay's Hill District where an elf bard has been singing since she came back from Giustenal — songs about places she has never been and things that have not happened yet, which everyone puts down to the drink.

## Classification
- Location subtype: tavern
- Region: Nibenay, Hill District
- Terrain: city district
- Parent location: `location-nibenay`
- Status: open
- Controller: **unnamed.** The book never gives the tavern an owner, and none is invented — left to the GM if it ever matters (owner's ruling, 13 August 2026).
- Also called the **Happy Hurrum Inn**, in the Monstrous Compendium's silt serpent entry, which names it as a place to sample silt serpent meat alongside the Sky Singers' trade road bazaars.
- **The name is a creature, not a person.** A *hurrum* is a Dark Sun beetle roughly an inch long, with an opalescent shell from cobalt-blue to copper-green, kept as a luxury pet by wealthy merchants. It hums by beating its wings against the underside of its carapace, and the sound calms humanoids — the beetle uses it to pacify the insects it eats. An hour in direct sunlight kills it: it beats harder to cool itself, the hum rises in pitch, and it dies of the exertion.
- Contested by: nobody

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
An ordinary Hill District tavern with one thing worth stopping for: the elf woman with the lyran, whose voice does not sound like it is entirely in the room with her.

She will never manage to perform it for a crowd. She might perform it once, for the right listeners.
