# Location: Karand-Vath

*(**New location.** Proposed id `location-karand-vath`. No record exists. Karand-Vath is named across seven sandbox files and has no location of its own — it lives as one feature line and one tag on `location-klarbu`, which carries it as: *"Beyond the gate lies **Karand-Vath**, an ancient dwarven royal necropolis and settlement."*

**Spelling.** The sandbox is unanimous: **`Karand-Vath`**, with the *n*, in `actors/gmohh.json`, `actors/mirabel.json`, `actors/jinx.json`, `actors/rava.json`, `actors/pogona-barbata.json`, `events/day-0103.json` and `world-state.json`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
An ancient dwarven royal necropolis and settlement beneath Klarbu, behind a crushing gate that only fire holds open, where a defiler's altar feeds a red crystal and the kings hang in their coffins from chains.

## Classification
- **Origin: campaign-original, introduced in play by the Dune Runners** (`party-dune-runners`) — episode 15, in-world day 103. Nothing about Karand-Vath is printed; it does not come from *Black Spine* or any other sourcebook.
- Location subtype: ancient dwarven royal necropolis and settlement
- `canonStatus`: **campaign-original**
- Region: Black Spine Region · Terrain: **underground**
- Parent location: **`location-klarbu`** — references both ways
- Status: intact and inhabited by the dead; currently sheltering Klarbu's surviving population
- Control: none. The oath, not an owner, governs it

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
Black granite corridors under the village, lit by purple crystals that shed light and do nothing else. Then a vast carved cavern — stairs, columns, and a monumental gate flanked by dwarf statues holding clay vessels. The vessels must hold fire. If they go out, the gate snaps shut on whatever is passing through.

## Physical Features

**The approach, from Klarbu** *(retained from `location-klarbu`, which keeps them)*:
- A bone-filled well leads into the former Black Widow lair and deeper passages.
- Ancient dwarven corridors of black granite, with light-shedding purple crystals similar to Crystal Dust. **The crystals have no other magical properties.**
- A vast carved cavern with stairs, columns and a **monumental crushing gate** flanked by dwarf statues holding clay fire vessels. **The vessels must hold fire or the gate snaps shut.**

**Inside Karand-Vath:**
- **The royal tombs.** Sarcophagi **hang from chains**, strapped, above a small stone bridge. A **lever mechanism one floor above** lowers them. **Servant graves** lie apart from the kings and the oath forbids disturbing them.
- **The tomb of Karad II and his wife.** Its sarcophagus is **broken from above** — someone tried to force it before.
- **The shaft is a trap, built on purpose.** Karad II's sarcophagus was hung above it by his own subjects so that **if he ever broke out he would fall deep and never climb back**. It is the containment device of this necropolis, not an architectural feature.
- **The forbidden corridor, and the queen's remains** *(day 105)*: a **pitch-black, tarry trough in which her body dissolved**, with a **bone ornament floating on the surface**. It draws people in — Jinx was nearly taken before Gmohh pulled her back.
- **The altar, in the depths where all the burrows converge** *(day 105)*: a massive red gem with **a heart beating inside it**, dried blood all around. Above it the roof opens into a **shaft over thirty metres** to the surface, and blood falls from above onto the crystal.
- **The Spider Witch's nest**: bedding, a sack of dried mushrooms, and hidden among the stones a **white mask of Nibenay's high shadow wives** and a **black metal dagger bearing the Sorcerer-King's eye on the hilt**, which has never rusted.
- **Ankhegs** in the burrows — burrowing arthropods that spit acid and seize with their jaws. Four ambushed the party in the dark; two were killed, one withdrew.
- **Spiders reach the necropolis by another route** roughly a day behind anyone who comes through the gate.

## Population and Occupants
- **The dead**, in the royal tombs, and at least one of them is no longer lying down.
- **Klarbu's survivors.** Approximately 130 people were spared on day 103; **over a hundred** were sheltering in the tunnels on day 105, most wounded and starving.
- **Gmohh**, oath-bound guardian of the royal gate, who **must remain here while he serves**.

## Resources
- Food and water: **none the place produces.** It cannot feed the mouths now in it.
- **An exception that may not be one:** Gmohh perceives that he needs neither food nor water while near the burial monuments — *and the GM stated in the same breath that this may be an illusion, and that he remains a biological organism with needs.* Recorded as perception, not as a property of the location.

## Dangers
Ankhegs in the burrows. Spiders arriving by the side route. The crushing gate, if the fires go out. And **Karad II**, standing.

## History
*(All from the former undead guardian's testimony on day 103, and recorded in `events/day-0103.json` as testimony — **not independently verified historical fact within play**.)*
- Karand-Vath was one of **five dwarven kingdoms**: **Karand-Vath, Durak-Kal, Karum-Draz, Kurad-Mor and Dras-Karan.**
- The guardian attributed the dwarves' scattering to **Borys's massacre of Kemalok**.
- He claimed **usurpers killed him and he returned the next day as undead**.
- He was bound by oath to protect the tombs of dead kings, and **passed that oath and his cursed double-headed hammer to Gmohh**.

**How the last king ended** *(episode 18, day 105 — Gmohh's vision through the hammer)*: **Karad II** was once merciful, took a **philtre of immortality from Nibenay himself** for himself and his queen, became a bloodthirsty monster, **killed his own children**, and was **imprisoned alive** by his subjects in a sarcophagus hung over the shaft-trap. That is why this necropolis is built the way it is.

## Local Clocks and Pressures
- **Gmohh's oath binds him to this place**, and to keeping the king's coffin shut. **The coffin is now open.**
- **His hundred-day transformation into a banshee** runs from episode 15.
- **The Spider Witch's altar no longer receives sacrifices**, and Klarbu's protective field died with her.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The altar is the campaign's live moral problem, not a puzzle.** Mirabel said openly that there are people who deserve to die and that the sacrifices could continue without the spider. The table did not settle it.
- **The party's own plan is to drop a king's corpse down the shaft onto the crystal** so his blood feeds it forever. That plan is what opened the coffin — and **the shaft is already the trap built to hold him**, which nobody at the table has noticed. Executing the plan and executing the original containment are the same act.
- **The Spider Witch wanted the corpse for the Nibenay magic inside it**, to use against Nibenay. She is dead and the corpse is walking.
- **The dagger and the mask place Nibenay inside this necropolis.** A high shadow wife's mask and a blade carrying the Sorcerer-King's own eye were in a spider's nest under a dwarven city, and nothing explains how.
