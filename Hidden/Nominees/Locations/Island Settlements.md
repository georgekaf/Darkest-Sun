# Location: The Island Settlements — Vakura, Pelcunal and Liuss

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-island-settlements`. Historical: these places are gone, and the record exists so the tunnel that once served them has somewhere to point.)*

## Summary
Three settlements on islands off the coast of ancient Giustenal, back when there was a coast and water to have islands in — reachable in the Green Age by a psionic tunnel that now ends in fallen stone a mile short of them.

## Classification
- Location subtype: lost settlements (three)
- Region: off the coast of ancient Giustenal, in what is now the Sea of Silt
- Terrain: islands — in a sea of water, not silt
- Parent location: —
- Status: **destroyed or lost.** Nothing confirms they still exist in any form.
- Controller: none
- Contested by: none

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
Unreachable. The only surviving approach ends in a jumble of fallen stone, and the psyche who used to make the trip knows it.

## Physical Features
- Three named settlements: **Vakura**, **Pelcunal**, **Liuss**.
- Nothing else recorded. They exist in the book as destinations of a broken transport system and in one halfling's memory.

## Population and Occupants
- None now. Green Age populations, unspecified.

## Resources
- None reachable.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-kataal-the-mover` — **existing sandbox record**, status inactive. A halfling psyche from the Green Age, still running the transport in Tunnel B. **He knows the tunnel has been destroyed well short of the islands, and is often lost in memory of them anyway.**
- `actor-jessareen` — existing record. Kataal filled her with visions of this era, and she has not recovered.

## Dangers
- None physical. The hazard is Kataal: refuse his offer of transport and he floods the refuser with visions of Athas's history, which is what happened to Jessareen.

## Opportunities
- **Proof the Sea of Silt was a sea.** A tunnel built to carry travellers to islands is not ambiguous.
- Kataal is a first-hand Green Age witness who can still be talked to.

## Connections
- Destination: **Tunnel B**, part of `location-under-region-tunnels` (existing record)
  - Travel notes: the tunnel runs almost a mile before ending in fallen stone. A few psionic orbs still work along it — light from the walls, a burst of fresh air, and a quick trip to the end for anyone Kataal takes to.

## GM-Only Secrets
- Kataal controls the transport and knows it goes nowhere. He offers the trip anyway.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
- Whether anything survives on the islands is entirely open, and the campaign already has a psionic tunnel network with a Kharanok gate room attached to it. The two systems are not currently linked on any record.
