# Location: The Dirty Lizard

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — `location-cromlin` currently carries the tavern only as a feature. Filed as its own record on the campaign owner's ruling of 12 August 2026: sub-places of Cromlin with named actors living in them get their own records. Proposed id `location-dirty-lizard`. Its owner, Jaksot Han, **resolves to an existing sandbox record** — `actor-jaksot-han`, revision 1, filed as #388 and landed.)*

## Summary
Cromlin's large tavern and inn, and the village's centre of entertainment and information — run by a one-eyed former silt pirate, famous for its fermented kank-honey-mead and for the brawls that decide whether a stranger gets told anything worth hearing.

## Classification
- Location subtype: tavern and inn
- Region: Giustenal Environs — Cromlin village, area 6
- Terrain: village building
- Parent location: `location-cromlin` — **resolves to an existing sandbox record** (revision 9)
- Status: open and operating
- Controller: Jaksot Han, owner
- Contested by: nobody — though it operates inside Hurdll Crost's village

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
A big, loud room that smells of spilled mead. Heads turn when strangers walk in, and they keep turning. Somebody says something insulting, loudly, to see what you do about it.

## Physical Features
- The largest building of its kind in the village; tavern below, inn above.
- Ceramic bottles, chairs and tables — all of which end up airborne during a brawl.

## Population and Occupants
- **From midday onward, as many as forty patrons**: skimmer crews, merchants, thieves, con artists and free citizens.
- Jaksot Han, present and running the place.

## Resources
- Trade: **thick, fermented kank-honey-mead**, the drink the house is known for.
- Information: the real commodity. What the room knows is not given to strangers who keep to themselves.
- Guards: none. The crowd polices itself, badly.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-jaksot-han` — **owner, existing sandbox record** (revision 1). Former silt pirate who took up a safer trade after **losing his right eye in a raid**.
- `actor-passk` — a regular; the shack at area 10 is his. **Existing sandbox record** (revision 1).
- House Shom — Crost's authority covers the village, but the tavern is not his.

## Dangers
- **Fights and drunken arguments break out constantly**, and the skimmer crews test newcomers on sight.
- Adventurers who walk in are gawked at, loudly insulted and pushed around.
- If a fight starts, **1d6 of the instigator's companions join in**, and there is a **75% chance the whole room goes up in a free-for-all**. Chairs fly, bottles break over heads, and sometimes a bone knife goes quietly between somebody's ribs.

## Opportunities
- **Winning a brawl without killing or seriously injuring anyone earns the room's respect** — after which the crowd asks for news of the party's travels and draws them into local talk.
- Avoiding violence altogether costs nothing but earns nothing: the crowd ignores them and gives up meaningless chatter. The good information is reserved for whoever entertains the room.

## Connections
- Destination: **Cromlin Marketplace** — nearby, same village. Part of `location-cromlin`.
- Destination: **Passk's Shack** — where its most useful regular drinks himself insensible. Proposed id `location-passks-shack`.

## GM-Only Secrets
- The brawl is the gate. A party that reads the room as hostile and withdraws never learns that the tavern is where Cromlin's real news is traded — including anything anyone knows about Giustenal.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
- The fastest legitimate route to Passk is through this room, on a night he is drinking in it.
- A party that makes a good showing here has a standing source in the Giustenal Environs that costs them nothing to maintain.
