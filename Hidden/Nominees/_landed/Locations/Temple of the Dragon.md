# Location: Temple of the Dragon

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-temple-of-the-dragon`. Parent `location-new-giustenal` resolves.)*

## Summary
The religious heart of New Giustenal — three structures of gray stone scaled in crimson, with doorways shaped as dragon's maws, where Dregoth's own High Templar and High Priest preach his godhood over altars that drip blood on holy days.

## Classification
- Location subtype: temple complex (three buildings)
- Region: New Giustenal, area 2
- Terrain: subterranean city district
- Parent location: `location-new-giustenal`
- Status: active, central to city life
- Controller: Dregoth's priesthood — [[Mon-Adderath]] and [[Absalom]]
- Contested by: nobody

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
Three buildings of gray stone, scaled in crimson. You enter through a dragon's open mouth; the portcullis above the doorway is cut to look like teeth. Inside, an altar of black obsidian bears a roaring dragon's head inside a crimson circle.

## Physical Features
- Three structures. **The largest stands nearest the Dread Palace** and is treated as the temple of the wealthy — attending sermons there is a status symbol, and lesser dray are treated badly.
- Each temple holds **an altar of blachest obsidian** etched with Dregoth's symbol. **On holy days the stone drips with steaming blood.**
- Each temple contains four barracks areas for low-level templars, plus training halls, dining rooms, sanctuaries and study areas. **Each barracks is supervised by a 4th-level templar.**

## Population and Occupants
- Templars in quantity — **338 across the corps, printed** (Campaign Book, Chapter Six, "Dregoth's Templars"), against a dray population of only 3,000. Every other dray of fighting age belongs to a militia that musters on Dregoth's word, leaving only the old and the very young behind. Escalation is printed too: **four 7th-level templars under a Templar of the Eighth Scale (10th level) in 2d10 rounds** plus travel, if the templars can call for help.
- Wealthy second-generation dray, at the largest temple.
- Mon-Adderath and Absalom deliver sermons here.

## Resources
- Trade: none. This is an institution, not a market.
- Other: the templar corps' housing and training capacity.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-mon-adderath` — existing record. High Templar.
- `actor-high-priest-absalom` — existing record. High Priest, and secretly the owner of [[Akrag's Pools]].
- `actor-dregoth` — existing record. The object of worship.

## Dangers
- Sacrifice is practised doctrine, not rumour. Animals and demihumans have both been used.
- A non-dray discovered inside is a candidate for the altar.

## Opportunities
- **The Coruscation.** Every dray knows the prophecy: Dregoth the Godking will lead them to the surface, fall on Raam, and kill everyone in it as sacrifice — at which point the crimson sun bursts with light and a new Blue Age begins. Any party that hears a sermon learns the plan.
