# Location: The Dread Palace

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-dread-palace`. Parent `location-new-giustenal` resolves.)*

## Summary
Dregoth's palace in the northern section of New Giustenal — black obsidian faced with the bones of great wyrms, holding his planar gate, his treasures, and the relic he intends to become a god with.

## Classification
- Location subtype: palace / seat of power
- Region: New Giustenal, northern section, area 9
- Terrain: subterranean city district
- Parent location: `location-new-giustenal`
- Status: occupied and in use
- Controller: [[Dregoth]], the Dread King
- Contested by: nobody. This is the safest place in the setting for its owner.

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
Nothing else in the city is this stark. The walls are the blackest obsidian, and the bones of enormous wyrms are set into their surface. It is oppressive on purpose.

## Physical Features
- Walls of blachest obsidian, decorated with great wyrm bone; great black spines, scaled facing and a tall spire, and a maw-like door.
- **The planar gate** — kept in this area, along with many of Dregoth's other treasures.
- **Every room is built to accommodate Dregoth's great size.**

### Interior — the nine rooms *(Adventure Book, Part Seven B, p. 53; map p. 60)*
1. **Entrance Hall** — gray marble floor inlaid with a turquoise dragon; a polished marble throne on a stepped platform. **Four templars of the Fifth Scale (7th level) are always stationed here.**
2. **Banquet Hall** — where the templars dine with their leader, [[Mon-Adderath]].
3. **Templar Meeting Room** — rows of marble benches facing a slate wall for writing in chalk.
4. **Servants' Quarters** — the dray who serve the palace.
5. **Templars' Quarters** — the templars assigned directly to the palace.
6. **Hall of the Dragon King** — Dregoth's private room, filled with paintings and statues of himself. The crowning item is **the great black altar with the dragon skull on it — his holy relic** (`artifact-dregoths-dragon-skull-font`).
7. **Dregoth's Library** — arcane and religious works from every plane he has visited across the centuries. **1d4 random magical items** can be found among them.
8. **Transformation Room** — the equipment for turning humans into dray. Anyone subjected to it saves versus spells or the process begins; **10% of humans and 25% of half-elves become first-generation dray**, then must make a system shock check or die. **There is no way to reverse it.**
9. **Chamber of the Gate** — where the planar gate is stored. See Card #1 and Part Seven C.

## Population and Occupants
- Dregoth — **25% chance he is in any given room** when the party is there.
- Templars roam the palace, plus **six elite kalin riders**.
- **Two abishai** guard the gate chamber, if they survived Part Six D.
- Household and servant dray, per rooms 4 and 5.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The font of power.** The last secret of Dregoth's godhood is a relic already resting deep within the palace: a huge dragon skull taken from one of the creatures he killed.
- Dregoth found the planar gate — a Green Age psionic focus for planar travel — before his transformation, and has used it since to walk many planes. What he learned there is why he believes he can become a real god rather than a pretender.
