# Location: Bodach

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-bodach`.)*

**Ruled 13 August 2026 by the campaign owner: file it as a canonical location.** This is the Bodach of wider Dark Sun canon, not a settlement that happens to share the name, and it is a real destination rather than an off-screen reason the Twilightcatchers are unavailable. Where *City by the Silt Sea* leaves the site blank — which is nearly everywhere — the gaps are filled from the wider setting rather than invented here. The book's one characterisation is kept as the campaign-facing contrast: **"by no means safe"**, but **"guarded by horrors that can be seen and fought with honest steel"**, unlike Giustenal and its Caller in Darkness.

## Summary
A ruined city the Twilightcatcher clan turned to after Giustenal killed one of their own — not because it is safe, but because everything in it can be seen and hit.
