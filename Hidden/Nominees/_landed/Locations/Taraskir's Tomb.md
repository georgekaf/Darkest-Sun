# Location: Taraskir's Tomb

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-taraskirs-tomb`. Parent `location-sunken-city` resolves; `location-lions-den` beneath it also resolves, as do `actor-taraskir` and both of his artifacts.)*

## Summary
A lion-headed temple in the Sunken City built as a prison rather than an honour — Dregoth sealed his rival inside it, chained the coffin, and wrote on the doors that Taraskir rests here in eternal punishment.

## Classification
- Location subtype: tomb / sealed temple
- Region: The Sunken City, area 6
- Terrain: sealed under Abalach-Re's solidified sand dome
- Parent location: `location-sunken-city`
- Status: sealed; guarded
- Controller: the dead
- Contested by: nobody who has come back

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
A larger copy of the temple Tenpug's Band lives in, reaching almost to the sand dome sixty feet overhead. The lion-headed statues that flanked the double doors are gone, though the sockets where they stood are obvious. The doors are sealed and covered in writing.

## Physical Features
- The inscription, readable with any method for ancient text: **"Here lies Taraskir the Lion. He dared challenge Dregoth, the Ravager of Giants, and so rests here in eternal punishment."**
- A huge stone coffin, **bound with enchanted chains — 50 hit points of damage from a +1 or better weapon to break.**
- **A secret door in the north wall**, once used by Taraskir's surviving followers to reach the tomb, leading down to the Lion's Den.

## Population and Occupants
- **Three undead honour guards** — a dwarf and two elves. Metal helms with lion motifs; they fight with metal claw-gauntlets, 1d6 plus Strength bonus. The lion-claw proficiency grants **+1 initiative**.
- Taraskir's skeleton, in the coffin.

## Resources
- **Cursed armor** on the body: +2 AC, but grants opponents **+2 to attack rolls**, and the curse persists after removal until the armor is returned to Taraskir's body.
- **A metal two-handed sword: +2 dragon slayer**, not cursed. Wieldable only by characters **over six feet tall with Strength 17+**.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-taraskir` — existing record, status **dead**. The occupant.
- `actor-dregoth` — existing record. The jailer, and the author of the inscription.
- `artifact-taraskirs-dragon-slayer` and `artifact-taraskirs-lion-armor` — **both already exist in the sandbox.** This nominee is where they physically are.

## Dangers
- The three guards.
- The armor, which punishes whoever takes it and follows them until it goes back.

## Opportunities
- A +2 dragon slayer, in a setting with a Dragon and several dragon kings, sitting in a chained box.
- The secret door is a two-way route to the Lion's Den.

## Connections
- Parent: `location-sunken-city`.
- Destination: **The Lion's Den** — `location-lions-den`, existing record; through the secret door in the floor / north wall.
- Comparison, not a route: the temple west of Cromlin that Tenpug's Band occupies (`location-tenpugs-temple`) is the smaller model of this building.

## GM-Only Secrets
- Taraskir's followers built a way in after the sealing. Somebody wanted access to a tomb meant to be permanent, and the graffito in Dregoth's palace — *"The Lion-King Lives!"* — says they were not quiet about their opinion of it.

## Unresolved Questions

*(All three answered 13 August 2026 — the first two from the printed text, the third by the campaign owner.)*

- ~~Whether the chains are Dregoth's work or the sorcerer-kings'~~ — **Dregoth's.** He sealed the coffin because rumours ran that Taraskir would return and he would not risk the Lion King's followers stealing the body. Enchanted chains: **50 hit points of damage from a +1 weapon or better** to break.
- ~~Whether the honour guards were volunteers~~ — **no.** Dregoth had Taraskir's top lieutenants — a dwarf and two elves — **entombed alive**, and they became undead bent on protecting the leader they loved. 1,400 XP each; **+1 weapon or better to hit**.
- ~~Whether the campaign wants the dragon slayer reachable at this stage~~ — **reachable, as printed.** The obstacles are the price: sealed doors covered in strange writing, the three undead guards, the chains. The **armour is cursed** for anyone who takes it, until it is returned to the body; the **+2 dragon slayer** beside it is not, and needs a wielder **over six feet tall with Strength 17 or better**.
