# Location: Upside-Down Forest

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-upside-down-forest`. Parent `location-new-giustenal` resolves.)*

## Summary
Trees growing downward out of the cavern ceiling of New Giustenal, their limbs spreading into a canopy overhead and their roots reaching up toward a sun none of them can reach — and they still fruit.

## Classification
- Location subtype: natural feature / orchard
- Region: New Giustenal, area 10
- Terrain: subterranean cavern ceiling
- Parent location: `location-new-giustenal`
- Status: living and productive
- Controller: the city; worked as a food source
- Contested by: nobody

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
Look up and the forest is above you the wrong way round. The trunks come out of the ceiling, the branches hang down and open into a canopy, and the roots climb away into the rock toward a sky that has not been there for centuries.

## Physical Features
- Trees rooted in the cavern ceiling, limbs reaching down and out into a canopy of leaves.
- Pathways of different-coloured living stone winding among them *(vault page)*.

## Population and Occupants
- No settled population. Worked by common dray from the [[Common Section]].

## Resources
- Food: **sweet fruit**, a staple of the dray diet — one of the few things down here that is neither fish nor fungus.
- Water: not recorded.

## Important Actors and Factions
- None named. This is infrastructure, not a stage.

## Dangers
- None printed.

## Opportunities
- The strongest single piece of evidence a party can *see* that this cavern predates Dregoth — a Blue Age remnant still growing.

## Connections
- Parent: `location-new-giustenal`.
- Destination: **Common Section** — a feature of the parent record, not a separate location. The labourers come from there.

## GM-Only Secrets
- The vault page calls it an ancient remnant of the Blue Age. The campaign book's area entry does not say so; **that attribution needs a source before it enters a record.**

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
- If the campaign wants a reason New Giustenal can feed itself indefinitely, this and the fungi fields are it.

- ~~Whether the roots reaching upward are literal, and whether anything has ever followed them~~ — **literal and purposeful**: "The roots wind their way upward, **seeking the sunlight far above**", from a grove that grows downward out of a cavern ceiling below New Giustenal. **Nothing has followed them yet** (owner's ruling) — the way up stays open as a possibility, and no creature has used it.
