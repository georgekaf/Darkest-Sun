# Location: The Shifting Slope

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-shifting-slope`. **Ghodan's Gap is folded into this record** as its only crossing rather than filed separately.)*

## Summary
A flake drift over a mile long and more than fifteen feet high, five miles south-west of Giustenal, that cannot be climbed and punishes anyone who tries — with exactly one gap through it, wide enough for a wagon.

## Classification
- Location subtype: terrain obstacle
- Region: Giustenal Environs, south-west
- Terrain: flake drift
- Parent location: — (regional feature)
- Status: permanent
- Controller: nobody
- Contested by: nobody. It is a wall, and it is treated as one.

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
A long ridge of loose volcanic flakes, higher than two men, running further than you want to walk. It looks climbable. It is not.

## Physical Features
- **Over a mile long, more than 15 feet high.**
- **Ghodan's Gap** — at least one opening, named for the dwarf brothers who use it often, **just wide enough to let a wagon pass**.

## Population and Occupants
- None. Traffic only: the Ghodan brothers' wagons between their tar mine and Cromlin.

## Resources
- None. Its whole value is the gap.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-durex-ghodan`, `actor-haltham-ghodan` — proposed ids, both filed as issues. The gap carries their name because they use it.
- `location-tar-mine` — **existing sandbox record**; the wagons that use the gap run to and from it.

## Dangers
- **Flake drifts cannot be climbed without magical aid.** Anyone attempting a high slope sinks into a rough pit made by their own weight.
- Damage while caught: **Armor Class minus 1d2 points per round**, to a minimum of 1 hit point per round, for as long as they remain in the drift.
- The gap is the only crossing, which makes it the obvious place to wait for a wagon.

## Opportunities
- A chokepoint with a name, a known user and a predictable schedule — everything an ambush or an interception needs, on either side of the law.

## Connections
- Destination: **Ghodan's Tar Mine**
  - Existing location ID: `location-tar-mine`
  - Travel notes: through Ghodan's Gap. The brothers use it regularly.
- Destination: **Cromlin**
  - Existing location ID: `location-cromlin`
  - Travel notes: the far end of the same wagon run.
