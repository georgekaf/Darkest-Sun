# Location: College of Blackspire

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-college-of-blackspire`. Parent `location-new-giustenal` resolves and already lists "a college" among its features; this record makes that feature addressable.)*

## Summary
The wizard school of New Giustenal — ivory sandstone capped in black obsidian — where Dregoth, alone among sorcerer-kings, deliberately trains mages, and where every kalin squadron gets its defiler.

## Classification
- Location subtype: academy / arcane institution
- Region: New Giustenal, area 13
- Terrain: subterranean city district
- Parent location: `location-new-giustenal`
- Status: operating
- Controller: Dregoth, personally invested
- Contested by: nobody

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
A beautiful building, which is not a thing said about much down here: ivory sandstone rising in three scaled spires, each capped in the blackest obsidian.

## Physical Features
- Ivory sandstone construction, capped in blachest obsidian, in three scaled spires.

## Population and Occupants
- Dray students, brainwashed to loyalty.
- Graduating defilers, of whom **one is assigned to every kalin squadron** — 7th to 10th level (1d6+6), each carrying five randomly assigned magical items.

## Resources
- Other: **trained arcane casters at scale**, which no other city-state produces on purpose.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-dregoth` — existing record. He personally encourages mage development, unlike every other sorcerer-king.

## Dangers
- Its graduates. Four squadrons of 25 kalin riders exist, each with a college defiler attached.

## Opportunities
- The single clearest evidence that Dregoth is preparing an army rather than defending a city.

## Connections
- Parent: `location-new-giustenal`.
- Destination: **Dread Palace** — proposed `location-dread-palace`.
- Destination: **Temple of the Dragon** — proposed `location-temple-of-the-dragon`.

## GM-Only Secrets
- Students undergo **several years of near-brainwashing** to ensure they are dedicated to Dregoth, while instructors **psionically probe their minds for signs of treachery**. Betraying the College means expulsion that is **"quick and bloody"** *(printed — Campaign Book pp. 85–86, verified 13 August 2026)*.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
- The college is the supply line for the invasion force. If the campaign ever wants to slow Dregoth down without fighting him, this is the building to attack.
