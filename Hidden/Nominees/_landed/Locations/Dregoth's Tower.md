# Location: Dregoth's Tower (The Black Tower)

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-dregoths-tower`. Reachable only through [[Dregoth's Palace]], proposed `location-dregoths-palace`, which must be filed first.)*

## Summary
The Black Tower, rising out of the palace and hanging over the Sea of Silt, with a bleached skeleton spiked to its face by the sorcerer-kings as a statement — and a failed experiment still alive in a cell at its base.

## Classification
- Location subtype: ruined tower, three floors
- Region: Giustenal ruins — above Dregoth's Palace
- Terrain: obsidian tower overhanging the silt
- Parent location: proposed `location-dregoths-palace`. **The tower is its own record** (owner's ruling, 13 August 2026), not a sub-location of the palace — a separate structure counts as a place you travel to, unlike rooms inside one building. Access still runs only through the palace's room 12 staircase, and **both records carry the link**.
- Status: ruined, structurally precarious
- Controller: nobody
- Contested by: —

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
Blackest obsidian, pitted and rough where the silt-laden wind has been at it for centuries, cracked and holed. Staked to the centre of the tower by iron spikes is a single bleached skeleton in a tattered faded-red tunic, flapping.

## Physical Features
- **Exterior:** the staked skeleton — Dregoth's High Priest, hung there by the sorcerer-kings as a sign that they, not some upstart dragon king, were the most powerful beings on Athas. **Nobody alive knows who he was.** Pulling him down collapses him into loose bones, and reaching him needs levitation.
- **3rd floor — Dregoth's Chamber:** small glass windows shattered, furniture collapsed, books rotted. A balcony with a clear view over the Silt Sea.
- **2nd floor:** nearly barren, large windows, a turquoise dragon inlaid in the floor (10 gp loose, 40 gp reconstructed). **The staircase is damaged: 25% chance of falling.**
- **1st floor — Laboratory:** looted and burned. Glass and metal machines, broken vials, ash. **Three stone-barred holding cells.**

## Population and Occupants
- **A venger in one of the cells** — a failed transformation experiment, still there.

## Resources
- The turquoise inlay. Nothing else of value survived the looting and the fire.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-dregoth` — existing record. His laboratory; his experiments.
- The unnamed High Priest — **gets his own actor record as an unnamed historical figure** (owner's ruling, 13 August 2026). No name is invented; the record carries the office, the execution by the sorcerer-kings, and the corpse still spiked to this tower. He held the post [[Absalom]] holds now.

## Dangers
- The venger, at the bottom of the tower, behind stone bars of unstated condition.
- The damaged staircase — 25% per use.
- The tower overhangs the Silt Sea and is visibly failing.

## Opportunities
- The laboratory is where the dray were made. Anything the campaign wants to say about how is said here.

## Connections

## GM-Only Secrets
- The skeleton is a message from the other sorcerer-kings, still nailed up centuries later. Dregoth has never taken it down. He has also never come back for it.
