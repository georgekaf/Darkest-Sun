# Location: Sharg Island

*(New location. **No record exists in mk-sandbox** — verified 2026-08-12 against `locations/` on the default branch. Proposed id `location-sharg-island`. Parent `location-new-giustenal` resolves.)*

## Summary
An abandoned estate on an island in New Giustenal, empty because the thing living in the cave beneath it eats anyone who stays — and the templars have standing orders to leave it exactly as it is.

## Classification
- Location subtype: abandoned estate
- Region: New Giustenal, area 4
- Terrain: island in the Blackjaw River
- Parent location: `location-new-giustenal`
- Status: **abandoned, and deliberately not cleared**
- Controller: nominally Dregoth; in practice the sharg
- Contested by: nothing that has survived trying

## Player-Safe Arrival Description
A lonely estate on an island, in good enough condition that its emptiness is the strange part. Nobody goes there. Nobody will say much about why.

## Physical Features
- The estate, abandoned.
- **A cave beneath the island** — the sharg's lair.

## Population and Occupants
- One sharg. Unique on Athas.

## Resources
- Whatever the estate still holds, unlooted precisely because of the tenant. Not itemised in the book.

## Important Actors and Factions
- `actor-dregoth` — existing record. **He ordered the templars to leave the creature alone.**
- The templar corps, who know it is there and obey.

## Dangers
- **The sharg attacks anyone who explores the island.** The book is explicit: it will slip out of its lair and try to catch them.
- Statistics: AC 4, MV 3 / Sw 9, HD 11 (64 hp), THAC0 9, 5 attacks, damage 1d8×4 / 5d20, constriction, size H (40 feet), morale steady (12), XP 3,000. A cross between a giant shark and a squid, dull black, four tentacles ridged like teeth.

## Opportunities
- The one monster in the city that Dregoth has publicly declined to kill — which tells a party more about him than any sermon.

## Connections
- Parent: `location-new-giustenal`.
- Destination: **Blackjaw River** — a feature of the parent record. The sharg hunts the barges from here.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Dregoth brought the sharg to Athas himself**, from another plane, and released it. He wants to study it until he can control it. Most of the city believes it swam in from wherever the Blackjaw comes from.
- The fishermen are organising their own answer to it because the templars will not act. They do not know the templars have been ordered not to.

## Proposed Developments
All unapproved.
- The gap between what the fishermen believe and what Dregoth ordered is a ready-made wedge between the city and its god.
