## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Proposed. The source gives none.)* Remembered rather than seen — an older woman, spare, with the ledger-keeper's habit of counting things while talking about something else. What the band recalls most is that she was never still.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)* Practical to the point of brusqueness, and quietly generous underneath it. The one story anyone tells about Alshal is the one where she picks up a half-dead stranger in the wastes because it did not occur to her to do otherwise, and then spends two years making her competent.

### Public Reputation
A name attached to an office. The band's older members speak of her; the younger ones know the title and not the woman.

## Confirmed Facts
- Alshal was Tenpug's **Keeper of Supplies** before her successor.
- She found a young woman near death in the wastes two days out from Tyr — a household slave who had fled her templar owner's house during a raid and who knew nothing about surviving in the desert.
- **She took pity on the city girl and brought her back to the slave tribe.**
- She then taught her successor how to provide work materials, food, and clothing for two hundred people in the wastes of Athas — a training that took roughly two years.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Alshal is the cleanest fix available for the Danya problem.** The campaign's Day 104 correction moved Danya to Kharanok as an Order psionicist, which vacates the Keeper of Supplies post and orphans the rescue story attached to it. Three options, in descending order of tidiness:
  1. **Alshal never had a successor, or outlived hers, and still holds the office.** The post is filled, the band keeps a quartermaster, and Danya's Black Spine background simply detaches. Cheapest fix; requires only that Alshal be alive.
  2. **Alshal rescued and trained someone else**, and Danya's version of the story was always a different woman's. Requires naming a replacement.
  3. **Alshal is dead and the post is genuinely vacant**, which is dramatically useful during a siege and creates a real logistics crisis for Adventures Two and Three.
- **She is also the cleanest fix for [[Arcus]].** The printed text has Danya nursing the lost half-giant back to health. Alshal — whose one recorded act is rescuing a dying stranger — is a far more natural fit, and reassigning it costs nothing.
- *(Proposed.)* If option 3 is taken, the manner of her death is unwritten and available. A Keeper of Supplies dies on a supply run.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: option 1.** Bring Alshal forward as the band's living Keeper of Supplies. It repairs the Danya hole, gives the band back a needed office, and requires inventing almost nothing — the source already establishes her competence, her tenure, and her character.
- If she lives, she is the natural person to argue the flight question from the only angle nobody in either tent covers: whether two hundred people can actually be *provisioned* on the move. Jolon has the money side; nobody has the water side.
- She and [[Jolon]] between them are the band's entire logistics capability, and Adventure Two opens with the band trying to field an army with insufficient food, water, and pack animals.
