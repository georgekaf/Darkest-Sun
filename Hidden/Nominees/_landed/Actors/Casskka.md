# Actor: Casskka

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-casskka`. **The instigator of the only civilian crime plot in New Giustenal. The shared record for the Pit and munge lives on [[Freiha]].**)*

A greedy dray whose tavern has been dying ever since the competition invented a drink she cannot reproduce, and whose solution was to hire a rogue to steal the recipe or murder the couple who own it — and who is now impatient about the delay.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the plot, and she is beatable without a fight.** She has no muscle of her own, no standing, no protection, and a failing business. **Exposing her ends the Pit plot completely** — and in a city where the party's stated goal is *"to keep themselves from being noticed by Dregoth,"* solving a tavern murder quietly is exactly the right scale of heroism.
- **Her brother is the seam.** *"Some of the fishermen (location 5)"* are among the NPCs the module names as **potential friends of the PCs**. **Casskka's brother is a fisherman.** A party that befriends the fishermen has a direct line to the sister who is arranging a double murder — **and the brother is the one person with a reason to talk her out of it and a reason not to talk about it.** The module does not connect these two facts. **It should.**
- **What is she paying with?** She is broke. **A rogue of Lodgden's calibre — level 8, with a wild talent — does not work a serving job for coin from a failing tavern.** Either the payment is not money, or Lodgden has her own reason to be there. **Both readings are good.** See [[Lodgden]].
- **The impatience is the crack.** *"The impatient Casskka is urging her to finish her business quickly."* Pressure makes clients careless: **she will meet her agent somewhere, send a message, or say something in her own taproom.** That is how the party finds out.
- **The two descriptions of munge do not match** — *"a strong elixir"* in one entry, *"a sweet tasting drink"* in the other. **A GM should pick one.** If it is both — strong *and* sweet — that is actually the best explanation for why it destroyed her trade.
- *(Proposed.)* She does not want anyone killed. She wants the recipe. **"Or send them to the Gray" may be Lodgden's interpretation of an instruction Casskka gave in temper.** If so, the party can end this by telling her what her own contract is about to become.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: run the Beetle's Bite as the cheap tavern the party can actually afford**, and let them like it before they find out what its owner is doing.
- **Connect her to the fishermen through her brother.** It turns two isolated printed details into one investigation.
- Decide what she is paying Lodgden with, and let the party work it out.
- Give her the chance to call it off. She is a small, frightened business owner, not a crime lord — **and a party that talks her down has made a friend in New Giustenal instead of a corpse.**
- Settle whether munge is strong, sweet, or both.
