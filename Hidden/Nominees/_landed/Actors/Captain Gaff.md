## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the party's transport, and the module prices the job at five silver a day.** Chapter Two ends and Part Two of the adventure begins with **silt skimmer travel** — Gaff is how the party gets onto the Silt Sea, and hiring on as fighting crew is the printed route in.
- **The Sky Singer sword is an unexplained gift and an unclaimed hook.** An elf tribe gave a House Shom captain an enchanted weapon. **The module never says why.** That is a favour, a debt, or a history, and it connects Cromlin directly to [[Muuton]]'s tribe.
- **The shoals route is deliberately unmapped and that makes navigators valuable.** *None of them ever make a map, for to do so would endanger their livelihood.* **A party that obtains or makes such a map has something several factions would pay or kill for.**
- **He is only 5th level.** Gaff commands respect, not power. **If the pirate campaign goes badly, he is not the one who survives it** — and his twelve crew are 3rd-level. **The refit is Crost's plan, and Gaff and his men are the ones who will pay for it.**
- *(Proposed.)* He did not ask for the ballista ports.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** hire the party as fighting crew at five silver a day and let the pirate ambush in Part Two happen with Gaff on the deck beside them.
- The Sky Singer gift is a free scene: ask him about the sword and let him have an answer.
- If the campaign wants a sympathetic casualty to make the pirates real, Gaff and his twelve are already established as people the party has met.
- The unmapped shoals route is a genuinely valuable secret sitting in a friendly NPC's head.
