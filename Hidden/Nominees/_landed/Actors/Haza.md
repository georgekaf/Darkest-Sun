# Actor: Haza

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-haza`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The "super-lord gith warrior" the party heard about three adventures early — a gith who has wandered Athas collecting human weapons, human armour and human experience, and has come back to spearhead the Reclamation.

- Role: Gith champion; senior commander of Zigath's Nest

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A **tall, muscular** gith — which is already wrong, because gith are hunched and wiry. He wears **plate mail** and carries a steel impaler and a wrist razor, all human-made, all far better than anything his troops have.

The module's own phrasing: *his sophisticated equipment is a stark contrast to his bestial visage.* He looks like a gith wearing a soldier, and that is the intended effect.

He has taken the raised ledge in the barracks for his personal quarters and erected tents there for himself, Toogo and the sergeants.

### Manner and Voice
*(Manner proposed; the interrogation behaviour is printed.)* Controlled and unhurried, in a species that is neither. Haza has spent years among humans and has learned to imitate the parts that work — patience, discipline, a commander's stillness — without acquiring any of the softness underneath.

**If captured, he firmly resists any interrogation.** He does not bluster, bargain or babble. That distinction matters, because his companion [[Toogo]] does nothing but.

### Public Reputation
Among the gith of the nest: the arriving hero, the one who has seen the world above and come back for them. He has upstaged Zigath simply by existing, and both of them know it.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Male gith, 12th-level fighter**, chaotic evil — the highest-level individual gith in the entire campaign.
- Tall and muscular, and uses **human-made weapons and armour**: plate mail, a steel impaler +3, a wrist razor, and three steel-tipped javelins +2.
- Carries a *fruit of speed* and a *fruit of fire resistance*.
- Psionic: 90 PSPs, telepathy and psychokinesis — project force, telekinesis, animate object, animate shadow, awe, ballistic attack, contact, control body, control flames, id insinuation, mind thrust.
- **More than almost any other gith, Haza has wandered Athas in search of experience and power.**
- He now hopes to prove himself **so that he can spearhead the Reclamation**.
- He and [[Zigath]] are the brains behind the gith forces at the nest, and are in charge of 62 gith warriors plus two sergeants. [[Toogo]] is a powerful warrior but not a tactician.
- The three of them rotate shifts so that at least one is always on duty in the Great Hall and only one is asleep at any time.
- **He has prepared contingency plans for attacks from the surface** — the module explicitly attributes the nest's defensive doctrine to his experience.
- If the nest is on alert, Haza keeps more gith on duty: one extra sergeant and four extra warriors.
- Gith who displease Haza are sent to perimeter duty, where the survival rate in an attack is close to zero.
- In the defensive plan, **Haza and ten warriors wait on the Stand of the Mighty** — a raised platform in the middle of the Great Hall — crouching out of sight behind its stone railing.
- If captured, he **knows about the Square of Gurdek**, the earth clerics in Yathazor helping the gith.
- He also believes the gith have established contact with **"long lost kin"** who will help them conquer Athas. **These kin are githyanki, and Haza does not know this.**
- He is the "super-lord gith warrior… coming to join the reclamation" that [[Durdon]] overheard a gith guard boasting about in Adventure Three.

## Traits and Pressures
- Ambition: **Very high, and the defining trait.** Everything he has done was to qualify for this.
- Caution: High (4) — he plans, rotates guards, and fights from cover.
- Secrecy: 5 under interrogation.
- Loyalty: To the gith cause and to his own place at the head of it.
- Cruelty: High. Perimeter duty is a punishment posting with a zero survival rate and he uses it as one.
- Risk tolerance: Moderate. He waits on the Stand of the Mighty rather than charging.
- Safety: Excellent — the best-armoured individual in the campaign so far, in a prepared position, with sixty warriors.
- Wealth: Human plate mail and three enchanted steel weapons, which on Athas is a fortune.
- Status: The nest's arriving champion, resented by the woman who actually runs it.

## Resources and Capabilities
- **The best personal combat capability in the first three adventures.** Plate mail, three attacks a round, an enchanted impaler, and psionics on top.
- Command of 62 warriors and two sergeants, jointly with Zigath.
- Genuine tactical doctrine: delays, massed assaults, forward guards, cover, anti-invisibility sweeps, psionic distraction. The module lists these as *his* contribution.
- Two consumable *fruits* — speed and fire resistance — which he will use.
- Experience of the surface world that no other gith in the nest possesses.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the payoff for a line delivered three adventures earlier.** Durdon quotes a gith guard boasting about a super-lord warrior coming to join the Reclamation. If the party rescued Durdon and remembered the line, Haza's introduction should explicitly land as *that's him*. If they let Durdon die, it lands as nothing.
- **He does not know the mining camp has fallen.** The module says so outright: the gith are preparing to send reinforcements to a garrison that is no more, and the reinforcements are too late. Haza is planning an operation into a hole in the ground. A party that realises this has a timing advantage.
- **The Haza–Zigath resentment is the encounter's exploitable seam.** Two commanders who dislike each other, on rotating shifts, with a stone seal that only one of them can pass. Zigath's own mistrust of her fellow gith is why nobody will climb her stairs to help her — printed. A party that attacks during the right shift is fighting one commander, not three.
- **He believes he is dealing with kin. He is dealing with a race that finds him disgusting.** The githyanki regard gith as a repulsive offshoot and intend to exterminate them once they are no longer useful. Haza's entire ambition is built on a relationship that does not exist in the direction he thinks it does. If the party ever gets to tell him, that is a scene.
- *(Proposed.)* His human gear is not loot. He acquired it deliberately, over years, because he concluded gith equipment was the reason gith lose. He is the only gith in the campaign to have had that thought.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** let the party take him alive and never crack him. His silence is characterful and it protects the reveal; use [[Toogo]]'s babbling as the comic counterweight in the next cell.
- Play the shift table honestly. A party that scouts and waits gets a materially easier fight, and that should be visibly rewarded.
- If he survives Adventure Three, he is the natural recurring antagonist for Adventures Four and Five — a gith commander who has now seen the surface *and* been beaten by it.
- The "long lost kin" misapprehension is worth preserving all the way to Adventure Six, so that the githyanki's contempt for the gith lands on a character the party has a history with.

  - *Steel wrist razor* +6, 1d6+4 (off-hand)
  - *Steel-tipped javelin +2* +9, 1d6+5 (thrown, near/far)
- **Springing Attack:** may leap 20 feet as part of a move and attack with advantage on landing. **Only usable in chambers** — the module notes low ceilings restrict gith springing to open rooms, so this works in the Great Hall and not in the corridors.
- **Psionics (90 PSP equivalent):** project force, telekinesis, mind thrust, id insinuation, awe, animate shadow, control flames, control body.
- **Defence Modes:** mental barrier, tower of iron will.
- **Fruit of speed:** once, doubles his attacks for a short time. He will use it when the fight turns.
- **Fruit of fire resistance:** once. Note that he is standing next to a fire cleric who casts *wall of fire* and *flame strike* into her own hall — this is not a coincidence and should not be played as one.
- **Prepared Ground:** while fighting from the Stand of the Mighty, he and his ten warriors have cover and cannot be seen from the hall entrance until they rise.
- **Running him at the table:** he is a commander, not a berserker. He holds position behind the railing, commits when the party is engaged and surrounded, and uses the fruits without hesitation. Under interrogation he says nothing at all.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Male Gith Fighter 12, Str 18/51, hp 80, AC 0, THAC0 9, 3 attacks (impaler ×2 & wrist razor), steel impaler +3, wrist razor, javelins +2 (×3), PSPs 90. Scaled to Shadowdark level 9 — deliberately the highest-level antagonist the party has faced to this point.
