# Actor: Gort

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-gort`. **Dead before the adventure begins. Known only through the testimony of two ghosts.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The human gladiator who started the rebellion — who tried to drive armed gith off the edge of a four-hundred-foot chasm with his bare hands, took two of them with him, and got some of the slaves across.

- Subtype: named-npc

- Faction or allegiance: none; a captive of the gith
- Current location: his remains are among the twenty skeletons on the promenade floor, Cave of the Winds
- Current route, when traveling: —

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
One of some twenty skeletal remains lying in crumpled heaps near the centre of the cavern floor, **stripped clean by giant ants.** Nothing distinguishes his bones from the rest.

### Manner and Voice
None surviving. Everything known about Gort comes secondhand, from [[Pimns]] and [[Solaq]], and only if the party can speak with the dead.

### Public Reputation
Nothing. He died in a cavern under a mountain, in a rebellion nobody above ground knows happened.

## Confirmed Facts
- **A human gladiator named Gort started the rebellion.**
- **He tried to drive the dwarves' captors off the edge of the chasm.**
- **Two guards died along with Gort and many slaves.**
- The rebellion was **unsuccessful**; the slaves **paid dearly for their insolence.**
- Roughly **twenty captured slaves** died here. The flesh was stripped from their bodies by giant ants.
- The captors were **powerful gith with magical weapons.**
- **The survivors crossed the chasm.** Pimns and Solaq do not know how — **they were dead at the time.**
- All of the above is available **only** through magical means of speaking with the banshees or with the skeletal remains.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the campaign's only successful slave rebellion, and it is reported in one sentence by a ghost.** Some of the captives got across the chasm because a man with no weapon decided to use the geography. The module spends a paragraph on it and moves on.
- **He is the argument for the revolt at the mine.** Adventure Five's mining facility holds around a hundred slaves and the module explicitly invites the party to lead a rising if Zuzar's guards get diverted. Gort is the precedent: it has been tried here before, unarmed, and it partly worked. **A party that learns his story at the chasm and then reaches the pens has been handed a thematic argument for the exact tactic the adventure wants them to use.**
- **The survivors are unaccounted for.** *"The survivors crossed the chasm, but Pimns and Solaq do not know how."* Some number of Nibenese slaves got over a hundred-foot gap with the bridge sections drawn back to the far side, and the module never says who they were or where they went. **They are somewhere in the mine, or they are dead, or they are still down there.** This is a completely open hook.
- *(Proposed.)* If [[Tormar]] is to be found alive anywhere, "he was one of the ones who got across" is the most satisfying answer available, and it costs nothing.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** let his bones be identifiable and let him be nameable. A party that asks *which one was Gort* and gets an answer has a body to stand over, and that is worth more than another combat.
- **Consider a separate speak-with-dead result for Gort himself.** The banshees can only report what they saw before they died. Gort died *after* them — he was still fighting when they went down. He is the only source who could say how the crossing was made.
- Tie the mine revolt back to him explicitly. One line from a PC at the slave pens does the whole job.
- The unaccounted-for survivors are a free, fully-supported hook for the mine, the pens, or the cold smelter.
