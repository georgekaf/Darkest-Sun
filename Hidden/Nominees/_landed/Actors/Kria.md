# Actor: Kria

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-kria`. **A half-elf potter having a bad month, who will sell the party a worthless-looking trinket for eight silver. It is the key to New Giustenal.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A half-elf potter of [[Tenpug]]'s Band whose sales have collapsed and whose best friend walked into Giustenal a month ago and never came back — and who, out of pure desperation, is about to sell the party the holy symbol he left with her for safekeeping.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Not printed.)* A half-elf with pottery to sell.

### Manner and Voice
**Desperate, and audibly so.** The module directs it precisely: she calls the PCs over, tries to interest them in her pottery, and **if they are totally uninterested, desperately calls them back.**

> *"I see that you are shrewd traders, my friends,"* Kria says quickly, **with a hint of desperation in her voice.** *"No simple pottery for you. You seek something special."*

Then she checks both ways to be sure they are not being watched, pulls something from her pocket, and says:

> *"Adventurers such as yourselves need the protection of the Dragon."*

### Public Reputation
A member of Tenpug's Band who makes pots. **Nobody's idea of a problem.**

## Confirmed Facts
- **The encounter starts when Kria the half-elf calls to the PCs.** The DM places it wherever suits.
- **Kria tries her best to interest the PCs in her pottery. She is at her wit's end, however, as sales have not been good lately and her best friend [[Nallan]] has failed to return from a trip into the wilderness.**
- **If they are totally uninterested in buying any pottery, Kria desperately calls them back**, and offers **"something special."**
- **She looks both ways, making sure they are not being watched.** Then she produces the amulet: **"Adventurers such as yourselves need the protection of the Dragon."**
- **The amulet does not radiate magic, though it will prove useful should the PCs try to visit New Giustenal later in the adventure.**
- **She asks for two pieces of gold for the "ancient artifact", claiming that its magic will protect the wearer from all dangers — but she will settle for 8 sp after a few rounds of haggling.**
- **This amulet is one of the holy symbols of [[Dregoth]], as worn by the templars of Giustenal at the time of the city's destruction.**
- **[[Nallan]] found it on his last trip to the ruins of the dead city. Before he left again, he gave it to Kria for safekeeping.** **Since he has been gone for almost a month, and because Kria needs to make a sale desperately, she has decided to offer Nallan's amulet.**
- **Kria already tried to sell the amulet to a disguised templar from Nibenay, but the templar didn't have the necessary silver on hand to complete the deal.** **During the haggling session with the PCs, the templar could return to demand the amulet if you want to spice things up.**
- **Kria needs to raise funds, though most of her desperation has to do with worry over Nallan.**
- **It doesn't take long for her to show off the metal amulet.** **If the PCs have befriended Tenpug's Band before, or if they seem particularly sympathetic, she will tell them about poor Nallan.**
- **Nallan is her missing friend** — and he is dead in the ancient temple, a suicide at the [[The Caller in Darkness|Caller]]'s behest.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is handing the party the key to Chapter Five for eight silver pieces and neither of them knows it.** **Primik the Mover will let travellers descend to the lowest tunnels if they carry one of Dregoth's holy symbols — even one from an earlier age, such as those found in the upper ruins.** **This is exactly that.** Without it, the party descends *"the hard way"* and **Primik alerts the guards below that unwelcome visitors are coming.**
- **The module tells the DM it *"does not radiate magic"* for a reason.** A party that tests it will conclude it is junk and may not buy it, or may sell it. **Let them. Then let them stand in front of Primik.**
- **The disguised Nibenay templar is a loaded gun the module hands the DM and shrugs about.** *"During the haggling session with the PCs, the templar could return to demand the amulet if you want to spice things up."* **A templar of Nibenay, in disguise, in the Giustenal environs, who specifically wants a holy symbol of Dregoth.** Nothing else in the campaign explains why. **That is a whole plot thread lying in the open.**
- **She is the emotional entry point to the Caller.** [[Nallan]] is a corpse with an empty sheath; **Kria is why the party goes looking for him.** *"Most of her desperation has to do with worry over Nallan."* **Give her the news, or let the party bring back the body.**
- **The reward for kindness is printed:** she tells them about Nallan *"if the PCs have befriended Tenpug's Band before, or if they seem particularly sympathetic."* **Being decent to a struggling potter unlocks the ruins plot. Haggling her down to 8 sp and walking off does not.**
- **She is selling a friend's property while he is missing, and she knows it.** That is a small, real, human shame, and it should show. **She will not say the amulet was his unless she trusts them.**
- *(Proposed.)* If the party returns Nallan's body — or just the truth — **she has nothing to give them but the pottery and the amulet, and she will try to give them both.**

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: run the haggle exactly as printed**, including the opening price of 2 gp and the 8 sp floor, and **do not hint that the amulet matters.**
- **Bring the disguised templar back mid-scene at least once.** It costs nothing, it raises the stakes on a pottery sale, and it plants a question the campaign never answers.
- Let sympathy, not silver, unlock Nallan. That is the printed design and it is a good one.
- **Decide what the party owes her afterwards.** They will be carrying her missing friend's amulet into the place that killed him.
- Give the pottery a description. She is a craftsperson and nobody has said what she makes.
