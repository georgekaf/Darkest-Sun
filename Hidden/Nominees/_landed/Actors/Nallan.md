# Actor: Nallan

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-nallan`. **Dead before the campaign starts. He is a corpse, a mystery, and the delivery mechanism for a sorcerer-queen's signet ring.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
An artist of [[Tenpug]]'s Band who went quietly into Giustenal, came back rich, went again, and was found in the ancient temple with his own bone dagger in his heart — the [[The Caller in Darkness|Caller]]'s most traceable victim, carrying loot he never knew was a sorcerer-king's.

## Player-Safe Description

**A body in the ancient temple.** **He is unarmed, but a bone dagger juts from his heart. A dagger sheath on his belt is empty.**

### Manner and Voice
*(Nothing is printed. He is an artist who twice slipped away from his own band without telling anyone where he was going.)*

### Public Reputation
Within the band, **a missing man.** Tenpug believes he **fell to some nameless horror in an attempt to find additional profit in the ruins** — which is close enough to true. Among the wider world, nothing.

## Confirmed Facts
- **An artist named Nallan** of Tenpug's Band **once disappeared for several days, and when he returned he sold several objects to traders from House Shom.**
- **One of the objects was an amulet that bore the design of a dragon and was made from reddish-orange stone.**
- **Nallan disappeared again some weeks later, and has not been heard from in several months.**
- **Tenpug has seen the dragon design on stone guardians in Giustenal, and he believes Nallan fell to some nameless horror in an attempt to find additional profit in the ruins.**
- **Tenpug suspects that at least one of the band's number visited Giustenal and coaxed a bauble or two from its shattered depths.** He is right, and it was Nallan.
- **His body is in the ancient temple (area 3) of the Giustenal ruins.** **He killed himself at the behest of the [[The Caller in Darkness|Caller in Darkness]].**
- **The crockery he sold to House Shom came from the Banquet Room** of the palace — *"This is where Nallan of Tenpug's Band retrieved most of the crockery that he sold to House Shom, though many pieces remain buried beneath the drifting silt."*
- **He missed things.** In the **Dining Hall**, *"Nallan missed a few of the items buried beneath the silt in here, so there is a 5% chance per turn spent searching that a character can uncover a plate or piece of metal cutlery worth 2d6 sp."*
- **He is [[Kria]]'s missing friend.**
- **In his backpack is an item he picked up in the palace before the Caller drove him to kill himself: a ring bearing the royal signet of Raam.**
- **The ring belongs to Abalach-Re.** **It took many bribes and threats before [[Curlonus Skaff]] was able to secure it**, and she **left it in the palace for [[Dregoth]]** — who **wants a personal item of the sorcerer-queen to use as a focus for spells he is developing, as part of the Dread King's plans to gain revenge.**
- **Nallan found it before Dregoth was able to retrieve it.**
- **The ring is worth 400 gold pieces** — *"if the bearer can locate a buyer willing to risk Raam's wrath."*
- The loot he sold fetched a stated price band, **doubled if the seller can prove the items come from Giustenal.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the proof.** Everything about the [[The Caller in Darkness|Caller]] is rumour and terror until the party finds a body with an **empty sheath on the belt and the owner's own dagger in the heart.** That is the moment the ghost story becomes a mechanism. **Show them the sheath. Let them work it out.**
- **He is also the recruiting poster for going in anyway.** He walked into Giustenal alone and came back with saleable goods; the price band **doubles if the seller can prove the items came from Giustenal.** Tenpug's whole band knows someone got rich in there once. **That is why they keep looking at the ruins.**
- **The ring is a delayed-action bomb.** A sorcerer-queen's personal signet, extorted by a pirate chief on the orders of an undead dragon king who wants it as a **spell focus for revenge**, found by accident on a corpse by a party who will assume it is treasure. **Dregoth still wants it. Abalach-Re does not know it is gone. It is worth 400 gp to anyone brave enough to sell it.** Every one of those three facts can hurt the party separately.
- **Selling it is the trap.** *"If the bearer can locate a buyer willing to risk Raam's wrath."* Any fence who recognises it has a reason to talk to templars.
- **Kria gives the body a name and a reason.** Recovering Nallan for her is a small, clean, human motive to enter the worst place in the region — and it puts the party in the ancient temple, where the Caller is strongest.
- *(Proposed.)* **He was an artist.** Give him a sketchbook. Drawings of the stone guardians, the dragon motifs, the palace rooms — and, in the last pages, the same face over and over, drawn worse each time. It costs nothing and it tells the party exactly what killed him.
- *(Proposed.)* He may not have been alone the second time. Tenpug only counts the ones who came back.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: run the body as a scene, not a loot drop.** The empty sheath, the temple, the silence. No roll needed to understand it.
- **Give him a sketchbook** and let the artist's own hand document his decline. Best available way to teach the Caller's method without a lecture.
- Have Kria ask the party to find him. It is the cheapest possible hook into Chapter Four.
- **Decide in advance what happens when the party tries to sell the ring** — and who notices.
- Consider whether the dragon-design amulet he sold to House Shom is still in Cromlin, and what it actually is. The module drops it and never returns.

Everything numeric above is constructed to the "artist, not fighter" framing.
