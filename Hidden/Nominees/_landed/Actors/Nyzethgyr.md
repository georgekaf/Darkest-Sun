# Actor: Nyzethgyr

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-nyzethgyr`. **A name and nothing else. The module uses it once, in reported speech, and never returns to it.**)*

## ⚠ What this file is

Nyzethgyr appears **exactly once** in the whole of Black Spine, inside a single line of [[Quixoth]]'s testimony:

> *"I overheard the gith commander talking to one of the guards, regarding the preparation for someone he called 'queen or nest-mother.' I remember the commander said that all was going well, but someone or something named 'Nyzethgyr' was still displeased with the progress of the mine."*

That is the entire printed record. **No stats, no species, no gender, no location, no appearance, no second mention, and no resolution anywhere in the remaining three adventures.** The module drops a name and forgets it.

This file exists to stop that name being lost, and to lay out what it could be. **Everything below the Confirmed Facts section is proposal.**

## One-Sentence Summary
A name spoken once by a frightened gith commander — someone or something with the standing to be displeased with an entire mining operation, and to have that displeasure reported upward as a threat.

- The testimony comes from **Quixoth**, who overheard it during captivity using a spell to comprehend the gith language. He is certain of the words and does not understand them.

## GM-Only Secrets

**Candidate identities**, in descending order of how well they fit the one line of evidence:

1. **[[Raskon]], under a githyanki name.** He is the general responsible for the Nightmare Gate, the iron is for the Gate, and two githyanki commanders inspected Zuzar's mine and threatened her life over exactly this. The cleanest fit, and it costs nothing — *Raskon* is what the gith call him; *Nyzethgyr* is what he is. **Recommended.**
2. **One of the two visiting githyanki commanders** who toured the mine and sentenced Zuzar. The module never names them. This is the same fit as option 1 with an extra body.
3. **A separate githyanki overseer of the Black Spine operation**, senior to Raskon and never encountered. Makes the campaign bigger and leaves a thread hanging past Adventure Seven.
4. **[[Queen Trinth]] herself**, under her true or formal name — **weakened** by the fact that the speaker distinguishes Nyzethgyr from the "queen or nest-mother" in the same sentence.
5. **Not a person.** *"Someone or something named Nyzethgyr."* Quixoth's own phrasing leaves it open. A construct, a gate-spirit, an artifact — **[[Yrinolir]]**, Trinth's sentient scepter, is the campaign's one attested non-person with opinions and influence. This is the strangest option and the most interesting.

**Why it matters:** the name arrives in Adventure Five, three adventures before the campaign's climax, from a reliable witness, attached to authority over the whole operation. If it is left unresolved the players will remember it, and the campaign will end without answering. **Pick one before running Adventure Six.**

*(Proposed.)* Option 5, if adopted, retroactively makes Yrinolir the mover behind the entire Black Spine operation rather than Trinth's tool — which is consistent with the module's own hedge about the scepter: *"Whether this was her doing, the item's doing, or a combination of the two is impossible to say."*

## Proposed Developments

**Ruled 13 August 2026 by the campaign owner: leave it ambiguous.** No candidate is adopted. The record stands as the attested line plus an unadopted list of possibilities, and "someone or something named Nyzethgyr" stays exactly as printed. The name remains available to attach to whatever the campaign later needs — including nothing.

- ~~Recommended: adopt option 1.~~ Give Raskon a githyanki name and let Nyzethgyr be it. **Not adopted** — retained only as one of the open possibilities.
- If the campaign prefers a longer thread, option 3 leaves a named authority alive and unaccounted for after the Gate is destroyed.
- Whatever is chosen, **have the name spoken a second time**, by a gith under interrogation or by a githyanki in Adventure Six. One repetition converts a forgotten line into a payoff.
- If option 5 is taken, Yrinolir should be given the name in Adventure Seven and nothing else — no explanation, just the recognition.
