# Actor: Olton

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-olton`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
An 8th-level gladiator whose mind Slate destroyed by initiating her into something she lacked the wisdom to survive — she kept every one of her fighting skills and none of herself, and now follows him around like a dog.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: **dependent** — she does what Slate is near
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: attached to the Square of Gurdek; **not a functioning member**
- Current location: `location-yathazor` — the Refuge, wherever Slate is
- Current route, when traveling: follows Slate
- Role: Slate's bodyguard, by her own choice, for reasons she can no longer articulate

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
**Her face is a study in opposites.** A network of scars, a nose repeatedly broken and left deformed, and cauliflower ears speak of a violent life — **but her guileless mind gives her an expression of innocence.**

**In battle her expression changes to that of a hurt and angry child.**

Her brown, streaked hair grows long, and **she sometimes spends hours just playing with it.**

Hide armour and a shield +1, a steel morning star, a long bow, and fifteen steel-tipped sheaf arrows.

### Manner and Voice
**Olton has nothing to say about Slate's plans**, or about anything else. The module gives her no dialogue and there is no indication she has any.

She is not aggressive, not frightened, and not present in any way a conversation could reach. She stands near Slate. When something threatens him, she stops being calm.

### Public Reputation
Within the Square: Slate's failure, kept close and not discussed. To anyone else: an enormous scarred woman who does not speak and does not leave his side.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Human female gladiator, 8th level**, chaotic neutral. **Strength 20, Constitution 18 — and Intelligence 5, Wisdom 4.**
- **She is one of Slate's failures.** Lacking the Wisdom necessary to become a dual-class earth cleric, **she was driven crazy by the failed initiation.**
- **Though she has been reduced to idiocy, she retains her fighting skills.**
- **She now follows Slate around like a loyal dog, and protects him from harm.**
- Equipment: hide armour, shield +1, steel morning star, long bow, fifteen steel-tipped sheaf arrows. AC 3, from hide, shield, Dexterity **and Armor Optimization** — she still has her gladiator training.
- She cannot *ignore earth*. **Ari Evan had to *teleport other* her into Yathazor**, along with Morlah.
- Under the Square's recruitment mechanic, she must have had Wisdom below 17 — Gurdek rejected her. **The rejection is what broke her.**
- She is, mechanically, **the second-highest-level fighter in the Refuge** and the highest-Strength individual in the whole of Adventure Four.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the evidence.** Slate's recruitment method — charm the wise, initiate them, and let Gurdek sort them — has a failure rate, and Olton is what failure looks like walking around. The Square does not hide her. They have simply stopped seeing her. A party that works out what she is has the moral case against Slate without needing anything else.
- **Killing her is the module's quiet moral trap.** She is a bodyguard with 76 hit points and Strength 20 who will absolutely attack the party, and she is also a victim standing in the room where she was destroyed. There is no clean answer and the GM should not supply one.
- **She is why attacking Slate directly is expensive.** Any plan that targets him has to get through the highest-Strength combatant in the adventure first, and she does not check morale, because morale requires an assessment she cannot make.
- *(Proposed.)* Something in her still tracks Slate specifically — not the Square, not the Refuge, him. Whatever Gurdek left, it left that.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** if Slate uses *ignore stone* to escape at half hit points, Olton cannot follow. She cannot pass through stone. Play that: the bodyguard left standing in a room she cannot leave, still fighting for a man who has already gone. That single beat is worth the whole character.
- If the party subdues rather than kills her, they have a problem with no good solution, which is the correct outcome.
- Pair her with [[Morlah]] deliberately — the two failures, one silent and one singing — as the Square's actual argument against itself.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Gladiator
- Level: 7 *(Shadowdark scaling of 2e 8)*
- Armor Class: 16 (hide, shield +1, Dexterity, Armor Optimization)
- Hit Points: 62
- Movement: near
- Strength **+4**, Dexterity +1, Constitution +3, Intelligence −3, Wisdom −4, Charisma −2
- Alignment: Chaotic (neutral)
- Morale: **—. She does not check morale.** She has no capacity to evaluate a situation and withdraw from it.
- Attacks: 2 attacks per round.
  - *Steel morning star* +8, 2d4+4
  - *Long bow, steel-tipped sheaf arrows* +6, 1d8+4 (far range; carries fifteen)
- **Armor Optimization:** −1 AC, included above. Retained gladiator training.
- **Guard:** while Slate is within near range, any attack targeting him may be redirected to Olton as her reaction. She always uses this.
- **Cannot Ignore Earth:** she cannot pass through stone, and cannot follow Slate when he escapes.
- **Cannot Be Reasoned With:** immune to persuasion, intimidation, negotiation and parley. Also cannot be usefully interrogated.
- **Resistant to mind reading:** as all who were touched by Gurdek — Intelligence check to read her; a natural 20 dazes the reader 1d6 rounds. **What is in there to read is a GM decision and should probably be awful.**
- **Running her at the table:** silent, huge, and playing with her hair until the moment Slate is threatened. Do not give her lines. Do not make her sympathetic on purpose — just play her exactly as written and let the players arrive at it.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Female Human Gladiator 8, Str 20, Con 18, Int 5, Wis 4, hp 76, AC 3, THAC0 13, steel morning star 2d4+10. Scaled to Shadowdark level 7.
