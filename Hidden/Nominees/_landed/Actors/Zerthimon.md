# Actor: Zerthimon

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-zerthimon`. **Historical/legendary — dead. Paired with [[Gith the Deliverer|Gith]].** Central figure of githzerai religion, and a live political problem for the githzerai ruler.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The man who stood up after the revolt and told Gith she was unfit to lead, died in the battle that followed, freed half a species by dying — and is now expected back, which the current githzerai god-king finds extremely inconvenient.

- Faction or allegiance: the githzerai — their central revered figure
- Current location: —
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Founder-figure of the githzerai; object of worship

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Unrecorded.

### Manner and Voice
One attested act, and it is a speech: **when the original race broke free of the mind flayers, it was Zerthimon who opposed Gith, claiming that she was hateful and unfit to lead the people.**

That is the whole of his recorded character — a man who, having just been freed from centuries of slavery, immediately said the unwelcome thing to the person who freed him.

### Public Reputation
**In githzerai lore there is a central figure that is revered above all others — Zerthimon.** He is believed to be **a great god that was once a man.**

## Confirmed Facts
- **In githzerai lore there is a central figure revered above all others — Zerthimon**, believed to be **a great god that was once a man.**
- **According to githzerai lore, when the original race broke free of the mind flayers, it was Zerthimon that opposed [[Gith the Deliverer|Gith]], claiming that she was hateful and unfit to lead the people.**
- **There ensued a great battle and the people were polarised by the two powers. Those that chose to support Gith became the githyanki. Those that supported Zerthimon became the githzerai.**
- **Zerthimon died in the battle, but in his sacrifice he freed the githzerai from Gith.**
- **The githzerai believe that someday Zerthimon, in his new godly form, will return and take them to a place on another plane.**
- **Zerths are special among the githzerai, acting as focal points for the attention of Zerthimon.** The githzerai believe **that when Zerthimon returns for them, he will first gather all of the zerths and lead them to their new paradise.**
- **It might be said that the zerths are the centre of githzerai religion. Unfortunately, they are not free from religious persecution.**
- **The wizard-king who rules the highly superstitious githzerai — whose name is not known — would like very much to stamp out the legend of Zerthimon.**
- **The wizard-king believes that this legend challenges his authority, and very likely it does.** However, **he has never been able to rid the githzerai of this legend and he is now forced to tolerate it.**
- Separately: the githzerai **fell sway to the teachings of a powerful wizard who proclaimed himself king — and later god — of the people.** The githzerai **worship a powerful and ancient wizard as though he were a god (he is not)**, and are **destroyed before they have enough power to become a threat to their ruler.**
- Githzerai never progress beyond 9th level, and rarely above 7th — **except thieves, who are an exception to the maximum level and often progress up to 12th. Just what role these thieves play is unknown.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The githzerai are ruled by a false god who is losing a millennia-long argument with a dead man.** The wizard-king proclaimed himself god of the people, **is not one**, kills any githzerai who grows powerful enough to threaten him, and **cannot suppress the legend of a man who died before he took power.** That is a live political situation attached to the party's only ally in Book Three, and the module states it plainly and does nothing with it.
- **[[Krika]] is a zealot of a persecuted faith and it explains her.** Her long tirades about cruel injustices with no specifics; her absolute, non-negotiable positions on the dead and on illithids; her willingness to abandon a mission over the desecration of githzerai bodies. **She is not merely a soldier — she belongs to a religion her own ruler is trying to erase.** Whether she is a zerth is unstated and would be a very cheap, very good addition.
- **The thief anomaly is the module's own dangling thread.** Githzerai cap at 9th level — **except thieves, who reach 12th, and "just what role these thieves play is unknown."** The source raises this and abandons it. Given a god-king killing anyone who becomes a threat, an exempt caste of high-level rogues is not a loose end so much as an answer waiting for a question.
- **Zerthimon's return is the githzerai's exit strategy** — he will gather the zerths and lead them *to a place on another plane.* The githzerai are not trying to win their war. They are waiting to leave. **That reframes Krika's entire mission**, and her insistence on a strike force to take Galnaki, as something more desperate than it appears.
- *(Proposed.)* Every account of the schism the party can obtain comes from one side or the other. Zerthimon's version survives because his followers survived. **Whether Gith was hateful is unknowable and both species have built themselves on the answer.**

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** make Krika a zerth. It costs one sentence, explains her fanaticism, her rank, and her captain's choice of her for the mission, and gives her a reason to be persecuted at home as well as hunted abroad.
- Let a captured githyanki and Krika each tell the schism story. Two irreconcilable accounts of the same event, from two people in the same room, is better than either.
- The wizard-king is a fully-described, unnamed, unrecorded ruler of an entire species. **If the campaign wants a githzerai faction record, he is its head and he needs a name.**
- The high-level thieves are an unexplained exception in a society that kills the powerful. That is a conspiracy the module has already written and not noticed.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
**None. Zerthimon is dead before the campaign's history begins and does not appear.**

If the campaign ever stages his return — which githzerai religion actively expects — he should be handled as a divine event rather than a stat block.

Related mechanics that **do** apply, from the githzerai racial entry:
- **Githzerai never progress beyond 9th level**, and rarely above 7th, **because the wizard-king destroys them before they become a threat.**
- **Exception: thieves**, who often reach 12th. **Role unknown.**
- **Githzerai fighters of at least 5th level carry silver swords** — two-handed swords +3 with a 5% chance per hit of cutting an opponent's silver cord on the Astral Plane. **These are of powerful religious value and will never willingly be allowed to fall into outsiders' hands.**
- **50% magic resistance**, and **plane shift at will.**
- **Rrakkma bands:** 30–60 githzerai warriors formed to hunt mind flayers, roaming the planes for roughly three months. **Serving in one is considered an honour.**
