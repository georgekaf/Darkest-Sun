# Actor: Moraz the Defiler

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-moraz-the-defiler`. **Thin source — a stat block, a room, and one printed behaviour. That one behaviour is the whole character.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The Dust Devils' house wizard, who rides the lead skimmer into every raid and keeps one fifth-level spell permanently unspent so that he can *teleport* home the moment the raid stops going well.

- Subtype: named-npc

- Current location: `location-dust-devil-island` — **Room A** of the ancient ruin, shared with Skaff and the two skimmer captains
- Current route, when traveling: aboard one of the two raiding skimmers, in the shoals between Cromlin and Break Shore
- Role: Combat defiler; the tribe's only arcane caster

### Public Reputation
To the merchant crews of the shoals, the reason a Dust Devil raid opens with spells rather than arrows.

## Confirmed Facts
- **One of the two raiding skimmers carries a defiler.** That defiler is Moraz.
- **The pirates' tactics are simple: they rush toward their target, firing ballista bolts, arrows, and spells to incapacitate as many of the opposing crew as possible.** Then they board, and **seek to kill the crew so they can plunder the craft and its cargo at their leisure.**
- **Moraz uses his spells to best advantage. He saves one 5th-level spell so he can use *teleport* to return to the pirate camp if the raid doesn't work out.**
- **Room A of the ancient ruin serves as the living area for Moraz the defiler and the two silt skimmer captains.** Hidden there: **1d4 gold, 5d6 silver, 1d10 × 10 ceramic pieces**, plus **his store of spell components and his spellbook.**
- **His spellbook is printed in full** — see Rules Notes. It runs to fifth level and includes ***teleport***, ***cloudkill***, ***lightning bolt***, ***transmute rock to mud*** and ***sending***.
- **If the PCs kill all of the pirates on one attacking skimmer, the other retreats into the swirling dust.**
- **If the PCs chase the pirates back to the island, the PCs can gain the magical items used by Skaff and Moraz.**
- **Alignment: Lawful Evil.** **Morale 15.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The reserved *teleport* is the encounter.** The module tells the DM outright that he leaves when it goes badly. **That means the party will almost certainly not kill him in the raid** — they will meet him again, on his island, with his spellbook and his components to hand and Skaff's earth elemental beside him. **Run him as a recurring problem, not a speed bump.**
- **It also means he can be followed.** A party that anticipates the *teleport* — by scrying, by a tracking effect, by grappling him as he casts — gets handed the pirate island's location without needing Gaff's pursuit at all.
- **The spellbook is the real treasure and it is worth more than everything else on the island combined.** Five levels of arcane magic including *teleport*, *cloudkill*, *lightning bolt*, *dispel magic*, *fire shield* and *transmute rock to mud*. In a setting where wizards are hunted and books are irreplaceable, **this is a campaign-altering item.** It is hidden in Room A.
- **A defiler and an earth cleric sharing quarters is a contradiction the module never notices.** Every spell Moraz casts strips the life from the ground. **Skaff serves elemental earth.** They live in the same room. **Either she has made peace with it, or she does not know how his magic works, or he has been lying to her about it for months.** All three are good, and one of them is a lever to split the pirate leadership without a fight.
- ***Transmute rock to mud*, on an island in the Silt Sea.** He has, in his book, a spell that turns the ground the camp stands on into slurry. A GM should know he has it before the party invades.
- *(Proposed.)* He is not a believer. Skaff has visions and a holy mission; Moraz has a wage and an escape route. **He is the one member of the Dust Devils' leadership who can be bought.**

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: let him escape the first encounter as written**, and make sure the party notices he chose to. It sets up the island.
- Give the party a chance to work out that he *teleports* to a fixed point, and reward them with the island's location for it.
- **Use the defiler/earth-cleric contradiction.** Telling Skaff exactly what her wizard's magic does to the element she serves is a genuine, non-combat attack on the Dust Devils.
- Decide where the spellbook ends up. It should not be a casual pickup.
- If he is bought rather than killed, he is an arcane contact in a region that has none.
