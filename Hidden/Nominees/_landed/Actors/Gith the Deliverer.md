# Actor: Gith the Deliverer

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-gith-the-deliverer`. **Historical/legendary — millennia dead or departed. Paired with [[Zerthimon]].**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The woman who broke a species out of illithid slavery, gave the githyanki their name, and was immediately accused by her own lieutenant of being hateful and unfit to lead — splitting the people she had just freed.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Unrecorded. No Black Spine text describes her.

### Manner and Voice
Unrecorded, beyond her one attested act: **she convinced the people to rise up against their cruel masters.**

The only characterisation of her anywhere in the source is her opponent's: **Zerthimon claimed that she was hateful and unfit to lead the people.** That is a githzerai account, and the module presents it as githzerai lore rather than fact.

### Public Reputation
**Among the githyanki: absolute.** Their name means *sons of Gith* in their own tongue.

**Among the githzerai: the reason for the schism.**

## Confirmed Facts
- Sages believe the githyanki **once were humans, captured by mind flayers to serve as slaves and cattle.**
- **The mind flayers treated their human slaves cruelly, and the people harboured a deep resentment toward the illithids.** For centuries they increased their hatred but could not summon the strength to break free, **waiting many years, developing their power in secret**, for an opportunity to strike.
- **"Finally, a woman of power came forth among them, a deliverer by the name of Gith. She convinced the people to rise up against their cruel masters."**
- **"The struggle was long and vicious, but eventually the people freed themselves. They had earned their freedom and become the githyanki (which, in their tongue, means sons of Gith)."**
- **The githzerai were originally offspring of that same race of humans, freed from slavery under the mind flayers by a great female warrior named Gith.**
- **These men and women did not, however, choose to follow Gith's ways after they revolted against their slavers.** Instead **they fell sway to the teachings of a powerful wizard who proclaimed himself king — and later god — of the people.**
- According to githzerai lore: **when the original race broke free of the mind flayers, it was [[Zerthimon]] who opposed Gith, claiming that she was hateful and unfit to lead the people.**
- **There ensued a great battle and the people were polarised by the two powers. Those that chose to support Gith became the githyanki. Those that supported Zerthimon became the githzerai.**
- **Zerthimon died in the battle**, and in his sacrifice **freed the githzerai from Gith.**
- **The split of the githyanki and the githzerai is the closest thing the gith races have known to civil war.** Since then, **githyanki never battle githyanki. It is the unwritten rule of gith society and is never broken** — which the module suggests **may be an effect of the race's enslavement.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is why the campaign's three gith races exist**, and the party can learn the whole story from the Monstrous Compendium entries reprinted at the back of Book Three — which is to say, **from a captured githzerai or githyanki who will tell it very differently depending on which one they ask.**
- **Every surviving account of Gith is partisan.** The githyanki entry calls her *a deliverer*. The githzerai entry reports that Zerthimon called her *hateful and unfit*. **There is no neutral source.** A campaign that lets the party hear both versions — from [[Krika]] and from [[Trasrani]], for instance — is handing them a genuine historical argument with two living partisans.
- **The unwritten rule is hers.** *Githyanki never battle githyanki.* That is the scar left by her civil war, and it is the single most important fact about the finale: **[[Queen Trinth]] intends to attack the lich-queen, and her own followers would find the idea obscene.** Gith's schism is the reason Trinth has to keep her real objective secret from her own army.
- **Athas's gith are her descendants and would not recognise the name.** The Reclamation — the gith belief that they once ruled Athas and are merely taking back what is theirs — is a garbled folk memory of githyanki occupation. **[[Haza]] believes he is contacting "long lost kin."** He is right, and they intend to exterminate him.
- *(Proposed.)* If the campaign ever wants a reason the gith races are the way they are, everything traces to one revolt that succeeded and one succession that did not.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** never resolve which account is true. Let Krika and a captured githyanki each tell it, and let the players notice the discrepancy.
- The Gith/lich-queen identity question is a large, cheap piece of campaign lore. Deciding it changes what the lich-queen is; leaving it open is also a choice.
- If the party ever tells [[Haza]] what the githyanki actually think of the gith, Gith's name is the shortest route to making it land.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
**None. Gith does not appear in Black Spine in any form** — she exists only in the historical passages of the githyanki and githzerai Monstrous Compendium entries reprinted in Book Three.

If the campaign ever needs her, she should be built well above any githyanki the party has met, and should not be statted at all unless she is going to be fought.
