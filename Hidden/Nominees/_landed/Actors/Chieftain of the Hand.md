# Actor: Chieftain of the Hand

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-hand-chieftain`. **Unnamed in the printed source.** Proposed personal names, if wanted: **Khagra**, **Ruskt**, **Ghelt**. Id is role-based so a naming ruling costs nothing.)*

The gith chieftain who is planning to betray his allies in the middle of the battle and run off with their share of the loot, and who does not know that they are watching him plan it.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A gith chieftain in a tent flying an elaborate banner emblazoned with a **three-fingered hand**. The same device stands on a battle standard planted in the sand outside, with roughly seventy gith sleeping around it.

He and his advisers keep their croaking voices low so that others will not hear — the whispering is audible through the thick hide of the tent even when the words are not.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)* Conspiratorial by default. The Hand's chieftain does not plan aloud so much as negotiate constantly with the two advisers he keeps close, and he is at his most animated when describing what he intends to take rather than what he intends to kill.

### Public Reputation
Within the war band: the weakest of the three tribes and the most resented, because the Hand's warriors squabble with everyone. Their unit is the least well armoured of the three.

## Confirmed Facts
- Leads the Tribe of the Hand — roughly 70 gith, the smallest of the three tribes in the encampment.
- His banner and battle standard show a three-fingered hand.
- Inside his tent he confers with **two advisers**, whispering, keeping their voices down so others do not hear.
- **He is planning to betray the Tribe of the Eye during the final stages of the attack on Tenpug's Band, and carry away most of the Eye's share of the loot.**
- If the PCs get close enough and understand the gith language, they can listen in on this conversation.
- Gith from the Hand squabble with arriving units from rival tribes as they pass through the camp.
- In the battle plan, **the Hand comes over the sand dune first and engages whatever is in its path** — the Rune commits last, from the high ground.
- In Adventure Two, the surviving members of the Hand — with their leader, if he is still alive — are the rear guard left hidden in the abandoned camp to ambush Tenpug's people when they enter it.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **This is the module's best-designed intelligence prize and it is trivially missable.** A party that gets close to the Hand's tent and can understand gith hears a chieftain planning to betray a co-belligerent mid-battle. A party that gets close to the Eye's tent sees that chieftain watching the same conversation through a scrying stone. Either half is useful. Both halves together hand the PCs the war band's fault line, ready-made.
- **The frame-up is the intended use.** The module's "Sorties Against the Gith" section explicitly invites the PCs to attack one tribe and blame another. Given what the Hand is already planning, the Hand is the correct tribe to frame, and it will be believed. The cost is about two dozen gith dead before the Rune shuts it down.
- **He is the most likely of the three chieftains to survive Adventure One and the least likely to matter afterwards** — he ends up commanding a rear-guard ambush in an abandoned camp, which is what betrayal-minded subordinates get assigned.
- *(Proposed.)* His plan is not greed so much as arithmetic. The Hand is going in first and will be gutted. He is trying to make the losses worth something, and everything else about him follows from that.

## Proposed Developments
- Stage the two tents as a pair on the same scouting night. It costs one extra paragraph and turns a monster camp into a political situation.
- If the PCs frame the Hand, let the Eye act on it immediately and violently — the Eye already believes it.
- If he survives to the Adventure Two ambush, the party meeting him again in the abandoned camp is a free callback.
