## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
**A strange-looking gith wearing gaudy, ornate clothing.** He stands surrounded by fifteen gith guards, and **rolls a long staff back and forth between his hands. The staff is adorned with a human skull.**

Around him: thirty human slaves gouging the walls with picks or marring the stone with hammers, twenty gith working the walls to the left, and a dozen more slaves crawling on the floor collecting the fallen rock.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the hardest single fight in the mine and the module presents him as scenery.** Fifteen guards in attendance, twenty more gith at the face, thirty-five warriors in the sub-encounter, and an officer with psionics — against a party that has already fought through the camp above. **B4 is a wall, not an encounter, and should be signposted as one.**
- **He is also the reason a frontal assault on the mine fails.** The module's printed doctrine is that guards "fall back," travelling deeper to ally with other armed guards, and send a runner down each path to alert the Overlord. Every one of those routes converges on Keehya's position. **The mine is designed to funnel the party into the strongest concentration in the adventure.**
- **The counter is the slave revolt, and Keehya is holding the largest reservoir of it** — around seventy people at his face alone, plus thirty in the quarry and thirty-eight in the pens. If Zuzar diverts guards to hunt the PCs, this is where the arithmetic changes.
- **The skull is human.** The mine has been consuming Nibenese miners for weeks and the overseer is wearing one of them as a decoration. Nobody in the module remarks on it. Someone at the table should.
- *(Proposed.)* If the campaign has been carrying [[Tormar]] since Adventure Three, this face is the natural place to find him — alive, on a pick, twenty feet from an officer wearing a skull.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** use him as the reason the party stops trying to fight through and starts trying to break the mine instead. He should be encountered from concealment, counted, and avoided.
- If the party frees the face, Keehya's fifteen guards are suddenly outnumbered three to one by people holding picks and hammers. That is a genuinely satisfying reversal and the module supplies every number needed for it.
- The staff is lootable and should be identified — whose skull, and does anyone recognise it.
- If Tormar is placed here, do not announce it. Let a player recognise a name on a work gang.
