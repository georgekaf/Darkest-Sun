# Actor: Ela of Balic

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-ela-of-balic`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A half-giant gladiator who won her freedom in Balic's arena and is now fighting for the amusement of a githyanki queen in a cell across the aisle from the party — the first friendly voice they hear after waking up in chains.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: **captive** — chained, collared, stripped of equipment
- Faction or allegiance: none; a captured Athasian
- Current location: the Galnaki prison complex, area 5
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Gladiator; fellow prisoner; the party's first source

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A female half-giant **chained to the wall of the cell opposite the party's own** — the only other occupant visible from where they wake.

**She wears the same sort of silken tunic the characters do.** Like them she has been **stripped of all her weapons and equipment and fitted with a collar that smothers her psionic abilities.**

### Manner and Voice
*(Manner proposed; the content is printed.)* Matter-of-fact and without self-pity. Ela introduces herself, gives her name and her city, and answers what she is asked.

**She does not believe there is a way out**, and says so — not to discourage them, but because it is what she has observed. **Many others have tried; all were recaptured or killed.**

### Public Reputation
In Balic, once: a gladiator who won her freedom. Here: a body the queen has not finished with.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Female half-giant gladiator**, neutral good. Strength 21, Constitution 21, Charisma 15. Wild talent **shadow-form**; PSPs 24+4/level.
- **She introduces herself as Ela and says that she is from Balic.**
- **She was a gladiator in Balic who won her freedom. Now she is forced to fight again, this time for the amusement of the githyanki queen.**
- **She knows the patrol schedule.**
- **She knows the queen has been capturing citizens of Athas. Some of them — especially the psionicists — usually don't come back.**
- **She does not believe there is a way out of here.** Many others have tried; all were recaptured or killed.
- **She knows there are several other important prisoners nearby**, and observes that if the characters have any tricks up their sleeves, **it wouldn't be hard to get some help from the other prisoners.**
- **She speaks to the party after the first patrol passes**, roughly five minutes after Raskon leaves, if they have not already spoken to her.
- **If the players wish to free Ela, she will join their party gladly.**
- She may become an NPC ally or **replace a lost character**. **The DM assigns her a level to fit the rest of the party.**

## Current Activity

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the tutorial, and she is good at it.** Patrol timings, which cells matter, what the queen wants, and an accurate read that escape is impossible — delivered by someone with no reason to lie, in the first five minutes of Book Three. **Run her before the players start experimenting with the shackles.**
- **Her line about the psionicists is the whole campaign in one sentence.** Trinth is harvesting Athasian minds to breed githyanki psionicists. Ela does not know that. She has simply noticed that the ones with the Way go out and do not come back, and she says it flatly, in a cell block, to a party that probably contains one.
- **She is the strongest replacement PC on offer** — Strength 21, Constitution 21, and a level the DM sets to match the party. If someone died at the Nightmare Gate, this is the answer, and she is a considerably better character than a stat line suggests.
- **The collar is the mechanical point.** A half-giant gladiator with a psionic wild talent, rendered ordinary by a black gem too small to see without deliberate looking and cooperation between prisoners. **The gems are in every collar, including the party's.**
- *(Proposed.)* She won her freedom once and had it taken by something that came out of the sky. Whatever she thinks about that, she has not said it, and a player who asks should get something worth having.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** give her the patrol schedule early and let it be correct. It rewards the players for talking to an NPC in a situation where their instinct is to solve the problem alone.
- If a PC died in Adventure Six, offer her before Bardak — she is the better fit for most parties and her introduction is already written.
- Pair her with [[Karasus]] deliberately: she says psionicists do not come back, and then the party walks past a mul who did.
- If she survives to the arena, a Balic-trained gladiator dropped into Trinth's arena is a callback worth staging.
