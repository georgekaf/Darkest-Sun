# Actor: Pargo

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-pargo`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
Slate's first convert and his early-warning system — a dreamy, withdrawn psionicist whose precognition is the reason the party will never catch the Square of Gurdek by surprise.

- Role: Psionicist; the Square's scout and sentry

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
**Pale skin, very long blond hair, and light eyes that suggest a bright, unclouded mind** — the module's phrasing, and it is a deliberate misdirection: *he is as crazed as any other member.*

Hide armour and a wooden shield. A steel hand axe +2 at the belt. His room's floor is covered with a bright, convoluted design, painted quite recently.

### Manner and Voice
**Often dreamy and distracted.** As a psionicist he was already withdrawn; since Gurdek's touch he has been even more aloof and removed.

**He follows Slate almost without thought, and the PCs can learn nothing of value from him.** That is printed, and it should be honoured — he is not a source.

### Public Reputation
Within the Square: the first convert and the one who keeps them safe. Beyond it: unknown.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Human male psionicist 5 / earth cleric 7**, chaotic neutral. Wisdom 18.
- **Slate's first convert.** Several years ago, once Pargo had learned to *ignore earth*, the two of them entered Yathazor together and began exploring — and found the pooled water that became the Refuge.
- 102 PSPs. Clairsentience and psychometabolism: **clairaudience, precognition, shadow-form**, adrenalin control, biofeedback, body control, cell adjustment, chameleon power, combat mind, danger sense, displacement, enhanced strength, poison sense, spirit sense.
- Cleric spells to 4th level, including *hold person*, *silence 15' radius*, *wyvern watch*, *dispel magic*, *meld into stone*, *thorns of binding*, *stone of sharpening*.
- Equipment: steel hand axe +2, hide armour, wooden shield, **amulet of proof against detection and location**, scroll of protection from evil 10' radius, *fruit of growth*.
- **Before Slate makes his daily trip to the eastern cavern mouth, Pargo uses *precognition* to be sure he will be safe.** If the PCs are likely to attack, Pargo warns him not to go.
- **At least once per day — usually when the shadows are longest in the Refuge — Pargo takes shadow-form and scouts the area.** Since he is effectively invisible and intelligent enough to warrant a saving throw when confronting invisible things, **he may spot PC spies.**
- **His psionic sciences allow him to easily spy on others, and he may use them to check out the PCs.**
- **He is not afraid of a good fight, and can use his psionics to boost his martial ability.**
- The design painted on his floor is a meditation aid. Any psionicist recognises it as such. It grants **+1 to harness subconscious, rejuvenation, and meditative focus** performed in that room.
- **Pargo has no valuables.**
- Like the rest of the Square: recruited by charm, initiated by Gurdek, left insane but with identity and memory intact, and **resistant to mind reading**.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Pargo is why the surprise attack fails.** The module builds a specific defence into the Refuge: precognition gates Slate's movements, and daily shadow-form sweeps catch spies. A party planning to scout and then strike should discover that the Square already knew. **This is not GM fiat — it is a printed capability with a printed schedule, and the party can defeat it** (by acting unpredictably, by moving at a time other than longest-shadows, or by killing the scout).
- **The precognition also protects the party from themselves.** If they are likely to attack Slate at the cavern mouth, he simply does not go, and they encounter something else instead. The module handles this with a re-roll. It means the party can never assassinate him on the errand, and they should be allowed to waste one attempt discovering that.
- **He is a dead end as an information source, deliberately.** Of the Square's members, Ari Evan will talk ideology, Mefse will talk philosophy, Ranza can be turned, and Pargo has nothing. Use him to make the others feel like real sources by contrast.
- *(Proposed.)* The dreaminess is not vagueness. He is listening to something.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** signal the precognition. Have Slate be conspicuously absent once, or have the Square be conspicuously ready once, before the players understand why. Then let them work out that the pale dreamy one is the problem.
- He is the correct first target for a party that has scouted well, and killing him should visibly degrade the Square's defence.
- The anti-detection amulet is a good piece of loot for a party that has spent the adventure being scried.
