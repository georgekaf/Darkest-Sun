# Actor: Umor the Poet

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-umor`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A young bard who sang about battles for the sorcerer-queen of Gulg without ever seeing one, escaped a master who was genuinely kind to him, and is now the loudest voice in camp demanding a war he will regret within an hour of its starting.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A young human man, well-fed by the standards of the camp and better-looking than the life warrants — his master kept him presentable and the habits have outlasted the ownership. Leather armour he wears because it looks like something a hero wears. A bone dagger he has never drawn in anger.

He is the only person in the temple who arranges his own clothing before entering a room.

### Manner and Voice
Charming, quick, and enormously earnest. Umor performs constantly, including in conversation, and has the genuine article — Charisma that stops a tent arguing and turns it toward him. When he speaks about the war, people listen, and this is a problem.

He has never once been in danger and it is audible in everything he says about danger.

### Public Reputation
Adored by the camp's young people and by the artists' tent, tolerated fondly by everyone else. His work is one of the reasons the band eats well: the artists' tent produces few physical goods and brings in a great deal of money, and Umor is a large part of why.

## Confirmed Facts
- Human bard, young, with exceptional Charisma.
- Has performed for great audiences across the Tyr region — nobles, templars, and **the sorcerer-queen of Gulg herself**.
- Escaped into the night during a journey with his master. His master was a kind man who could not imagine a slave as well-treated as Umor wanting anything more.
- Had sung of wide open places, great warriors, battles, loves and tragedy — and as a slave had seen almost none of these things.
- Works out of the artists' tent, which produces decoration, jewellery, painting, and music, and which is often the source of the band's surplus cash.
- Young and hungry for glory. Wants to fight the gith.
- Minor debater in the first meeting tent, arguing that flight is cowardly and will lead to death.
- **Too young to understand what war is, and will quickly begin wishing they had run once the fighting starts.** This is printed, not inferred.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the reason the vote goes the way it goes.** Jolon has the arithmetic, Teva has the experience, and Umor has Charisma 18 and a tent full of people who want to be told they are brave. If the campaign wants the band to choose war without the PCs having to force it, Umor is the mechanism already on the board.
- **His collapse is scripted and should be played in full.** The printed source states he will begin wishing they had run once the fighting starts. Give him a specific moment in the first battle — not death, but the instant the singing stops.
- **His master was kind, and he left anyway.** This is the most interesting thing about him and he has never said it out loud to anyone in the camp, where every other person escaped something brutal. He suspects his freedom is worth less than theirs because it cost less.
- If he survives Adventure One, the *Warsong of Tenpug* quoted at the head of the module is plausibly his. That is a quiet, excellent payoff and requires nothing but letting him live.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** he lives, he stops singing about battles, and he writes the Warsong instead. The module's own epigraph becomes an in-world artifact the players watched get made.
- Alternatively, he dies in the first battle mid-performance, and the band never gets its song. Cheaper, and effective exactly once.
- His court access is an unused route into Gulg or Nibenay if the campaign ever needs a party to get in front of someone important.
- Pair him with [[Roi]] for the two ends of the same argument: the boy who has never seen it and the old man who has seen fifty years of it.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Bard
- Level: 3
- Armor Class: 12 (leather)
- Hit Points: 14
- Movement: near
- Strength +1, Dexterity +1, Constitution +1, Intelligence +2, Wisdom +0, Charisma **+4**
- Alignment: Neutral
- Morale: 10 before the first battle, 5 after it
- Attacks: 1 attack per round.
  - *Bone dagger* +1, 1d4−1 (attack penalty as printed; he is not a fighter and should not be treated as one)
- **Inspire:** allies within near range who can hear him gain +1 to attack rolls, or +2 to morale, while he keeps performing. This is his entire battlefield value and it is not nothing.
- **Sway the Room:** advantage on Charisma checks made to move a crowd rather than an individual. In the tent debates, treat him as the strongest single voice present.
- **Never Seen It:** disadvantage on any check to assess a military situation, estimate enemy strength, or judge whether a plan is survivable.
- Bard spellcasting: minimal, if the campaign uses it. His function is social, not arcane.
- **Running him at the table:** let him be genuinely persuasive and genuinely wrong. He should win the argument the players may be trying to lose, and the GM should not soften what that costs.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Human Bard 3, Cha 18, hp 15, AC 8 (leather), THAC0 20, bone dagger 1d4−1 with −1 to hit. Kept at level 3.
