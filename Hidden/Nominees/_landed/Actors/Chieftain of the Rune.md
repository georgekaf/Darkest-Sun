# Actor: Chieftain of the Rune

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-rune-chieftain`. **Unnamed in the printed source — see Naming, below.**)*

## Naming

The module never names him. Three options, in order of preference:

1. **Keep the title.** "The Chieftain of the Rune" is usable as-is and matches how the band and the PCs would ever refer to him.
2. **Adopt a proposed personal name.** Offered for approval, in the register of the module's other gith (Zigath, Haza, Toogo, Drak, Zuzar, Keehya, Askai): **Vorreth**, **Gharuk**, or **Sekkath**.
3. Leave blank and let the table produce one.

The id is deliberately role-based rather than name-based, so a later naming ruling costs nothing.

## One-Sentence Summary
The gith psionicist who united three raiding tribes under a banner none of them can read, and who is the first evidence in the entire campaign that something above the gith is giving orders.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A hulking gith, crooked-formed, squatting over a brazier in the largest tent in the camp. Thick black smoke creeps out from under the tent flaps and makes onlookers cough and wheeze; he breathes it in deliberately, once every minute or so, to help him meditate.

Over the tent flies a banner: a black circle and starburst on a yellow field. The same device stands on a battle standard planted in the sand outside, with nearly a hundred gith sleeping around it.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)* Slow, deliberate, and unnervingly patient for a gith. Where the other two chieftains scheme against each other, the Rune simply waits, and both of them are frightened of him without being able to say why.

### Public Reputation
Among the gith: the one who brought the tribes together, which no gith in living memory has managed. Among everyone else: unknown, because nobody outside that camp knows he exists.

## Confirmed Facts
- Leads the Tribe of the Rune, the largest of the three tribes besieging Tenpug's Band — nearly 100 gith encamped around his standard, against the Hand's ~70 and the Eye's ~80.
- His tent is the largest of the three that dominate the camp.
- He is a **very powerful gith psionicist**.
- Sits alone in the middle of his tent, hunched over a brazier of incense, breathing the smoke to aid meditation.
- He is working on two problems: how best to attack Tenpug, and **how to get the two other tribes to work together**.
- His banner and battle standard both show a black rune on a yellow background: a black circle and starburst.
- **A successful spellcraft check reveals this is an ancient representation of the Astral Plane** — which the module flags outright as "a strange rune for a gith war band."
- In the battle plan, the Rune holds the high ground on the dunes and commits where the fight is going worst. If the other two tribes get into difficulty, the Rune sweeps down.
- If the PCs succeed in setting the tribes against each other, the fighting stops when **the leader of the Rune steps in and ends it** — after a couple of dozen gith have died.
- In Adventure Two, it is the commander of the Rune who sends a messenger to the gith base camp asking for help, which is what brings [[Askai]] into the campaign.

- Secrecy: 4.

- Risk tolerance: Low personally, high with other tribes' warriors.

- Status: The only gith any of the three tribes will all take orders from.

## Resources and Capabilities
- Command of the combined war band: roughly 250 gith on the PCs' first scouting, rising to about 320 within a week.
- Powerful psionics — the module gives the Rune's units the best statistics of the three tribes and marks the tribe as the reason the band cannot win the first battle.
- Steel weapons of uniform, ornamental, master-forged manufacture, well beyond gith capability.
- Sole authority to stop inter-tribal fighting, which he demonstrably has and uses.
- A messenger line to the main gith base camp, and the standing to have a request for help answered by something like Askai.

## Current Activity
Meditating over a brazier in the largest tent in the camp, deciding how to attack, and holding two treacherous allies in line by presence alone.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the campaign's first thread.** The Astral rune is the earliest githyanki fingerprint in the entire seven-adventure arc, and it is available to any PC with a spellcraft proficiency on the very first night of scouting. Everything — Yathazor, the Nightmare Gate, Trinth — is downstream of that banner. Play the reveal for exactly what it is worth: a party that clocks it in Adventure One is chasing the right question four adventures early.
- **The tribal alliance is a designed weakness.** The module explicitly invites the PCs to attack one tribe and frame another. The Rune is the reason it only half works: he can and will stop the fighting. He cannot stop it from happening again.
- **He is not the top of the chain and does not pretend to be.** He sends for help in Adventure Two rather than improvising, and what answers is an ancient gith lord of enormously greater power. Whoever gave him the rune outranks him.
- *(Proposed.)* He does not know what the rune means either. He knows what it is worth — three tribes and a supply of steel — and he has never asked. That is a better answer than him being an initiate.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** make the banner legible. One spellcraft check, one line of description, and the campaign's spine is visible from adventure one.
- His survival is unstated. If he lives through the second battle and the desert pursuit, he becomes the natural recurring face of the surface gith across Adventures Two and Three, and eventually a source under interrogation.
- If the party frames one tribe against another and he intervenes, he should intervene *visibly* — the moment a hundred gith stop fighting because one gith walked out of a tent is worth staging.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Psionicist [UC]
- Level: 7
- Armor Class: 13 (inix shell)
- Hit Points: 38
- Movement: near
- Strength +2, Dexterity +2, Constitution +2, Intelligence +2, Wisdom +3, Charisma +2
- Alignment: Chaotic (evil)
- Morale: 13
- Attacks: 2 attacks per round.
  - *Steel-tipped spear* +6, 1d6+2
  - *Claws* +6, 1d4+2 (×2) — gith prefer to throw the spear and close with claws
- **Springing Attack:** may leap 20 feet as part of a move and attack with advantage on landing. **Restricted underground** — the module notes gith can only spring in open chambers.
- **Mind Thrust** +6, 2d6 psychic, near range, ignores armour.
- **Id Insinuation:** target makes a Wisdom check or loses its next action.
- **Tower of Iron Will:** as a reaction, he and allies within near range gain advantage against psionic attack.
- **Hold the Alliance:** once per scene, he can end an ongoing fight between gith units by presence alone, no check.
- **Running him at the table:** he is the reason the band loses. Keep him off the field until the moment the Hand and the Eye are committed, then bring the Rune down off the dunes. He should be seen meditating long before he is seen fighting.
- **Conversion note:** the module gives the Rune only BATTLESYSTEM unit statistics (AD 8, AR 8 inix shell, Hits 2, ML 13, MV 9) and a leader line (AD 6, AR 8, Hits 3, ML 13). No personal stat block exists. The above is built from the printed "very powerful gith psionicist" description and the standard gith sergeant psionics list.
