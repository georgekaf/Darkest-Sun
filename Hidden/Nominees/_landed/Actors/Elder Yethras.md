# Actor: Elder Yethras

## One-Sentence Summary
Half-elf head of Kharanok's village council — the public face of a leadership he doesn't fully control.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: Kharanok village council
- Current location: Kharanok (Shade Houses)
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Village council head / spokesperson

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A thin, weathered human man in his late sixties, though exhaustion makes him look older. His skin is dry and deeply lined, darkened by years of sun, dust, and bad water. His face has the drawn, hollow look of someone who has slept lightly for too many years — heavy lids, sunken cheeks, and a mouth that rests in a permanent line of restrained worry. His hair is grey-white, sparse, combed back without vanity; a short, uneven beard clings to his jaw, more neglected than styled. His eyes are pale brown, tired but still alert, often fixed on whoever is speaking as if measuring every word against some older failure. He wears plain council robes, once formal but faded to a dull, dusty brown, clean but worn thin at the cuffs, elbows, and hem. A simple cord belt holds a small key-ring, a cracked wax tablet, and a council seal he touches unconsciously when decisions become difficult — nothing about him suggests wealth or comfort. He carries himself with slow, deliberate control, standing slightly stooped not from weakness but from the habit of bearing responsibility too long; when seated he keeps both hands folded over his walking stick, knuckles tense, as if bracing for another piece of bad news. He does not look like a tyrant, a fool, or a coward — he looks like the last beam holding up a roof that has already begun to collapse.

### Manner and Voice
Pragmatic, exhausted. Speaks plainly about how little the village has to offer.

**Sample dialogue / introduction scene (as written by the GM):**
> The Shade Houses are quiet when Elder Yethras comes to meet the new arrivals.
>
> He does not arrive with guards, ceremony, or threats. Only a walking stick in one hand, a faded council robe hanging loosely from his narrow shoulders, and a cracked council seal tied at his belt. He stops a few paces away and studies each of them in silence, not with suspicion exactly, but with the tired caution of a man who has learned that every new face brings another decision.
>
> For a long moment, he says nothing.
>
> Then he inclines his head.
>
> "You have come to Kharanok."
>
> His voice is low, dry, and measured. He speaks as if each word has weight, and as if wasting words would be an act of weakness.
>
> "That is either misfortune, purpose, or someone else's mistake."
>
> He lets the sentence settle before continuing.
>
> "I am Yethras. Elder of the council, though there is little council left to speak of. If you have come seeking shelter, we have less than we should. If you have come seeking work, there is more than anyone can carry. If you have come seeking answers…"
>
> He looks toward the deeper shadows of the Shade Houses, then back to them.
>
> "…then you should understand that answers are not the same as comfort."
>
> Yethras shifts his grip on the walking stick. His knuckles tighten, then ease.
>
> "You will hear many things in this village. Some true. Some repeated because repetition is easier than courage. Speak carefully. Listen better. And if you find something broken, do not assume it broke recently."
>
> Another pause.
>
> "If you mean to help, do what you think is necessary. If you mean to take advantage of us, then at least have the decency to do it openly. Kharanok has had enough damage done in whispers."
>
> He steps aside, granting them entry deeper into the Shade Houses.
>
> "Come. There are things you should see before anyone tells you what to believe."

### Public Reputation
Seen by newcomers and villagers as Kharanok's leader and its voice in dealings with outsiders.

## Confirmed Facts
- Welcomed the party coldly on arrival (ad1): the village has almost nothing to offer newcomers — no spare food, no surplus water, no trade.
- Warned them that Kharanok's problems predate their arrival — conditions were already bad before the Gith started coming for Crystal Dust.
- Pressured by the growing number of Quel Nas refugees and the ongoing Gith threat, agreed to let the party stay in exchange for work.
- Laid out the village's needs: gate repair, a polished obsidian piece for the watchtower's cracked signal mirror, hands to stand watch.
- Introduced the party to Sira and the Syggra-recovery problem.
- Design doc quest-giver credit: Act 1, Hook 1 ("The Tower Is Blind"), alongside Breck.

His council-head position was not independently earned — Kalia organized the council and put him forward as its spokesperson. Whether Yethras consciously knows/accepts this arrangement, or genuinely believes himself the primary leader, is undetermined.
