# Actor: Slinnasia

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-slinnasia`. **Dead before the campaign begins. Present entirely through the two people ruined by her death.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A young preserver destined to lead her tribe, who went into Giustenal with her best friend, had her mind taken by the Caller in Darkness, and was made to kill herself — leaving one elf branded a murderer and her own sister the one who branded him.

- **Jessix's best friend.**
- **[[Jessareen]]'s sister.**
- **She was a young and beautiful preserver destined to become a powerful tribal leader.**
- She accompanied [[Jessix the Wanderer]] on **the second expedition into Giustenal.**
- **She fell prey to the [[Caller in Darkness]].**
- **The Caller dominated her mind and urged her to take her own life inside the ruins of Giustenal.**
- **Jessix tried his best to save her** — but **an encounter with a pit snatcher delayed him from reaching her in time.**
- **When she died, Jessix was held responsible.** The dark stains on his hands — actually pit snatcher marks — **are held as evidence of his guilt.**
- **[[The Loyal]] were present.** They **were there when the Caller turned Slinnasia against them, and they believe that Jessix did everything he could to prevent her death.**
- **The Twilightcatchers haven't returned to Giustenal since Slinnasia's death and Jessareen's infliction.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the reason the entire Chapter Two social map looks the way it does.** Jessix's exile, the Loyal's existence, Jessareen's vendetta, Guvaano's standing order, and the Twilightcatchers' pivot to Bodach **all trace to one death that nobody present has described accurately except five elves nobody listens to.**
- **The Caller still has her.** This is the thread the module leaves lying on the floor. The Caller in Darkness **absorbs the psyches of those it kills** — that is what it *is*. **Slinnasia did not simply die in Giustenal; she is plausibly still in there, as part of it.** If the party ever confronts the Caller, **the module gives the GM a name, a sister, and a wrongly-blamed best friend already fully established.**
- **Her body is unaccounted for.** Nobody recovered it. That is a recoverable object in a ruin the party is going to enter.
- **The exoneration is physically verifiable.** Pit snatcher stains are distinctive, the creature is in the set's own *Monstrous Compendium* appendix, and any party that fights one can clear Jessix. **Slinnasia's death is a mystery with a printed solution that no NPC has bothered to check.**
- *(Proposed.)* She was the one who wanted to go back, not Jessix. The module says Jessix insisted — which is the version that survived, told by people who were not there.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** if the party ever faces the Caller in Darkness, use Slinnasia. A voice the Loyal recognise is worth more than any amount of description.
- Her remains in Giustenal are a small, achievable quest with an enormous social payoff in the environs.
- If the campaign wants Jessix cleared, she is the mechanism: her own testimony, out of the Caller.
- **Do not resurrect her.** Everything she is worth is in what her death did to four other characters.
