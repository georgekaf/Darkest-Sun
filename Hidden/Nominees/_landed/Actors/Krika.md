## One-Sentence Summary
A githzerai spy who has spent long enough polymorphed as a githyanki to walk Galnaki freely, who kills six of them with poison gas to reach the escaping PCs, and who is a fanatic — she will tell you at length about millennia of injustice and cannot name a single specific example.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
First seen **as a githyanki** — a slender, unarmoured female standing at the base of a stair, **apparently unharmed among half a dozen dead githyanki warriors whose hands clutch at their throats and whose skin has gone a sickly green.**

Then she removes an amulet and **resumes her true form.** Githzerai look much more human than their cousins: unremarkable features with a vaguely noble cast, ordinary caucasian skin tone, **dressing simply in functional clothing and conservative tones.**

She fights in **chainmail +3** with a **silver sword +3**.

### Manner and Voice
Direct and urgent while she has a job to do. **If asked about the githzerai war with the githyanki she goes into long tirades about the cruel injustices done to her people since the dawn of time — she is a fanatic, and can provide few actual examples.**

She has one other register, and the party will see it: at the ruined githzerai base she **lets out a gasp of surprise and horror, her eyes dart back and forth, then open wide, and she begins to scream in terror. There is nothing but wild panic in her eyes.**

**Quieting her is not difficult** — she just needs reminding of the importance of her mission, her dedication to the githzerai cause, or something else vital to her.

### Public Reputation
In Galnaki: a githyanki nobody has looked at twice. Among the githzerai: the agent Captain [[Bahkus]] sent in.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Female githzerai fighter / wizard / psionicist**, lawful good. AC −2, hp 40, 175 PSPs, Intelligence 17, Dexterity 18.
- **She is a githzerai spy, polymorphed to resemble a githyanki**, operating inside Galnaki.
- Her **magical amulet functions only for her** and allows her to assume the shape of a githyanki woman **three times each day.**
- **When she learned of the escape attempt she raced to the landing above area 22**, hoping to aid the PCs and convince them to help her. **The githyanki on the floor were racing up the stairs to bring reinforcements; she killed them with a cloud of poisonous gas.**
- **She may also appear earlier**, in the dungeon, if the PCs cannot escape on their own: after two days of confinement she slips up to their cell, identifies herself, **tosses a metal key to the nearest prisoner**, whispers for them to look for her at the top of the stairs if they make it that far, and leaves.
- If the PCs agree to help her, **she opens a secret door in the landing wall** revealing a vertical shaft with a narrow ladder. **The ladder takes over an hour to ascend** and opens into a comfortably furnished chamber where the party can rest and recover.
- **If the adventurers did not recover their equipment from the storage areas, Krika has a few of their possessions there — each player reclaims 1d4 of their character's items.**
- She then uses **a transparent crystal** to teleport the party to the githzerai asteroid base.
- If the PCs refuse to help, **she points out that the stairway continues up into Galnaki**, that there is no way they can survive a battle against an entire city of githyanki, and lets the sound of armoured troops descending make the argument for her.
- On arriving at the ruined base she panics, then insists on investigating. **She assumes the githyanki are responsible but knows better than to assume it without investigation.**
- **She objects to the looting of githzerai bodies**, insisting they will be buried with dignity, though she permits the PCs to take the keys. **She objects to ransacking the barracks and even to searching the bodies.**
- **If the characters do not respect her wishes, she plane shifts back to Limbo** rather than be party to desecration — **returning in a few hours with a company of githzerai warriors to clean out the base herself, at which point the PCs are intruders and will be killed on sight.** The module tells the GM to make this a very unattractive option.
- **She will not leave the base until the creature that attacked the githzerai has been found and destroyed.**
- She **cannot defeat the PCs in open combat and knows it** — so she does not attack them over the looting; she leaves.
- **Her plan:** she returns to Limbo to assemble a strike force while the PCs return to the githyanki city and destroy Yrinolir. **If they refuse, she reminds them that their only way back to Athas is through the portal at the heart of Galnaki.**
- She can provide **patrol schedules, the locations of her hideouts, and rough sketches of several secret tunnels.**
- **She suggests entering Trinth's palace through the Royal Garden** — one of her secret tunnels opens near its edge — **posing as garden workers.** Because the githyanki keep slaves from countless unusual races, **thri-kreen and half-giant characters will not be conspicuous.**
- At the climax, **Krika and a group of githzerai in armour appear in the corridor behind the githyanki and fall upon them with a roar.**
- **If Krika is not with the group or has been killed, the PCs encounter another githzerai spy who takes her place for the rest of the adventure.**

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the party's only way home and she is prepared to walk away over a looted corpse.** The module is explicit: desecrate githzerai dead and she plane shifts to Limbo, comes back with soldiers, and the PCs become intruders to be killed on sight. **The GM is told to make this very unattractive.** It is the sharpest values-clash the campaign offers and it costs nothing to set up — the bodies have keys on them and the party will want the keys.
- **Her fanaticism is played as a flaw and is also correct.** Long tirades, no specifics — she is a true believer repeating inherited grievance. And the githzerai *were* enslaved by illithids for centuries, *were* split by Gith and Zerthimon, and *did* get devastated. The module makes her ridiculous and factually right at the same time, and does not comment on it.
- **She is wrong about the most important thing.** She assumes the githyanki destroyed her base. It was [[Alukas]], a beholder her own people never knew was imprisoned there, released by a githyanki spy's malfunctioning teleport. **Every conclusion she draws before the library is unreliable.**
- **The Maelactorus problem is hers, not the party's.** If the PCs open the vault and try to negotiate with the mind flayer, Krika will insist it must be destroyed — and she is the one who can leave. The module hands the GM a three-way standoff and never resolves it.
- **She is replaceable and the module says so**, which is a tell: her *function* is load-bearing and her *person* is not. Recommended: make the person load-bearing anyway. She is the only lawful good NPC in Book Three.
- *(Proposed.)* If Bahkus dies and the outpost is gone, Krika is the senior githzerai in the system. She has not thought about that yet.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** stage the looting confrontation deliberately. Put something the party actively wants on a githzerai body, then let Krika watch.
- Let her be wrong about the beholder in front of them, and let her have to revise. It is the fastest way to make a fanatic into a person.
- The silver sword is a major artifact of her people. If she dies and the party takes it, **githzerai will come for it** — that is printed racial behaviour.
- If the campaign wants a githzerai faction record, she is the natural anchor for it.
