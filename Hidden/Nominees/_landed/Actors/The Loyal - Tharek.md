# Actor: The Loyal — Tharek

## One-Sentence Summary
The Loyal's skirmisher — the one who learned to fight because somebody in the band had to, and who now stands between his companions and whatever comes out of the ruins by pure reflex.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: [[The Loyal]] (`faction-the-loyal`), follower
- Current location: the wastes within 20 miles of the Ruins of Giustenal (`location-giustenal-ruins`)
- Current route, when traveling: with the band
- Role: Front line; escort protection

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Broad-shouldered by elf standards but still lean and long-limbed, with a hard square face, a broken nose, closely cropped dark hair and several old scars. Heavier reinforced leather armor of layered desert-creature hide, wrapped shoulders, thick bracers, dusty boots. A heavy bone short sword and a bone shortbow. Confrontational stance.

### Manner and Voice
Blunt. Short sentences. Gets loud only on one subject: anyone repeating Jessareen's accusation against Jessix.

### Public Reputation
The one travelers notice first and are relieved to find on their side.

## Confirmed Facts
- Source states only that Jessix roams with five young elves called the Loyal, preservers and thieves, who witnessed Slinnasia's death and believe him innocent (Campaign Book p. 11-12).
- Both Giustenal expeditions are established in the source's account of Jessix. These five survived both.
