# Actor: Karros

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-karros`. **Dead when found.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A githyanki spy wearing a githzerai body, who slipped into a dead-end tunnel to teleport home with news of the outpost, triggered his icon next to a beholder's wards by accident, and was the first thing it killed.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the cause of everything in Book Three's middle act, and he is a corpse with three sentences.** The githzerai outpost is destroyed, the party's ally is shattered, and a beholder is loose in a library — **all because one spy triggered a teleport in the wrong room.** Nobody in the campaign ever finds out except the reader.
- **The icon is the clue and Krika hands it to them without understanding it.** She recognises a githyanki portal symbol in the hand of a githzerai soldier and says so out loud. **A party that hears that and thinks about it has solved the mystery of the base before they reach the library.**
- **The chamber itself is the second clue.** Krika says it was not here when she left, *and yet it does not look like new construction* — because it was never built, it was **revealed** when the wards collapsed. That is a genuinely good detail and it is easy to skate past.
- **Galnaki never got the message.** Trinth does not know the githzerai were watching her, and does not know their outpost is gone. **That is an intelligence gap the party can exploit** and the module never mentions it.
- **Whether Krika learns the truth is an open and pointed question.** She is currently burying a traitor with full honours and objecting to anyone searching his body. **If the party works out what he was and tells her, the scene with the bodies changes completely.**
- *(Proposed.)* He may not have been the only one.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** let the party assemble it themselves from the icon, the room, the empty scabbards and the beholder. It is the best piece of environmental deduction in Book Three.
- **The Krika confrontation is worth staging.** She insisted the dead be buried with dignity. Telling her one of them sold the outpost is a real cost to a real relationship.
- The unreported intelligence is a usable advantage for the palace infiltration.
- If the campaign wants a second infiltrator still in place among the githzerai reinforcements Krika brings back, the precedent is established.
