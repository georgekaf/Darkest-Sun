# Actor: Pimns

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-pimns`. Paired with [[Solaq]] — the two should be adopted or dropped together.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A dwarven gladiator who won her freedom in the Nibenay arena, was enslaved again, died at a chasm's edge screaming *death before slavery* with her focus unfulfilled and her life with Solaq unlived — and is still there.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc — **undead (dwarven banshee)**
- Control: autonomous
- Status: **dead, and present**
- Faction or allegiance: none
- Current location: the Cave of the Winds, encounter 4 — the promenade above the great chasm, below Yathazor
- Current route, when traveling: **cannot leave.** *Here they remain.*
- Role: Dwarven banshee; obstacle; witness

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
Not a body. The party sees the scene first: **the skeletal remains of some twenty captured slaves lying in crumpled heaps near the centre of the cavern floor**, flesh stripped clean by giant ants, in a vast room where a low eerie moaning echoes — wind through the chasm.

**Two of the fallen were dwarven warriors.** They have returned to haunt the area.

### Manner and Voice
A malediction — a scream. That is her language now.

In life, she and Solaq **vowed death over captivity, were rebellious against their captors, and were often beaten.** They **leapt into the battle screaming *"Death before slavery!"*** and that is the note the encounter is pitched at.

### Public Reputation
None. Twenty dead slaves in a cavern nobody living has walked through since.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Pimns, 5th-level dwarven female gladiator.** Strength 18/00, Constitution 18. 42 hit points. Neutral evil in undeath.
- She and [[Solaq]] **won their freedom in the Nibenay arena.**
- **Vowing death over captivity, they were rebellious against their captors and were often beaten.**
- They were among some twenty captured slaves who **attempted an unsuccessful rebellion at this juncture and paid dearly for their insolence.**
- **They saw the rebellion as their one chance for escape. They leapt into the battle screaming, "Death before slavery!"**
- **"Their focus, their dreams, and their chance for a life together all died at the chasm's edge. Here they remain."**
- The flesh of the fallen was **stripped clean by giant ants.**
- **The players must pass them to continue.**
- Printed combat sequence: **first round — Pimns screams a malediction while Solaq creates a 20-point death field. Second round — Solaq screams a malediction while Pimns psionically attempts to double pain.**
- If the players use magical means to speak with the banshees or with the skeletal remains, they may learn:
  - **The dwarves' captors were powerful gith with magical weapons.**
  - **The gith walked to the two great archways and entered each for a few moments. They seemed very angry upon their return.**
  - **A human gladiator named [[Gort]] started the rebellion**, and tried to drive the captors off the edge of the chasm. **Two guards died along with Gort and many slaves.**
  - **The survivors crossed the chasm, but Pimns and Solaq do not know how. They were dead at the time.**

## Current Activity
Standing at the edge of the chasm where she died, with the wind moaning through it, waiting for the next thing to come along the promenade.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **This is the payoff for [[Roi]], if the campaign took that thread.** The vault's own `Rules/Ancestries.md` states that dwarves pursue a personal focus they must fulfil before death **for fear of undeath otherwise.** The module says of Pimns and Solaq: *"Their focus, their dreams, and their chance for a life together all died at the chasm's edge. Here they remain."* **That is the rule, executed.** If Roi charged into the first battle at Tenpug's temple and died with nothing finished, a party meeting these two several adventures later has been shown exactly what they did not prevent. No exposition required.
- **The intelligence is worth more than the fight.** Speaking with the dead here yields the archway behaviour — the gith entered both great arches, briefly, and came out angry. That tells the party the arches are checked, are not passable to everyone, and disappointed someone. It is the earliest working information about the Gray Arch, delivered by a corpse.
- **She and Solaq have a fixed two-round opening and it is printed.** Malediction plus a 20-point death field, then malediction plus double pain. Run it as written; the coordination is characterisation.
- **They cannot be talked down without magic**, and with magic they will talk. A party that comes prepared to speak with the dead converts an unavoidable undead fight into the best briefing available before the mine.
- *(Proposed.)* Laying them to rest is possible and the module does not provide a method. Anything that completes the thing they died holding — an actual escape, or the destruction of the gith who took them — is defensible.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** if Roi died at the temple, foreground the connection. Have a dwarf PC or NPC recognise what these two are without being told.
- Offer a resolution other than combat. The module offers none, and one is clearly available.
- The twenty skeletons are also speakable-with, and the module says so. Gort's name comes out of them.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Gladiator *(in life)* — **undead: dwarven banshee**
- Level: 4 *(Shadowdark scaling of 2e 5)*
- Armor Class: 10 — **but see below**
- Hit Points: 42
- Movement: near
- Strength **+4**, Dexterity +1, Constitution +3, Intelligence −1, Wisdom −1, Charisma +0
- Alignment: Chaotic (evil)
- Morale: 12 — **and she does not leave; she cannot**
- Attacks: 2 attacks per round.
  - *Fists* +5, 1d2+4 — **her physical attack is negligible and that is not what she is**
- **Malediction (scream):** the banshee's primary weapon. Save or suffer severe damage; a failed save at low hit points should be lethal, per the traditional banshee wail.
- **Double Pain (psionic):** doubles damage suffered by one target for a short duration.
- **Undead:** immune to sleep, charm, hold, fear and cold; cannot be turned by an untrained character.
- **Bound to the Site:** she cannot leave the promenade. The party can retreat; they cannot pass.
- **Speak With Dead:** she will answer. See Knowledge.
- **Running her at the table:** the encounter is a haunting first and a fight second. Describe the twenty stripped skeletons, then the moaning, then the two that rise. Give the players a beat to realise what they are looking at before rolling anything.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Pimns, 5th-level Dwarven Female Gladiator, AL NE, AC 10, MV 12, HD 5, hp 42, THAC0 16, #AT 2, Dmg 1d2 (fist), ML Steady (12), Str 18/00, Dex 14, Con 18, Int 9, Wis 8, Cha 10. **The source gives banshee abilities in the combat sequence but no banshee stat line**; malediction and death field are drawn from that sequence. Note the printed text alternates between "Pimns" and "Pimn" — **"Pimns" adopted**, matching the stat block heading.
