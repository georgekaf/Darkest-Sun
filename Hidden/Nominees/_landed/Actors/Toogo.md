# Actor: Toogo

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-toogo`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
Haza's companion — a spectacularly fast, spectacularly stupid gith who fights with his bare claws and, if captured, will confidently invent an entire war plan on the spot rather than admit he knows nothing.

- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: `faction-black-spine-gith`
- Current location: `location-zigaths-nest` — the Great Hall, or the barracks ledge
- Current route, when traveling: follows [[Haza]]
- Role: Gith champion's companion; shock troop

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
**Exceptionally thin and lanky**, even by gith standards, in **banded armour and clothes the module describes simply as bizarre**. He is an alarming figure — the printed text says so — and never more so than when he is in the air coming at you.

He carries nine steel-tipped darts and does not appear to carry anything else. His claws are the weapon.

### Manner and Voice
Eager, loud, and empty. Toogo talks a great deal and knows nothing, and the gap between those two facts never seems to occur to him.

**If interrogated he babbles endlessly** about great plans, mighty spells, unstoppable armies, great secrets and devastating magical items. **All of it is invented on the spot.** He is not lying to protect anything; he simply cannot bear to be a person with nothing to say.

### Public Reputation
Haza's shadow. The troops know him as the one who goes first and enjoys it.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Male gith, 7th-level fighter**, chaotic evil. Wisdom 6, Charisma 5 — the module's numbers, and they are doing characterisation work.
- Exceptionally thin and lanky; his armour and clothes are bizarre; he is an alarming figure, especially when leaping at his enemy.
- **Specialised with claws** — four attacks a round unarmed.
- Equipment: banded armour, nine steel-tipped darts +1, a *fruit of extra healing*, a *fruit of polymorph self*.
- **A companion of Haza. He follows him faithfully, launching into an attack whenever he gets the chance.**
- One of the three exceptional gith at Zigath's Nest, rotating shifts with Haza and Zigath so that at least one is always on duty in the Great Hall and only one asleep.
- Described as a powerful warrior **but not a skilled tactician** — the brains are Haza and Zigath.
- **Toogo knows nothing of the gith's plans.**
- In the nest's defensive plan, Toogo waits in the corridors and behind the Stand of the Mighty with the remaining sergeant and 35 warriors.
- He has a personal tent on the raised barracks ledge that Haza adopted for their quarters.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **Toogo is a deliberate disinformation trap and it is beautifully placed.** The party captures a senior gith. He talks freely, at length, with total confidence, about mighty spells and unstoppable armies and devastating magical items. **None of it is true.** Meanwhile [[Haza]] — who actually knows about the Square of Gurdek and the "long lost kin" — says nothing at all, and [[Zigath]] — who knows about the githyanki — might talk if pressed properly.
  Three prisoners, three information behaviours, and the most forthcoming one is worthless. Run all three and let the players sort it out.
- **Do not signpost that he is lying.** He is not lying. He believes he is being helpful, and he is very convincing, because he has no idea he is wrong. If the party acts on Toogo's intelligence and walks into something on the strength of it, that is the encounter working as designed.
- *(Proposed.)* He is not stupid so much as hollow. Wisdom 6 and Charisma 5 in a creature that leads charges suggests something that only functions when pointed. Without Haza he is genuinely lost, and a party that kills Haza in front of him gets a very different fight for the rest of the round.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** capture at least two of the three nest leaders, and stage the interrogations back to back. The comedy and the horror are both free.
- If Haza falls first, Toogo should visibly break — not flee, but stop being effective. That is a cheap, legible consequence for good target priority.
- The *fruit of polymorph self* on a creature with Wisdom 6 is a gift to any GM who wants the fight to get strange.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Fighter
- Level: 6
- Armor Class: 16 (banded armour and Dexterity)
- Hit Points: 44
- Movement: near — **double near when springing**
- Strength +3, Dexterity +4, Constitution +2, Intelligence −1, Wisdom −2, Charisma −3
- Alignment: Chaotic (evil)
- Morale: 13 with Haza present; **8 if Haza falls**
- Attacks: 4 attacks per round.
  - *Claws* +7, 1d4+3 (×4 — specialised)
  - *Steel-tipped dart +1* +8, 1d3+2 (thrown; carries nine)
- **Claw Specialisation:** the four-attack routine is his defining feature. He does not use a weapon in melee and should not be given one.
- **Springing Attack:** may leap 20 feet as part of a move and attack with advantage on landing. **Chambers only** — restricted in the corridors, which is why he is stationed in the Great Hall.
- **Fruit of extra healing:** one use, substantial.
- **Fruit of polymorph self:** one use. He will use it badly.
- **Follows Haza:** while Haza is conscious and visible, Toogo attacks whatever Haza is engaged with, regardless of tactical sense.
- **Running him at the table:** a blur of claws that arrives before anyone expected it, and a chatterbox in the cell afterwards. Never give him a true fact.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Male Gith Fighter 7, Str 17, Dex 18, hp 57, AC 0 (banded and Dex), THAC0 14, 4 attacks or 7/2 darts, claws 1d4+3, darts 1d3+2. Scaled to Shadowdark level 6; the four-attack claw routine is preserved because it is the character.
