# Actor: Ara

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-ara`. **Thin source — see Sources for what is printed and what is proposed.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The camp lookout who walks the dune screen at all hours and who saw the gith first — the reason the band had any warning at all, and the only person in the temple whose whole job is to be outside it.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
*(Printed source gives no description. The following is proposed.)*

Wind-scoured and habitually squinting, in wrappings the colour of the dunes she works — the only person in the band whose clothing is chosen to be invisible from a distance rather than to be worn indoors. Sand in everything. A horn at her belt and a spear used mostly as a walking staff.

She stands very still for long periods and then moves quickly, which unnerves people who have not watched her work.

### Manner and Voice
*(Proposed.)*

Terse, literal, and impatient with anyone who wants to discuss what she has reported rather than act on it. Ara delivers observations as observations — number, direction, distance, time — and visibly resents being asked what she thinks it means.

She does not come to the meeting tents. She has opinions and considers voicing them a waste of a watch.

### Public Reputation
Barely thought about, which is precisely the measure of how well she does the job. The band notices the lookout only in the interval between her warning and the danger arriving.

## Confirmed Facts
- Ara is the camp's lookout.
- She often prowls the sand-screen dunes above the camp, watching for unusual dust clouds, distant fires, or anything else out of the ordinary.
- **She was the one who first observed the gith.**
- The dunes she patrols are natural, formed by wind around the temple, and serve as the screen that conceals the camp's location.
- Fresh footprints along the dune tops are a standing feature of the approach — a diligent sentry walked there not long ago.

## Current Activity
Walking the dune tops, counting, and waiting for the camp to finish arguing.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the second answer to the carpenters problem.** Between Ara's patrol timings and [[Fala the Huntress|Fala]]'s forest knowledge, a party has everything needed to extract the thirty carpenters and engineers stranded outside the screen — which the module makes the difference between fielding 18 figures and 15 in the first battle. Neither NPC volunteers this.
- **She has been on watch continuously since the gith appeared** and is running on nothing. A GM who wants a small, human failure at a bad moment has one available and pre-justified.
- *(Proposed.)* She blames herself for the camp being found. She is probably wrong — see [[Raxxon]] — and the guilt is making her push her watches past the point of usefulness.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** give her one scene. A PC climbs the dune at night, and instead of a nameless sentry there is a specific exhausted person with an exact and immediately actionable report. It costs two minutes and converts a map feature into a character.
- She is a natural casualty for a gith scout ambush outside the walls — the band's first death, offscreen, discovered rather than witnessed.
- If the campaign wants the leak question raised, Ara raising it is cleaner than the party working it out.
