## GM-Only Secrets
- **The module gave her a full stat block and no plot resolution.** In a chapter where [[Freiha]], [[Gatlakk]] and [[Casskka]] get nothing but a name and a sentence, **Lodgden gets AC, hit points, ability scores, psionics and a wild talent.** That is the designers signalling that she is meant to be met, fought, or hired. **The GM has to decide which.**
- **"Little success" over months is the tell.** She is a level 8 professional with unrestricted access to two sleeping targets and a stockroom. **She could have finished this in a week.** The most interesting reading is that **she does not want to** — that she took the job, discovered she liked the people, and has been running out the clock. **Lawful Neutral supports it: she will not break the contract, but she will not hurry it either.**
- **She is the best recruit in New Giustenal.** A skilled rogue with no loyalty to the templarate, no religious investment in Dregoth, an alignment that makes her reliable once given a word, and a wild talent for **appraisal** — which is precisely the skill a party looting a dead sorcerer-king's city needs most. **Turning her is worth more than saving the Pit.**
- **Appraisal is a strange talent to give a hired killer**, and it fits a *recipe thief* perfectly. **She was hired to value and steal a process, not to murder people** — and the murder clause may be Casskka's temper rather than the job she agreed to. See [[Casskka]].
- **Casskka's impatience is what will force the issue.** Left alone, Lodgden would probably stall forever. **The party's arrival, or one more prodding message, is what makes something happen.**
- **She is also a clean way for the party to get caught.** She is the one person in New Giustenal already watching everyone in a room professionally. **She will notice strangers before anyone else does** — and what she does with that is entirely up to what she thinks of them.
- *(Proposed.)* She is not from New Giustenal originally. A level 8 rogue in a sealed city of 3,000 with no slaves and no thieves' guild has a history nobody has written.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: play her as the sympathetic one and let the party work out what she is.** She should serve them, be pleasant, and be the first person to warn them they are being watched.
- **Give the party the choice of turning her rather than fighting her.** She is Lawful — a better offer, honestly made, is a legitimate route.
- Make Casskka's pressure the trigger. Something happens in the Pit because the client got impatient, not because the party rolled a check.
- Use *appraisal* as her ongoing value to the party if recruited. It solves the "what is this thing worth" problem for the rest of the campaign.
- Decide where she came from.
