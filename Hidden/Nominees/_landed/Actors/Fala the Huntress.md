# Actor: Fala the Huntress

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-fala`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The Huntress — an elven ranger who is not a member of the band, feeds it anyway, lives very comfortably off the arrangement, and will fight the gith for exactly one reason: she does not want to lose her best customer.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: active
- Faction or allegiance: **none** — independent contractor to `faction-tenpugs-band`
- Current location: the Crescent Forest; visits `location-tenpugs-temple`
- Current route, when traveling: forest → dune screen → temple, on her own schedule
- Role: Hunter and meat supplier

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
A tall elf in studded leather that is noticeably better maintained than anything in the camp — she can afford it and wants that noticed. Bow, spear, and a bronze dagger, all kept clean. She moves through the temple like a guest who knows she is welcome.

Physically striking and entirely aware of it.

### Manner and Voice
Loud, funny, and openly flirtatious. Fala flirts with attractive men as a matter of course, and her taste is specific and unsubtle: big muscles, little brains. She is friendly to everyone and close to no one.

She talks about her own kills at length. She is very good, the stories are true, and she tells them one time too many, every time.

### Public Reputation
Well trusted and well liked, with a hard edge to it — her ego has genuinely made her a few enemies in the camp. She is the only regular visitor who is not a member, and the band's tolerance of that is a measure of how much they need the meat.

## Confirmed Facts
- Elven ranger. **Not a member of Tenpug's Band** — one of very few outsiders who visits the camp at all.
- Hunts for the band and trades her kills for a variety of goods, which keeps her living quite comfortably in the Crescent Forest.
- Very friendly and well trusted.
- Very flirtatious with attractive men; the printed source defines her taste as big muscles and little brains.
- Very good at what she does, and her ego has made her a few enemies in the camp.
- Does not wish to fight in any war against the gith — **but will if asked.**
- Her stated reason is self-interest: she could not live nearly as well without the band, and wants it still there to trade with.

## Traits and Pressures
- Ambition: Moderate, and almost entirely about reputation rather than gain.
- Caution: Moderate (3) — competent in the field, careless socially.
- Secrecy: 1. She says what she thinks, at volume.
- Loyalty: Contractual and honest about being contractual, which the band finds refreshing and occasionally infuriating.
- Cruelty: None. She kills animals for a living and takes no pleasure in killing anything else.
- Risk tolerance: High in the field, low about commitments.
- Safety: Good. She is the second-most capable combatant available to the band and has the least reason to be there.
- Wealth: The best-off individual in the story — she lives comfortably, alone, in a forest, on Athas.
- Status: Welcome guest, permanent outsider, and entirely comfortable with the distinction.

## Resources and Capabilities
- Ranger fieldcraft: tracking, stealth, survival, and navigation across the Crescent Forest and the sandy wastes between.
- The band's meat supply — a materially significant part of how two hundred people eat.
- Genuine combat capability with bow and spear at range, which the band has almost none of.
- Independent mobility. She is not besieged, not committed, and can leave whenever she chooses.
- Knowledge of the Crescent Forest, including the routes the band's carpenters use for timber.

## Current Activity
Working the Crescent Forest and the approaches, bringing in what she can, and calculating how much longer the trade is going to be worth the risk.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the answer to the carpenters problem.** Adventure One hinges on whether the band's carpenters and engineers — about thirty people, including its only construction expertise — can get home through the gith patrol screen. Fala knows the forest, knows the patrols, and is not besieged. A party that thinks to ask her has a route. The module never points this out.
- **She will not volunteer.** She has to be asked, directly, and the printed source is explicit that she will say yes when she is. Players who treat her as scenery lose one of the best assets in the adventure.
- **Her self-interest is genuine and so is she.** Do not play the mercenary framing as a betrayal setup. She says exactly what she is and then does exactly that, which on Athas makes her more reliable than most of the people who claim otherwise.
- If the band is destroyed, she survives, moves on, and finds another buyer. That is the honest outcome and it should be allowed to sting.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** she is the natural guide for any PC sortie — the raids on the gith camp, the obsidian quarry run, the sink worm expedition, and above all the carpenter extraction.
- The Arcus running joke is worth playing and worth eventually subverting; she is genuinely fond of him and the camp's amusement is at both their expense.
- If the campaign wants the Crescent Forest developed later, she is the resident expert already on the board.
- A good late beat: she stays for the second battle without being asked, and does not explain why, and does not want it mentioned.

- **Running her at the table:** she is loud, capable, and self-interested, and she should never be punished for the last of those. The design intent is that a party who engages her socially gains a solution to a logistics problem they may not have realised they had.
