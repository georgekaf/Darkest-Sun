# Actor: Mefse

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-mefse`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
A large, jovial, soft-faced man whom people keep mistaking for slow — a fighter-cleric with genuinely deep insight, a placid expression he keeps even when facing his own death, and a considered philosophical argument for refusing to tell you anything.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
**A large, round-faced, jovial fellow** with a soft, clean-shaven face that **has fooled some into thinking he is slow or harmless.**

Banded armour, a steel long sword and a wrist razor, and a Strength long bow with seven sheaf arrows +2. He wears a *girdle of many pouches* and a *ring of x-ray vision*, and carries an empty iron flask.

His rooms are a study in contradiction: one is in complete disarray — unfolded clothes, half-eaten food, blankets piled by a cot, a stone chest gaping open with a disorderly heap inside. The other is **completely bare and clean, and every noise echoes in it.** He meditates there once per day.

### Manner and Voice
Placid, unhurried and rarely talkative. **Since he is not talkative or well-spoken, he strikes some as a mindless fellow, but his insight into life is actually quite deep — he is simply slow to share his wisdom.**

**Even when facing combat or his own death, Mefse's expression remains placid.**

Pressed to comment on the Square, he gives what is probably the best-written speech in the module:

> *"People are born pretty much alike. But as one travels any path of wisdom, ever he moves further away from this common ground. You are experienced people, I am experienced, but we have had different experiences, so we share little common ground. What I could say would only interest you if you misunderstood it. Please allow me the privilege of silence."*

### Public Reputation
Underestimated by outsiders, valued within the Square, and entirely uninterested in either.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Human male fighter 3 / earth cleric 6**, chaotic neutral. Strength 18/76, Dexterity 17, Wisdom 17.
- Equipment: banded armour, Strength long bow, seven sheaf arrows +2, **ring of x-ray vision**, **girdle of many pouches**, an **empty iron flask**, and a *fruit of speed*.
- Cleric spells to 3rd level: animal friendship, cure light wounds ×3, detect magic, aid, dust devil, hold person, obscurement, wyvern watch, dispel magic, magical vestment, spike growth.
- **Combat routine, as printed:** eats his *fruit of speed*, casts *aid* on himself, then attacks. **He prefers to open by casting *hold person* and then using his bow.** If he needs to escape he uses *obscurement* and *spike growth*.
- **He is more concerned about his daily devotions and peace of mind than about political or military conflict.**
- **He follows Slate, considering his service to be part of his priestly duty**, and he believes the gith to be the rightful heirs of glory — **but it is only Slate's leadership that really keeps him involved.**
- His chest in area 4 contains **a peridot (75 sp)** mixed in among worthless personal items.
- Like the rest of the Square: recruited by charm, initiated by Gurdek, insane behind a maintained facade of normality, and **resistant to mind reading**.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is the Square's least mad member and its most conditional.** Everyone else has a reason to be in Yathazor — Slate has the theory, Ari Evan has the ambition, Pargo has the devotion, Ranza has nowhere else. Mefse has *Slate*. If Slate falls or flees, Mefse is the member most likely to simply stop.
- **The ring of x-ray vision is a scouting problem the party will not anticipate.** He can see through the Refuge's stone-shaped seals and walls. Combined with [[Pargo]]'s shadow-form sweeps and [[Ranza]]'s perimeter checks, the Refuge has three independent detection layers, and this is the one with no tell at all.
- **His speech is a genuine refusal, not a bluff.** He is not hiding information; he has concluded that explaining himself to strangers is pointless. Do not let players "crack" it with a Charisma check. Let it stand — it characterises the whole Square better than any secret would.
- **Two rooms, one filthy and one immaculate.** That is the character in a single image: a man who does not care about his life at all and cares enormously about one hour of it. Show the players both rooms.
- *(Proposed.)* He knows the theory is thin. He has decided that the peace it buys him is worth more than the truth, and he would say so if asked in the right way — which is the only interesting thing anyone could get out of him.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** if Slate flees at half hit points as written, have Mefse notice, and stop fighting. A member of an ambushing cult simply lowering his bow is a better beat than another round of combat.
- Use the ring of x-ray vision to defeat one clever party plan, then let them find it on his body and understand what happened.
- If the campaign wants a survivor of the Square who is neither villain nor wreckage, Mefse is the only candidate.
