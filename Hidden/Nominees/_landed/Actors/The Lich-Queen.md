# Actor: The Githyanki Lich-Queen

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-githyanki-lich-queen`. **Unnamed in the source — she is only ever "the lich-queen." Offscreen throughout Black Spine, and never encountered.**)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The supreme ruler of githyanki society — a cruel demi-goddess who eats her own subjects when they become too powerful, whose image nobody is permitted to display, and whom Trinth intends to overthrow with an army bred on stolen Athasian minds.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc — **world-power, offscreen**
- Control: **not playable.** She does not appear in Black Spine at any point.
- Status: active, elsewhere
- Faction or allegiance: githyanki society entire — its supreme ruler
- Current location: unstated. Somewhere on the Astral Plane.
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Supreme ruler of the githyanki; demi-goddess

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
**Nobody knows.** The one image of her the party will ever see is explicitly wrong:

> *"In the room is a terrible marble statue of the githyanki's mysterious lich-queen. Since Overseer Nlakni had never seen the lich-queen, and the queen does not want her image displayed where others can defile it, this statue does not actually look like her — it is only the likeness of a tall, thin, fierce woman. The significance of the hood over her head without eyes is known only to the ancient githyanki."*

A ten-foot marble figure of **a hooded woman holding manacles before her**, the hood having **no eye slits.**

### Manner and Voice
No dialogue anywhere in the campaign. What survives is [[Nlakni]]'s *magic mouth*, cast in her name millennia ago:

> *"Beware, mortal, the wrath of the Queen. I shall bind my enemies and feed them to the Blind Dark."*

The module notes this **represents some insight that Nlakni had into the mysteries of the lich-queen.**

### Public Reputation
Absolute among the githyanki. **Her knights are evil champions who take up her causes and are very powerful and highly revered.** Beyond her own race, she is a rumour.

## Confirmed Facts
- **The supreme ruler of githyanki society.**
- **A cruel demi-goddess.**
- **When a githyanki advances to 9th level, he is tested by the lich-queen.** The test **involves survival in one of the lower planes for a number of weeks. Failure quite obviously results in death.**
- **Githyanki who reach 12th level of experience are immediately drawn out of the Astral Plane and into the presence of the lich-queen, where their life force is drawn to feed the ravenous hunger of the cruel demi-goddess.**
- **There has never been a githyanki that has progressed beyond the 11th level of experience, and very few progress beyond 9th.** *(This is why [[Haza]], at 12th level, is remarkable — and why he may be on borrowed time.)*
- **Githyanki knights are evil champions who take up her causes**, and have the powers of paladins turned toward evil.
- **Failure to recover a special silver sword that falls into non-githyanki hands surely means instant death to all the githyanki involved, at the hands of their merciless lich-queen.**
- **She does not want her image displayed where others can defile it.**
- **The four githyanki political prisoners in Galnaki are her agents**, held captive by Trinth **so that word of the Athasian conquest does not spread.**
- **[[Queen Trinth]] intends to overthrow her.** With an army of githyanki psionicists bred from what she learns on Athas, **Trinth would return to the Astral Plane, drive out the lich-queen, and claim the entire realm for herself.**
- **Trinth's plan is not known to her followers. To almost all of them, the idea of any githyanki attacking another is obscene.** Only the corrupting influence of **Yrinolir** enabled Trinth to think of such a thing.
- **"The Blind Dark"** appears twice: in Nlakni's *magic mouth*, and carved over the entrance archway to Yathazor — ***"The Blind Dark is beneath you."*** The module says the meaning is unclear, **as this phrase refers to an obscure mystic aspect of the githyanki's adoration of their lich-queen.**

## Goals
Unstated. Nothing in Black Spine attributes a goal to her — she is a condition of githyanki existence rather than an actor with intentions.

The only behaviours on record are **consumption** (12th-level githyanki), **testing** (9th-level githyanki), and **retribution** (lost silver swords).

Not stated in Black Spine. What is implied:

## GM-Only Secrets
- **She is the campaign's horizon, not its villain.** Black Spine ends with Trinth dead and the Gate destroyed — **and the lich-queen still there, unaware, undisturbed, and infinitely more dangerous than the queen the party just killed.** If the campaign continues past Adventure Seven, she is what is on the other side of it.
- **The level cap is her.** *No githyanki has ever progressed beyond 11th level, and very few beyond 9th.* Not because they cannot, but because **she eats them at 12.** That is a horror worth making explicit at the table: an entire species held below a ceiling by its own god.
- **"The Blind Dark" is the campaign's best unexplained phrase**, appearing twice in Yathazor and never resolved. Nlakni threatened to *bind my enemies and feed them to the Blind Dark*, and the archway says it *is beneath you.* The module explicitly declines to explain. **See [[Nlakni]] — recommended that the campaign also decline, at least until it wants something very bad.**
- **The four political prisoners are a live thread the party can pull.** Trinth is holding the lich-queen's own agents in her dungeon **specifically so that word of Athas does not spread.** If the party frees them — and the module says they will expect to take command, will treat the PCs as inferior slaves, and will sacrifice them freely — **the news reaches the lich-queen.** That is a consequence the module never scores.
- **Trinth's treason is obscene to her own people.** Any githyanki NPC who learns what Trinth actually intends has a crisis. **This is a lever on [[Raskon]], [[Trasrani]], and every githyanki in Galnaki**, and it is the one thing the party could say that would be believed.
- *(Proposed.)* Nothing in the campaign requires the lich-queen to be hostile to the party. She would regard Trinth as a traitor, the Nightmare Gate as an unsanctioned adventure, and the destruction of both as a service. **An alliance is available and would be appalling.**

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** keep her entirely offstage. Her value in Black Spine is as the thing standing behind the final boss, and she loses that the moment she is statted.
- Use the level cap once, out loud, when someone examines Haza — the party should understand that githyanki have a ceiling and what enforces it.
- The freed political prisoners are the natural bridge if the campaign wants a sequel. Whoever they tell, and what she does about it, is a whole further arc.
- **"The Blind Dark" should be said three times across the campaign and explained zero times.**

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
**None, and deliberately so. The lich-queen does not appear in Black Spine in any form.**

The only mechanics attached to her are demographic constraints on her own race:

- **9th level:** a githyanki is tested by the lich-queen — survival in one of the lower planes for several weeks. **Failure means death.**
- **12th level:** the githyanki is **immediately drawn out of the Astral Plane into her presence and their life force consumed.** No save is described. This is not a fight; it is a fact.
- **Effective githyanki level cap: 11**, with very few above 9.
- **Silver sword doctrine:** githyanki will never willingly allow a silver sword to fall into non-githyanki hands. If a special silver sword is lost, **powerful raiding parties are sent to recover it, and failure means instant death to all the githyanki involved.**
- **Her knights:** 9th-level evil champions with all the powers of paladins turned toward evil — detect good instead of detect evil, command undead instead of turning undead. A stronghold keeps **20–80 of them** as fanatically loyal shock troops.
- **If the campaign ever does stat her**, she should sit above [[Queen Trinth]]'s world-power tier by a clear margin. Trinth at hp 102 and 200 PSPs is a queen. This is what queens are afraid of.
