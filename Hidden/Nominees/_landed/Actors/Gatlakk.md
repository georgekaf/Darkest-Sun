## GM-Only Secrets
- **The only meaningful GM note about Gatlakk is that he does not exist yet.** The module needed a couple to make the Pit a domestic story and never wrote the second half. **He is a name on a marriage and a business.**
- **One decision makes him real, and it is a mechanically important one: does he know the recipe?**
  - **If both know it**, Lodgden can complete her contract by turning or killing either one — **two targets, one job**, and the party has two people to protect.
  - **If only Freiha knows it**, Gatlakk is leverage rather than a target, and the printed *"send them to the Gray"* becomes a threat to be used against her. **This is the more dramatic reading and the module's phrasing supports it either way.**
- **He is not on the friends list and Freiha is.** That asymmetry is almost certainly an oversight, but it can be played: **he may be the one who distrusts the strangers** while his mate is charmed by them.
- *(Proposed.)* Suggested splits, any of which the printed material supports:
  - **The floor and the still.** One works the room; the other never leaves the back where the mushrooms ferment. **This is the most useful split** — it decides where Lodgden has to get to, and which of the two the party actually meets.
  - **The optimist and the worrier.** Someone in that building has noticed the new server asking questions.
  - **The dray and the older dray.** Second generation dray are Dregoth's "perfected" work; a couple spanning the change would be a quiet way to show the party what the first generation lost.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: adopt the floor/still split**, and put the munge secret with whichever of the two the party is less likely to protect.
- Give him one physical distinguishing feature. The party needs to tell the owners apart at a glance.
- Decide whether he is a target or a hostage. Everything else about the Pit plot follows from that.
- Consider putting him on the potential-friends list alongside Freiha, or deliberately not — a divided household is more interesting than a united one.
