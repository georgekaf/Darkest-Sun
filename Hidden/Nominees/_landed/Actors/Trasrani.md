# Actor: Trasrani

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-trasrani`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The githyanki captain Raskon blamed for the Nightmare Gate, sitting unchained in a cell he knows he did not earn, who will travel with the party for a while and then sell them to buy his way back.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: autonomous
- Status: **captive** — collared but not chained
- Faction or allegiance: `faction-trinth-githyanki` — **in disgrace, and trying to get back in**
- Current location: the Galnaki prison complex, area 7
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Githyanki captain; scapegoat; the party's designated traitor

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
> *"This cell holds a single githyanki prisoner. He is not chained, although he does have a steel collar on his neck. An air of despondency hangs over the slender, elf-like figure as he sits crosslegged on the floor of his cell. It is hard to tell if he is awake or in some sort of trance."*

Note the detail: **unchained.** Every other prisoner of consequence in the block is manacled to a wall. He is not.

### Manner and Voice
Despondent, controlled, and privately contemptuous. **In his eyes the characters are nothing more than filthy barbarians**, and everything he says to them is filtered through that.

**He guards what he says very carefully and may even lie** to avoid revealing anything genuinely important.

### Public Reputation
Within Galnaki: the captain who lost the Gate. **He recognises that he has been unfairly imprisoned**, and so, probably, does everyone else — which is a different kind of problem.

## Confirmed Facts
- **A githyanki captain.** Statistics as the module's githyanki captain line: THAC0 17, AC 2 (chain mail +3), HD 7, hp 45, INT Genius, ML 14, PSPs 180, all attack and defence modes, plus spells to 3rd level.
- **He is the captain that [[Raskon]] blamed for the damage to the Nightmare Gate on Athas.**
- **Trasrani recognises that he has been unfairly imprisoned** and **looks upon the characters as a chance to win his way back into the good graces of his leaders.**
- **If the adventurers release him, Trasrani will travel with them for a time and then betray them.**
- **If it looks as though he will be left behind, he will attempt to buy his way out of the cell with information.**
- **He knows a great deal about the layout of the dungeon complex, the city above, and the catacombs in between.**
- **He will guard what he says very carefully and may even lie to avoid revealing any truly important information.**
- He is **unchained**, unlike the more powerful prisoners.

## Current Activity
Sitting cross-legged on a cell floor, possibly meditating, possibly waiting, calculating what a group of escaping barbarians is worth in restored rank.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is a betrayal on a fuse, and the module states it flatly:** release him and *he will travel with them for a time and then betray them.* **The interesting part is that he is also the best guide available.** He knows the dungeon, the catacombs and the city. The party's choice is between the most useful ally in Book Three and a knife that has already been scheduled.
- **The party broke the Gate and he was blamed for it.** They are the reason he is in this cell, and he does not know it. **If they tell him — or if he works it out — the betrayal stops being opportunistic and becomes personal.** Recommended: let a player realise this mid-escape.
- **His information is real and he will sell it rather than be abandoned.** A party that leaves him locked up still gets the dungeon layout, because being left behind is worse than being useful. **The safe play is available and most parties will not take it.**
- **He is unchained.** In a block where Ela, Bardak and the PCs are manacled to walls, the githyanki captain sits loose in his cell. That is either an oversight or a courtesy extended to one of their own, and either reading is worth a player noticing.
- **He and [[Krika]] cannot coexist.** If the party takes Trasrani up the shaft and Krika is waiting at the top, one of them dies or the escape collapses. **The module runs these as separate options and never considers them together.** A party that has both is holding a live grenade.
- *(Proposed.)* He may be innocent in a more interesting way than he claims — the Gate was struck by a stray spell from one of the gish, or by a PC, and Raskon needed someone to blame in front of the queen. Trasrani was available.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** foreground the choice. Make his knowledge visibly valuable — have the party get lost once before they reach his cell — so that releasing him is genuinely tempting rather than obviously stupid.
- Time the betrayal for maximum inconvenience: not in a fight, but at the moment the party needs to move quietly through Galnaki.
- If Raskine is in play instead of Raskon, the accusation may be revisitable, and Trasrani could be turned rather than merely used.
- Putting him in a room with Krika is the sharpest scene available in the escape sequence.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: Fighter / Wizard (githyanki captain)
- Level: 7
- Armor Class: 10 while imprisoned — **18 (chain mail +3) once he recovers equipment**
- Hit Points: 45
- Movement: near
- Strength +2, Dexterity +3, Constitution +2, Intelligence **+4**, Wisdom +2, Charisma +2
- Alignment: Chaotic (evil) — printed NE
- Morale: 14
- Attacks: 2 attacks per round, once armed.
  - *Long sword +1* +8, 1d8+3
- **Psionics (180 PSP):** complete healing, detonate, project force; adrenalin control, animate shadow, catfall, control body, control light, displacement, heightened senses, inertial barrier, levitation, molecular manipulation. **All attack and defence modes.** **Suppressed by the collar.**
- **Spells:** charm person, colour spray, magic missile ×2, invisibility, stinking cloud, lightning bolt.
- **Plane Shift** at will — **suppressed by the collar. If the collar comes off and he is left alone for a round, he is gone.**
- **Guarded Tongue:** any information he gives has a chance of being incomplete or false. The GM should decide in advance which pieces are true rather than rolling for it.
- **Scheduled Betrayal:** he travels with the party for a time and then betrays them. **Pick the moment in advance.**
- **Running him at the table:** courteous, useful, and visibly contemptuous. He should be helpful enough that the players start relying on him, and never once warm.
- **Conversion note:** the module gives Trasrani no personal stat block — only the description and behaviour. The block above uses the printed **Githyanki Captain** line (THAC0 17, AC 2 chain mail +3, HD 7, hp 45, MV 12, INT Genius, AL NE, ML 14, XP 1000, PSPs 180, Score 18) from the same section.
