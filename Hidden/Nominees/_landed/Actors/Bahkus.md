# Actor: Captain Bahkus

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-bahkus`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The githzerai captain who lost her entire command to something she could not fight, and who has spent the days since sitting at her desk with a tripwire looped around her fingers, waiting for it to come and eat her.

## Classification
- Subtype: named-npc
- Control: **catatonic** — she does not act until touched
- Status: **alive, and broken**
- Faction or allegiance: the githzerai; no faction record
- Current location: the githzerai asteroid base, area 5 — the Captain's Quarters
- Current route, when traveling: —
- Role: Commander of the githzerai outpost

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
> *"A single githzerai soldier sits at the desk with a blank expression on her face. She stares straight ahead; unblinking and seemingly unaware of anything that transpires."*

Her room is **well appointed but spartan** — a wooden desk, a comfortable bed, carefully crafted furniture. **A silk standard bearing an ornate crest hangs on the wall between two shelves** holding books, bottles of wine and keepsakes.

**Half a dozen figurines of githzerai warriors stand along the front edge of the desk, each apparently an award for some outstanding deed.**

**A close examination reveals a thin cord looped around the fingers of her left hand.**

### Manner and Voice
Nothing. She is **almost comatose** and has been for days.

**If anyone touches her she does two things. First, she screams in absolute terror. Then she releases the thread.**

### Public Reputation
Among the githzerai: the captain who commanded this outpost, decorated six times over. Among the party: an unmoving woman at a desk in a base full of corpses.

## Confirmed Facts
- **Bahkus, the captain who commanded this place.** Female githzerai.
- **It was Bahkus who entrusted [[Krika]] with the mission of infiltrating the githyanki base.** Krika identifies her on sight.
- **She is almost comatose and has been sitting in her seat for several days, waiting for the beholder to come and devour her.**
- **As a last act of defiance, Bahkus has rigged a trap to destroy the creature that shattered her command.**
- **A thin cord is looped around the fingers of her left hand.** **If anyone touches her, she screams in absolute terror and then releases the thread, setting off the trap.**
- **The trap:** the released cord tips one of the figurines off the desk. As it falls, **an almost invisible cord is yanked tight and a large glass bottle topples off the shelves.** When the bottle hits the floor it shatters, and **the liquid inside ignites on contact with air, filling the entire room with flames.**
- **The detonation is almost exactly a *fireball* cast by a 10th-level wizard — 10d6 damage**, filling **not only Bahkus's office but also the officers' barracks (area 4) and the warriors' barracks (area 6).** Save vs breath weapons for half.
- **Unlike a true fireball, the flames burn longer.** On the round after the detonation the flames withdraw from the warriors' barracks **but still do full damage in areas 4 and 5.** On the second round after, **they are still burning in area 5.** **A new saving throw is required for every character in an affected area, every round.**
- **After the fire dies down there is little but smoking debris in areas 4, 5 and 6.**
- **The trap will also be set off if the cord is cut, broken or disturbed** — not only by touching her.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **The trap will almost certainly kill the party, not the beholder.** She rigged it for a creature that has not come. **The people who will actually walk through that door are the PCs and Krika** — and the trigger is *touching her*, which is exactly what a party finding a catatonic survivor will do. **This is one of the nastiest traps in the campaign and it is entirely sympathetic in origin.**
- **It is also findable.** A close examination reveals the cord around her fingers. **Give the players the chance.** The scene should reward looking before touching, and it is a genuinely fair test.
- **She is the only living witness and she cannot speak.** Krika spends the whole base assuming githyanki did this. The one person who knows better is sitting right there, six feet from the answer, unable to say it. **If the party can restore her — psionically, magically, or by patience — they short-circuit the entire mystery.** The module offers no method and does not consider the possibility.
- **The three-room burn is the real danger.** 10d6 in her office *and* both barracks, then two more rounds of burning with fresh saves. **A party spread out looting bodies in areas 4 and 6 when someone touches her in area 5 will take the full sequence in three separate rooms.** This interacts directly with Krika's objection to looting — **a party that respects her wishes and stays together is measurably safer.**
- **She is a mirror of Krika.** Both were given a job by the githzerai; one carried it out and came home to ruins, the other sat in the ruins and built a bomb. **Play them against each other when Krika identifies her.**
- *(Proposed.)* She has not eaten or drunk in days. Whatever the party does, she is dying anyway unless they intervene.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** describe the room fully — the standard, the figurines, the wine, the six commendations — before anyone gets close enough to touch her. The trap is fair only if the players have been given the room.
- Let Krika react first and badly. She will go straight to her captain.
- **Provide a route to restoring her**, even a hard one. She is the answer to the base's central question and the module wastes her.
- The six figurines are a lootable, worthless, characterful object. Somebody should notice what they are.

- Class: Fighter (githzerai captain)
- Level: 6
- Armor Class: 15
- Hit Points: 34
- Movement: near
- Strength +2, Dexterity +2, Constitution +1, Intelligence +2, Wisdom +2, Charisma +2
- Alignment: Neutral — githzerai are chaotic neutral as a race
- Morale: **—. She is catatonic and will not act, fight, flee, or defend herself.**
- Magic resistance: **50%** *(githzerai racial)*
- Attacks: **none.** She does not fight. If attacked, she does not respond.
- **Catatonic:** unresponsive to speech, light, movement or injury. **The only stimulus she reacts to is being touched — and that reaction is the trap.**
- **The Trigger:** touching Bahkus, or cutting, breaking or disturbing the cord, releases it.
  - **Round 0:** she screams; the figurine falls; the bottle shatters. **10d6 fire damage in areas 4, 5 and 6.** Save for half.
  - **Round 1:** flames withdraw from area 6. **Full damage again in areas 4 and 5.** New save.
  - **Round 2:** flames still burning in **area 5.** New save.
  - Afterwards: smoking debris in areas 4, 5 and 6.
- **Plane Shift** at will *(githzerai racial)* — **unused. She has not left, and could have at any time.**
- **Running her at the table:** an unmoving woman in a chair, in a room full of her own commendations, in a base full of her dead. Let the players decide how long to look before they reach out.
- **Conversion note:** the module gives Bahkus **no stat block** — only her rank, her condition and her trap. The block above is constructed from the githzerai racial entry reprinted in Book Three (p.82) at captain grade. The trap's damage, radius and duration are printed exactly as given.
