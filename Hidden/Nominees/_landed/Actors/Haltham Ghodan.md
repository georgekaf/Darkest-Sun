## GM-Only Secrets
- **The only meaningful GM note about Haltham is that he does not exist yet.** The module needed two dwarves to make "brothers" work and never wrote the second one. **He is a name on a business.**
- **That is worth fixing at the table, cheaply.** The party will meet the Ghodans as a unit and will immediately want to know which one they are talking to. **One distinguishing line each is enough and it should be decided before first contact.** Suggested splits, any of which the printed material supports:
  - **The road and the works.** One runs caravans and hires the guards; the other never leaves the pit. **This is the most useful split** — it means the party deals with one brother on the escort job and the other at the site, and the two have different information.
  - **The trade and the craft.** One negotiates with House Shom and the Sky Singers; the other runs the twenty crafters.
  - **The optimist and the one who wants out.** They came from Raam on a trading trip and never went back. **One of them may want to.**
- **The slaves are the pressure point either way.** Twenty crafters and labourers, an unwalled site, and two owners. **If the brothers ever disagree about anything, it will be about them.**
- *(Proposed.)* If the campaign is running the dwarven focus thread — [[Roi]], [[Pimns]], [[Passk]] — give the brothers a **shared** focus. Two dwarves bound to one unfinished work is a stronger version of the rule than any individual case in the campaign so far, and it makes the works impossible for either of them to abandon.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended: adopt the road/works split.** It is free, it makes both brothers usable, and it gives the escort job a face distinct from the site.
- Give him one physical distinguishing feature. The party needs to tell them apart at a glance.
- The shared-focus idea makes the pair genuinely memorable and ties them into a thread the campaign is already running across three other dwarves.
