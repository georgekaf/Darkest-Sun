# Actor: Jolon

*(New actor. Proposed id `actor-jolon`.)*

## One-Sentence Summary
The Keeper of Wages — a grumpy, stingy, self-important clerk who is also scrupulously fair, holds every coin the band owns, and is the only person in camp doing the arithmetic on whether two hundred craftsmen can survive a war.

## Player-Safe Description

### Appearance
An unremarkable human man in padded armour he wears indoors, which nobody else does, because he considers himself a custodian of valuables and dresses for the role. Neat where the rest of the camp is dusty. A bone dagger at the belt that he is visibly unqualified to use.

He carries a tally-board and consults it during conversations that do not require it.

### Manner and Voice
Officious, clipped, and faintly aggrieved. Jolon announces things rather than saying them, and prefaces his opinions with reminders of his office. He is the kind of man who says *as Keeper of Wages, I must point out* in a room of eleven people who have known him for twenty years.

Under the pomposity he is precise, patient with figures, and completely honest. He will argue with you for an hour and then give you exactly what you are owed, to the ceramic bit.

### Public Reputation
Disliked and entirely trusted, which is a rare and useful combination. Nobody enjoys dealing with him. Nobody has ever suggested anyone else hold the money.

## Confirmed Facts
- Human, 0 level. Was a household slave who managed his master's finances in a major noble house.
- His master's confidence rested on the fact that a slave cannot spend money without attracting attention — so there was little worry he would embezzle. He learned a great deal about handling large sums.
- One of the original band that escaped with Tenpug when the artisan column was ambushed in the Crescent Forest. A founding member.
- Collects all wages earned by artisans working abroad and distributes them so the band can buy supplies.
- Considered grumpy and stingy, and is **extremely fair**. The source is explicit on both halves.
- Carries himself with self-importance far exceeding his actual position.
- Believes a handful of artisans and craftsmen cannot possibly hold out against a gith war band, adventurers or no adventurers.
- Wants to bribe the gith, or take what the band can carry and run.
- Principal debater in the first meeting tent, arguing for flight.
- Cannot offer any answer to *where the band would go*, and knows it. This is the weakness that loses him the debate.

## Resources and Capabilities
- **The band's entire liquid wealth.** In a genuine pinch, Tenpug's people can raise 1,000 sp, and Jolon is the man who knows exactly how, from where, and what it costs them.
- Real financial competence learned inside a noble house — pricing, ledgers, distributions, and the ability to spot when a merchant is lying.
- Complete knowledge of what the band can and cannot afford, which is the actual constraint on every plan the PCs propose.
- Institutional memory: he was there at the ambush in the Crescent Forest.

## Current Activity
Holding the first meeting tent against Danya's now-vacant chair, arguing for flight, and being asked the one question he cannot answer.

## GM-Only Secrets
- **He is right.** This is the important thing about Jolon. Two hundred craftsmen with ten combat veterans cannot beat three hundred organised gith with steel weapons, and the module agrees — the band loses the first battle as written. Jolon is the voice of correct analysis, and the adventure requires him to lose the argument anyway.
- **He is also wrong about the bribe.** The gith will not take one. Teva has already worked this out; Jolon has not, because it is the only part of his plan that is not arithmetic.
- **His self-importance is armour.** He was a slave whose entire worth was that he could be trusted with money he was structurally unable to steal. The pomposity is a man insisting he is more than a safe with legs, to an audience who have never once suggested otherwise.
- If the party treats him as a serious adviser rather than an obstacle, he becomes the single most useful NPC in Adventure One — he can cost out every plan they propose, and he wants very badly to be asked.
- **He does not fight.** Five hit points. If the temple is breached and Jolon is in it, he dies, and the ledgers go with him unless someone thought about that in advance.

## Proposed Developments
- **Recommended:** let the PCs win the tent-8 debate against him honestly, and then have him immediately and without resentment start costing out the war they voted for. That turn is the whole character.
- His flight plan has a real gap — no destination. A party that supplies one changes the adventure substantially and legitimately.
- The ledgers are a physical object worth protecting during the siege. Nobody in the module thinks of this. A GM can.
- If the band survives and later has to deal with Nibenay over the iron mine, Jolon is the only person in the camp who can negotiate a contract.

## Stat Block or Rules Notes
- Class: none — 0-level functionary
- Level: 0
- Armor Class: 12 (padded)
- Hit Points: 5
- Movement: near
- Strength +1, Dexterity +2, Constitution +0, Intelligence +2, Wisdom +3, Charisma −1
- Alignment: Lawful (good)
- Morale: 4 — he will flee, immediately, and he has a written plan for it
- Attacks: 1 attack per round.
  - *Bone dagger* +0, 1d4−1 (he is worse with it than an untrained man would be; the source applies an explicit attack penalty)
- **Keeper of Wages:** knows the exact value, provenance and availability of every coin, trade good and obligation the band holds. Any plan that costs money should be run past him, in character, and he will have a number.
- **Ledger Mind:** advantage on checks to appraise, detect a false price, recall a transaction, or calculate logistics — including the food-and-water arithmetic for a march.
- **Non-combatant:** flatly. Do not put him in the battle line. The module gives him five hit points for a reason.
- Not a spellcaster. No psionic talent.
- **Running him at the table:** he interrupts, he cites his office, and he is never once wrong about a number. Play the pomposity for comedy and the arithmetic completely straight, so that when the players realise he has been correct the whole time, it lands.
- **Conversion note:** printed 2e stats are Human, 0 level, Wis 17, Cha 8, hp 5, AC 8 (padded), THAC0 20, bone dagger 1d4−1 with −1 to hit. Kept at level 0.
