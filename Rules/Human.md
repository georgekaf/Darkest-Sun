title: "Human"
icon: "scroll-old"
---

Humans are adaptable, resourceful survivors thriving across Athas, whether in oppressive city-states or nomadic desert tribes. Ambitious and pragmatic, they learn quickly, excel at trade, and possess a deep capacity for innovation or cruelty, shaped by Athas’ harsh environment. Humans often lead struggles against tyranny or oppression, yet just as commonly enforce it, creating vast social diversity. They are versatile enough to assume nearly any role, from gladiators and merchants to sorcerers or templars.

---

> **Languages:** Common and one additional common language.

> **Ambitious.** Humans can make an additional talent roll at 1st level.

> **Diplomats.** You may reroll a reaction check when dealing with creatures you can understand. You know one additional common or rare language.
