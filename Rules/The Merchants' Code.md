# The Merchants' Code

1. I will deal with strangers, friends, and foes alike, as any deal is a deal.
2. A deal is sacred — I either pay with gold or my blood.
3. The House will always pay for my services, as any thing has a price.
4. I will uphold the laws of the city in which I am stationed.
5. I will cooperate with other Houses to bring down those who hurt, imprison, blackmail, or otherwise harass any merchant.
6. The House is my new home — the House is my new Tribe.
7. I will respect other Houses as my own.
