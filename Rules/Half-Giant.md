---
title: "Half-Giant"
icon: "scroll-old"
---

Towering and immensely strong, half-giants were magically created for servitude and war. Their impressionable personalities can cause sudden shifts in loyalty, mood, and behavior, making them unpredictable companions but terrifying survivors in the wastes.

---

> **Languages.** Common and Giant.

> **Giant’s Grip.** You can only comfortably use normal-sized or large-sized weapons and items. You can wield a two-handed melee weapon in one hand. Your melee weapon attacks that use Strength deal +2 damage.

> **Durability.** Half-Giants gain +2 Hit Points every time they reach a level.

> **Huge Body.** Due to their height, Half-Giants are forced to live and adapt to a world not suited to their size. Half-Giants must pay 100% more for armor, weapons and commodities. If there is no cover large enough to conceal you, hiding is impossible. Additionally, Half-Giants need double the amount of food and water to survive each day.
