---
title: "Downtime"
icon: "scroll-old"
---

You gain 1 **Downtime point** each time you don't participate in an adventure. 

- 1 DT = 1 XP
- 1 DT + 30 gold = 2 XP
- 1 DT + 10 gold = 1 Rumor
- 7 DT = reroll 1 talent
- 1 DT = learn a magical scroll
- 5 DT = mount riding training
- 1 DT = acquire a mount

 A fighter of level 6 or more can supervise the construction of defenses. These include ditches and pits, fields of stakes, hasty stone and wooden barricades, and even semi-permanent stone fortifications.
