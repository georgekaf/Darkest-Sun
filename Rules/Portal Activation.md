# Portal Activation

*From the one-way teleportation room below Kharanok's Obsidian Mines ([[01. Obsidian Mines AKA The Cannibal Caves]]) — reached via hidden passages from the Barracks (#9) or Storehouse (#10).*

The portal runs on Crystal Dust.

- **10 Crystal Dust** to activate the portal.
- **1 Crystal Dust** per use after that, while it stays active.
