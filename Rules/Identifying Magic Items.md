# Identifying Magic Items

*Replaces trial-and-error identification. No downtime, no sage, no gold.*

**One `detect magic` + one focus = you learn what the item does.**

- Cast **detect magic** on the item and hold **focus** on it.
- When the focus ends, you learn the item's properties — what it does and how to use it.
- One casting covers **one item**. Identifying a second item takes a second casting.

## Notes

- **Focus breaks, nothing learned.** If the focus drops early — damage, another spell, whatever — the casting is spent and the item stays a mystery. Try again with a fresh casting.
- **Cursed items lie.** A curse reads as whatever the item pretends to be. You learn the cover story, not the catch.
- **Artifacts don't answer.** Intelligent or artifact-tier items reveal only what they want revealed. See the DM.
