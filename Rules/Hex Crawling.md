Hexes are 6-miles across.

Terrain impacts the number of miles a PCs can travel per hour.

PCs can undertake tasks while traveling, and they may encounter harsh weather or points of interest.

PCs and their mounts can travel for up to 8 hours a day.

## Hex Visibility

The PCs can see the terrain and points of interest in all adjacent hexes. They can also see major terrain features, such as mountains, from any hex while their visibility is not hindered. PCs can try to enter an adjacent hex they can't see into, but they bounce off it if they don't have enough available movement.

Some conditions in the PCs' current hex can affect the visibility of surrounding hexes:

# Travel Procedure

1. **Weather. **The GM determines the weather (temperature & wind)
2. **Visibility.** The PCs make task checks in any order they choose.
3. **Travel Method.** The PCs decide their mode of travel.
4. **Travel Speed.** The PCs decide whether to move at normal speed or push.
5. **Traveling**. The PCs move, expending as much available movement as they want. Update the visible hexes as they travel.
6. **Points of Interest.** 
7. **Traveling Encounters.** Generate the hex's danger level. Check for a random encounter every other hex using the hex's danger level. Check for and trigger random encounters while the PCs travel.

### **Encounters**

Unsafe: 1 in 6 Risky: 2 in 6 Deadly: 3 in 6 

#### **Food and Water**

- PCs and mounts need one ration ped day (consumed during a rest).
- They take 1 CON damage the first day they have not consumed any rations and 1d4 CON damage any following day.
- Mounts find their rations during a rest unless in a barren location.
- A PC can forage for 1 ration a day with an INT check.

PUSHING 

At the start of the day, the PCs can choose to push. They move an additional 50% of their speed. Increase the random encounter chance to 2:6 that day. PCs can't forage on a day they push.

### **Tasks**

Each PC can attempt one task per day. PCs can do the same task. Each task effect is limited to once per day. The GM determines the DC of each check based on the situation. Default to DC 12 

**March Song.**CHA. Grant one luck token. 

**Scout.DEX.**Reduce the danger level of all hexes by one step until the next rest (minimum unsafe). 

**Lookout.**WIS. Begin encounters at a far distance. Spot one additional point of interest. 

 Arrive on target and on time. Failure means you either are late or off target.

## Hex Crawling

### TRAVEL PER DAY

Hexes are **6-miles** across. The PCs can enter a number of hexes per day based on travel method.

### MOVEMENT

PCs move from hex to hex as a group while traveling overland. Shift into combat or crawling rounds if an encounter occurs.

Check for a **random encounter** twice a day and twice overnight with a 1:6 chance each time. Roll a d12 to determine what time of day the encounters happen.

### WEATHER

Check the weather each day by rolling a d6.

On a 1, the weather is bad. Storms make normal **terrain** difficult for 1d4 days or impassible while in an extreme climate (see pg. 48).

On a 6, the weather is excellent. Roll the next weather with ADV.

### ENTERING HEXES

| Travel Method | Hexes per Day |
| --- | --- |
| Walking | 4 |
| Mounted | 6 |
| Sailing | 8* |

### TERRAIN TYPES

| Terrain | Hex Cost to Enter |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | 1 |
| Difficult | 2 |

### FOOD AND WATER

PCs and mounts must each consume one ration per day, taking 1d4 CON damage (death at 0) if they do not. A PC can **forage** for 1 ration a day during travel with an INT check.

### PUSHING

At the start of the day, the PCs can choose to **push** to multiply their hexes per day by 1.5 times. Increase the random encounter chance by 1:6 that day. PCs can't **forage** on a day they push.

## Camping

Optionally, characters can choose to undertake camping procedures during a rest.

### CAMPFIRE

The PCs can make a **campfire** using 3 torches; it lasts 8 hours while a PC remains near to it and casts light out to a near distance. Without a campfire, the PCs have DISADV on tasks marked with an asterisk*.

### TASKS

Each PC can do one task while camping and still get a full rest. PCs can do the same task.

The GM determines the DC of each task check based on the situation. Default to DC 12.

**Batten Down.*** INT or CON. You do not need to make checks to benefit from rest if your sleep is interrupted.

**Cook.*** INT or WIS. Each PC who consumes a ration gains +2 temporary HP that lasts 1 day.

**Craft.*** DEX. Create 2d4 pieces of mundane weapon ammunition or one torch. Alternatively, repair a broken piece of mundane gear.

**Entertain.*** CHA. Grant 1 luck token to another PC.

**Firewood.** STR or CON. Make one free campfire this rest without expending torches.

**Hunt.** STR or DEX. Find 1d4 rations. You cannot hunt if you pushed during today's travel.

**Keep Watch.*** WIS. You cannot be surprised during one half of the rest (you choose which).

**Predict.** INT or WIS. You may force a re-roll of tomorrow's weather after learning the result.

### PROCEDURE

1. 

 The PCs make task checks in any order they choose.

1. 

 After resolving tasks, the GM follows normal resting and random encounter procedures.

## Fast Travel

### OVERVIEW

Optionally, the GM may allow the PCs to **fast travel** between two hexes they have previously entered.

The PCs must use a path they have traveled previously; they cannot enter unexplored hexes.

### TIME

The GM estimates the time needed for the PCs to reach their intended destination.

The GM should factor in the travel method (mounted, sailing, etc.), distance, and terrain.

When needing a specific time length, roll 2d6 per estimated week of travel to determine how many days the journey takes.

| FAST TRAVEL |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Time Needed** | **Wounded** |
| A few days | 1:6 |
| About a week | 2:6 |
| Several weeks | 3:6 |

### WOUNDED

A longer voyage means more risk and danger. PCs who are **wounded** during the journey arrive with half their current HP.

### RATIONS

PCs who do not have a reasonable supply of rations to consume arrive drained.

**Drained.** You have DISADV on all checks and attack rolls until you successfully rest.

PCs can each make a DC 12 INT check to forage along the way and avoid becoming drained.

### PROCEDURE

1. 

 The GM estimates the time needed for the journey.

1. 

 The GM checks if the PCs are wounded. The PCs may make forage checks, if relevant.

1. 

 The PCs reach their intended destination.
