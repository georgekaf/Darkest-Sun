# Psicrystals

*Source: "Psicrystals" v1-2 (newest), by Mikrokouneli — homebrew supplement for the Darkest Sun campaign (references: Shadowdark, AD&D 2e Dark Sun, D&D 3.5 Psionics Handbook)*

A psicrystal is a Tiny construct that carries a sliver of its owner's psyche. It can be used to channel and amplify psionics, and to craft a psyblade.

## Creating One
Any manifester can "awaken" a suitable shard of crystal worth at least 50 gp. Deep-crystal veins in the Ringing Mountains, molten "sun-glass" harvested at dawn on the Obsidian Plains, or even a shard of a broken focus stone once owned by a sorcerer-king are all suitable as a psicrystal.

You must spend 5 downtime points and make a DC 12 CHA check to attune with a psicrystal.

## Loss / Destruction
Losing it imposes DISADV on CHA checks until it is recovered or replaced.

If the psicrystal is destroyed, the master is stunned for 1 round and loses 1d4 hit points permanently.

## Rarity & Stigma
True crystals are scarce; templars may confiscate them as "arcane contraband," and slave-hunters recognize that a hovering gem usually marks a valuable psion.

## Traits
Each psicrystal has one minor trait reflecting its birthplace:
- Sun-baked (faint red glow at night)
- Silt-encrusted (white color)
- Obsidian heart (grey-shadow color)

Because the crystal is literally a personality fragment, Athasian psions sometimes name the shard after ancestors or fallen clutch-mates (common among thri-kreen).

## Benefits
Psicrystals come in different qualities — uncommon (+1), rare (+2), and legendary (+3) — each granting a bonus to spellcasting checks when attuned.

# Psionic Blades

To anyone who has never seen its like, the hilt appears little more than a scavenger's curio — a hand-span metal rod wrapped in cracked hide thongs. At one end, a thumb-sized shard of cloudy quartz is socketed by bronze rivets gone green with age. The whole thing looks frail, almost makeshift — something a dune-trader might worry in his palm while haggling, or a templar's scribe might use as a ceremonial pointer.

## Composition
To craft a psionic blade, a psionicist must be at least level 3 and find a suitable metallic rod to house a psicrystal. The process is delicate and requires downtime and a DC 12 DEX check.

## Activation
Only psionicists can activate or use a psionic blade.

Once per turn, a psion can activate a psionic blade as a free action. The blade extends from the crystal for about 3 feet, made of pure psionic energy, and emits light equivalent to a *light* spell. The color of the blade matches the shard's hue.

A low audible humming sound is heard whenever the blade is out — useful for intimidation, but not stealth.

## Properties
The blade is a finesse longsword (1d8) that counts as a magical weapon. The magical bonus is tied to the rarity of the psicrystal used.

- **Psionic Blade Mastery.** A psionicist gains mastery with the psionic blade (equivalent to a fighter).
- **Defensive Flurry.** As an action, a psionicist can use the blade to make a flurry of cuts and moves to deter opponents and protect themself from melee and ranged attacks. Make an attack roll with the blade — the result becomes your AC until the start of your next turn.
- **Throw.** A psionicist can throw the psyblade with DISADV at a target within near range. The blade always returns to their hand after a hit or miss.
