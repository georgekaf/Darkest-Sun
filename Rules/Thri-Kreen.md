---
title: "Thri-Kreen"
icon: "scroll-old"
---

Thri-kreen are insectoid hunters with four arms, chitinous bodies, and alien instincts. Nomadic, pragmatic, and efficient, they survive the wastes with little need for sleep or comfort. Their minds are strange to other peoples, and they often struggle to understand soft humanoid emotions.

---

> **Languages.** Thri-Kreen

> **Natural Weapons.** They use the Gythka and Chatkcha. When attacking unarmed, the Thri-kreen claws make 1d6 damage.  Once per day, you may make a bite attack that deals 1d4 damage. If it hits, the target must pass a DC 12 CON check or become paralyzed for 1 round.

> **Multiple Limbs.** You have four arms. You can use them to hold tools, carry items, open doors, manipulate objects, or hold weapons. This does not give you extra actions or extra attacks. You may only benefit from one shield at a time.

> **Sleepless.** You do not need to sleep and can remain active day and night. You can still be affected by sleep venom, sleep magic, or similar effects.

> **Exoskeleton.** Your base AC is 14. You cannot wear any armor.
