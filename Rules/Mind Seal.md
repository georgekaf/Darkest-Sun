# Mind Seal

A worn ward against the prying mind — a band, pendant, or circlet kept against the skin.

## Effect

While worn:

- **Thoughts sealed.** Your thoughts cannot be read.
- **Silence.** You cannot receive or send telepathic messages.
- **Not hidden.** Psionic creatures can still sense that *something* is blocking them — they learn a seal is present, but not what lies behind it.
