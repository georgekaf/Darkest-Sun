# Arcane Magic on Athas

Arcane spells on Athas must draw power from life. Every meaningful area has a limited amount of usable life-force called **Verdance**.

When the party enters a meaningful area, the GM determines its **Verdance Level**. Arcane spells spend this Verdance. When it is gone, the caster must draw power from living creatures or from their own body.

|  | Terrain | **Verdance  Level** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dead** | Obsidian field, ash plain, sterile ruin, deep silt | 0 |
| **Barren** | Salt flat, sandy waste, dead canyon | 1 |
| **Sparse** | Rocky badlands, stony barrens, dry cave | 2 |
| **Living** | Scrub plain, poor field, village edge | 3 |
| **Rich** | Oasis, cultivated garden, living cavern | 4 |
| **Lush** | Forest, Tree of Life garden | 5 |

Each arcane spell costs **Verdance equal to its spell tier**.

Verdance does not usually recover during an adventure. An area that is drained remains spiritually dim, quiet, and weakened until the GM decides enough time or restoration has passed.

> # Defiler Casting
> 
> A defiler spends Verdance normally. If there is not enough Verdance, the missing amount must be taken from living creatures within the spell’s **defiling radius**.
> 
> For each missing Verdance, roll **1d6 damage**. Spread the total damage as evenly as possible among living creatures in the radius. Assign leftover damage randomly.
> 
> **Example:** A defiler casts a Tier 3 spell in a dead ruin. The spell needs 3 Verdance, but the area has 0. The defiler rolls **3d6**. If the result is 11 and there are four living creatures in range, each takes 2 damage, then the 3 leftover damage is assigned randomly.
> 
> If there are no living creatures in the radius, the defiler takes the damage instead. Because a defiler is not trained to safely channel their own vitality, this damage is **doubled**.
> 
> Undead, constructs, elementals, and spirits do not count as living creatures. Mounts, hirelings, animals, slaves, insects, and plants do.

| Spell Tier | Defile Radius |
| --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | Close |
| Tier 2 | Near |
| Tier 3 | 2× Near |
| Tier 4 | 3× Near |
| Tier 5 | Far |

> # Preserver Casting
> 
> A **Preserver** casts carefully. They spend Verdance from the area without visibly killing it. The land feels dimmer, colder, and quieter, but it is not scarred.
> 
> If the area does not have enough Verdance left, the preserver chooses one:
> 
> **Careful Casting.** If the spell is missing only **1 Verdance**, the preserver may cast the spell with **DISADV** on the spellcasting check.
> 
> Example: A preserver casts a Tier 3 spell in an area with 2 Verdance left. The spell is missing 1 Verdance, so the preserver may cast with DISADV.
> 
> **Self-Sacrifice.** Spend their own lifeforce and take **1d6 damage per missing Verdance**.
> 
> A preserver who uses their own lifeforce does not harm nearby creatures or scar the land.

> [!info]
> # Dun'qir (Crystal Dust)
> 
> A preserver or defiler can expend a pouch of **dun'qir** to enhance spellcasting.
> 
> When a caster spends a pouch of dun'qir, they gain **ADV** on their next arcane spellcasting check.
> 
> Dun'qir is rare, dangerous, and associated with the deep wounds of Athas. It is often found near obsidian, ancient ruins, dead magical sites, or places where the world was violently changed.
