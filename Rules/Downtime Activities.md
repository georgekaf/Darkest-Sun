---
title: "Downtime Activities"
icon: "file"
---

Each downtime activity requires **one day (of ingame time)** and appropriate equipment and lodging. A character must spend **Downtime Points equal to 3 + the players level.**

To succeed on a downtime activity, a character must pass the associated check. 

<u>PCs can't use luck tokens on these checks.</u>

|  | Check | DC |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Find personal insight | WIS | 12 | Find personal insight. Reroll one prior talent roll. |
| Spiritual cleansing | WIS | 18 | End one curse afflicting you. |
| Assassinate | DEX | 18 | Attempt to murder an NPC of level 4 or lower |
| Combat Riding | INT, STR or DEX | 12 | Learn to handle mount spooking and the Charge ability. |
| Weapon Training I | INT, STR or DEX | 12 | Learn to wield a new weapon (d4 damage max). |
| Weapon Training II | INT, STR or DEX | 15 | Learn to wield a new weapon (d6 damage max) |
| Weapon Training III | INT, STR or DEX | 18 | Learn to wield a new weapon (d10 damage max) |
| Armor Proficiency | INT, STR or DEX | 15 | The next step up in armor you know |
| Scribe Scroll | INT or CHA | 15 | Create a scroll of one spell you know of tier 3 or less. |
| Brew Potion | INT, WIS, CHA | 18 | Brew a potion from a spell you know. |
| Pray to the Elements | WIS | 15 | Trade one spell you know for another (same tier). |
| Craft Wand | INT, WIS or CHA | 20 | Create a wand of a spell you know of tier 3 or less. You can't create a new wand of the same spell until the prior wand breaks. |
