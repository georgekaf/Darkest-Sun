Each downtime activity requires **one day (of ingame time)** and appropriate equipment and lodging. 

Activities require **1 Downtime Point per PC level**

To succeed on a downtime activity, a character must pass the associated check. 

<u>PCs can't use luck tokens on these checks.</u>

|  | DTP Cost | Check | DC |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Find personal insight | 3 + PCL | WIS | 15 | Find personal insight. Reroll one prior talent roll. |
| Spiritual cleansing | 3 + PCL | WIS | 18 | End one curse afflicting you. |
| Assassinate | 3 + PCL | DEX | 18 |  |

## **DEX Check **

- DC 15*: Get away with a minor crime. 1:6 a party to come after you after 1d4 days.
- DC 18*: Get away with a major crime, such as murder. 1:6 a party to come after you after 1d4 days.

#  MARTIAL TRAINING (ORDER & CHAOS)

Weapons and armor training by a suitable expert. Different training is available to PCs based on their class's hit points die.

## d4. INT, STR, or DEX Check 

- DC 15*: +1 to hit or damage with one weapon. Limit once of each per weapon. 
- DC 18*: Learn to wield a new weapon (d6 damage max).

## d6. INT, STR, or DEX Check 

- DC 12*: +1 to hit and damage with one weapon. Limit once per weapon. 
- DC 15*: Learn to wield a new weapon (d10 damage max) or the next step up in armor.

## d8+. INT, STR, or DEX Check

- DC 9*: Learn to wield a new armor or weapon.
- DC 12*: +1 to hit and damage with one weapon. Limit once per weapon. 

- DC 15*: Increase the damage die of one weapon by one step (d12 damage max). Limit twice per weapon.

#  MAGICAL RESEARCH (ORDER)

Studying spells, praying to the gods, or consulting oracles. 

Spellcasters make checks for the below activities.

## INT or CHA Spellcasters 

- **DC 12:** Gain ADV on your next check to learn from a scroll. 
- **DC 15*:** Create a scroll of one spell you know of tier 3 or less. 
- **DC 15*: **Create one Potion from the following list: **Flying, Giant Strength, Invisibility. **
- **DC 20*:** Create a wand of a spell you know of tier 3 or less. You can't create a new wand of the same spell until the prior wand breaks.

## WIS or CHA Spellcasters 

- **DC 15*: **Create a scroll of one spell you know of tier 3 or less. 
- **DC 15*:** Trade one spell you know for another (same tier). 
- **DC 18*:** Create a **Potion of Healing.**
- **DC 20*:** Create a wand of a spell you know of tier 3 or less. You can't create a new wand of the same spell until the prior wand breaks.

#  SORCERY (CHAOS)

Spellcasters make checks for the below activities.

## CHA Spellcasters 

- **DC 15*:** Create a scroll of one spell you know of tier 3 or less. 
- **DC 15*:** Create one Potion from the following list: **Flying, Giant Strength, Invisibility. **
- **DC 15*:** Trade one spell you know for another (same tier). 
- **DC 18*:** Create a **Potion of Healing.**
- **DC 20*:** Create a wand of a spell you know of tier 3 or less. You can't create a new wand of the same spell until the prior wand breaks.
