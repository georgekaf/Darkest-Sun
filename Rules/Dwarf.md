title: "Dwarf"
icon: "scroll-old"
---

Determined and single-minded, dwarves pursue personal “focuses” they must fulfill before death, fearing undeath otherwise. Stocky and sturdy, they excel at labor and craftsmanship, living stubbornly against Athas’ brutality.

---

> **Languages:** Common and Dwarvish.

> **Stout.** Start with +2 HP. Roll hit points per level with advantage.

> **Stubborn Mind.** You cannot choose the Wizard class. You have advantage on checks to resist psionic effects.

> **Focus.**  Choose a sworn task, oath, or life-goal that would take at least one week to complete. Once per day, when you take an action that directly advances your Focus, gain advantage on that action. When your Focus is completed, choose a new one after your next rest.
