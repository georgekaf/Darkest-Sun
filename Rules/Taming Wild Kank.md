# Taming Wild Kank

*From actual play (ad5) — a village kank-keeper at Kharanok taught this technique to a party member, who used it to calm and lead home two wild kank instead of fighting them.*

Kank respond to a specific soothing clicking sound that mimics how domesticated kank are handled and calmed by their keepers. A character who has been shown this sound by someone experienced with kank can attempt to use it on wild kank instead of fighting them.

- **DC 18 CHA check.** Wild kank have never been tamed before, so this is hard by default — no assumed familiarity or advantage just for knowing the sound.
- On a success, the kank stop fleeing/fighting and calm down, becoming docile toward the character making the sound.
- Calmed kank will follow the character at a walking pace back to wherever they're led, without resistance.
- This does not work on kank guarding a queen or an active nest — only on wild kank encountered away from a defended hive.
