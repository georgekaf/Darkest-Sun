# Kragling

**Type:** Undead (Spawn)

**Location:** [[Blasted Spire]] (Room F)

**Description:** Kraglings are lesser undead creatures spawned from the corrupted essence of a [[Krag]]. They appear as shrunken, emaciated elf corpses with elongated limbs and needle-like fingers. Their mouths hang open in silent screams, and they move with a jerky, unnatural gait.

**Lesser Kragling:** A diminished spawn with minimal intelligence, driven only by hunger for living warmth. Easily destroyed but dangerous in numbers.

**Greater Kragling:** A more powerful spawn that retains some of the krag's weather-touched essence. Can exhale a cloud of frigid mist and may possess a crude cunning, setting ambushes among the ruins of the spire.

**Associations:** [[Krag]], [[Blasted Spire]]
