# Fire Drake

**Type:** Dragon (Young)

**Location:** [[Kragmorta]], Island of the Fire Drake

**Description:** A fire drake is a young dragon, not yet fully grown but already a fearsome predator. It has a long, sinuous body covered in rust-red scales, a crest of horn plates running from its snout to the base of its tail, and leathery wings that allow it to traverse the volcanic landscape of [[Kragmorta]]. Its breath is a cone of searing flame.

**Behaviour:** The fire drake lairs on a small island within the lava fields of [[Kragmorta]], known as the Island of the Fire Drake. It is territorial and aggressive, attacking any creature that enters its domain. It has a hoard of glittering volcanic glass and heat-warped metal.

**Associations:** [[Kragmorta]]
