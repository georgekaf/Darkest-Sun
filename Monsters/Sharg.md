# Sharg

**Type:** Magical Beast (Aquatic Monster)

**Location:** [[Blackjaw River]], [[Sharg Island]]

**Description:** The sharg is a fearsome aquatic predator whose lairs are found beneath [[Sharg Island]] in the [[Blackjaw River]]. It has a broad, armoured body covered in dark green and brown scales, six powerful webbed limbs, and a massive jaw lined with rows of serrated teeth. Its eyes are set high on its skull, allowing it to watch for prey while submerged.

**Behaviour:** A sharg is an ambush predator. It lies motionless in murky river water, its armoured back resembling a sunken log or a silt-bank. When prey comes within range, it explodes upward with terrifying speed. Its bite can crush bone and sever limbs.

**Lair:** The sharg's lair is a submerged cave system beneath [[Sharg Island]]. Bones and debris from its victims litter the cave floor. The island itself is named after the creature.

**Associations:** [[Blackjaw River]], [[Sharg Island]]
