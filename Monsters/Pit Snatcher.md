# Pit Snatcher

**Type:** Magical Beast (Tar-dwelling Predator)

**Location:** [[Tar Pits of Giustenal]]

**Description:** The pit snatcher is perfectly adapted to life in the bubbling tar pits. Its body is long and sinuous, covered in slick, black hide that sheds tar effortlessly. It has no visible eyes — it navigates by heat and vibration. A ring of segmented tentacles surrounds its circular, lamprey-like mouth.

**Behaviour:** Pit snatchers lurk just beneath the surface of the tar, invisible among the black bubbles and noxious fumes. When prey draws near, a tentacle lashes out to ensnare and drag the victim into the tar. The creature's mouth is lined with rows of sharp, chitinous teeth designed to grind bone.

**Lair:** Nests of pit snatchers dwell within submerged tunnels beneath the tar. These tunnels connect to the deeper ruins of [[Giustenal]].

**Associations:** [[Tar Pits of Giustenal]], [[Giustenal]]
