# Taraskir the Lion

**Role:** Beast-head giant — former king of Giustenal, now entombed and cursed.

**Location:** Taraskir's Tomb, Sunken City (Area 6)

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 67-68

---

## Description

Taraskir was a giant with a lion's head who once ruled Giustenal before [[Dregoth]]'s rise. He dared to challenge Dregoth and was defeated. His tomb lies in the Sunken City, sealed with enchanted chains. His skeleton lies within, wearing cursed armor and holding a +2 dragon slayer sword. He became a god-like figure to demihuman followers.

## Agenda

- *(Deceased)* His cursed tomb punishes those who steal from it

## Relationships

- **Defeated by:** [[Dregoth]]
- **Tomb located in:** Sunken City
- **His followers:** Demihumans who worshipped him as a god
- **His tomb was looted by:** [[Nallan]] (who stole the amulet)
