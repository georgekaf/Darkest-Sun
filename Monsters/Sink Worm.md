# Sink Worm

**Type:** Magical Beast (Tunnel Predator)

**Location:** [[Dregoth's Palace]] Banquet Hall

**Description:** A sink worm is a large, burrowing predator that dwells beneath stone floors, waiting to drag prey into the depths. Its body is thick and segmented, resembling a monstrous earthworm, with rings of chitinous ridges circling its flesh. Its mouth is a circular maw filled with concentric rings of backward-curving teeth.

**Behaviour:** The sink worm senses vibrations through the ground. When prey walks above its hiding spot, it bursts upward through the floor, creating a sinkhole. It attempts to drag victims into the tunnel below, where it constricts and consumes them.

**Location:** A sink worm lairs beneath the Banquet Hall of [[Dregoth's Palace]], where the flagstone floor shows hairline cracks — evidence of the creature's movements below.

**Associations:** [[Dregoth's Palace]], [[Dregoth]]
