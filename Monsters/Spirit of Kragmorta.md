# Spirit of Kragmorta

**Role:** Illusion/entity — actually created by [[Dregoth]] to manipulate the first-generation [[Dray]].

**Location:** Kragmorta

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 63-64

---

## Description

The Spirit of Kragmorta is not a real entity but an illusion and psionic manipulation created by [[Dregoth]]. It terrorizes the first-generation dray in Kragmorta, driving them to attack New Giustenal. Their deaths swell Dregoth's undead army. [[Mosak Eggstealer]]'s clan suffers under this deception.

## Agenda

- Manipulate first-generation [[Dray]] into attacking New Giustenal
- Increase [[Dregoth]]'s undead army
- Sow fear and confusion

## Relationships

- **Created by:** [[Dregoth]]
- **Torments:** [[Mosak Eggstealer]]'s clan
- **Serves:** [[Dregoth]]'s expansion plans
