# Silt Horror

**Type:** Aberration

**Location:** [[Giustenal]] Templar District shores

**Description:** A silt horror is a massive, amorphous entity that dwells in the shallow silt along the Templar District shores of [[Giustenal]]. It has no fixed form, its body a shifting mass of grey silt dotted with dozens of lidless yellow eyes and ringed with whip-like tentacles. It rises from the silt like a slow-motion eruption.

**Behaviour:** Silt horrors are patient predators. They remain submerged in the silt for long periods, their eyes just visible at the surface. When prey enters the water or draws too close to the shore, the horror erupts, engulfing victims with its body and dragging them down into the suffocating silt.

**Associations:** [[Giustenal]]
