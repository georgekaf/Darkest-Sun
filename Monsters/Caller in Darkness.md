# Caller in Darkness

**Role:** Malevolent psionic entity haunting the ruins of Giustenal.

**Location:** Giustenal ruins (the spirit-storm above and throughout the city)

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 22-25, 42-43; Monster entry

---

## Description

The Caller in Darkness is a unique psionic entity — a storm of trapped souls and psychic terror that haunts the ruins of Giustenal. It was created or empowered by [[Dregoth]]'s death and the massacre of the city. It reaches out to psionically sensitive minds, luring them to Giustenal with visions of loved ones and treasures, then drives them to madness and death. Their souls join the Caller's vortex.

It killed [[Slinnasia]], destroyed [[Passk]]'s pirate crew, and [[Nallan]] committed suicide under its influence. [[Abdaleem]] is immune (no psionic abilities).

## Agenda

- Lure psionic minds to Giustenal
- Trap souls in its vortex
- Drive victims to madness and suicide
- Unwittingly serves [[Dregoth]]'s interests by bringing victims to the city

## Relationships

- **Killed:** [[Slinnasia]], [[Nallan]], and [[Passk]]'s entire crew
- **Targets:** [[Jessix the Wanderer]], [[Jessareen]], and all psionic visitors
- **Immune to:** [[Abdaleem]] (non-psionic)
- **Master:** [[Dregoth]] (de facto — the Caller's activity serves Dregoth's purposes)
- **Feared by:** Everyone who knows of Giustenal
