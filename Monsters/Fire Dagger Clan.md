# Fire Dagger Clan

**Role:** Elf raider clan — all now undead servants of [[Dregoth]].

**Location:** Giustenal / Dregoth's fold

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 13-14

---

## Description

The Fire Dagger Clan was the first group of Silt Stalker raiders sent to Giustenal by [[Luubarra Fire Dagger]]. All were killed and raised as undead servants of [[Dregoth]]. They now serve as part of Dregoth's growing undead army beneath the ruins.

## Agenda

- *(Undead)* Serve [[Dregoth]]
- Attack any living beings entering Giustenal

## Relationships

- **Created by:** [[Luubarra Fire Dagger]]
- **Served to:** [[Dregoth]] (as undead soldiers)
- **Former tribe:** Silt Stalkers (under Chief [[Eevuu Silt Stalker]])
