# Dragon Beetle

**Type:** Magical Beast (Giant Beetle)

**Location:** [[The Lion's Den]]

**Description:** The dragon beetle is a massive, armoured insect the size of a cart. Its carapace is thick and chitinous, mottled with dark reds and blacks, and marked with the scars of old battles. It has six powerful legs tipped with hooked claws, and a pair of massive mandibles that can snap a man in two. Its antennae are long and constantly twitching, sensing the air for prey.

**Behaviour:** Dragon beetles are territorial and aggressive. They burrow into the earth to create lairs, emerging at dusk to hunt. Their mandibles deliver a crushing bite, and they can release a foul-smelling spray from their abdomen to deter larger predators.

**Location:** A dragon beetle lairs in [[The Lion's Den]], the territory of [[Taraskir the Lion]]. It competes with Taraskir for prey and the two are known to clash.

**Associations:** [[The Lion's Den]], [[Taraskir the Lion]]
