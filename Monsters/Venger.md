# Venger

**Type:** Aberration (Failed Experiment)

**Location:** [[Dregoth's Tower]] laboratory cell

**Description:** Vengers are the wretched products of [[Dregoth]]'s failed experiments in transformation. Once living creatures — humans, elves, or [[Dray]] — they have been twisted into horrible, unstable forms. A venger might have too many limbs arranged at wrong angles, flesh that ripples and bulges uncontrollably, or partial ossification that turns sections of its body to brittle bone.

**Behaviour:** Kept in laboratory cells beneath [[Dregoth's Tower]], vengers are mindless and violent. They exist in constant agony, lashing out at anything that approaches their cages. Some have developed rudimentary psionic abilities as a side effect of the failed transformation, able to project their pain as a psychic scream.

**Purpose:** Dregoth occasionally studies surviving vengers to refine his transformation processes. Most are left to rot in their cells, forgotten experiments that outlived their usefulness.

**Associations:** [[Dregoth]], [[Dregoth's Tower]]
