# Silt Serpent

**Type:** Magical Beast (Venomous Serpent)

**Location:** [[Giustenal]] ruins

**Description:** Despite its name, the silt serpent measures barely a foot in length. Its scales are the colour of dried silt — pale grey-brown with flecks of rust — making it nearly invisible among the rubble and dust of the ruined city. It possesses a flat, wedge-shaped head and two small, jet-black eyes.

**Venom:** The silt serpent's bite delivers a devastating neurotoxin. Victims feel a cold numbness that spreads from the wound, followed by paralysis and, without treatment, death within minutes. The venom is highly prized by alchemists and assassins. A single dose can fetch a high price in the markets of [[Map/Nibenay]] or [[New Giustenal]].

**Behaviour:** Silt serpents nest in cracks and crevices within Giustenal's structures. They are ambush predators, striking from cover and retreating to hidden bolt-holes. They are most active during the cool hours of dusk and dawn.

**Associations:** [[Giustenal]], [[Map/Nibenay]]
