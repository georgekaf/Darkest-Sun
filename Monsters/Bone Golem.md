# Bone Golem

**Type:** Construct (Golem)

**Location:** [[Central Gates]], [[New Giustenal]]

**Description:** A bone golem is a construct assembled from the bones of multiple creatures — humanoid, animal, and draconic — fused together by dark magic. It stands nine feet tall, a roughly humanoid shape of interlocking bones bound with strips of sinew and dark iron. Its eye sockets glow with eldritch green light.

**Behaviour:** Bone golems are mindless guardians, programmed to obey simple commands. They stand motionless at their posts until a trespasser triggers their activation. They attack with heavy bone-club fists and show no fear or hesitation. They are immune to most forms of magic that affect the mind and resistant to non-magical weapons.

**Location:** A bone golem guards the [[Central Gates]] of [[New Giustenal]], serving as the first line of defence against intruders to the Dray city.

**Associations:** [[Central Gates]], [[New Giustenal]], [[Dray]]
