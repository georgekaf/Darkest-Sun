# Krag

**Type:** Undead Elf (Rain Cleric)

**Location:** [[Blasted Spire]]

**Description:** A krag is the undead remains of an elf rain cleric, twisted by dark magic into a hateful, desiccated corpse. It retains a fragment of its former mastery over weather, summoning chilling rains and conjuring gales to harry its foes. Krags are gaunt, with cracked grey skin, hollow eyes that glow with pale light, and tattered prayer shawls still bearing the symbols of forgotten rain spirits.

**Combat:** A krag can call down stinging rain that obscures vision and extinguishes torches. It wields a staff of petrified wood and strikes with frost-rimed claws. It is resistant to cold damage and can command lesser undead.

**Head:** The head of a krag is sought by [[Abdaleem]], who pays well for proof of its destruction.

**Associations:** [[Abdaleem]], [[Blasted Spire]], [[Fire Dagger Clan]]
