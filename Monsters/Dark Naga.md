# Dark Naga

**Type:** Aberration (Intelligent Serpent)

**Location:** [[Kragmorta]], Island of the Dark Naga

**Description:** The dark naga is a highly intelligent snake-like creature with a humanoid face and a long, powerful serpentine body covered in glossy black scales. It has a crown of horny ridges around its head and eyes that glow with cold, malevolent intelligence. It is a spellcaster and a schemer, using its cunning to manipulate lesser creatures.

**Behaviour:** Unlike most monsters, the dark naga is patient and calculating. It prefers to parley, bargain, or deceive before resorting to violence. It has a hypnotic gaze that can charm the unwary, and its bite carries a potent venom. It is a capable spellcaster with access to both arcane and psionic powers.

**Lair:** The dark naga has claimed a small island within [[Kragmorta]] as its domain, known as the Island of the Dark Naga. It surrounds itself with guardian creatures and traps.

**Associations:** [[Kragmorta]]
