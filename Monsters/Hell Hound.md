# Hell Hound

**Type:** Magical Beast (Fire-breathing Hound)

**Location:** [[Kragmorta]]

**Description:** Hell hounds are muscular, dog-like creatures the size of a small pony, with sleek, black or dark crimson hide, burning red eyes, and a mouth that glows with inner fire. Their breath comes in smoky exhalations, and their footsteps leave scorch marks on stone.

**Behaviour:** Hell hounds hunt in packs, using coordinated tactics to corner prey. They breathe gouts of flame from a distance before closing for the kill. They are fearless and will pursue prey through fire and across treacherous volcanic terrain.

**Habitat:** Hell hounds roam the volcanic wastes of [[Kragmorta]], often found near thermal vents and lava flows. They are drawn to heat and fire, and their lairs are invariably near sources of geothermal activity.

**Associations:** [[Kragmorta]]
