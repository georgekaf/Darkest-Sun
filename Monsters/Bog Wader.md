# Bog Wader

**Type:** Magical Beast (Swamp Predator)

**Location:** [[Giustenal]] (dormant in a cistern)

**Description:** The bog wader is a lanky, long-limbed creature adapted to life in stagnant water. It stands nearly eight feet tall on impossibly thin legs, with a tubular body and a small, featureless head dominated by a circular maw lined with concentric rings of teeth. Its skin is grey-green and slick with slime, allowing it to remain submerged for hours.

**Behaviour:** Bog waders are ambush predators. They stand motionless in murky water, their thin legs easily mistaken for reeds or submerged roots. When prey passes close, the bog wader strikes with blinding speed, its mouth extending outward to engulf the victim.

**Location:** One such creature lies dormant in a flooded cistern within the ruins of [[Giustenal]]. It may awaken if disturbed.

**Associations:** [[Giustenal]]
