# Razorwing

**Type:** Magical Beast (Flying Predator)

**Location:** [[The Silt Road]], [[Giustenal Environs]]

**Description:** The razorwing is a winged predator whose name comes from the knife-like edge of its wing bones. Its body is slender and aerodynamic, covered in leathery hide the colour of dried blood. Its wingspan reaches twelve feet, and each wing is tipped with a razor-sharp spur of bone. Its beak is hooked and powerful.

**Behaviour:** Razorwings soar on thermals high above the wastelands, scanning for movement below. They dive at incredible speed, using their wing-spurs to slash prey before snatching it with their talons. They are known to target lone travellers and small animals along [[The Silt Road]].

**Habitat:** Razorwings nest on high cliff faces and atop crumbling ruins in the [[Giustenal Environs]]. They are a constant threat to caravans travelling [[The Silt Road]].

**Associations:** [[The Silt Road]], [[Giustenal Environs]]
