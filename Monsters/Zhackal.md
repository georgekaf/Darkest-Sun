# Zhackal

**Type:** Magical Beast (Pack Predator)

**Location:** [[Giustenal]] Merchant District cellar

**Description:** A zhackal is a gaunt, hyena-like predator with mottled brown and black fur, a sloping back, and powerful jaws. Its most distinctive feature is its unsettling, chittering laugh — a sound it makes when closing in on prey. Its eyes are bright yellow with vertical pupils.

**Behaviour:** Zhackals hunt in packs, using coordinated tactics to surround and confuse prey. They are highly intelligent for beasts and can open simple latches and work together to move obstacles. The pack in the Merchant District cellar uses the ruins as a den, emerging at night to hunt in the surrounding district.

**Habitat:** A pack of zhackals has made its lair in the cellar of a collapsed merchant house in the Merchant District of [[Giustenal]].

**Associations:** [[Giustenal]]
