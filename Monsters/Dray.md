# Dray

**Role:** Dragon-human hybrid race created by [[Dregoth]].

**Location:** New Giustenal (Kragmorta), some first-generation in Kragmorta

**Page References:** Campaign Book pp. 59-64; Monstrous Compendium

---

## Description

The dray are a race created by [[Dregoth]] through magical experimentation, blending human and dragon essence. They are his chosen people for the new Blue Age. There are two generations:

**First Generation:** Earlier, imperfect creations. Many were abandoned or struggle under Dregoth's rule. Led by [[Mosak Eggstealer]] in Kragmorta.

**Second Generation:** More stable, loyal to [[Dregoth]]. The dominant population of New Giustenal.

Notable dray include: [[Absalom]] (the first dray, now undead), [[Freiha]], [[Gatlakk]], [[Casskka]], [[Lodgden]], [[Mosak Eggstealer]].

## Agenda

- Second gen: Serve [[Dregoth]] and prepare for the Day of Light
- First gen: Survive and resist [[Dregoth]]'s manipulations
