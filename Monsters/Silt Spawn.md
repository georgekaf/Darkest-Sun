# Silt Spawn

**Type:** Aberration

**Location:** [[The Silt Road]], [[Sea of Silt]]

**Description:** A silt spawn is a nightmarish creature born from the toxic depths of the [[Sea of Silt]]. It resembles a bloated, translucent sac of murky fluid, dragging itself across the silt flats with whip-like tentacles. Within its pulsing body, the shapes of half-digested victims can sometimes be seen.

**Behaviour:** Silt spawn rise from the silt when they sense vibrations — the footfalls of travellers or the wheels of a silt skimmer. They are patient hunters, able to remain submerged beneath the silt for days. Their tentacles can reach surprising distances, and they drag prey toward the creature's central maw.

**Habitat:** Most commonly encountered along [[The Silt Road]], where they prey on caravans and lone travellers. They are also found in the shallows of the [[Sea of Silt]] near silt-shore communities.

**Associations:** [[Sea of Silt]], [[The Silt Road]], [[Giustenal Environs]]
