# Cursed Dead Dwarf

**Type:** Undead (Dwarf)

**Location:** [[The Groaning City]], Avenue of the Hanged

**Description:** The cursed dead are animated corpses of dwarves who died in agony and despair, their spirits bound to their rotting flesh by ancient curses. They appear as withered, bearded dwarves in tattered clothing, their skin stretched tight over prominent bones. Their eyes glow with a dull amber light, and they move with a relentless, shambling gait.

**Behaviour:** Cursed dead dwarves are driven by a lingering sense of duty or vengeance. They patrol the Avenue of the Hanged in [[The Groaning City]], attacking any who trespass. They feel no pain and fight until destroyed. Some retain fragments of their former skills and may wield rusted axes or hammers.

**Associations:** [[The Groaning City]]
