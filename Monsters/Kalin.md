# Kalin

**Type:** Animal (Riding Beast)

**Location:** [[New Giustenal]]

**Description:** The kalin is a powerful, scaled riding beast bred by the [[Dray]] of [[New Giustenal]]. It stands as tall as a man at the shoulder, with a long, muscular body, four sturdy legs, and a sweeping tail tipped with a bony club. Its hide is thick and leathery, ranging in colour from deep ochre to rust-red, with a ridge of horny plates running down its spine.

**Temperament:** Kalins are loyal to their riders but aggressive toward strangers. They are trained for war and respond to commands given in the Dray tongue. A kalin's bite and tail club are formidable weapons.

**Usage:** Kalin are the primary mounts of Dray templar squadrons. A squadron of kalin-riders is a terrifying sight — organised, disciplined, and capable of coordinated charges. They are also used as beasts of burden, hauling supplies across the harsh landscape surrounding the city.

**Associations:** [[Dray]], [[New Giustenal]]
