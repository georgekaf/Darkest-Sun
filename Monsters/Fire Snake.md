# Fire Snake

**Type:** Magical Beast (Lava-dwelling Serpent)

**Location:** [[Kragmorta]]

**Description:** The fire snake is a serpent of considerable size that dwells within the volcanic depths of [[Kragmorta]]. Its scales shimmer with a metallic sheen, glowing dull red to brilliant orange when heated. It has a flat, angular head and eyes like chips of molten obsidian. Its body radiates warmth even in cooler surroundings.

**Behaviour:** Fire snakes are drawn to heat and are most active near lava flows and thermal vents. They can burrow through soft volcanic rock and strike with explosive speed. Their bite delivers intense heat, and they can exhale a plume of superheated ash.

**Habitat:** The volcanic tunnels and lava tubes of [[Kragmorta]] are home to several fire snakes.

**Associations:** [[Kragmorta]]
