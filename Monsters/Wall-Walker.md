# Wall-Walker

**Type:** Magical Beast (Spider-like)

**Location:** [[Kragmorta]] hive

**Description:** Wall-walkers are pale, hairless, spider-like creatures with eight long, jointed legs ending in hooked claws. Their bodies are roughly the size of a human torso, mottled white and grey to blend with the bone-white stone of [[Kragmorta]]. They have eight gleaming black eyes arranged in two rows and mandibles that drip a corrosive saliva.

**Hive:** A colony of 24 wall-walkers inhabits a hive deep within [[Kragmorta]]. They are fiercely territorial and will pursue intruders through tunnels and across vertical surfaces with equal ease.

**Behaviour:** Wall-walkers are named for their ability to scale sheer surfaces effortlessly, including ceilings. They hunt by ambush, dropping onto prey from above or emerging from hidden fissures. Their corrosive saliva can weaken armour and flesh alike.

**Associations:** [[Kragmorta]]
