# Tembo

**Type:** Magical Beast (Wasteland Predator)

**Location:** [[Giustenal Environs]]

**Description:** The tembo is a lean, muscular predator built for speed and endurance across the blasted wastelands surrounding [[Giustenal]]. It has a sleek, tawny coat mottled with darker spots, a long tail that aids in balance during high-speed chases, and a narrow skull with keen, forward-facing eyes. Its jaws are lined with teeth designed for shearing flesh.

**Behaviour:** Tembos hunt in loose packs, using speed and coordinated tactics to run down prey across open terrain. They are tireless runners capable of maintaining pursuit for hours. They prefer to isolate a single target from a group before closing for the kill.

**Habitat:** Tembos are most commonly encountered as part of random encounter tables in the [[Giustenal Environs]]. They avoid dense ruins, favouring open silt flats and rocky badlands.

**Associations:** [[Giustenal Environs]]
