# Kharanok: Quest Chain — From Ruin to Restock

> A linked campaign arc. Each Set must be substantially completed before the next unlocks.
> Final goal: restock the Pens and restore the village to working order.

---

## Quest Givers

**Kharanok Locals**

| NPC | Role | Location |
|-----|------|----------|
| **Elder Yethras** | Aged half-elf, head of the village council. Pragmatic, exhausted, not above bending rules to survive. | Village council chamber, Shade Houses |
| **Breck** | Scarred human male, the last gate guard. Bitter about being abandoned, loyal to the village. | Main Gate |
| **Sira** | Young mul woman, head herder. Blunt, worried, knows the mountain trails better than anyone inside the walls. | Pens |
| **Dorak** | Old dwarf, ex-mine foreman. Hasn't entered the quarry in years. Knows exactly what they found — won't say yet. | Old Quarry Yard (edge) |
| **Yelka** | Middle-aged human woman, Cistern overseer. Efficient. Too efficient. Something is off. | The Cistern |
| **Oren** | Nervous young male elf, Watch Tower sentry (the corrupt one — players may not know this yet). | Watch Tower |

**Outside NPCs (Pass Through or Can Be Found)**

| NPC | Role | Where They Appear |
|-----|------|----------|
| **Jessix the Wanderer** | Roaming elf rogue-preserver, knows every Black Spine pass. Cautious, useful, expensive. | Arrives at the gate mid-Set 2 |
| **Muuton** (Sky Singers) | Elf tribe chief, trades into Nibenay. One of the few caravan leaders still willing to risk the mountain roads. | Caravans area, Set 3 |
| **Jessareen** | Elf bard in Nibenay. Contact for establishing the Nibenay trade route. Knows which merchant houses are hungry. | Nibenay (players travel to her in Set 4) |

---

## The Chain at a Glance

```
SET 1: Eyes Open          → establish what is broken and how bad it is
SET 2: Clear the Mountain → deal with gith, the lower cave threat, and bridge creatures
SET 3: Open the Roads     → physically clear routes and stop the information leak
SET 4: Bring the Traders  → establish trade identity, fix the gate toll, recruit traders
SET 5: The Water Problem  → cistern corruption, darkening water, failing spring
SET 6: Feed the Village   → audit stores, stop the animal deaths, go out and hunt
FINAL: Restock the Pens   → recover a breeding herd from the mountains
```

---

## SET 1 — EYES OPEN
*"Before you fix anything, you need to know what's broken."*

Complete all three before Set 2 unlocks. Elder Yethras gives the opening briefing and directs players to each quest giver.

---

### 1-A: The Tower Is Blind
**Quest Giver:** Elder Yethras, who brings Breck along as the person who first reported the problem.

**Situation:** Two of the three Watch Tower sentries have abandoned their posts. The remaining sentry, Oren, is sleep-deprived and unreliable. Signal mirrors are cracked. The beacon fire hasn't been tested in months. The village is functionally blind.

**Goal:** Restore the Watch Tower to full operational status: replace or recruit at least two new sentries, repair the signal equipment, and run a live test of the warning system.

**Hints:**
- Oren is grateful for help but oddly nervous when players ask about the other two sentries. He changes the subject.
- The two deserters didn't go far — one is drinking in the Shade Houses, the other left with a caravan but is camped a half-day out, too ashamed to come back. Both can be persuaded.
- The mirror repair requires black mineral polish found in the Storage Pits — Yelka controls access and will ask a favour in return.
- During the live signal test, players spot dust-movement in the eastern pass that doesn't look like weather.

**Leads to:** Set 2 (the dust-movement spotted during the test is the first sign of gith activity). Also triggers 3-C when players learn Oren has been sending covert signals.

---

### 1-B: The Guards Have Deserted
**Quest Giver:** Breck, at the Main Gate.

**Situation:** Breck is running the gate alone. He hasn't slept a full night in two weeks. The gate has technically been standing open between the third and fifth hours of morning because no one is covering the shift. Breck can't name who is responsible without starting a blood feud — the former guard captain is the brother-in-law of one of the village council members.

**Goal:** Recruit at least three new gate guards, expose and remove the former captain without fracturing the council, and establish a reliable shift rota that Breck can actually enforce.

**Hints:**
- Two strong candidates are a half-giant woman named Keth who arrived with the last caravan and hasn't left (she has nowhere else to go), and a young mul gladiator who lost his sponsor and wandered up from the lowlands.
- The former guard captain is hiding something bigger than laziness — he was bribed to leave the gate open on specific nights. The players can find out by searching his quarters or pressuring the one guard who knows.
- The open gate is directly connected to whoever has been testing the Lower Cave seal. Something has been coming and going.
- Breck will hint: "Check what's been walked through that gate at night. Look at the dust."

**Leads to:** Set 2 (the deliberate open gate feeds directly into the Lower Cave seal investigation).

---

### 1-C: The Herder Knows Something
**Quest Giver:** Sira, at the Pens.

**Situation:** Sira has been tracking strange signs in the gorge paths for weeks — dust movement, claw marks, a sound like something heavy dragging. She reported it to Elder Yethras and was dismissed. She's now keeping records on her own, in secret, and will share them only if the players can demonstrate they take it seriously.

**Goal:** Earn Sira's trust, review her findings, scout the three locations she has marked on her rough map, and produce a report for Elder Yethras detailed enough that she can no longer dismiss it.

**Hints:**
- Sira's marked locations are: the base of the eastern cliff below the hanging bridges, a dry-wash gully that leads toward the sealed cave entrance, and a spot in the Old Quarry Yard where the ground has been disturbed repeatedly.
- At one of the three locations, players find tracks that are not from below — they come from outside the village, from the mountain pass. This is gith scout sign.
- At the quarry, the disturbed ground has a partial bootprint on top of the stranger tracks. Someone inside the village has been to the same spot. The boot matches Oren's size.
- Sira will say: "I don't know what it is. But it moves at night and it goes to the sealed door and it comes back. And sometimes it doesn't come alone."

**Leads to:** Set 2 directly (confirms two threats: something inside the mountain and gith outside it).

---

## SET 2 — CLEAR THE MOUNTAIN
*"You know what's out there. Now deal with it."*

Jessix the Wanderer arrives at the gate between Set 1 and Set 2 — he spotted the players' signal test from the pass and came to investigate. He becomes available as a guide and advisor for mountain-based quests. Complete all three before Set 3 unlocks.

---

### 2-A: Gith on the Eastern Pass
**Quest Giver:** Elder Yethras (with the report from 1-C and 1-A as justification), assisted by Jessix who brings additional intelligence.

**Situation:** A gith raiding band has established a camp in the eastern pass — the same pass all caravans must use. They have been hitting caravans, picking off stragglers, and scouting Kharanok's defences. Until they are driven off or destroyed, no caravan route is safe and no trade recovery is possible.

**Goal:** Locate the gith camp, determine the band's strength, and either destroy it, drive it far enough back to clear the pass, or negotiate a passage arrangement (difficult — gith rarely negotiate, but Jessix knows one who might).

**Hints:**
- The gith have been here longer than the players think — there are three layers of camp, suggesting they've been rotating in scouts for months. Someone tipped them off that Kharanok was vulnerable.
- Jessix knows the pass well and can lead players to a high approach that avoids the gith's sentries — but he'll want payment upfront: information about what is happening in the Lower Caves.
- The gith leader carries a folded piece of leather with Kharanok's gate schedule on it — written in Common. This is Oren's handwriting.
- Among the gith camp's stolen goods: a merchant manifest from a caravan three months ago, listing goods that never arrived — including "the jar" (see Storage Pits hooks).

**Leads to:** 3-C (Oren's betrayal confirmed). Set 3 cannot open while the pass is held.

---

### 2-B: The Seal Is Failing
**Quest Giver:** Dorak, who finally speaks after players deliver the report from 1-C.

**Situation:** Something has been testing the Lower Cave seal from the inside and from outside simultaneously. Dorak believes the ritual markings are degrading. He will not go to the entrance himself — he knows what the original miners found — but he will give players the original sealing instructions, which are scratched into a stone he has kept hidden in the Quarry Yard for thirty years.

**Goal:** Reinforce the seal using Dorak's instructions, identify and deal with whatever has been clawing at it from inside, and find out who has been visiting the entrance from the outside and why.

**Hints:**
- Dorak's stone has the sealing rite and a single warning: "Do not bring light. Do not speak names. Do not look at what stands behind the door."
- The external visitor trail (from 1-C) leads to the sealed entrance and ends there — whoever it was never came back the same way. Look inside.
- What is testing the seal from inside is not a monster. It is intelligent, it is patient, and it has been here for a very long time. It cannot break the seal alone — it needs the marks removed from outside. Someone outside has been slowly chipping them.
- That someone is the same person who left the boot-print in 1-C. Confronting Oren before 3-C can accelerate the revelation, but also tips him off to run.

**Leads to:** Lower Caves remain an ongoing threat throughout the campaign, but the immediate danger is contained. Feeds into Set 5 (the darkening of the Cistern water is connected to what is below).

---

### 2-C: Something Lives Under the Bridges
**Quest Giver:** Sira (follow-up from 1-C) and Breck, who has now lost a guard to the eastern bridge.

**Situation:** The eastern bridge is impassable after dark. One of Breck's new guards crossed it at night and didn't arrive. The animals in the Pens closest to the gorge have stopped eating. Sira has seen hands on the underside of the planks.

**Goal:** Identify and clear whatever has nested under the bridge, recover the missing guard (alive or dead), and restore the bridge to safe use.

**Hints:**
- The creatures are wall-walkers — they came up from the gorge below, which connects (far down) to cave systems. They are not the same thing as what is in the Lower Caves, but they were driven upward by it.
- The missing guard is alive, wedged on a ledge fifteen feet below the bridge deck. He cannot climb out alone but can hear the players if they call down.
- Killing the wall-walkers is possible but they will return unless whatever drove them up is addressed — a partial solution. Driving them off with fire and smoke from below buys time.
- Under the bridge planks, scratched into the wood, is the same symbol that appears on the Lower Cave seal markings. Dorak, when shown it, will go pale and say nothing for a long time.

**Leads to:** Hanging Bridge defence prep (from the original hooks) can now proceed. Bridge is passable for Set 3 travel.

---

## SET 3 — OPEN THE ROADS
*"The threats are behind you. Now cut a path out."*

Muuton and the Sky Singers arrive at the gate as the gith are cleared — they were waiting in the foothills and watched the pass open. They know trade routes but want concessions before they share. Complete all three before Set 4 unlocks.

---

### 3-A: The Last Route Is Closed
**Quest Giver:** Muuton (Sky Singers), negotiating at the Caravans area with Elder Yethras present.

**Situation:** The primary caravan road through the pass is physically damaged — a rock collapse, possibly helped along by the gith. The secondary route through the Blackjaw valley is longer but passable. Muuton knows the secondary route but will not travel it until it has been scouted and confirmed safe. He offers: if players clear and verify the route, he will commit his tribe to regular trade runs between Kharanok and Nibenay.

**Goal:** Scout the Blackjaw valley route, deal with any hazards encountered, confirm it is safe for caravan travel, and report back with enough detail that Muuton will commit. Then escort his first test run.

**Hints:**
- The route passes three significant hazards: a razorwing nesting spot, a section of silt-dusted ground that hides sink-worm burrows, and a narrow defile where ambushers could box in a caravan.
- Jessix knows the route and will accompany the scout for a second payment — this time he asks for a favour: a letter of introduction to Elder Yethras endorsing him as a trusted ally of the village. He wants a safe place to operate from.
- At the razorwing spot, players find the remains of the caravan from 2-A's stolen manifest. The "jar" is missing from the wreckage. A gith corpse nearby was not killed by razorwings — it was killed by something with obsidian-tipped weapons, not gith-made.
- Muuton says casually after the route is confirmed: "There's a bard in the Happy Hurrum in Nibenay. Jessareen. She knows every merchant house in the city. Tell her Muuton sent you."

**Leads to:** Trade route to Nibenay is now viable. Unlocks Jessareen contact in Set 4.

---

### 3-B: The Caravan That Arrived Empty
**Quest Giver:** Elder Yethras, who has been sitting on this problem since before the players arrived.

**Situation:** Two months ago a wagon arrived through the gate — animals dead, driver gone, cargo looted. The manifest listed goods that were a secret: obsidian tools, pre-human artefacts, and "the jar." Yethras knows the manifest was wrong — those items were never supposed to be on a public caravan. Someone leaked the contents. She needs players to find the driver (or what remains), recover the jar, and identify who leaked the manifest.

**Goal:** Trace the lost caravan's route, find the driver, recover the jar, and identify the leak inside Kharanok.

**Hints:**
- The driver's body is at the razorwing site from 3-A (players may have already found it). He was not killed by gith or razorwings — he was killed by someone travelling with him who was waiting for the ambush.
- The jar is not with the gith wreckage. The killer took it. The killer arrived back in Kharanok before the empty wagon did.
- The manifest was leaked by a council member's spouse who owed a significant debt to an outside party. The outside party is connected to the psionic trader who has been asking about the Lower Caves.
- The jar itself: smooth walls, pre-human markings, sealed. If opened (DC 14 INT check to translate the seal-text warns against this): it contains preserved dust from a pre-mountain-age ritual. What that means — and why someone wanted it badly enough to kill for it — is a thread that extends beyond this arc.

**Leads to:** The leak is closed. The council member's spouse can be used as leverage or punished publicly. Either way, Kharanok's internal security improves.

---

### 3-C: The Tower Guard Is Selling Information
**Quest Giver:** Breck — he figured it out but needs the players to build a case that will hold up before the council.

**Situation:** Oren has been providing the gith (and whoever hired them) with Kharanok's gate schedule, caravan arrival times, and supply levels for at least four months. The evidence trail connects him to 2-A, 2-B, and 3-B. Breck won't act without proof that can be put before Yethras — he knows Oren's family will scream conspiracy otherwise.

**Goal:** Gather three independent pieces of evidence linking Oren to the information sales, present them to Elder Yethras, and handle Oren's arrest without allowing him to destroy the remaining evidence or flee.

**Hints:**
- Evidence piece one: the manifest in the gith camp (from 2-A) in Oren's handwriting.
- Evidence piece two: a small pouch of Nibenese coins in Oren's quarters, denomination consistent with what a merchant house pays for intelligence, not a guard's wage.
- Evidence piece three: a second sentry saw Oren signal the eastern pass with the mirror at odd hours three times in the past month — the sentry said nothing because Oren outranked him.
- Oren, when cornered, will give up the name of his handler — a factor working for a merchant house that has been deliberately keeping Kharanok weak to suppress competition on the black stone trade. This connects to Set 4 (the merchant houses players need to negotiate with include this one's competitors).

**Leads to:** Kharanok is no longer leaking intelligence. The merchant house name is a chip players can use in Set 4 negotiations.

---

## SET 4 — BRING THE TRADERS
*"The road is open. Now give them a reason to use it."*

Complete all three before Set 5 unlocks. Players will likely need to travel to Nibenay for at least part of this set.

---

### 4-A: The Toll System Is Broken
**Quest Giver:** Breck and Elder Yethras jointly.

**Situation:** The gate toll — Kharanok's only consistent income — is dysfunctional. Caravans bypass it or bribe their way through. The village's operating fund is effectively empty. Without revenue, guards can't be paid, repairs can't be made, and no trader will respect a settlement that can't enforce its own gate.

**Goal:** Audit the current toll records, recover what can be proven was stolen (three months of underpaid tolls from two caravan operators), establish a fair and enforceable toll rate, and collect the first full week of legitimate toll revenue.

**Hints:**
- The audit reveals the records were deliberately confused — it is not sloppiness, it is sabotage. The same hand that confused the records is the same one that leaked the manifest in 3-B.
- The two caravan operators who have been underpaying are known to Muuton. He'll lean on them if the players ask — he has leverage (shared routes, shared enemies).
- One of the two operators is the one who will eventually bring Freiha back to Kharanok (from 4-B). Players need to handle this carefully — heavy-handed recovery of back-tolls could make him refuse to carry her.
- Breck says: "We don't need to be feared. We need to be taken seriously. There's a difference."

**Leads to:** Kharanok now has operating income. Guards, repairs, and basic services are fundable again.

---

### 4-B: Bring the First Merchant Back
**Quest Giver:** Elder Yethras, who gives players Freiha's last known location (Nibenay, via Jessareen's contact).

**Situation:** Freiha ran the only permanent trading stall in Kharanok. She left eight months ago after two robberies and zero response from the council. She is now in Nibenay running a smaller but stable operation. She has no reason to return unless players can demonstrate the village has fundamentally changed — and give her a specific guarantee, not vague assurances.

**Goal:** Travel to Nibenay, find Freiha through Jessareen, hear her conditions, fulfil them (the council must formally apologise and guarantee a response time to any theft; she wants a lockable stone stall, not a tent; she wants access to the Storage Pits for overflow inventory), and escort her back.

**Hints:**
- Jessareen knows Freiha and will arrange a meeting — but she also wants something: news from the mountain roads. She has been tracking reports of something moving in the Black Spine passes and wants the players' direct account of what they found. She does not explain why.
- Freiha's conditions are achievable. The stone stall requires Dorak's help (he knows the quarry). The Storage Pit access requires Yelka's sign-off (which leads directly into Set 5 complications).
- Freiha mentions, off-hand, that someone in Nibenay has been asking questions about Kharanok's cistern specifically — a psionic trader. She doesn't know the name but the description matches the person from 3-B.
- Once Freiha returns, two smaller traders follow within two weeks. Kharanok's market begins to function again.

**Leads to:** The Cistern's role as a bottleneck becomes unavoidable. Yelka's stonewalling on Storage Pit access triggers Set 5.

---

### 4-C: The Rare Thing No One Else Has
**Quest Giver:** Muuton, on behalf of his trading contacts in Nibenay (and ideally in the same Nibenay trip as 4-B).

**Situation:** Kharanok has access to black stone, rare minerals, and pre-human artefacts recovered from the lower paths. Muuton's contacts are interested but need a consistent supply with consistent quality. Kharanok currently has no process for cataloguing, grading, or packaging what it exports — every sale is ad hoc. Muuton says: "Give me something I can promise to my buyers. A specific thing, a specific amount, a specific schedule."

**Goal:** Identify what Kharanok's actual exportable speciality is (survey the Quarry Yard remnants with Dorak, the Storage Pits with Yethras, and what the Old Quarry illegal trade was actually selling), establish a formal export catalogue, and make the first contracted deal with a Nibenay buyer Muuton introduces.

**Hints:**
- The illegal trade from the Quarry Yard was selling something genuinely rare: a specific black mineral found only in the deepest Black Spine cuts, which has psionic-dampening properties. The buyer was a psionic institution in Nibenay — and they will pay extremely well for a reliable supply.
- Dorak knows where the mineral is but refuses to go back to the quarry. Players must convince him (a short but meaningful NPC moment — Dorak's refusal is about the sound the stone makes when cut, and what he heard behind the wall).
- The formal contract deal gives Kharanok a trade identity: it is the only source of this specific mineral. This matters in Set 4-A — it upgrades the toll, because caravans now need Kharanok, not the other way around.
- The psionic institution's representative in Nibenay, when approached, mentions the jar from 3-B unprompted: "We heard someone was bringing us something extraordinary. It never arrived." They do not explain further. But they are very calm about it.

**Leads to:** Kharanok has a trade identity and an anchor contract. Commerce is now self-sustaining in principle. The Cistern must be solved or the growing population of traders and workers will break it.

## SET 5 — THE WATER PROBLEM
*"You can't run a village on a broken cistern. Something down there has been waiting."*

Complete all three (or at least 5-A and 5-C) before Set 6 unlocks. These can be run partly in parallel.

---

### 5-A: The Water Is Darkening
**Quest Giver:** Elder Yethras, who can no longer deny the problem now that Freiha and visiting traders are complaining.

**Situation:** The Cistern water has been turning black at the water-level marking the old texts label "do not drink." It clears by morning but the frequency is increasing. The Altar of Dust priests believe it is a spiritual warning. Yelka insists it is mineral runoff. Dorak, quietly, says it is neither.

**Goal:** Determine what is causing the darkening, whether it is safe or a genuine threat, and stop it. This requires access to the spring channel below the Cistern — which Yelka has been blocking.

**Hints:**
- The ancient water-level marking translates (with the text from the Lower Cave seal, now available after 2-B) as: "When the deep thing stirs, the water remembers it."
- The darkening is not the water being contaminated. It is the water reacting to something moving in the cave system below — specifically, to the thing behind the Lower Cave seal. The seal, even reinforced, does not fully stop the resonance.
- Yelka has been blocking access to the spring channel because she found something down there three months ago and reported it to no one. She sealed the access herself. What she found was a second, smaller sealed chamber — older than the first — with markings she doesn't understand.
- The solution requires the Altar of Dust priests to perform a specific rite that re-establishes the spiritual link between the Altar smoke and the underground water. The rite requires three things: a live flame from the lower depths (players must go down), an offering of water from the mountain's highest visible spring, and the original sealing formula (which Dorak has).

**Leads to:** The underground connection between the Cistern and the Lower Caves is now mapped. This becomes permanently useful information. 5-B and 5-C can now proceed.

---

### 5-B: The Overseer Is Corrupt
**Quest Giver:** Breck, who has been quietly tracking which households are running short on water and cross-referencing with which ones have aligned with Yelka politically.

**Situation:** Yelka has been granting double rations to allied families while shorting others. Two families have sick members — dehydration-related, though the village healer has been calling it a seasonal fever. Yelka controls the access ledger and will destroy it if threatened. She has a faction of four council-aligned families backing her.

**Goal:** Gather proof of the ration manipulation without Yelka destroying the ledger, expose her to Elder Yethras in a way that the four aligned families cannot simply override, remove her from the position, and install a replacement the village will accept.

**Hints:**
- The proof is not just in the ledger — the sick families' water vessels can be weighed and compared against recorded rations, showing a systematic shortfall going back five months.
- The replacement candidate Breck has in mind is a young half-elf apprentice who has been keeping her own shadow ledger because she suspected what was happening. She has every entry Yelka tried to hide.
- Yelka, when cornered, reveals what she found in the spring channel (from 5-A) as a bargaining chip — she will share it in exchange for being allowed to leave quietly instead of being publicly shamed. She is not malicious. She is afraid. What she found in that chamber frightened her badly enough that she has been trying to hoard the water as if preparing for something she cannot name.
- Elder Yethras, after the confrontation, says to the players: "How many other things in this village are held together by someone's private fear?"

**Leads to:** Water distribution is now fair and trustworthy. The village morale noticeably improves. Freiha and the other traders extend their stay.

---

### 5-C: The Spring Is Weakening
**Quest Giver:** The new Cistern overseer (Yelka's replacement), who finds the actual flow measurement after taking over.

**Situation:** The underground spring is producing less water each month. At the current improved-demand rate (more people staying, more traders, the pens being restocked), it will be critically insufficient within two months. A second source is needed.

**Goal:** Find the secondary spring that old village maps suggest exists somewhere in the Black Spine face above the gorge, clear whatever is blocking or diverting its flow, and connect it to the Cistern channel system.

**Hints:**
- Sira knows approximately where the secondary spring was — her grandmother filled water from it as a child, before it "went dry." It didn't go dry. It was diverted underground.
- Jessix, if still present, knows the cliff face above the gorge. He can lead players to the spring's approximate location. Finding the blockage requires a half-day of cliff climbing.
- The diversion is not natural. A section of shaped stone — old, pre-village construction — was placed to redirect the spring downward into the cave system rather than outward to the surface. It is not sealed; it is angled. Shifting it requires combined physical effort and, ideally, something to brace the new flow direction.
- At the diverted spring location: carvings on the shaped stone in the same script as the Lower Cave seal. Whoever built the original settlement also deliberately split the water — some for the surface, some for below. The proportions were meant to be managed. At some point, the below-portion was opened wider, starving the surface flow.

**Leads to:** Water supply is stable and growing. The village can now support the expanded population a functioning market and full Pens will require. Set 6 is unlocked.

---

## SET 6 — FEED THE VILLAGE
*"Water is solved. Now the village needs to eat."*

Complete all three before the Final quest unlocks.

---

### 6-A: The Stores Are Nearly Empty
**Quest Giver:** Elder Yethras, with the new Cistern overseer's audit as context.

**Situation:** With better visibility into the village's real resource state, the audit of the Storage Pits reveals six weeks of food. The communal reserve should be two full seasons. A respected family — the Vakreen household — has been quietly liquidating community storage goods to fund their own escape from Kharanok. They are not villains; they gave up on the place and have been preparing to leave for months. But the goods they sold belong to everyone.

**Goal:** Reconstruct the missing inventory, recover what can be recovered (the Vakreen family has already sold some goods to passing caravans, but more remains in their private pit), and decide — with Elder Yethras — what to do with the Vakreens. Leave, stay, or make restitution?

**Hints:**
- The Vakreen matriarch, confronted privately, will explain everything without resistance. She is ashamed but not apologetic — she is a mother and she did what she had to. She will offer restitution if it means they can stay without being branded criminals.
- Part of the missing inventory was sold to Muuton's last caravan at below-market price. Muuton didn't know it was stolen goods. He will return them, but wants an equivalent favour — a specific job the players owe him.
- The reconstructed inventory, plus Freiha's new supply contracts, plus what the Vakreens return, brings the village to eight weeks of food. Not two seasons. Set 6-C closes the gap.
- One of the Vakreen children found something in the storage pit while helping to secretly move goods out: a sealed clay tablet in a foreign script, apparently very old. Dorak, when shown it, takes it without a word and doesn't return it for two days.

**Leads to:** Community trust is repaired or fractured depending on how players handle the Vakreens. A bad outcome here lowers Kharanok's population before the Final quest.

---

### 6-B: The Animals Are Dying
**Quest Giver:** Sira, increasingly distressed.

**Situation:** In the past week, three goats and a mule have died with no visible cause — stiff, found in the morning. The remaining animals are restless. Sira suspects the water trough but the water tests clean. The village healer has no explanation.

**Goal:** Identify and stop the cause of death before the remaining animals — already dangerously few — are lost.

**Hints:**
- The cause is not poison or disease. A creature from the gorge below — a small, eyeless thing that resembles a pale lizard — has been getting into the pen enclosure at night through a drainage gap. It doesn't kill the animals directly; it drains warmth in a way that leaves no mark. A wall-walker juvenile, or something like one.
- Sira noticed the drainage gap a month ago and reported it for repair. The repair was never done because the stoneworker assigned to it is the same man who was doing the Vakreen family's private storage work.
- Once the creature is removed, the surviving animals stabilise. But there are now only seven animals in the Pens. The Final quest requires a minimum of twelve to be viable.
- Sira says: "Seven animals can't support this village. We need a breeding group, not a memorial."

**Leads to:** Final quest confirmed as the necessary endpoint.

---

### 6-C: Hunt for Food and Scraps
**Quest Giver:** Sira and Breck jointly, with Elder Yethras's reluctant endorsement.

**Situation:** The village cannot wait for trade to fill the food gap. Players must lead — or organise — hunting and foraging expeditions into the Black Spine Mountains to supplement the stores immediately. This also serves as a scouting function: the routes used for hunting will be the same routes used to find and recover a breeding herd in the Final quest.

**Goal:** Run at least two successful expeditions, bring back enough food to raise the reserve to twelve weeks, chart the locations of huntable animals, and identify where larger herds are moving in the pass system.

**Hints:**
- Jessix, if still around, knows where mountain goats and kank packs water during the dry months — specific waterholes that most people have forgotten. He will guide one expedition.
- The second expedition, independently run, finds signs of a large gith herd-raid to the north: a nomadic tribe's penned animals were taken, not killed. The herd is probably being driven somewhere. ## FINAL — RESTOCK THE PENS
