## **Mounts**

Mounts include beasts of burden and creatures with riders.

### **Mount**

- You have a common mount with a reliable or lovely demeanor. It comes when you call and never spooks.
- You can only have one such mount at a time.
- While riding your mount, you both get a bonus to AC equal to half your level (round down).
- You can freely leap on or off your mount once per round.
- While mounted you gain: Charge. you can charge into combat by moving at least near before attacking. Each time you do this, your melee attacks deal double damage that round.
- If your mount takes any damage you must make a DC 9 Check to remain on saddle.

### **Gear Slots**

Mounts have a number of gear slots equal to 5x their STR bonus. A rider and the rider’s carried gear (such as a backpack and worn items) occupy 10 slots.

### **Train & Acquire**

Training to use a mount requires 5x Downtime. You can acquire a mount by using 1x Downtime and pass a DC 15 CHA check for the new creature to become your mount. Lower the DC by 5 steps for each additional Downtime spent.

### **Barding**

Set of bone plate armor designed to be placed on a mount. It affords the mount the same protection as bone plate armor in melee. Due to its high cost, barding is typically only fielded by the wealthiest riders.

- Cost: 6 Salvage.
