# Welcome to ATHAS REBORN 🌞

*Darkest Sun* is a **West-Marches–style survival sandbox** set in the Tyr Region.  
We use the **Shadowdark RPG** chassis, spiced with psionics, defiling magic, and iron-scarcity gear.

| Core Tenets | What they mean here |
|---|---|
| **Open Table** | Any crew of 5 can book an expedition. |
| **Player Agency** | You choose the hex, goal, and hirelings. |
| **Persistent World** | Time passes—the desert never forgets. |

> ### Quick Start
> 
> 1. Join **#Darkest-Sun**
>    on Discord to propose a destination pin.
> 2. Assemble a party; claim a 4-hour slot with the DM.
> 3. Load extra water, one glow-rock per PC, and **TWO** light sources—Athas nights devour the unprepared!

## Harsh-World Etiquette

- **X-Card & Open Door** keep us safe.
- Recap writers earn **1 Inspiration** (usable as Advantage).
- We cheer clever conservation of torches and water!

<details>  
<summary>How does leveling work?</summary>  
Shadowdark XP-by-gold becomes **XP-by-survival**: 1 XP per ceramic piece of loot *returned alive* or for uncovering ancient psionic lore worth the same value.  
</details>
