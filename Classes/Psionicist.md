## Ubound Step

# Path of the Ubound Step

1. **Float - Focus, Self.** This power allows you float an inch above surfaces. You can move while floating.
2. ** Levitate - Focus, Self.** This power allows you lift up to a near distance vertically. You must stay within a close radius of where you left the ground. This is useful for reaching high ledges or viewing your surroundings without obstructions blocking your vision 
3. **Drift - Focus, Self. **You can fly near for the spell's duration and are able to hover in place. You cannot go higher than near distance above ground. If you fail your focus check you descend gently to the ground.
4. ** Misty Step - Focus, Self.** As your move you can teleport a double-near distance to an area you can see.
5. **Teleport - Focus, Self. **This power allows you teleport instantly to any location that you have been previously. All of your gear and up to 5 people touched are transported with you.

## Far Eye

# Path of the Far Eye

## **Sense - **Focus, Far

This power allows you to sense if an **living creature** is present within range.

As long as you focus, you can maintain a sense of its general direction but not distance. You cannot be surprised while you maintain sense.

## Burning Thoughts

# Path of the Burning Thoughts

1. Mindfire. One creature within near takes 2d6 damage. If reduced to 0 HP, it screams one hidden fear aloud.
2. Psychic Storm. All enemies within near take 3d6 damage. Mindless creatures are immune. Creatures reduced to half HP by this power must make a morale check.

## Living Flesh

# Path of the Living Flesh

1. **Mend.** One willing creature regains 4d6 HP and ends one poison, disease, paralysis, or stat-damaging bodily affliction of mundane or psionic origin.
2. **Pain.** One creature within near takes 1d6 damage and his dazed for 1 round.
3. **Heal.** One creature within close regains 2d6 HP.
4. **Lock. **One creature of LV 7 or less is paralyzed while you focus.
5. **Restore. **	One willing creature regains 4d6 HP and ends one poison, disease, paralysis, or stat-damaging affliction.
