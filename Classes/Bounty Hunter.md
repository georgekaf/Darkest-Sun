Relentless Athasian trackers who mark their quarry, hound them across silt and sun, and bring them back dead or alive. Masters of nets, bolas, whips, restraints, and cruel patience.

---

**Weapons**:  All Weapons

**Armor**: Hunter's armor

**Hit Points**: 1d8 per level

---

# Class Features

> ### Hunter's Armor
> 
> You own a personalized suit of hunter armor built from leather, bone, chitin, obsidian, cord-spools, trophy hooks, restraint clips, and scavenged metal. It is your badge, your tool rack, and your protection in the wastes.
> 
> Your Hunter’s Armor is AC 13 + your DEX modifier.
> 
> Your armor counts as 2 gear slots while it has 1-2 Fittings, or 3 gear slots while it has 3-4 Fittings. These slots include the installed Fittings.
> 
> Your armor begins with one Fitting. You gain one additional Fitting at **4th**, **7th**, and **10th **level.
> 
> If your Hunter’s Armor is lost or destroyed, you lose access to its Fittings until you recover it or rebuild it during downtime.

> ### Hunter’s Weapon
> 
> You begin play with one customized ranged weapon known as your **Hunter’s Weapon**. It is perfectly weighted, sighted, and fitted to your exact hunting style.
> 
> Choose one ranged weapon you are trained with. This weapon gains the **Precise** property.
> 
> **Precise.** When you hit your Quarry with this weapon, you deal your extra Quarry damage.
> 
> **Precious.** A Hunter’s Weapon requires rare metal fittings, such as sight pins, trigger parts, counterweights, tension rings, bolt channels, or restraint hooks. It cannot be rebuilt as a cheap, purely bone, wood, horn, or chitin weapon. If your Hunter’s Weapon is lost, stolen, or destroyed, you can replace it during downtime by acquiring rare parts and passing a DC 15 DEX or WIS check.

> ### Quarry
> 
> As your move or action, study one creature you can see or have solid sign (tracks, a firm lead, a name). Make a DC 12 WIS check to mark your quarry. Only one mark can be active at a time. It ends if you rest, the target dies, or you mark another quarry.
> 
> While a creature is your quarry:
> 
> - You have ADV on checks to gain information about and track your quarry.
> - Your attacks have ADV against your quarry.

> ### Dead or Alive
> 
> If your blow would drop a creature to 0 HP, you may choose for it to fall unconscious (stable) instead.

# Armor Fittings

Choose one Fitting each time you gain this feature. You can choose each fitting only once. Each fitting requires one slot.

| d8 | Hunter Fitting | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Reinforced Carapace** | Gain +2 AC while wearing your Bounty Armor. |
| 2 | **Locking Bracers** | You have ADV on checks to keep hold of a creature you are grappling, restraining, or netting. |
| 3 | **Heat Resistant** | You have ADV on CON checks to resist heat, thirst, exhaustion, and forced marches. |
| 4 | **Obsidian Ablator** | 1/day, when you take damage from a melee attack, reduce the damage by 1d8. |
| 5 | **Cord-Spool Harness** | 1/day, pull yourself to a near surface, ledge, beast, vehicle, or larger creature. |
| 6 | **Barbed Capture Hooks** | 1/day. When a creature escapes your grapple, you may force a re-roll on that check. |
| 7 | **Trophy Helm** | 1/day, you may force one creature within near that can see you to make a morale check. |
| 8 | **Quickdraw Rig** | Your bolas, net, and whip count as quickdraw items and do not count against your normal quickdraw limit. |
