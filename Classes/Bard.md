Dark Sun bards are cunning tricksters, poisoners, spies, performers, informants, and manipulators. They survive by wit, charm, deception, secret lore, and mastery of toxins rather than spells or magical music. In the courts of nobles and merchant houses, a bard is never merely an entertainer - they are a gift, a threat, or a knife wrapped in silk.

---

**Weapons:** Composite Bow, dagger, mace, shortbow, shortsword, spear, staff

**Armor:** Leather armor, shields

**Hit Points**: 1d6 per level

Languages**.** You know four additional common languages and one rare language.

---

# Class Features

> ### Bardic Arts
> 
> You’re trained in oration, performing arts, lore, and diplomacy. You have advantage on related checks.

> ### Trained Assassin
> 
> You have advantage on checks to sneak and hide. Your attacks deal double damage against targets that are unaware of your presence or fascinated.

> ### Presence
> 
> Make a DC 12 CHA check to enact one of the following effects. If you fail (excluding focus), you can’t use that effect again until you rest.
> 
> - **Inspire.** One target in near gains a luck token.
> - **Fascinate (Focus)**. You transfix all chosen targets of level 4 or less within near.

> ### Poisoncraft
> 
> You are trained in the use, creation, identification, and safe handling of poisons, venoms, antidotes, and toxins. You have advantage on checks related to poisons.
> 
> Make an **INT check** to prepare one poison or antitoxin. You must have your poisoner's kit and access to basic toxic materials, such as venom sacs, bitter herbs, mineral dust, insect glands, fungal spores, spoiled resin, or alchemical scraps.
> 
> Once applied to a weapon, needle, dart, cup, food, drink, salve, incense, or touched surface, it remains potent for **10 rounds**.
> 
> When you use a poison, you must pass a **DC 12 DEX check** or poison yourself by mistake. Since you are trained, you only poison yourself on a **natural 1**.
> 
> Only living creatures of **LV 10 or less** are affected by poisons.
