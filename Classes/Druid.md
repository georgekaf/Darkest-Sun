Druids are independent priests who ally themselves with various spirits of the land. He shares power with the spirit he worships, nurturing and protecting the geographical feature to which the spirit is tied. Virtually every feature of the land on Athas has a druid to protect it, but there is no worldwide organization of druids-they serve independently, living patient, solitary lives of guardianship

---

**Weapons:** Club, staff, spear, sling, whip

**Armor:** Leather armor and wooden shields

**Hit Points:** 1d6 per level

Languages. Primordial

---

# Class Features

> ### **Ethos **
> 
> You are Neutral and devoted to protecting your guarded land, preserving wilderness, and maintaining the harsh balance of Athas. If you knowingly help destroy, defile, poison, or exploit the land, you lose Shapechange and Spellcasting until you perform an act of atonement chosen by the GM.

> ### One With Nature
> 
> You have advantage on checks to identify plants & animals, survive in the wild and pathfind through wilderness. You also have advantage on reaction checks with animals. In natural terrain, you leave no tracks and can move through undergrowth at normal speed without impediment.

> ### Spellcasting
> 
> You can cast druid spells you know. You may choose three tier 1 spells from the druid spell list.
> 
> Each time you gain a level, you choose new spells based on the Druid Spells Known table.
> 
> Add your Wisdom modifier when casting druid spells. The DC is 10 + the spell’s tier.
> 
> If you fail a spellcasting check, you can’t cast that spell again until you complete a rest.
> 
> If you roll a natural 1 on a spellcasting check, you must also roll on the corresponding Wizard  Mishap table for the spell's tier

# Shapechange

1/day. You have the power to change into an animal for 10 rounds. The LV of the animal is half your level (min 0). All equipment carried by the druid is absorbed into the animal form and reappears when the druid changes back.

If a druid has lost hit points, they regain 1d4 hit points per level upon changing into an animal. If you go to 0 HP, you revert to your true form at 1 HP.

## Creature Type

- **Tracker**: HD d6, 1d6 damage, adv on WIS checks (keen senses)
- **Stalker**: HD d6, 1d4 damage, adv on DEX and stealth checks
- **Brute**: HD d8+4, 1d8 damage, adv on STR checks
- **Borrower**: HD d4, 1d4 damage + poison, burrow speed
- **Flyer**: HD d4, 1d4 damage, fly speed
