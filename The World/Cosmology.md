## **Cosmology**

Athas is a world where planar travel is very difficult, or at least travel to other planes connected with the wider multiverse. This does not mean that there are no other planes in Athas, however, but it does mean these would be vastly different from other worlds.

### **Material World**

This is the physical world, where Athas and most inhabitants live.

### **The Gray**

It is a vast, featureless plane that stretches out into infinity. This acts as a barrier between Athas and the Ethereal and Astral Planes. Athasian wizards theorize that whereas normally a soul of a deceased mortal would travel via a conduit the Outer Plane matching its alignment in life, Athas has no conduits and souls here are trapped in an eddy, until they fade away to nothing.

### **The Black**

This vast dimension of darkness separates everything that exists from everything that doesn’t. It’s an absence filled with numbing cold, endless darkness, and even living beings. The Black lurks below the surface of all things, like the leathery shell of a great egg, buried shallow and about to hatch.

### **The Hollow**

Deep within the black, inside the deepest of its shells lies the Hollow, a realm of absolute nothingness. Very few have ventured near it, having gone insane from the journey, but before turning to madness they describe the presence of a being, or maybe a force, one of true evil and darkness, lurking and languishing, as if it were trapped inside, probably for the best.
