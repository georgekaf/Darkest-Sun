## 1-A: The Tower Is Blind
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]] + [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Watch Tower]]

**Situation:** Two of three sentries have abandoned their posts. The remaining sentry [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]] is sleep-deprived and unreliable. Signal mirrors are cracked. The beacon fire hasn't been tested in months.

**Goal:** Recruit at least two new sentries, repair the signal equipment, and run a live test of the warning system.

**Hints:**
- [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]] is oddly nervous when asked about the other two sentries — he changes the subject.
- The two deserters didn't go far. One is drinking in the [[Shade Houses]], the other is camped a half-day out, too ashamed to return. Both can be persuaded.
- Mirror polish is in the [[Storage Pits]] — [[NPCs/Yelka|Yelka]] controls access and will ask a favour.
- During the live signal test, players spot dust-movement in the eastern pass that doesn't look like weather.

**Leads to:** [[Set 2 - Clear the Mountain]] (dust-movement is first gith sign). Also plants the seed for Set 3 quest 3-C when players learn Oren has been sending covert signals.

## 1-B: The Guards Have Deserted
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Main Gate]]

**Situation:** Breck is running the gate alone. It has been standing open between the third and fifth hours of morning — no one covers the shift. The former guard captain was bribed to leave posts open on specific nights, but is the brother-in-law of a council member, making him untouchable by direct accusation.

**Goal:** Recruit three new guards, expose the former captain without fracturing the council, establish a shift rota Breck can enforce.

**Hints:**
- Two candidates: a half-giant woman who arrived with the last caravan and has nowhere to go, and a young mul gladiator who lost his sponsor.
- The former captain's quarters hold payment evidence — specific coins not matching a guard's wage.
- The open gate is directly connected to whatever has been testing the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Cave seal]]. Something has been coming and going.
- Breck: *"Check what's been walked through that gate at night. Look at the dust."*

**Leads to:** [[Set 2 - Clear the Mountain]] — the deliberate open gate feeds directly into the seal investigation.

## 1-C: The Herder Knows Something
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Sira|Sira]]
**Location:** [[Pens]]

**Situation:** [[NPCs/Sira|Sira]] has been tracking strange signs for weeks — dust trails, claw marks, dragging sounds. She reported it to [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]] and was dismissed. She now keeps secret records and will share them only if players take it seriously.

**Goal:** Earn Sira's trust, scout the three locations she has marked, and produce a report detailed enough that Elder Yethras can no longer dismiss it.

**Hints:**
- Her three marked locations: base of the eastern cliff below the [[Hanging Bridges]], a dry-wash gully near the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|sealed entrance]], and disturbed ground in the [[Old Quarry Yard]].
- At the quarry: a partial bootprint on top of stranger tracks. The boot matches Oren's size.
- The pass tracks are gith scout sign, coming from outside the village.
- Sira: *"I don't know what it is. But it goes to the sealed door and comes back. And sometimes it doesn't come alone."*

**Leads to:** [[Set 2 - Clear the Mountain]] — confirms two threats: something inside the mountain and gith outside it.
