## Pit-A: The Pit Has No Master
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Shade Houses]]

**Situation:** An informal fighting pit behind the Shade Houses has been running for months — underground betting, no oversight, two fighters already badly hurt with no compensation. Breck knows it won't stop. He wants it surfaced and controlled.

**Goal:** Find the informal organiser, negotiate or pressure them into a sanctioned arrangement, establish pit rules, and set up a wager tax that goes to the village treasury.

**Hints:**
- The organiser is the half-giant woman who arrived with a caravan — possibly one of Breck's own guard recruits from 1-B. She is not a villain; she filled a vacuum.
- Two injured fighters have families prepared to cause trouble if there is no compensation structure. Breck will back whatever arrangement players reach, but won't front the compensation himself.
- [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Yethras]] opposes formalisation initially — blood sport looks bad for a village trying to attract merchants. Breck's counter: *"It's happening either way. Question is whether we eat the coin."*

**Leads to:** A sanctioned pit with a named pit master. Wager income begins flowing to the village. Side quests Pit-B and Pit-C unlock.

## Pit-B: Something Worth Watching
**Quest Giver:** [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]] (or the new pit master from Pit-A)
**Location:** [[Traders]] → [[Shade Houses]]

**Situation:** A mul fighter — known in Nibenay's pits — is passing through with Muuton's caravan. If he fights in Kharanok, merchants will talk. That talk means traffic. But a Nibenay merchant house carries a debt claim on him. Technically, he is property.

**Goal:** Get the mul to fight on his own terms, handle the merchant house agent who arrives to collect him, and run the match.

**Hints:**
- The agent has legal documentation. Players can buy out the debt (expensive), contest it through [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessareen|Jessareen]]'s Nibenay contacts (takes time), or let the mul fight and disappear before the agent acts.
- The mul is pragmatic and aware of his status. He will fight for his freedom, not for coin. He wants the documentation destroyed — not bought, destroyed.
- A full match draws every trader currently in Kharanok. Freiha's stall makes twice its usual daily income. The pit's reputation spreads down the trade road.

**Leads to:** Kharanok has a named fighter story. Traders arrive earlier and stay longer. The mul may remain in the village if his debt was resolved cleanly.

## Pit-C: The Blind Eye
**Quest Giver:** Pit master (Pit-A) + [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Shade Houses]] → [[Traders]]

**Situation:** Someone has been paying fighters to throw matches. Betting patterns show a single outside party consistently winning large. The money trail leads to the same merchant house behind the gith destabilisation from Set 3 — still working angles in Kharanok.

**Goal:** Identify the fixer, expose the network, and decide what to do with the fighters who took the money.

**Hints:**
- One of the fighters threw a match to pay for medicine for a sick child. Most were desperate, not corrupt. How players handle them shapes whether the pit keeps its fighters or loses them.
- The outside better is not in Kharanok — operating through a local intermediary who has also been skimming the pit's registered income.
- Exposing the merchant house connection gives players leverage for future negotiations — the same name that came up in 3-C. The house has been caught twice now.
- Breck, after: *"Same house. Different angle. They're patient."*

**Leads to:** Pit income is clean and growing. The merchant house is two-for-two exposed. Two fighters with nowhere better to go settle permanently in Kharanok.
