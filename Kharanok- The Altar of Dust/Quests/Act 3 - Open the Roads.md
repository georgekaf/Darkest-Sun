### Hook 1: The Last Route Is Closed
**Quest Giver:** [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]] + [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Caravans]] → Black Spine pass toward Nibenay

The primary pass collapsed — possibly helped by the gith. The secondary Blackjaw valley route is longer but passable. Muuton won't risk it unverified.

**Goal:** Scout the Blackjaw route, deal with hazards, confirm caravan-safe, escort Muuton's first test run.

**Clues:**
- Three hazards: razorwing nesting spot, sink-worm ground, narrow ambush defile
- Jessix will guide — wants a letter of introduction endorsing him as trusted village ally
- At the razorwing spot: remains of the caravan from Act 2. "The jar" missing. A gith corpse killed by obsidian weapons — not gith-made
- Muuton: *"There's a bard in the Happy Hurrum in Nibenay. Jessareen. She knows every merchant house. Tell her Muuton sent you."*

**Reward:** Nibenay trade route viable. Unlocks Jessareen contact for Act 4.

---

### Hook 2: The Caravan That Arrived Empty
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Caravans]] → route backtrack

Two months ago a wagon arrived through the [[Main Gate]] — animals dead, driver gone, cargo looted. The manifest listed secret goods: obsidian tools, pre-human artefacts, "the jar."

**Goal:** Trace the lost caravan's route, find the driver, recover the jar, identify the leak.

**Clues:**
- Driver's body at the razorwing site (Hook 1). Killed by someone travelling with him, waiting for the ambush
- The jar is not at the gith site. The killer brought it back to Kharanok before the empty wagon arrived
- The leak: a council member's spouse indebted to an outside party — connected to a psionic trader asking about the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Caves]]
- The jar: smooth walls, pre-human markings, sealed. The seal-text (translatable with Lower Cave inscription from Act 2) warns against opening it. Contents: preserved dust from a pre-mountain ritual

**Reward:** Internal leak closed. Council leverage secured.

---

### Hook 3: The Tower Guard Is Selling Information
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Watch Tower]]

Oren has been supplying the gith and a merchant house handler with gate schedules, caravan timings, and supply levels for four months. Breck needs a solid case — Oren's family will cry conspiracy without proof.

**Goal:** Gather three independent pieces of evidence. Present to Yethras. Arrest Oren before he destroys evidence or flees.

**Clues:**
- Evidence 1: gith camp manifest from Act 2 Hook 1 — Oren's handwriting
- Evidence 2: Nibenese coins in Oren's quarters — intelligence payment denomination, not a guard's wage
- Evidence 3: a second sentry saw Oren signal the eastern pass with the mirror at odd hours
- Oren, cornered, gives up his handler's name: a merchant house factor deliberately keeping Kharanok weak to suppress black stone competition

**Reward:** Leak stopped. Handler's identity is a leverage chip for Act 4 negotiations.
