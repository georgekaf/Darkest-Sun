### Hook 1: Gith on the Eastern Pass
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]] + [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer|Jessix]]
**Location:** Black Spine Mountains — eastern pass

A gith raiding band camps in the only caravan route into Kharanok. They hit caravans and scout defences. No trade recovery possible until they're gone.

**Goal:** Clear the pass — destroy, drive off, or negotiate passage.

**Clues:**
- Three camp layers suggest months of rotating scouts. Someone tipped them off
- Jessix knows a high approach that avoids sentries — his price: info about the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Caves]]
- Gith leader carries Kharanok's gate schedule written in Common — [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]]'s handwriting
- Stolen goods include a merchant manifest listing "the jar" — never arrived

**Reward:** Pass clear. Oren confirmed as traitor. Trade route accessible.

---

### Hook 2: The Seal Is Failing
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]] (speaks after players deliver Hook 3 report from Act 1)
**Location:** [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Caves]]

Something tests the seal from both inside and outside. Dorak won't approach — but gives players the original sealing instructions on a stone kept hidden for thirty years.

**Goal:** Reinforce the seal using Dorak's instructions. Identify what claws from inside and who visits from outside.

**Clues:**
- Dorak's stone: *"Do not bring light. Do not speak names. Do not look at what stands behind the door."*
- What tests from inside is intelligent, patient, and very old. It cannot break the seal alone — needs exterior markings removed. Someone has been slowly chipping them
- That someone left the boot-print found in Act 1 Hook 3
- Confronting Oren early accelerates revelation but tips him off

**Reward:** Immediate threat contained. Feeds into Act 5 (the [[The Cistern|Cistern]] darkening is connected to what is below).

---

### Hook 3: Something Lives Under the Bridges
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Sira|Sira]] + [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Hanging Bridges]]

The eastern bridge is impassable after dark. A guard crossed at night — didn't arrive. Pen animals near the gorge stopped eating. Sira saw hands gripping the underside of the planks.

**Goal:** Clear what nested under the bridge. Recover the missing guard (alive — wedged on a ledge 15 feet below deck). Restore the bridge.

**Clues:**
- Wall-walkers driven up from the gorge by whatever stirs in the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Caves]]. Connected but not the same thing
- Fire and smoke from below drives them off. Killing works but they return unless the root cause is addressed
- Under the planks: scratched symbol matching Lower Cave seal markings. Dorak, when shown, goes pale and says nothing

**Reward:** Bridge passable. Defensive prep possible. Travel route for Act 3 secured.
