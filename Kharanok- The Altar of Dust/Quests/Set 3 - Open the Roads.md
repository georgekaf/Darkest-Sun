## 3-A: The Last Route Is Closed
**Quest Giver:** [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]] (Sky Singers) + [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Caravans]] → Black Spine pass toward Nibenay

**Situation:** The primary pass is physically damaged — a collapse, possibly helped by the gith. The secondary Blackjaw valley route is longer but passable. Muuton knows it but won't risk it unverified. His offer: if players scout and confirm it safe, he commits his tribe to regular Kharanok–Nibenay runs.

**Goal:** Scout the Blackjaw valley route, deal with its hazards, confirm it is caravan-safe, then escort Muuton's first test run.

**Hints:**
- Three hazards on the route: a razorwing nesting spot, sink-worm ground, and a narrow ambush defile.
- [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer|Jessix]] will accompany the scout for a second payment — this time, a letter of introduction to [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]] endorsing him as a trusted village ally. He wants a base to operate from.
- At the razorwing spot: remains of the caravan from 2-A. "The jar" is missing from the wreckage. A gith corpse nearby was killed by obsidian weapons — not gith-made.
- [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]] after confirmation: *"There's a bard in the Happy Hurrum in Nibenay. [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessareen|Jessareen]]. She knows every merchant house in the city. Tell her Muuton sent you."*

**Leads to:** Nibenay trade route viable. Unlocks Jessareen contact for [[Set 4 - Bring the Traders]].

## 3-B: The Caravan That Arrived Empty
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]]
**Location:** [[Caravans]] → route backtrack

**Situation:** Two months ago a wagon arrived through the [[Main Gate]] — animals dead, driver gone, cargo looted. The manifest listed secret goods: obsidian tools, pre-human artefacts, and "the jar." Yethras knows this manifest was never supposed to be public. Someone inside the village leaked it.

**Goal:** Trace the lost caravan's route, find the driver, recover the jar, and identify the leak.

**Hints:**
- The driver's body is at the razorwing site from 3-A (players may have already found it). He was not killed by gith — he was killed by someone travelling with him, waiting for the ambush.
- The jar is not at the gith site. The killer brought it back to Kharanok before the empty wagon arrived.
- The leak came from a council member's spouse indebted to an outside party — connected to a psionic trader asking about the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Caves]].
- The jar: smooth walls, pre-human markings, sealed. The seal-text (translatable with the Lower Cave inscription from 2-B) warns against opening it. Contents: preserved dust from a pre-mountain ritual. Someone killed for it. Why is a thread beyond this arc.

**Leads to:** Internal leak closed. Council member's spouse can be used as leverage or punished.

## 3-C: The Tower Guard Is Selling Information
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Watch Tower]]

**Situation:** [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]] has been supplying the gith — and a merchant house handler — with Kharanok's gate schedules, caravan timings, and supply levels for at least four months. Evidence connects him to quests 2-A, 2-B, and 3-B. [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]] needs a case solid enough to stand before [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Yethras]] — [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]]'s family will cry conspiracy without solid proof.

**Goal:** Gather three independent pieces of evidence, present them to [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]], and arrest [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]] before he destroys remaining evidence or flees.

**Hints:**
- Evidence 1: the gith camp manifest from 2-A in [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]]'s handwriting.
- Evidence 2: Nibenese coins in [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]]'s quarters — denomination matching merchant house intelligence payments, not a guard's wage.
- Evidence 3: a second sentry saw [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]] signal the eastern pass with the mirror at odd hours three times in the past month. He said nothing because [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]] outranked him.
- [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]], when cornered, gives up his handler's name: a factor from a merchant house that has been deliberately keeping Kharanok weak to suppress competition on the black stone trade. This name is a leverage chip for [[Set 4 - Bring the Traders]] negotiations.

**Leads to:** Kharanok no longer leaks intelligence. The handler's identity is an asset going forward.
