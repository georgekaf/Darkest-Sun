> **Everything leads here.**

**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Sira|Sira]] + [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]], together.
**Location:** [[Pens]] → canyon in the Black Spine passes

**Situation:** The canyon herd from 6-C, the gith guards, the nomadic tribe who lost their animals, and [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]]'s hidden pen combine into a single operation. This is the culmination of the entire arc.

**Goal:** Recover the captured herd from the canyon, deal with the gith, negotiate with the nomadic tribe, bring the animals back to Kharanok, and establish them in the main [[Pens]] plus [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]]'s hidden second pen as a reserve.

**Hints:**
- Three canyon approaches: direct (twelve gith guards), high approach via [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer|Jessix]] (two hours longer, arrives above the guards), and a dry riverbed approach the nomadic tribe's scout will share — if players have committed to compensating his people.
- The gith are holding the animals for trade, not food. The buyer is the same merchant house from Set 3 quest 3-C — paying the gith to continue destabilising the region. Taking a gith trader alive confirms this.
- [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]]'s hidden pen: three hours from Kharanok, carved into a sheltered canyon fold with old water channels that can be reopened. It pre-dates the village by centuries. Holds forty animals.
- Minimum success: 20 animals returned (mix of goat, mule, kank). Full success: the entire herd of 80 plus a breeding agreement with the nomadic tribe for annual livestock exchange — making the [[Pens]] self-sustaining.

---

## Outcome

The [[Pens]] are restocked. Kharanok has:
- A food supply
- A stable water supply ([[The Cistern]])
- A trade identity and open road to Nibenay
- A functioning [[Main Gate]] and [[Watch Tower]]
- A sealed (if uneasy) [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Cave]]
- A working [[Traders]] district
