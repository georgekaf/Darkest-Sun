### Hook 1: The Tower Is Blind
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]] + [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Watch Tower]]

Two of three sentries abandoned their posts. The remaining sentry [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]] is sleep-deprived. Signal mirrors cracked. Beacon fire untested for months.

**Goal:** Recruit sentries, repair signals, run a live test.

**Clues:**
- Oren is nervous when asked about the deserters — changes the subject
- One deserter drinking in [[Shade Houses]], one camped a half-day out, ashamed
- Mirror polish in [[Storage Pits]] — [[NPCs/Yelka|Yelka]] controls access, wants a favour
- During the signal test: dust-movement in the eastern pass — not weather

**Reward:** Warning system restored. Player sees first gith sign → Act 2.

---

### Hook 2: The Guards Have Deserted
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Main Gate]]

Breck works the gate alone. It stands open between 3rd and 5th hour — no cover. The former guard captain was bribed to leave posts open on specific nights but is untouchable (council member's brother-in-law).

**Goal:** Recruit 3 guards, expose the former captain, establish a shift rota.

**Clues:**
- Two candidates: half-giant woman (last caravan, nowhere to go), young mul gladiator (lost sponsor)
- Former captain's quarters hold payment evidence — coins not matching a guard's wage
- The open gate connects to whatever tests the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Cave seal]]
- Breck: *"Check what's been walked through that gate at night. Look at the dust."*

**Reward:** Gate secured. Direct lead to the seal investigation in Act 2.

---

### Hook 3: The Herder Knows Something
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Sira|Sira]]
**Location:** [[Pens]]

Sira tracked strange signs for weeks — dust trails, claw marks, dragging sounds. Reported to Yethras but she was dismissed. She keeps secret records now. Will share only if players take her seriously.

**Goal:** Earn Sira's trust. Scout three marked locations. Produce a report Yethras cannot dismiss.

**Clues:**
- Three sites: base of eastern cliff below [[Hanging Bridges]], dry-wash gully near the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|sealed entrance]], disturbed ground in [[Old Quarry Yard]]
- At the quarry: partial bootprint on stranger tracks. Boot matches Oren's size
- Pass tracks are gith scout sign from outside
- Sira: *"I don't know what it is. But it goes to the sealed door and comes back. And sometimes it doesn't come alone."*

**Reward:** Confirms two threats — something inside the mountain and gith outside. Leads to Act 2.
