## 2-A: Gith on the Eastern Pass
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Elder Yethras|Elder Yethras]] + [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer|Jessix]]
**Location:** Black Spine Mountains — eastern pass

**Situation:** A gith raiding band has camped in the eastern pass — the only caravan route into Kharanok. They have been hitting caravans and scouting the village's defences. Until cleared, no trade recovery is possible.

**Goal:** Locate the gith camp, assess strength, and destroy it, drive it back, or negotiate passage ([[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer|Jessix]] knows one gith who might deal — rare, not reliable).

**Hints:**
- Three camp layers suggest months of rotating scouts. Someone tipped them off that Kharanok was vulnerable.
- [[Kharanok- The Altar of Dust/NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer|Jessix]] can lead players via a high approach that avoids the gith's sentries — his price: information about the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Caves]].
- The gith leader carries a folded leather with Kharanok's gate schedule written in Common. It is [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]]'s handwriting.
- Among stolen goods: a merchant manifest listing items that never arrived — including "the jar" (relevant to [[Storage Pits]]).

**Leads to:** Set 3 quest 3-C (Oren confirmed as traitor). [[Set 3 - Open the Roads]] cannot unlock while the pass is held.

## 2-B: The Seal Is Failing
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]] (speaks after players deliver the 1-C report)
**Location:** [[Lower Caves (Sealed)]]

**Situation:** Something tests the seal from both inside and outside simultaneously. [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]] won't approach the entrance himself — but he gives players the original sealing instructions scratched on a stone he has kept hidden in the [[Old Quarry Yard]] for thirty years.

**Goal:** Reinforce the seal using [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]]'s instructions, identify what has been clawing at it from inside, and find out who has been visiting the entrance from outside.

**Hints:**
- [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]]'s stone: *"Do not bring light. Do not speak names. Do not look at what stands behind the door."*
- What tests from inside is not a monster — it is intelligent, patient, and very old. It cannot break the seal alone; it needs the exterior markings removed. Someone outside has been slowly chipping them.
- That someone left the boot-print found in quest 1-C.
- Confronting [[NPCs/Oren|Oren]] before Set 3 quest 3-C can accelerate the revelation but tips him off to run.

**Leads to:** Immediate threat contained. Feeds into [[Set 5 - The Water Problem]] — the darkening [[The Cistern|Cistern]] water is connected to what is below.

## 2-C: Something Lives Under the Bridges
**Quest Giver:** [[NPCs/Sira|Sira]] + [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]
**Location:** [[Hanging Bridges]]

**Situation:** The eastern bridge is impassable after dark. One of [[NPCs/Breck|Breck]]'s new guards crossed it at night and didn't arrive on the other side. The pen animals closest to the gorge have stopped eating. [[NPCs/Sira|Sira]] has seen hands gripping the underside of the planks.

**Goal:** Identify and clear what has nested under the bridge, recover the missing guard (alive — wedged on a ledge fifteen feet below the deck), and restore the bridge to safe use.

**Hints:**
- Wall-walkers, driven up from the gorge below by whatever stirs in the [[Lower Caves (Sealed)|Lower Caves]]. Connected, but not the same thing.
- Fire and smoke from below drives them off. Killing them works, but they return unless the root cause is addressed.
- Under the bridge planks, scratched into the wood: the same symbol found on the Lower Cave seal markings. [[NPCs/Dorak|Dorak]], when shown it, goes pale and says nothing for a long time.

**Leads to:** [[Hanging Bridges]] defensive prep can now proceed. Bridge passable for [[Set 3 - Open the Roads]] travel.
