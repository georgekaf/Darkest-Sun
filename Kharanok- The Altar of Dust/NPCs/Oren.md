# Oren

**Role:** Watch Tower sentry — traitor, information broker for a merchant house
**Found at:** [[Watch Tower]]
**Faction:** Kharanok Local (compromised)

---

## Background

The first payment wasn't ideological. A merchant house factor handed Oren a coin purse and asked for a copy of the gate rotation schedule, framing it as a routine favour with unusual benefits. Oren was underpaid, under-appreciated, and certain the village was dying anyway. The second payment was bigger. The third required him to mirror-signal the eastern pass at odd hours three times. By the time the gith were using his information to raid caravans and people were dying, Oren had crossed so many lines he could no longer locate the first one. He is not a cruel man — he winces when he hears about the caravan deaths — but he is not brave enough to stop. He stays at his post because leaving would look suspicious and because the handler has made it clear, in careful indirect language, that loose ends get resolved. He wants out. He doesn't know how to get out without the handler finding out.

---

## Agenda

Keep the arrangement invisible for as long as possible. Don't confront anything directly. If evidence starts accumulating, destroy it first and run second. If cornered, give up the handler immediately — he prepared that trade a long time ago and has been waiting for someone to take it.

---

## Wants & Needs

- **Wants:** Enough money and enough distance to leave Kharanok before the handler decides he's a liability
- **Needs:** An exit that isn't death — he is not loyal to the merchant house, only trapped by it

---

## Goals

- Escape without arrest
- If caught: trade the handler's name and the merchant house affiliation for leniency — he has everything they need for the Set 3 and Set 4 investigations
- If players offer him a different deal before the arrest, he will take it

---

## Roleplay Notes

Nervous in a specific way: over-helpful, over-agreeable, slightly too fast to smile. Volunteers information he wasn't asked for as a way to redirect attention. Laughs slightly too quickly at other people's jokes. When directly questioned about something connected to his betrayal, he changes the subject by asking a question back — *"Why — did someone see something?"* He is not a skilled liar; he is a man who has been practicing being a mediocre liar for four months. When genuinely cornered — evidence in hand, no redirect possible — he deflates quickly. The handler's name comes out quietly. He sounds more relieved than afraid.

**If players choose to use rather than arrest him:** He becomes a motivated, frightened, extremely useful source who will do almost anything to stay in their protection rather than face the handler alone. Play that angle honestly — he is not heroic, but he is functional when he has something to lose.

---
