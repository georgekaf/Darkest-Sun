# Jessareen

**Role:** Elf bard — Nibenay merchant house contact, covert investigator
**Found at:** Happy Hurrum Tavern, Nibenay (Set 4)
**Faction:** Outsider — Nibenay / independent

> *Full NPC profile: [[../../NPCs/Jessareen|Jessareen]]*

---

## Background (Kharanok Arc)

Since the psychic influx from Kataal's visions, Jessareen has been doing the only thing that keeps the fragments organised: mapping them to physical places and events she can verify. Something is moving through the Black Spine passes — not bandits, not gith, something older and more deliberate — and fragments of what she was shown match the geography in ways she hasn't been able to explain to anyone without sounding broken. The merchant house relationships and the tavern intelligence network are, for her now, the instruments of a larger investigation. When Muuton tells her players came out of the Black Spine and survived what they found, she wants to meet them. She's been waiting for a source she can cross-reference her fragments against.

---

## Agenda (Kharanok)

Learn what the players found in the passes — specifically anything pre-human, bearing unfamiliar inscriptions, or connected to the deep geology. Cross-reference it with the psychic fragments and the merchant intelligence reports she's been collecting.

---

## Wants & Needs

- **Wants:** Information that fills the gaps in what Kataal showed her — specifically physical confirmation of things she has only seen as vision-fragments
- **Needs:** A partner who can believe her without requiring a full account of the visions — she is tired of having to justify what she knows before anyone will listen

---

## Goals

- Piece together the complete pattern of what is moving through the Black Spine
- Find whether the psionic trader asking about Kharanok's cistern connects to her Kataal fragments
- Keep the merchant house relationships intact long enough to be useful — they are cover as much as work

---

## Roleplay Notes

Charming, controlled, occasionally elsewhere. When something a player says connects to a vision fragment, she pauses mid-sentence — a full stop, not a stammer — and then returns to normal conversation as if nothing happened. Professionally warm; personally guarded. Will handle Freiha's return negotiation efficiently and well, because she is genuinely good at it, but her real interest is always the debrief about what players found in the passes. When she asks what they found, she already knows some of it from the merchant reports. She is checking whether they are telling the truth. If they are, her manner shifts — slightly, noticeably, in the direction of someone who just found out they are not alone with something large.
