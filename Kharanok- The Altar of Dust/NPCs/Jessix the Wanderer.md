# Jessix the Wanderer

**Role:** Elf rogue-preserver — mountain guide, information broker, outside investigator
**Found at:** Arrives at [[Main Gate]], Set 2
**Faction:** Outsider — independent

> *Full NPC profile: [[../../NPCs/Jessix the Wanderer|Jessix the Wanderer]]*

---

## Background (Kharanok Arc)

Jessix spotted Kharanok's signal test from the eastern pass — a flash pattern he hadn't seen in months from a direction that should have been dark. He came to investigate. He had already been watching something shift in the mountains: gith movements that don't fit raiding patterns, a route through the Black Spine that something is using regularly but that appears on no elf map he trusts, and a buyer in Nibenay asking questions about deep Black Spine geology that feel more like intelligence-gathering than commerce. He suspects the entity behind the Lower Cave seal and whatever he's been tracking in the passes are connected. He wants access to what Kharanok's people know about the caves. His knowledge of the mountain routes is his side of the trade.

---

## Agenda (Kharanok)

Establish himself as indispensable to the village as a mountain guide. Trade his pass knowledge for access to the Lower Cave research. Secure a letter of introduction from Elder Yethras — a legitimate base of operations he has never had.

---

## Wants & Needs

- **Wants:** A stable base in Kharanok and access to whatever Dorak and the players learn about the Lower Caves
- **Needs:** To know whether the thing moving through the passes and the thing behind the seal are the same — he has been tracking a half-picture for months

---

## Goals

- Secured and endorsed role as Kharanok's mountain advisor
- Full understanding of what is below Kharanok and what connects to the pass activity
- Letter of introduction from [[Elder Yethras]] (his stated price for the Blackjaw route escort)

---

## Roleplay Notes

Reads situations quickly and doesn't commit until he knows the price and the odds. Names his price directly and early — before you can make an offer — because ambiguity is how he has been underpaid before. Not mercenary in character: he genuinely wants to protect people, but he has been doing it alone long enough to know that vague goodwill doesn't pay for provisions. When the Lower Caves come up in conversation, his attention sharpens — the jokes stop, the angles close. His second ask (the letter from Yethras, Set 3) reveals what he actually wants: not money but legitimacy.
