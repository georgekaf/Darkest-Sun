# Breck

**Role:** Last gate guard — de facto head of security by default
**Found at:** [[Main Gate]]
**Faction:** Kharanok Local

---

## Background

Breck was never ambitious. He took the gate post because it was steady work and he was good at it. When the others left — first for better pay in Nibenay, then out of fear, then through bribery — Breck stayed. Not because he believed the village would survive, but because walking away from a post felt wrong. He is aware he is being used as a symbol of loyalty more than as a functional guard. He resents that, and resents the former captain who made him complicit by leaving him behind to clean up. His loyalty is real. The bitterness at what that loyalty has cost him is also real. He has been running the gate alone long enough that the anger is mostly buried under exhaustion now.

---

## Agenda

Restore the gate to function. Assemble a guard rotation he doesn't have to personally carry. Hold the former captain accountable without fracturing the council — because fracturing the council would leave him more alone than before.

---

## Wants & Needs

- **Wants:** To not be the only one doing this
- **Needs:** Respect rather than being merely relied upon — there is a difference, and he knows it

---

## Goals

- Full guard rotation staffed and functional
- Toll system clean and enforceable
- Oren arrested with evidence solid enough to survive a family challenge in council

---

## Roleplay Notes

Blunt. Doesn't soften things. Speaks in short sentences when tired — which is always. Asks direct questions: *"What did you see?" "Why?" "How many?"* Doesn't use the word "please" often. Will say thank you once, briefly, when something is actually fixed — and then not mention it again. The line *"We don't need to be feared. We need to be taken seriously. There's a difference"* is his philosophy. If players earn his respect it shows not in warmth but in him asking their opinion before acting.
