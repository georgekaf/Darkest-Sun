# Elder Yethras

**Role:** Half-elf village council head — de facto sole leader
**Found at:** [[Shade Houses]]
**Faction:** Kharanok Local

---

## Background

Yethras has run Kharanok's council for eleven years. The last six he has spent mostly alone, as the other members died, fled, or became dependent on him for decisions they didn't want to make themselves. He is not corrupt and was never cruel, but he dismissed Sira's warnings, ignored the open gate, and looked away from the darkening water — because each problem required energy he no longer had. He is not a coward; he is a man who has been governing a slow emergency for so long that the emergency became normal. He knew the village was failing. He had been hoping something would change before he had to admit it publicly. When the players arrive, they are that something.

---

## Agenda

Maintain the appearance of functional council authority while quietly allowing the players to do the work he cannot do alone. Stay close enough to stay relevant; stay far enough to avoid blame if things collapse.

---

## Wants & Needs

- **Wants:** The village to survive without full public exposure of how badly things have decayed under his watch
- **Needs:** Someone willing to take real responsibility and share the weight — he has been alone with it too long

---

## Goals

- See the quest chain completed before the village fractures irreparably
- Issue the formal council apology to Freiha without it being used as evidence of broader failure
- Leave something functional behind him when he is finally done

---

## Roleplay Notes

Speaks slowly. Thinks before answering — long enough that you wonder if he heard you. Has a habit of restating what you said before responding, as if verifying it was actually said. Not defensive; resigned. When he receives bad news he goes still before reacting. When he endorses something reluctantly, he doesn't say so directly — he just says *"Do what you think is necessary."* His one unguarded moment is the line: *"How many other things in this village are held together by someone's private fear?"* — if he says it, he is asking about himself.
