# Yelka

**Role:** Cistern overseer — corrupt administrator, keeper of a second secret
**Found at:** [[The Cistern]]
**Faction:** Kharanok Local

---

## Background

Yelka was exceptional at her job before she found the second sealed chamber. Three months ago, following a blocked drain into the spring channel, she descended further than she had before and discovered a hairline crack in a lower vault wall. Something behind the crack looked back. She closed it herself, packed it with mortar, told no one. Since then she has run the cistern with rigid, airless control — perfect records, strict procedures, no variance. The corruption was not calculated. It was a frightened woman doing the only thing that felt like safety: controlling what she could, giving advantages to people who could protect her, maintaining a surface so ordered that nothing could reach up through it. She blocked access to the spring channel so no one else would find what she found. The ration-skimming, the political favouritism — none of it started as malice. It started as fear.

---

## Agenda

Maintain control of the cistern at all costs. Keep the lower vault crack sealed. Prevent anyone from descending into the spring channel. Do not be the one responsible for what is below.

---

## Wants & Needs

- **Wants:** For the thing below to stop being her problem — handed off, sealed by someone else, anywhere but on her
- **Needs:** To not be alone with the knowledge of what she found — three months is a long time to know something that large in silence

---

## Goals

- Exit the overseer role without public shaming if possible — she will negotiate
- Transfer responsibility for the second chamber to someone capable of handling it
- Survive the exposure with some dignity intact

---

## Roleplay Notes

Efficient, clipped, professional. Answers questions with answers, not conversation. Records are perfect; procedures are followed; no wasted movement. The tell is geography: she does not stand at the south end of the cistern. All her business is conducted at the north end, and she steps back when anything draws her attention south. Players who notice this and press on it will see her composure fracture briefly. When cornered, she does not fight — she trades. Information for dignity. She is not a villain; she is exhausted and terrified and has been carrying this alone for three months. When she finally offers the second chamber as a bargaining chip, she sounds relieved rather than calculating.
