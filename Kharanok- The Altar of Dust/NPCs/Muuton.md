# Muuton

**Role:** Chief of the Sky Singers — primary trade route contact and caravan operator
**Found at:** Arrives at [[Caravans]], Set 3
**Faction:** Outsider — Sky Singers elf tribe

> *Full NPC profile: [[../../NPCs/Muuton|Muuton]]*

---

## Background (Kharanok Arc)

Muuton's tribe has been waiting in the foothills since the gith took the eastern pass — watching, running alternative routes at reduced profit, checking conditions. He knows the Black Spine well enough to know the difference between a temporarily clear pass and a structurally open one. That's why he waited. When the gith were cleared, he moved immediately. Kharanok is not sentiment for him — it's geography. The village sits on the only natural pass junction that cuts four days off the Nibenay run. If Kharanok is viable, the Sky Singers profit. If it isn't, Muuton needs to know quickly so he can stop betting on it. The offer to scout and commit to the Blackjaw valley route is not generosity — it's the best available deal structured to benefit both parties, which is the only kind of deal Muuton makes.

---

## Agenda (Kharanok)

Verify Kharanok's viability as a trade node. Commit to regular runs if the Blackjaw route is confirmed safe. Integrate Kharanok into the Sky Singer Nibenay network via [[Jessareen]].

---

## Wants & Needs

- **Wants:** A reliable, contracted trade route he can commit to his buyers on a schedule
- **Needs:** Partners who will be honest about what Kharanok can and cannot deliver — he has been burned by optimistic assessments before

---

## Goals

- Blackjaw valley route confirmed safe and running
- Kharanok integrated into Sky Singer trade network with anchor contracts
- Honest ongoing partnership — he is not interested in being told what he wants to hear

---

## Roleplay Notes

Direct, professional, not unfriendly. Doesn't waste time on pleasantries but will share food and ask about road conditions — because road conditions are information. Makes decisions quickly when he has enough data; will tell players explicitly when he needs more before deciding. Respects action over explanation. Will not hold a grudge over hard negotiations, but will remember if players tried to deceive him and will factor it into every deal afterward. When he introduces players to [[Jessareen]], he is making an endorsement — that means something in Nibenay's trade circles, and he knows it. His favour in Set 6 (six riders for the canyon operation) is given without ceremony: he agreed to a favour, he delivers a favour.

---
