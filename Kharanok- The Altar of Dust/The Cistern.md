# The Cistern

The **Cistern** is the most important point in the village. Without it, Kharanok could not exist. It collects water welling up from a hidden underground spring deep in the rock. The water is considered sacred — practically and symbolically — because it keeps the settlement alive.

Access to the Cistern is strictly controlled. Guards, overseers, and village elders record who takes water and how much. In dry periods, rations are cut and tensions rise. Small thefts of water are punished harshly, as they are treated as a threat to the entire community.

Around the Cistern are stone channels, vessels, carved water-level markings, and old prayers cut into the rock. Some say that when the water darkens, something moves deep below the village.
